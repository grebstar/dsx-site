He tore the labrum in his right shoulder and had season-ending surgery announced on Mar. 9, 2024. Indiana then declined to give him a rookie-scale extension in the autumn of 2025, and traded him four months later.

The trade, Feb. 5, 2026. Indiana sent Mathurin, Isaiah Jackson, a 2026 first-round pick, a 2029 first-round pick and a 2028 second to the Clippers, and received Ivica Zubac and Kobe Brown. The AP wire wording is flat: the Pacers traded Mathurin, Jackson and three draft picks to the Clippers for Zubac. He was hurt again in Los Angeles — a toe injury that had cost him three straight games with 11 to play — and the fit never arrived: he logged 69 possessions next to Darius Garland and Kawhi Leonard in total.

In August his representatives asked the Clippers to withdraw his $8.8 million qualifying offer, and on Aug. 26, 2026 he agreed a two-year, $16 million deal with New Orleans that includes a player option. He averaged 17.6 / 5.4 / 2.4 across 54 games with the two clubs.
