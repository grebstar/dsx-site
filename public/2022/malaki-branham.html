SCOUTING REPORT

San Antonio took him #20 overall out of Ohio State. He had just gone for 13.7 points, 3.6 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 32 games at 29.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #24, pick 20. The boards ranged from #15 to #58 on him, a spread of 43 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 66 games, 32 starts, 23.5 minutes — 10.2 / 2.7 / 1.9 on 44.0% shooting. That ranked him #19 in the class and marked him at $62.59, +57.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 28 games, 0 starts, 9.8 minutes — 4.6 / 1.6 / 0.8 on 47.3% shooting. That ranked him #40 in the class and marked him at $8.31, -79.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $39.70, today's mark of $8.31 is -79.1%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

The first trade, July 9, 2025. San Antonio sent Branham, Blake Wesley and a 2026 second-round pick to Washington and received Kelly Olynyk, who had been a Wizard for roughly 48 hours. His role in Washington was small: 28 games, all as a reserve, 4.6 points and 1.6 rebounds in 9.8 minutes.

The second, February 2026. In the three-team Anthony Davis deal, Washington received Davis, Jaden Hardy, D'Angelo Russell and Dante Exum; Dallas received Khris Middleton, AJ Johnson, Tyus Jones, Marvin Bagley III and picks; Charlotte received Branham. He had originally been routed to Dallas, and the Mavericks expanded the trade to send him on to Charlotte in exchange for Tyus Jones. Charlotte waived him shortly after the deadline.

He signed a G League deal with the Cleveland Charge, played Summer League with the Cavaliers, and on Aug. 26, 2026 took an Exhibit 10 — a non-guaranteed camp contract — with Orlando, where he projects behind Jevon Carter and Jase Richardson.
