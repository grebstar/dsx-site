Chicago took him #18 overall out of Arizona. He had just gone for 8.0 points, 4.8 rebounds and 3.9 assists in 37 games at 27.7 minutes a night. He went 18 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #36, drafted 18. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #23 to #58 on him, a spread of 35 places across 5 of them.

That ranked him #34 in the class and marked him at $16.15, +22.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $13.22, today's mark of $16.15 is +22.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Hours later New York sent Terry, two second-round picks and cash to New Orleans for Jose Alvarado. On Feb. 6 New Orleans, a million dollars from the tax line, waived him to open a roster spot.

Philadelphia signed him to a two-way on Feb. 10, calling him a high-energy player and versatile perimeter defender, converted him to a standard multi-year deal on Apr. 10 and picked up the option on June 29 — then waived him on July 25, 2026 as the cap casualty when the club signed LeBron James. He signed a one-year Exhibit 10 with Golden State on Aug. 28, 2026 to compete for a camp spot.
