Milwaukee took him #24 overall out of G League Ignite in the NBA G League. He had just gone for 15.3 points, 6.4 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 23 games at 34.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #22, pick 24. The boards ranged from #15 to #32 on him, a spread of 17 places across 5 of them.

That ranked him #37 in the class and marked him at $11.89, -74.9% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $47.43, today's mark of $11.89 is -74.9%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #18 in the All-Rookie vote among 2022 draftees.

The one trade, Feb. 6, 2025: Milwaukee sent Beauchamp to the Clippers and received Kevin Porter Jr. Everything after that is waivers and two-ways. The Clippers cut him on Mar. 1, 2025; the Knicks signed him to a two-way three days later. Philadelphia signed him in October 2025, waiving Emoni Bates to do it, then waived him on Oct. 18, then signed him to a two-way again on Dec. 26, 2025 off seven Delaware games at 20.7 points on 49.5% shooting.

The two levels diverged completely. In 14 NBA games he averaged 6.8 / 2.3 / 1.1 on 43.4% and 34.0% from three, with a season best of 17 points on 7-of-13 in 30 minutes against Detroit on Mar. 12, 2026. In 24 games for the Delaware Blue Coats he averaged 23.7 / 5.3 / 4.0 on 48.3% and 37.0%, with a 40-point game on 16-of-21 and six threes in February. Philadelphia released him on July 1, 2026 and on Aug. 12, 2026 he signed with FC Bayern Munich, saying he wanted to grow as a player there. He played 135 NBA games with twelve starts.
