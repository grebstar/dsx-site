SCOUTING REPORT

Sacramento took him fourth overall out of Iowa. He had just gone for 23.5 points, 8.7 rebounds and 1.5 assists in 35 games at 31.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #7, pick 4. The boards ranged from fifth to #16 on him, a spread of 11 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 80 games, 78 starts, 29.8 minutes — 12.2 / 4.6 / 1.2 on 45.3% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, +83.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 23 games, 22 starts, 34.5 minutes — 14.0 / 5.7 / 1.7 on 42.0% shooting. That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, -68.8% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $269.91, today's mark of $84.15 is -68.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished fourth in the All-Rookie vote among 2022 draftees.

Sacramento paid him first and lost him second. The extension came on Oct. 16, 2025 — five years, $140 million through 2030-31, signed on his rookie deal and therefore carrying poison-pill trade math of roughly $25.1 million incoming against $11.1 million outgoing. Three days short of a week later he tore the ulnar collateral ligament in his left thumb, had surgery on Oct. 13, and did not debut until Nov. 20.

What followed was a four-injury cascade: a mild right calf strain in late December, a left ankle sprain, then a re-aggravation of that ankle against Houston on Feb. 25, 2026 that ended his year. He started 22 of the 23 games he played, at 14.0 / 5.7 / 1.7 with 1.6 blocks, on career lows of 42.0% from the field and 27.7% from three — a number that has fallen every season he has played. An arthroscopic procedure on May 13, 2026 removed loose bodies from the same ankle.
