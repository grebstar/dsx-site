SCOUTING REPORT

New Orleans took him eighth overall out of G League Ignite in the NBA G League. He had just gone for 11.7 points, 6.9 rebounds and 4.9 assists in 28 games at 32.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #10, pick 8. The boards ranged from fifth to #17 on him, a spread of 12 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 59 games, 11 starts, 17.7 minutes — 3.8 / 3.2 / 2.3 on 41.8% shooting. That ranked him #22 in the class and marked him at $47.43, -72.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: 76 games, 76 starts, 33.2 minutes — 11.9 / 6.8 / 5.9 on 51.7% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, +132.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $170.70, today's mark of $396.43 is +132.2%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

The trade, agreed June 29 and finalised July 6, 2024. Atlanta received Daniels, Larry Nance Jr., E.J. Liddell, Cody Zeller, a 2025 first-round pick via the Lakers and a 2027 first; New Orleans received Dejounte Murray. It is the direction people invert, so it is worth stating plainly: Daniels was the asset going to Atlanta, not the return coming back.

He had a meniscectomy on his left knee in February 2024. Everything since has gone the other way. He won Most Improved Player for 2024-25 with 332 points on 44 first-place votes, finished second in Defensive Player of the Year voting to Evan Mobley by 35 first-place votes to 25, and made All-Defensive First Team. On Oct. 21, 2025, one day before the deadline, he signed a four-year, $100 million extension. In 2025-26 he made All-Defensive Second Team with nine first-place and 32 second-place votes on a Hawks team that went 46-36 and took the sixth seed in the East.
