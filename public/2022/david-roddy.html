SCOUTING REPORT

Philadelphia took him #23 overall out of Colorado State. He had just gone for 19.2 points, 7.5 rebounds and 2.9 assists in 31 games at 32.9 minutes a night. He went 18 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #41, drafted 23. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #29 to #58 on him, a spread of 29 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 70 games, 4 starts, 18.0 minutes — 6.7 / 2.8 / 0.8 on 42.9% shooting. That ranked him #21 in the class and marked him at $51.92, +613.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 5 games, 0 starts, 14.6 minutes — 8.0 / 4.0 / 0.8 on 50.0% shooting. That ranked him #44 in the class and marked him at $4.55, -37.5% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $7.28, today's mark of $4.55 is -37.5%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Draft night: Philadelphia made the 23rd pick and sent Roddy's draft rights and Danny Green to Memphis for De'Anthony Melton. February 2024, three teams: Phoenix received Royce O'Neale and Roddy; Memphis received Chimezie Metu, Yuta Watanabe and a 2026 first-round swap; Brooklyn received Keita Bates-Diop, Jordan Goodwin, three future seconds and the draft rights to Vanja Marinkovic. July 2024: Phoenix sent Roddy to Atlanta for E.J. Liddell, a straight swap of 2022 second-round-priced forwards.

After that it is waivers. Atlanta cut him in February 2025; he took short deals with Philadelphia and then a Houston two-way; Toronto gave him an Exhibit 10 on July 11, 2025 and waived him in October. He spent most of 2025-26 with Raptors 905, 35 games at 12.1 / 5.9 / 3.7 on 47.6% in 27.6 minutes, and Denver signed him to a two-way on Mar. 5, 2026. He played five Nuggets games at 8.0 and 4.0 in 14.6 minutes on 50% shooting, with a season-high 21 points on 8-of-12 in a 127-107 win over Oklahoma City on Apr. 11.

On July 24, 2026, after 181 NBA appearances, he signed with Hapoel Jerusalem of Israel's Winner League on a one-plus-one, playing outside the United States for the first time.
