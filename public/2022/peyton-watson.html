SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him #30 overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 3.3 points, 2.9 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 32 games at 12.7 minutes a night. He went 5 picks ahead of his composite, which was #35. The boards ranged from #33 to #58 on him, a spread of 25 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 23 games, 2 starts, 8.1 minutes — 3.3 / 1.6 / 0.5 on 49.2% shooting. That ranked him #36 in the class and marked him at $13.22, -9.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 54 games, 40 starts, 29.6 minutes — 14.6 / 4.9 / 2.1 on 49.1% shooting. That ranked him twelfth in the class and marked him at $131.64, +799.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $14.64, today's mark of $131.64 is +799.2%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Draft night, June 23, 2022. Oklahoma City held the 30th pick and made the selection; Denver paid for it, sending JaMychal Green and a protected 2027 first-round pick to Oklahoma City — along with the more favourable of two 2023 seconds and of two 2024 seconds on RealGM's log — and receiving the pick and Watson's rights. The slot on this board says Oklahoma City because Oklahoma City made the pick; the player was Denver's from the moment it was made.

2025-26 was the breakthrough and the injury in one season. He made 40 starts on efficient shooting and said afterwards he had been in line to win Most Improved Player when a Grade 2 hamstring strain arrived in New York. He aggravated it two to three weeks into rehab working out with coaches, re-injured it against Utah, and an MRI showed the same Grade 2 tear. He missed 25 of Denver's final 30 games and the playoff series against Minnesota, and said later that he had felt pressure to get back and get games in before the postseason.

He left in a five-team sign-and-trade agreed Aug. 19, 2026. Cleveland received Watson from Denver and Cam Whitmore from Washington; Denver received a 2031 unprotected Cleveland first, a 2032 Sacramento second and Julian Reese; the Clippers received Max Strus; Washington received Tre Mann, cash and a 2027 Clippers second; Charlotte received Dennis Schroder and cash; and the draft rights to Ismael Kamagate went from the Clippers to Cleveland in the same deal. Watson signed for four years and $88 million with a player option and a trade kicker.
