Washington took him tenth overall out of Wisconsin. He had just gone for 19.7 points, 8.2 rebounds and 2.1 assists in 31 games at 34.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #9, pick 10. The boards ranged from seventh to #13 on him, a spread of 6 places across 5 of them.

Against a frozen IPO of $196.79, today's mark of $0.81 is -99.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

The trade, Feb. 6, 2025. Washington sent Marvin Bagley III, Johnny Davis and a 2025 second-round pick to Memphis and received Marcus Smart and a 2025 first-round pick, in a three-team deal that also involved Sacramento. Five days later Memphis still had him listed as not with the team, and he was waived rather than played.

From there the path leaves the league. He signed in the G League with the Westchester Knicks, and on Oct. 8, 2025 the Wisconsin Herd — Milwaukee's affiliate — acquired his returning player rights from Westchester for the returning rights to Diego Bernard and Jalen Lewis, a 2026 G League draft pick and a 2026 international pick. He spent 2025-26 with the Herd at 15.6 / 5.9 / 1.7 with 1.6 steals in 27.8 minutes on 44.5% shooting and 34.8% from three. In June 2026 he signed with Glint Manisa Basket of the Turkish Basketball Super League, saying he left for Turkey on Aug. 5. He played 112 NBA games, all for Washington.
