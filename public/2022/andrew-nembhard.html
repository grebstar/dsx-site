SCOUTING REPORT

Indiana took him #31 overall out of Gonzaga. He had just gone for 11.8 points, 3.4 rebounds and 5.8 assists in 32 games at 32.1 minutes a night. He went 6 picks ahead of his composite, which was #37. The boards ranged from #31 to #58 on him, a spread of 27 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 75 games, 63 starts, 27.6 minutes — 9.5 / 2.7 / 4.5 on 44.1% shooting. That ranked him eleventh in the class and marked him at $149.42, +1157.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 57 games, 57 starts, 31.3 minutes — 16.9 / 2.8 / 7.7 on 44.2% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +2616.5% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $11.89, today's mark of $322.90 is +2616.5%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished eleventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2022 draftees.

He has never been traded. Indiana extended him on July 24, 2024 — three years, $58.65 million — running through 2027-28, and by June 2026 he was entering the second year of it.

The role changed because of one play. Tyrese Haliburton tore his right Achilles tendon in the first quarter of Game 7 of the 2025 NBA Finals on June 22, 2025; the MRI confirmed it two days later and he had surgery the same day with a roughly twelve-month timeline. Nembhard became the full-time starting point guard and primary ball handler on a team that went 19-63, playing a career-high 31.3 minutes across 57 games. Rick Carlisle said openly that Indiana could not play him into oblivion; Aaron Nesmith said he would have to learn to play at Nembhard's pace. Nembhard's own account is that he had to voice his opinion more.

He missed time in three separate blocks: a left shoulder strain in the opener that cost seven games, a right calf contusion that cost three in March, and cervical and lumbar soreness that ended his season with the final seven. There is a prior for the defensive work: in Game 6 of the 2025 Eastern Conference finals he took the Jalen Brunson assignment after Aaron Nesmith fouled out early and finished with 14 points, eight assists, three rebounds, six steals and a block at a team-high plus-25.
