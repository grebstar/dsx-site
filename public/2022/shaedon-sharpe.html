SCOUTING REPORT

Portland took him seventh overall out of Kentucky. He had no pre-draft season to price. He enrolled at Kentucky in January 2022 and never played a game before declaring. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #6, pick 7. The boards ranged from fifth to eighth on him, a spread of 3 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 80 games, 15 starts, 22.2 minutes — 9.9 / 3.0 / 1.2 on 47.2% shooting. That ranked him twelfth in the class and marked him at $131.64, -59.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: 50 games, 42 starts, 29.4 minutes — 20.8 / 4.3 / 2.6 on 45.2% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, -29.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $322.90, today's mark of $229.10 is -29.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished twelfth in the All-Rookie vote among 2022 draftees.

The player taken seventh without a college game has become a starting shooting guard and a chronic absence in the same career. In 2025-26 he averaged 20.8 / 4.3 / 2.6 with 1.4 steals on 45.2 / 33.7 / 78.7 splits across 50 games, reaching double figures in 44 of them — and missed 32 games with calf and fibula problems. Portland extended him anyway, four years and $90 million agreed Oct. 19, 2025 through 2029-30, paying $20 million in 2026-27.

Then the roster changed under him. Portland acquired Ja Morant from Memphis on June 30, 2026, sending Jerami Grant, Kris Murray and $1 million in cash the other way, and with Damian Lillard healthy Sharpe was set to come off the bench. On Aug. 24, 2026 he tore the lateral meniscus in his right knee in an individual workout. Surgery followed at UCLA; the reported timeline is six months, which puts him back around late February and out for more than 50 games. It is the third time in five seasons he has missed 30-plus.
