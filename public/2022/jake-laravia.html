Draft night, June 23, 2022. Minnesota made the pick at 19 and traded LaRavia's draft rights and a 2023 second-round pick to Memphis, receiving the 22nd pick, Walker Kessler, and the 29th pick, TyTy Washington Jr. Three years later Memphis sent him on: Feb. 6, 2025, LaRavia and a trade exception to Sacramento for Colby Jones, Alex Len and a 2028 second-round pick.

He signed with the Lakers in July 2025 on two years and $12 million, fully guaranteed with no options, and did the thing this class has found hardest: he played all 82 games, one of eighteen players in the league to do so. Through his first ten Lakers games he was at 12.2 / 5.2 / 2.7.
