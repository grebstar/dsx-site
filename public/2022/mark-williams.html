SCOUTING REPORT

Charlotte took him #15 overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 11.2 points, 7.4 rebounds and 0.9 assists in 39 games at 23.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #15, pick 15. The boards ranged from eleventh to #24 on him, a spread of 13 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 43 games, 17 starts, 19.3 minutes — 9.0 / 7.1 / 0.4 on 63.7% shooting. That ranked him #17 in the class and marked him at $75.99, -18.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 60 games, 55 starts, 23.6 minutes — 11.7 / 8.0 / 1.0 on 64.4% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, +25.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $93.47, today's mark of $116.93 is +25.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #17 in the All-Rookie vote among 2022 draftees.

He was traded twice, and the first one did not happen. On Feb. 6, 2025 Charlotte agreed to send him to the Lakers for Dalton Knecht, Cam Reddish, a 2030 pick swap and an unprotected 2031 first-round pick. The trade was rescinded on Feb. 9 after he failed his physical in Los Angeles; the deadline had passed, so it could not be amended, and every player and pick went back where it started.

The completed one came on draft night 2025. Charlotte sent Mark Williams to Phoenix and received Liam McNeeley, the 29th pick, and a 2029 first-round pick — the least favourable of Phoenix's, Cleveland's and Minnesota's, top-five protected. Vasilije Micic also went from Phoenix to Charlotte.

Phoenix got the healthiest season of his career: 60 games, 55 starts, roughly 11 and 8 with a block a night on 64% shooting in 23.6 minutes, after a back injury had held him to 19 games in 2023-24 and a strained foot tendon had cost him most of the 2024 training camp. A stress reaction in the third metatarsal of his left foot, announced Mar. 6, 2026, cost two to three weeks and the whole first-round series against Oklahoma City. Phoenix re-signed him anyway: three years, $38 million fully guaranteed, official July 7, 2026, at $11.7m, $12.7m and $13.6m, with no injury clauses.
