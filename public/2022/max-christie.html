The Lakers took him 35th on Jun. 23, 2022 and signed him to a multi-year contract on Jul. 8, 2022. After two seasons in a bench role, they tendered a qualifying offer on Jun. 29, 2024; he became a restricted free agent on Jul. 1 and agreed to a four-year, $32 million deal with a player option, reported by ESPN's Adrian Wojnarowski on Jun. 30, 2024 and signed on Jul. 6, 2024.

On Feb. 2, 2025 he was part of the three-team trade that sent Luka Doncic to the Lakers: Dallas received Anthony Davis and a 2029 first-round pick along with Christie, the Lakers also received Maxi Kleber and Markieff Morris, and Utah received Jalen Hood-Schifino and second-round picks. He played 78 games in 2024-25 (46 for the Lakers, 32 for Dallas, where his scoring rose from 8.5 to 11.2 a night) and became a starter for Dallas in 2025-26.
