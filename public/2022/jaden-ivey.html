SCOUTING REPORT

Detroit took him fifth overall out of Purdue. He had just gone for 17.3 points, 4.9 rebounds and 3.1 assists in 36 games at 31.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 5. The boards ranged from third to fourth on him, a spread of 1 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 74 games, 73 starts, 31.1 minutes — 16.3 / 3.9 / 5.2 on 41.6% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, -34.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: 37 games, 5 starts, 18.1 minutes — 8.5 / 2.5 / 1.8 on 44.5% shooting. That ranked him #26 in the class and marked him at $33.31, -93.3% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $33.31 is -93.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished sixth in the All-Rookie vote among 2022 draftees.

He broke his left fibula in 2024-25 and played 30 games. He had arthroscopic surgery on his right knee before the 2025-26 opener, missed the first month, and was carrying patellofemoral pain when Detroit moved him.

The trade, Feb. 3, 2026, three teams. Detroit sent Ivey to Chicago. In return Detroit received Kevin Huerter and Dario Saric from Chicago, plus a protected 2026 first-round pick swap from Minnesota; Minnesota sent Mike Conley to Chicago and took back cash. Spotrac's transaction line reads "Traded to Chicago (CHI) from Detroit (DET) as part of a 3-team trade." Ivey played four games for Chicago, three of them starts, before the knee was shut down for the season on Mar. 26.

On Mar. 30, 2026 Chicago waived him. The team's own transaction notice gives the reason as conduct detrimental to the team, following remarks he made on Instagram Live about the league's Pride Month; Billy Donovan said the comments did not reflect the organization's values. He cleared waivers before restricted free agency and was still unsigned on Aug. 21, 2026 — his four-year, $32,951,083 rookie deal remains his only NBA contract.
