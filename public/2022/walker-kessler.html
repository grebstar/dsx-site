SCOUTING REPORT

Memphis took him #22 overall out of Auburn. He had just gone for 11.4 points, 8.1 rebounds and 0.9 assists in 34 games at 25.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #23, pick 22. The boards ranged from #22 to #31 on him, a spread of 9 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 74 games, 40 starts, 23.0 minutes — 9.2 / 8.4 / 0.9 on 72.0% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +1336.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 5 games, 5 starts, 30.8 minutes — 14.4 / 10.8 / 3.0 on 70.3% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, +353.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $43.38, today's mark of $196.79 is +353.6%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished third in the All-Rookie vote among 2022 draftees.

He was moved twice before he played a game. Draft night: Minnesota sent the 19th pick, Jake LaRavia, and a future second-round pick to Memphis and received the 22nd pick, Kessler, and the 29th pick, TyTy Washington Jr. Then July 6, 2022: Utah sent Rudy Gobert to Minnesota and received Malik Beasley, Patrick Beverley, Jarred Vanderbilt, Leandro Bolmaro, Kessler's draft rights, unprotected 2023, 2025 and 2027 first-round picks, a top-five protected 2029 first and 2026 swap rights. Kessler went to Utah as part of the price of Gobert, not as the return for him. He did not play the 2022 Las Vegas Summer League: he signed on July 9 and was left off the roster.

The five-game 2025-26 line on this page is a shoulder. He had missed much of the preseason with a left shoulder problem, it resurfaced against Portland, and a torn labrum ended his season with surgery reported Nov. 5, 2025 — after five games at career highs of 14.4 points and 10.8 rebounds with 1.8 blocks. He and Utah had not agreed a rookie extension, and he said he was focusing on rehab rather than the contract.

In July 2026 he left in a sign-and-trade: Utah received unprotected 2031 and 2033 Lakers first-round picks plus 2028 and 2030 swap rights, and Kessler signed with the Lakers for four years and $130 million.
