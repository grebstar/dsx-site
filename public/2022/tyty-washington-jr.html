SCOUTING REPORT

Memphis took him #29 overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 12.5 points, 3.5 rebounds and 3.9 assists in 31 games at 29.1 minutes a night. The boards had him 9 places above where he went, at #20. The boards ranged from #14 to #28 on him, a spread of 14 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 31 games, 2 starts, 14.0 minutes — 4.7 / 1.5 / 1.5 on 36.3% shooting. That ranked him #28 in the class and marked him at $27.93, -50.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: 16 games, 0 starts, 5.5 minutes — 1.3 / 0.4 / 1.1 on 47.4% shooting. That ranked him #45 in the class and marked him at $3.76, -93.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $56.94, today's mark of $3.76 is -93.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Draft night, two legs. Minnesota sent the 19th pick, Jake LaRavia, and a 2023 second-round pick to Memphis for the 22nd, Walker Kessler, and the 29th, Washington. Minnesota then sent the 29th pick and two future seconds to Houston for the 26th, which became Wendell Moore Jr. So Memphis made the pick and Washington's rights finished in Houston, two teams away from the club whose name is on the slot.

He sprained his left knee in the 2022 preseason and was in the G League by November. In July 2023 Houston sent Washington, Usman Garuba, two future seconds and $1.1 million to Atlanta for the draft rights to Alpha Kaba; days later Atlanta sent Rudy Gay, Garuba, Washington and a second to Oklahoma City for Patty Mills. Oklahoma City waived him in August. Two-ways in Milwaukee and Phoenix followed, then a Clippers cycle of four signings and waivers across 2025.

The last of them worked. He averaged 19.9 / 5.8 / 5.1 with 1.7 steals in 16 G League games for the San Diego Clippers, and the LA Clippers signed him to a two-way on Dec. 27, 2025. He became a free agent on July 1, 2026 and on Aug. 24, 2026 signed with LDLC ASVEL of France's LNB Elite and the EuroLeague as their new point guard, wearing No. 3, after 74 NBA games.
