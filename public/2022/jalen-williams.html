SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him twelfth overall out of Santa Clara. He had just gone for 18.0 points, 4.4 rebounds and 4.2 assists in 33 games at 34.8 minutes a night. He went 13 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #25, drafted 12. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #18 to #58 on him, a spread of 40 places across 5 of them.

2022-23: 75 games, 62 starts, 30.3 minutes — 14.1 / 4.5 / 3.3 on 52.1% shooting. That ranked him second in the class and marked him at $808.24, +2121.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 33 games, 33 starts, 28.4 minutes — 17.1 / 4.6 / 5.5 on 48.4% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +369.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $36.38, today's mark of $170.70 is +369.2%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished second in the All-Rookie vote among 2022 draftees.

He has never been traded and he won a championship in June 2025 with a torn ligament in his shooting wrist. Sam Presti announced the surgery on June 30, 2025; Williams had played all 23 postseason games taped and braced. A follow-up procedure on Oct. 31, 2025 removed a screw that was causing irritation and cost him the first 19 games of 2025-26, which Oklahoma City went 18-1 without him. He returned on Nov. 28.

Both hamstrings then went. A right hamstring strain at Miami on Jan. 18, 2026 carried a two-week re-evaluation; a left hamstring injury in the third quarter of Game 2 against Phoenix on Apr. 23, 2026 cost him the rest of that series and all of the next, and he came back off the bench in Game 6 of the conference finals for the first time in his playoff career. San Antonio won Game 7 111-103.

The five-year rookie-scale maximum extension he agreed on July 11, 2025 carries All-NBA escalators. Because he appeared in fewer than 65 games he was ineligible for MVP, Defensive Player of the Year and All-NBA in 2025-26, which left the cap hit at 25% rather than 30% — an injury that paid the team.
