SCOUTING REPORT

Raynaud averaged 20.2 points and 10.6 rebounds with 1.9 made threes and 1.4 blocks as a Stanford senior, making first-team All-ACC. He joined Kevin Durant and Carmelo Anthony as the only major-conference players ever to average 20 and 10 while making 50-plus threes in a season. He also graduated with degrees in mathematics and computer science.

In a nine-category format that is a genuinely rare shape: a seven-footer who rebounds at double-digit volume, blocks shots, and spaces the floor at 34.7% on 5.5 attempts a game. Four categories, one roster slot.

He fell to 42 for a stack of reasons scouts agreed on — he struggles to score and defend through contact, has short arms for 7'1", posts a subpar block rate for a center, and turned 22 during his senior year. ESPN graded the Kings a C on the pick and noted its statistical model was lower on him than scouts were.

The boards split accordingly. NBA Draft Room 21st, RookieScale 28th, FantasyPros 30th, Rotoworld 35th, and RotoWire left him off their top 30 entirely. NBA.com's pre-draft piece filed him under "Sleepers." Only Yahoo, at 15th, was willing to pay a first-round price, and nobody was willing to say he could not play.
