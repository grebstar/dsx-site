SCOUTING REPORT

James spent four years at Tulane, finishing with 14.0 points, 5.4 rebounds, 2.7 assists and 1.6 steals in 2023-24, then transferred to Duke in May 2024. At Duke he started 32 of 39 games, took over at point guard about a month into the season, and averaged 8.6 points, 4.2 rebounds and 2.9 assists on 51.6% from the field and 41.3% from three. His 2.4-to-1 assist-to-turnover ratio was third in the ACC, he made the ACC All-Defensive Team, and Duke reached the Final Four.

The boards were cool on a 22-year-old senior with modest volume: RookieScale, Rotoworld, RotoWire, DraftRoom and FantasyPros never listed him, and the seven boards that did had him between 37th and 47th. Charlotte took him 33rd on Jun. 26, 2025 and signed him to a four-year deal on Jul. 3.

As a rookie he played all 82 games with 19 starts, but shot 37.1% from the field and 35.2% from three, below his Duke percentages. He remains a low-usage rotation guard on a Hornets team that added veteran wings this summer.

Charlotte drafted James 33rd overall on Jun. 26, 2025, and he signed a four-year, $9.97 million deal on Jul. 3, 2025, as reported by Michael Scotto of HoopsHype. Basketball-Reference lists his 2026 salary at $2,296,274.

His rookie year was a full one: 82 games, 19 starts, and the Hornets' run to the play-in. Charlotte's summer 2026 additions of Grayson Allen, Royce O'Neale and Dorian Finney-Smith, per NBA.com's player page, add competition for his minutes.
