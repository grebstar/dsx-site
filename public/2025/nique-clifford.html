SCOUTING REPORT

Clifford produced one of the most complete statistical seasons in college basketball: 18.9 points, 9.6 rebounds and 4.4 assists on 49.6% shooting with 1.8 made threes. A wing who rebounds like a power forward and passes like a guard fills more categories than almost anyone in this draft, and FantasyPros noticed — they ranked him 9th, ahead of every pick from 10 through 30.

Then he went to Las Vegas and ESPN named him the No. 1 rookie of the entire Summer League, ahead of Cooper Flagg, on 15.2 / 6.5 / 4.2 with 45.8% from three across six games. That is the single largest positive re-rating event of the 2025 summer.

The counterweight is severe and simple. He is 23.4 years old — the oldest player taken in the first round, nearly five years older than Flagg. A 23-year-old producing against 20-year-olds in the Mountain West is a different signal than the same line in the SEC, and every board that ranked him lower than FantasyPros was pricing exactly that.

Sacramento is a real opportunity, though. The Kings have no settled wing rotation and every reason to play him.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 18.9 / 9.6 / 4.4 — one of the most complete statistical seasons in college basketball
  • ESPN's No. 1 rookie of the 2025 Summer League, ahead of Cooper Flagg
  • FantasyPros ranked him 9th, ahead of every pick from 10 through 30
  • Sacramento has no settled wing rotation; the minutes are available immediately

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 23.4 years old — the oldest first-round pick in the class by a wide margin
  • Mountain West production against a much softer age curve than the power conferences
  • 37.7% from three on modest volume; the shot is good, not a weapon
  • Almost no developmental runway left — what you see is close to what you get
