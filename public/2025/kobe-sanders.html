SCOUTING REPORT

Sanders' last college season was his only one at Nevada, after four at Cal Poly: a 15.8 point, 3.9 rebound, 4.5 assist year in 31.7 minutes over 33 games (32 starts), with 1.1 steals and 46.0% shooting, 34.2% from three and 79.5% at the line. He made third-team All-Mountain West. He had averaged 19.6 points in his final Cal Poly season in 2023-24.

The Knicks took him 50th in the 2025 draft, on June 26, 2025, and traded the pick to the Clippers that night for the No. 51 pick and the rights to Luka Mitrovic. Nine boards covered him, placing him between 47th and 53rd, for a composite rank of 52nd.

He signed a two-way contract and played five Summer League games at 6.6 points on 39.4% shooting. He entered the Clippers' rotation on Nov. 12, 2025 against Denver with Bradley Beal and Kawhi Leonard out, scored a career-high 20 points with seven rebounds in a Jan. 5, 2026 start against Golden State, and was converted to a standard contract on Feb. 7, 2026. After the trade deadline he lost minutes to Bennedict Mathurin, with DNP-CDs on Feb. 19 and 20, before returning to the lineup late in the season. He finished with 68 games and 16 starts at 19.9 minutes, 7.3 points, 46.6% shooting and 40.8% from three. The Clippers went 42-40 and lost their play-in game to Golden State.

The February deal carried a club option for 2026-27. On June 29, 2026 the Clippers declined it and extended a qualifying offer, making him a restricted free agent, and he agreed the next day to a four-year, $11.2 million contract, signed July 7. Per SalarySwish the first two years ($2,622,139 and $2,753,246) are guaranteed, 2028-29 is non-guaranteed and 2029-30 is a team option.

A left hip impingement kept him out of preseason games in October 2026, but he was cleared for the opener. He came off the bench in the first three games and made his first start of the season on Oct. 28 against the Lakers in place of Rui Hachimura. Through four games he is at 7.0 points, 2.5 assists and 2.0 steals in 21.3 minutes, shooting 9-for-26 from the field.
