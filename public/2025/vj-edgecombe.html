SCOUTING REPORT

2.1 steals per game. Start there, because in a nine-category league that single number is worth more than any other line item in this draft. Steals are the most concentrated and least replaceable category in the format — there is no waiver-wire fix — and Edgecombe produced them at a rate almost nobody sustains at any level.

The rest of the Baylor line is quietly excellent rather than loud: 15.0 points, 5.6 rebounds, 3.2 assists and 1.6 made threes with only 1.9 turnovers. He rebounds like a forward from the two-guard slot and he does not cough the ball up. That is a clean, contribute-everywhere profile with no dead categories.

The knock is efficiency — 43.6% from the field and 34.0% from three — and the fact that his scoring is more athletic than skilled at this stage. He gets to the rim on explosion, and the NBA is where explosion stops being enough on its own.

Philadelphia is a genuinely good landing spot for volume. The Sixers' guard rotation has been an injury casualty ward for two years, and Edgecombe should be handed real minutes immediately. He ranks third on the composite behind two players with more scoring upside, but he has the highest floor of anyone outside Flagg.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2.1 steals per game — the single most valuable and least replaceable category line in the class
  • No dead categories anywhere in the profile; 1.9 turnovers is genuinely low for the usage
  • Rebounds like a forward from the guard slot (5.6 as a freshman)
  • Philadelphia's guard injury history means minutes are close to guaranteed

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 43.6% from the field and 34.0% from three — efficiency is the weak link
  • Scoring is athleticism-first; the halfcourt skill package is behind the physical tools
  • 0.6 blocks means he supplies only one of the two defensive categories
  • Only two Summer League games before a thumb sprain, split across two venues
