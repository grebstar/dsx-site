SCOUTING REPORT

Niederhäuser is a rim protector and nothing else, but he is a very good one: 2.3 blocks in 25.1 minutes on 61.1% shooting, with 12.9 points and 6.3 rebounds. Blocks are the second-scarcest category in a nine-cat format, and only Kalkbrenner and Sorber supply them at this rate in this class.

Everything around it is empty. 0.8 assists, 0.7 steals, zero three-point makes on 9.1% shooting, and 66.4% from the line. He is a one-category specialist who costs you in a second category, which is a hard thing to roster.

The board coverage is thin: NBA Draft Room and FantasyPros never listed him, and the ten that did had him between 26th and 53rd. That is close to a market consensus of "replacement level with one useful skill."

He is also 22.3, and the Clippers had Ivica Zubac entrenched at center when they took him at 30. Priced at the bottom of the first round because that is where the market put him and there is no obvious reason to argue.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2.3 blocks in 25.1 minutes — genuine elite rim protection, the second-scarcest category
  • 61.1% from the field; he finishes everything he is given
  • 10 rebounds and 4 blocks in one Summer League game showed the defensive tools travel
  • The Clippers had no long-term answer at center behind Ivica Zubac

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Zero made threes and 9.1% shooting from deep — no spacing of any kind
  • 66.4% from the line is an active nine-cat cost
  • Two of twelve boards never listed him at all
  • 22.3 years old with a one-category profile; there is not much left to develop
