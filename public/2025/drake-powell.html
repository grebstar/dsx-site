SCOUTING REPORT

Powell started 24 games at North Carolina and averaged 7.4 points. That is the entire statistical case, and it is thin. What is genuinely good inside it: 48.3% from the field, 37.9% from three, and 0.7 steals with 0.7 blocks from a wing, which is a real 3-and-D signature at low volume.

He was drafted 22nd on physical tools and defensive projection rather than production, and the boards responded coolly — RotoWire and NBA Draft Room both left him off their top 30, and the ten that ranked him had him between 27th and 34th. No source ranked him higher than 27th.

He then missed the entire 2025 Summer League with left knee tendinopathy, so there is no bridging sample at all between a 7.4-point college season and his NBA debut. His post-Vegas re-rating is blank for the same reason.

64.8% from the free-throw line is the number that should worry you most — it is the best available predictor of whether the 37.9% from three is real, and it says probably not.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 0.7 steals and 0.7 blocks from a wing — a genuine 3-and-D signature
  • 48.3% from the field and 37.9% from three, both efficient for a freshman
  • Real positional size and athleticism; the defensive projection is why he went 22nd
  • Brooklyn's rebuild guarantees he will get minutes to develop in

▼ BEAR CASE
