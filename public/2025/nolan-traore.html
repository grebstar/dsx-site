SCOUTING REPORT

Traoré put up 5.1 assists in 22.6 minutes for Saint-Quentin in the French Betclic Élite as an 18-year-old — a genuine lead-guard rate against grown professionals, and the reason he is here. He gets to the rim with real speed, and the passing is instinctive rather than schemed.

Everything else is a problem. 39.2% from the field, 30.4% from three, 71.0% from the line, 2.5 turnovers. That is four categories where he actively costs you, and unlike Demin — the other oversized young playmaker in this class — Traoré does not have size to fall back on if the shot never comes.

The boards were consistent and unenthusiastic: eleven of twelve had him between 15th and 26th, the other at 28th. No source loved him and none dismissed him, which is its own signal — this is a bet on a skill, priced as one.

Brooklyn drafted five players in this class and has nothing settled at point guard, so the ball will be in his hands. Whether that produces value or turnovers is the entire question, and the Pathway score of 78 against a model base of 60 says the opportunity is real even if the production is not yet.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 5.1 assists per game in a top European league at 18 — the passing rate is legitimately advanced
  • Brooklyn has nothing settled at point guard; the ball is his from October
  • 1.3 made threes suggests the shot is at least being taken, if not made
  • Real rim pressure — the speed translates regardless of the shooting

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 39.2% from the field and 30.4% from three — the efficiency is close to disqualifying
  • 2.5 turnovers against 5.1 assists is a mediocre ratio for a pure point guard
  • 1.7 rebounds; almost nothing outside the guard categories
  • Shot 30.4% across three Summer League games before sitting the finale
