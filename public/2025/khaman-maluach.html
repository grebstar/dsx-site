SCOUTING REPORT

Blocks are the second-scarcest category in the format and Maluach is the only true rim protector in this lottery. At Duke he put up 1.3 blocks and 6.6 rebounds in 21.2 minutes while shooting 71.2% from the field — a per-minute profile that projects to something considerably larger with starter usage. He is 7'2", 18 years old, and moves better than a player that size should.

The fantasy market liked him more than his draft slot: FantasyPros had him fifth pre-draft, and by September he was the third-most-drafted rookie in redraft leagues. Blocks-and-boards centers systematically over-index in category leagues relative to NBA draft capital, and that is the whole thesis here.

Phoenix is the problem. Mark Williams is the starting center and Oso Ighodaro is the backup. There is no version of the Suns' 2025-26 season where Maluach plays 24 minutes a night, and NBA Draft Room and Hollinger priced that in — they ranked him 14th and 19th while RookieScale, Rotoworld, RotoWire and FantasyPros sat at 7 or 8.

The offensive profile is also narrow: 0.5 assists, 0.2 steals, 0.1 made threes. He is a lob threat and a shot blocker. That is a real NBA player and a useful fantasy piece, but only if he is on the floor, and the Pathway score of 44 against a model base of 72 is the largest downgrade on this board outside Harper.
