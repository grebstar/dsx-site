SCOUTING REPORT

McNeeley was Big East Freshman of the Year and scored 38 with 10 rebounds against Creighton — the most points by a UConn freshman in conference history. He put up 14.5 points and 6.0 rebounds with 1.7 made threes, and the rebounding from a wing is a genuinely useful category contribution.

Then look at the shooting splits: 38.1% from the field and 31.7% from three, from a player drafted specifically as a shooter. The saving grace is 86.6% from the free-throw line — the third-best mark in this class and the strongest available evidence that the jumper is real and the college percentages were noise from a 27-game injury-shortened season.

That tension is exactly why the boards scattered: FantasyPros 20th, RookieScale 23rd, NBA Draft Room 29th, Rotoworld 37th, with RotoWire leaving him off. A 20-place spread across eleven sources, from 17th at ESPN and CBS to 37th at Rotoworld.

Charlotte took him 29th and already has Kon Knueppel and Brandon Miller on the wing, so the minutes are not obviously there. This is priced as a shooting bet with real category upside and a genuine chance of never getting on the floor.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 86.6% from the line — third-best in the class and strong evidence the jump shot is real
  • 6.0 rebounds from a wing is a valuable category contribution
  • Big East Freshman of the Year with a 38-point conference-record game
  • Only 27 games; the poor percentages come from a small, injury-shortened sample

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 38.1% from the field and 31.7% from three from a player drafted as a shooter
  • 0.2 blocks and 0.6 steals; almost no defensive category production
  • Kon Knueppel and Brandon Miller are both ahead of him on the Charlotte wing
  • 20-place spread across the boards, and RotoWire did not rank him at all
