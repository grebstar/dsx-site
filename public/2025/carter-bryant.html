SCOUTING REPORT

Bryant is the purest test of whether landing spot should override archetype. Pre-draft he was 10th on the consensus scouting board and 11th at FantasyPros. He went 14th to San Antonio and immediately fell to 17th, 20th and 20th on the dynasty boards. The player did not change. The depth chart did.

The archetype is desirable in a category league: 0.9 steals and 1.0 blocks with 1.1 made threes in only 19.3 minutes, and 37.1% from three on real volume. Per-minute, that is a legitimate 3-and-D contributor, and the two boards that ranked him 10th-11th were pricing the rate rather than the role.

The problem is that 6.5 points on 46.0% shooting with 1.0 assists is what he actually produced, and he started five games in 37 at Arizona. He was a rotation freshman, not a featured one, and there is no usage in the profile to project from.

San Antonio makes that worse in every direction: a 62-win-caliber roster with Wembanyama, Fox, Castle, Vassell and Barnes, no minutes to spare on the wing, and no organizational reason to force them. His Summer League was genuinely alarming — 28.6% from the field across six games, which is the specific reason his post-Vegas re-rating is the worst in the lottery.

Priced 14th because the tools are real and the road is closed.
