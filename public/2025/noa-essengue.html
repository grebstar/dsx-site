SCOUTING REPORT

Essengue is where the two markets diverge hardest. The final consensus scouting board had him ninth. The dynasty boards had him 18th and 19th. Both are looking at the same 18-year-old and reaching opposite conclusions, because they are pricing different things.

Scouts see a 6'9" 18-year-old already playing rotation minutes in the German BBL and EuroCup, with the length and mobility to defend multiple positions and years of runway. That is a legitimate top-ten NBA bet.

Fantasy managers see the box score: 9.7 points, 4.5 rebounds, 1.1 assists, 0.7 steals, 0.6 blocks in 22.8 BBL minutes, with 24.3% from three and 67.9% from the line. There is no category here he wins, and no category he is close to winning. NBC filed him under "wing depth play."

The EuroCup sample is meaningfully better — 12.4 / 5.3 with 1.4 steals on 56.0% shooting across 18 games — and that is the version worth underwriting. But Chicago is a crowded, directionless forward rotation and an 18-year-old international who cannot shoot is not going to force his way into it.

This is a stash, priced as one. The Pathway score of 52 against a model base of 74 is the honest markdown; the Long-Term score barely moves, because the talent case is real and the timeline is simply long.
