SCOUTING REPORT

Fleming has the best pre-draft board consensus of any second-round pick in this class. RotoWire 17th, Rotoworld 18th, RookieScale 27th, FantasyPros 27th — two sources ranked him ahead of a dozen first-rounders, and he went 31st.

The Saint Joseph's junior line explains why: 14.7 points and 8.5 rebounds on 53.1% shooting, with 1.4 steals, 1.5 blocks and 1.8 made threes at 39.0%. A power forward who supplies both defensive categories, shoots threes at volume and rebounds at 8.5 is filling six columns. In a category format that is a top-20 profile regardless of where he was drafted.

The gaps are role-shaped rather than skill-shaped. 1.3 assists is thin, the Atlantic 10 is a step below the power conferences, and at 20.9 he is neither young enough to be a project nor old enough to be finished.

Phoenix is the concern. He arrived in the seven-team Kevin Durant trade into a frontcourt already holding Mark Williams, Oso Ighodaro, Nick Richards and fellow rookie Khaman Maluach. His Pathway score of 58 against a model base of 42 is generous to the talent and honest about the traffic.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 1.4 steals and 1.5 blocks with 1.8 made threes — six categories from a power forward
  • RotoWire ranked him 17th and Rotoworld 18th, ahead of a dozen first-round picks
  • 53.1% from the field and 39.0% from three; efficient at both ranges
  • 8.5 rebounds gives the profile a floor even if the shooting cools

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Atlantic 10 production carries a real translation discount
  • 1.3 assists; the playmaking column is empty
  • Phoenix's frontcourt already holds Mark Williams, Ighodaro, Richards and Maluach
  • Second-round contract means no organizational obligation to find him minutes
