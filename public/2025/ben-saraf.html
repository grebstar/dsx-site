SCOUTING REPORT

Saraf produced 11.5 points and 3.9 assists in 23.0 minutes in the German BBL at 18, with a better EuroCup line on top of it. The passing feel is real and he draws fouls at a useful rate — 74.8% from the line on genuine volume.

The shot is the whole problem. 0.7 made threes on 31.5%, and then he went to Las Vegas and shot 0-for-the-event from three with 30.4% overall across three games. A guard who cannot shoot and is not fast enough to compensate has a narrow NBA path, and 2.7 turnovers makes it narrower.

The boards said so plainly: RotoWire and FantasyPros did not rank him at all, and the ten that did placed him between 15th and 32nd. He has one of the thinnest coverages of any first-round pick in this class.

Brooklyn drafted five rookies and will give him the runway. This is priced as a developmental hold on a passer who needs a jump shot to have a position.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 3.9 assists in a top European league at 18; the passing feel is genuine
  • 74.8% from the line on real volume — he gets to the rim and draws contact
  • 1.0 steals adds a second live category
  • Brooklyn has five rookies and no incentive to rush any of them off the floor

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 0.7 made threes on 31.5%, then 0-for-the-event from three in Summer League
  • 2.7 turnovers with only 3.9 assists is a poor ratio
  • Two of twelve boards did not rank him at all — tied for the thinnest coverage of any first-rounder
  • Not fast enough to compensate for the missing jump shot
