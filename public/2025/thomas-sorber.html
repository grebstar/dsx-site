SCOUTING REPORT

Sorber's Georgetown freshman season is one of the best two-way big-man lines in this draft: 14.5 points, 8.5 rebounds, 2.4 assists, 1.5 steals and 2.0 blocks on 53.2% shooting. A center who supplies both defensive categories at those rates while passing at 2.4 assists is exactly the archetype a nine-cat roster is built around, and Rotoworld ranked him 9th in the entire class because of it.

He also did it in only 24 games. His season ended in late February 2025 with surgery on turf toe in his left foot, and he did not play in the 2025 Summer League while still recovering. So you are underwriting three-quarters of a freshman year with no bridging sample.

Oklahoma City is simultaneously the best and worst destination available. Best because their development infrastructure is the league's envy and they have no pressure to rush him. Worst because they won 68 games the year before and there is no rotation gap for a raw rookie center — his Pathway score of 40 against a model base of 60 reflects a roster with nowhere to put him.

The board spread tells the story: 9th on Rotoworld, 22nd on RotoWire and FantasyPros. Nobody agrees whether you are buying a top-ten talent or a medical question.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 1.5 steals AND 2.0 blocks from a center — the rarest category combination in the draft
  • 2.4 assists from the five; the passing is genuinely advanced for a freshman
  • Rotoworld ranked him 9th in the class, well above his draft slot
  • Oklahoma City's development program is the best environment in the league for a raw big

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Only 24 college games — the season ended in February with turf-toe surgery
  • Did not play 2025 Summer League; there is no bridging sample at all
  • 16.2% from three on tiny volume; no floor spacing whatsoever
  • A 68-win roster has no rotation minutes for a rookie center
