SCOUTING REPORT

Brooklyn took Demin eighth and every dynasty board ranked him below that — as low as 18th at FantasyPros pre-draft. The objection is not subtle: he shot 41.2% from the field, 27.3% from three and 69.5% from the line at BYU. In a nine-category format, that is three separate ways to lose you a week.

What you are paying for is the 5.5 assists, and the fact that they come from a 6'9" frame. Oversized primary creators are a real archetype and there is nobody else in this class who fills the assist column at that rate. Add 1.2 steals and there is a category case here that does not depend on scoring.

The turnover figure — 2.9 — is the second-worst in the lottery and it travels with the archetype: a big handler who plays through traffic will cough it up.

The reason he sits sixteenth rather than eighteenth is opportunity. Brooklyn has nothing at point guard and will hand him the ball on day one. Preseason redraft lists noticed — RotoWire ranked him the second-best rookie target in October on a projected 4-6 assists per game. This is a bet that the assists and the runway outweigh a shooting profile that would otherwise be disqualifying.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 5.5 assists from a 6'9" creator — a genuinely scarce archetype with a real category floor
  • Brooklyn has no incumbent point guard; the ball is his from October
  • 1.2 steals adds a second contributing category to the passing
  • 43.5% from three across three Summer League games hints the shot may not be broken

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 41.2% FG / 27.3% 3P / 69.5% FT — the worst combined shooting profile in the lottery
  • 2.9 turnovers, second-worst in the class, and structural to how he plays
  • Ranked as low as 18th by FantasyPros; the market ranked him well below his draft slot
  • Brooklyn will be among the worst teams in the league, capping every counting stat
