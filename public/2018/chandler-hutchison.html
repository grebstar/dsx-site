SCOUTING REPORT

Chicago took him #22 overall out of Boise State. He had just gone for 20.0 points, 7.7 rebounds and 3.5 assists in 31 games at 31.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #26, pick 22. The boards ranged from #21 to #60 on him, a spread of 39 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 44 games, 14 starts, 20.3 minutes — 5.2 / 4.2 / 0.8 on 45.9% shooting. That ranked him #25 in the class and marked him at $36.38, +9.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. No recorded season anywhere — no NBA, G League or international row for 2025-26.

Against a frozen IPO of $33.31, today's mark of $14.64 is -56.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Chicago took him twenty-second on Jun. 21, 2018. On Mar. 25, 2021 he went to Washington with Daniel Gafford in the three-team trade that sent Troy Brown Jr. to Chicago and Moritz Wagner to Boston — three members of this class in one transaction. In August 2021 Washington moved him to San Antonio in the five-team Russell Westbrook deal; the Spurs waived him in September and Phoenix released him from a two-way in January 2022.

He announced his retirement in December 2022, at 26, and then came back. Cleveland's affiliate took him in the 2024 G League draft and he played four games for the Charge in 2024-25 at 8.8 a night before a right shoulder injury. For 2025-26 the record is empty: he was not on Cleveland's opening roster or any other G League roster, and no club anywhere records a game for him.
