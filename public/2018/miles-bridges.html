SCOUTING REPORT

the Clippers took him twelfth overall out of Michigan State. He had just gone for 17.1 points, 7.0 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 34 games at 31.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #10, pick 12. The boards ranged from fifth to #15 on him, a spread of 10 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 80 games, 25 starts, 21.2 minutes — 7.5 / 4.0 / 1.2 on 46.4% shooting. That ranked him #17 in the class and marked him at $75.99, -55.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 77 games, 77 starts, 31.0 minutes — 17.1 / 5.8 / 3.2 on 46.0% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, +132.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $170.70, today's mark of $396.43 is +132.2%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #17 in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

The Clippers drafted him twelfth on Jun. 21, 2018 and traded him to Charlotte that night for the eleventh pick, which became Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, plus future seconds. He then spent eight seasons in Charlotte and signed a three-year contract there on Jul. 6, 2024; the value is carried by one outlet and is not stated.

He served a 30-game NBA suspension arising from a 2022 domestic violence case. In 2025-26 he played 77 games at 17.1 / 5.8 / 3.2 for a Hornets team that traded LaMelo Ball to Minnesota mid-season and missed the playoffs. On Jun. 28, 2026, made official Jul. 13, Charlotte traded him to Phoenix for Grayson Allen, Royce O'Neale and a 2033 first, sending a 2029 first and a 2027 second the other way — two members of this class exchanged for each other, eight years after they were drafted nine picks apart.
