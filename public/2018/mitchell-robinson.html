SCOUTING REPORT

New York took him #36 overall out of Western Kentucky. He had no pre-draft season to price. The market liked him a great deal more than the room did: composite #17, drafted 36. The boards ranged from #13 to #31 on him, a spread of 18 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 66 games, 19 starts, 20.6 minutes — 7.3 / 6.4 / 0.6 on 69.4% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, +159.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 60 games, 16 starts, 19.6 minutes — 5.7 / 8.8 / 0.9 on 72.3% shooting. That ranked him #18 in the class and marked him at $68.95, -9.3% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $75.99, today's mark of $68.95 is -9.3%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished ninth in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

He never played college basketball. He enrolled at Western Kentucky, left before the season, and spent his draft year training rather than playing. New York took him thirty-sixth on Jun. 21, 2018 and he was never traded — eight seasons in one uniform, ending in a championship.

2025-26 was 60 games and 16 starts at 5.7 / 8.8, and eighth place in Sixth Man of the Year voting. He fractured his right little finger and had surgery on May 29, 2026, days before the Finals, and played through it — a game-sealing offensive rebound in Game 5 among the contributions — as New York beat San Antonio 4-1. He left as a free agent in July and signed with Boston; the reported salary is single-sourced and is not stated.
