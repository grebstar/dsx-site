SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him first overall out of Arizona. He had just gone for 20.1 points, 11.6 rebounds and 1.6 assists in 35 games at 33.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #3, pick 1. The boards ranged from first to seventh on him, a spread of 6 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 71 games, 70 starts, 30.7 minutes — 16.3 / 10.3 / 1.8 on 58.5% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +0.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 72 games, 72 starts, 27.2 minutes — 12.5 / 8.0 / 0.8 on 67.1% shooting. That ranked him twelfth in the class and marked him at $131.64, -78.9% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $623.16, today's mark of $131.64 is -78.9%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished third in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Phoenix took him first on Jun. 21, 2018 and kept him five seasons. On Sep. 27, 2023 he went to Portland in the three-team trade that sent Damian Lillard to Milwaukee: Portland received him, Toumani Camara, Jrue Holiday and a 2029 Milwaukee first, Phoenix received Jusuf Nurkic, Nassir Little, Keon Johnson and Grayson Allen, and Milwaukee received Lillard. Two members of this class moved in that one transaction, five years after they were drafted twenty picks apart.

Portland bought out the contract in the summer of 2025 rather than trading him, and he signed with the Lakers, starting all 72 games he played in 2025-26 at 12.5 / 8.0. Los Angeles was swept in the first round by Oklahoma City. On Jul. 3, 2026, days after he exercised his player option, the Lakers traded him to Washington for Jaden Hardy and two second-round picks. The option figure is carried only in a reporter's social post and is not stated here.
