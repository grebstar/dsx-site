SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took him #19 overall out of Maryland. He had just gone for 14.8 points, 5.0 rebounds and 3.4 assists in 32 games at 34.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #21, pick 19. The boards ranged from eleventh to #60 on him, a spread of 49 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 75 games, 59 starts, 27.3 minutes — 9.7 / 3.3 / 2.9 on 41.9% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +228.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: 69 games, 14 starts, 22.4 minutes — 10.0 / 3.5 / 2.6 on 45.1% shooting. That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, +62.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $51.92, today's mark of $84.15 is +62.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished tenth in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Atlanta took him nineteenth on Jun. 21, 2018. Three trades since: to Sacramento in July 2022 for Justin Holiday, Moe Harkless and a future first; to Chicago in February 2025 with Zach Collins and Tre Jones in the three-team deal that sent Zach LaVine to Sacramento and De'Aaron Fox to San Antonio; and to Detroit on Feb. 3, 2026 with Dario Saric and a protected swap, in a three-team deal that sent Jaden Ivey and Mike Conley to Chicago.

He played 44 games for the Bulls and 25 for the Pistons in 2025-26, 10.0 / 3.5 / 2.6 across the two, missing time with an adductor injury. Detroit reached the Eastern Conference semifinals and lost a Game 7 to Cleveland. He re-signed with the Pistons in late June 2026 on a three-year contract; the value is carried by one outlet and is not stated.
