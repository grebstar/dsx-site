Atlanta took him third overall out of Real Madrid in the Spanish ACB. He had just gone for 12.5 points, 5.7 rebounds and 4.7 assists in 37 games at 24.3 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #1, pick 3. The boards ranged from first to second on him, a spread of 1 places across 6 of them.

That ranked him second in the class and marked him at $808.24, -19.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $1,000.00, today's mark of $808.24 is -19.2%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT. Holding the top rung against a whole class trying to take it is the hardest outcome available here, not a neutral one. He finished first in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees. He made All-NBA First Team in 2025-26 with 482 vote points, which is why he sits where he does at that mark.

The trade that defines this class happened before either man played a game. Atlanta drafted him third on Jun. 21, 2018 and sent him to Dallas for the fifth pick, which became Trae Young, plus a protected 2019 first. Two franchises swapped their futures on draft night and both got a franchise player.

He was traded again in February 2025, to the Lakers, with Maxi Kleber and Markieff Morris, for Anthony Davis, Max Christie and a first-round pick, in a three-team deal also involving Utah. He signed a three-year, $165 million extension with Los Angeles on Aug. 2, 2025, running through 2027-28. In 2025-26 he played 64 games at 33.5 / 7.7 / 8.3, made the All-Star team and an All-NBA team, and finished fourth in Most Valuable Player voting; the Lakers were swept in the first round by Oklahoma City.
