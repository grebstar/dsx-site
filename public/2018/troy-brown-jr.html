Washington took him #15 overall out of Oregon. He had just gone for 11.3 points, 6.2 rebounds and 3.2 assists in 35 games at 31.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #18, pick 15. The boards ranged from #19 to #24 on him, a spread of 5 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 52 games, 10 starts, 14.0 minutes — 4.8 / 2.8 / 1.5 on 41.5% shooting. That ranked him #26 in the class and marked him at $33.31, -51.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Adelaide — Australian NBL.

Against a frozen IPO of $68.95, today's mark of $19.52 is -71.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Washington took him fifteenth on Jun. 21, 2018. On Mar. 25, 2021 he moved to Chicago in a three-team trade: Washington received Chandler Hutchison and Daniel Gafford from Chicago and sent Moritz Wagner to Boston, Boston sent Daniel Theis and Javonte Green to Chicago, and Chicago sent Luke Kornet to Boston. Three members of this class changed hands in that one transaction. He has played for Washington, Chicago, the Lakers, Minnesota and Detroit across 371 NBA games.

2025-26 was Australia, briefly. He signed with the Adelaide 36ers of the NBL as an import on Nov. 14, 2025, played 13 games at 4.6 / 4.5 / 1.8, and the club and player terminated the contract by mutual agreement around Jan. 12, 2026 so he could return to the United States for personal reasons. No NBA game followed.
