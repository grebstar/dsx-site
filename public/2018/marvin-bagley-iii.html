SCOUTING REPORT

Sacramento took him second overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 21.0 points, 11.1 rebounds and 1.5 assists in 33 games at 33.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 2. The boards ranged from first to eighth on him, a spread of 7 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 62 games, 4 starts, 25.3 minutes — 14.9 / 7.6 / 1.0 on 50.4% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, -20.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 60 games, 12 starts, 20.0 minutes — 10.5 / 6.1 / 1.4 on 61.8% shooting. That ranked him #17 in the class and marked him at $75.99, -84.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $75.99 is -84.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished fifth in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Sacramento took him second on Jun. 21, 2018, ahead of Luka Doncic and Jaren Jackson Jr., and traded him three and a half years later. On Feb. 10, 2022 he went to Detroit in a four-team deal in which Sacramento received Donte DiVincenzo, Trey Lyles and Josh Jackson, Milwaukee received Serge Ibaka and the Clippers received Rodney Hood and Semi Ojeleye — the second pick of the class exchanged, in part, for the seventeenth.

On Jan. 14, 2024 Detroit sent him and Isaiah Livers to Washington for Danilo Gallinari and Mike Muscala. On Feb. 4, 2026 he moved again, to Dallas, in the three-team trade that sent Anthony Davis, D'Angelo Russell, Jaden Hardy and Dante Exum to Washington. He played 38 games for the Wizards and 22 for the Mavericks, 10.5 / 6.1 across the two, and signed a one-year veteran-minimum contract with Denver in July 2026.
