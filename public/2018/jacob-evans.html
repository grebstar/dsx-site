Against a frozen IPO of $56.94, today's mark of $13.22 is -76.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Golden State took him twenty-eighth on Jun. 21, 2018 onto a roster that had just won a second straight title and had no minutes for him. He and Omari Spellman were then traded together twice. On Feb. 6, 2020 Golden State sent both, with D'Angelo Russell, to Minnesota for Andrew Wiggins, a protected 2021 first and a 2021 second; on Nov. 24, 2020 Minnesota sent both, with a 2026 second, to New York for Ed Davis. Both trades are confirmed from each club's own transaction log. New York waived him on Dec. 9, 2020 and he has not played an NBA game since.
