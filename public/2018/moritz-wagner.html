SCOUTING REPORT

the Lakers took him #25 overall out of Michigan. He had just gone for 14.6 points, 7.1 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 39 games at 27.6 minutes a night. He went 17 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #42, drafted 25. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #28 to #60 on him, a spread of 32 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 43 games, 5 starts, 10.4 minutes — 4.8 / 2.0 / 0.6 on 41.5% shooting. That ranked him #30 in the class and marked him at $23.39, +266.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 36 games, 0 starts, 11.9 minutes — 6.9 / 3.2 / 0.8 on 42.6% shooting. That ranked him #27 in the class and marked him at $30.50, +378.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $6.38, today's mark of $30.50 is +378.0%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

The Lakers took him twenty-fifth on Jun. 21, 2018 and moved him a year later. On Jun. 27, 2019 he went to Washington with Isaac Bonga and Jemerrio Jones, for a second-round pick, as Los Angeles cleared cap room after the Anthony Davis trade — two members of this class in one salary transaction. On Mar. 25, 2021 Washington sent him to Boston in the three-team trade that brought Chandler Hutchison to Washington and sent Troy Brown Jr. to Chicago. Boston waived him three weeks later.

Orlando signed him as a free agent in 2021 and he stayed five seasons. He tore his left ACL on Dec. 21, 2024 and needed season-ending surgery; 2025-26 was the return, 36 games at 6.9 / 3.2 on .426 shooting, with Goga Bitadze taking the backup centre minutes by the playoffs — he played ten postseason minutes as Orlando lost the first round to Detroit 3-4. He signed with Brooklyn in July 2026 on a two-year deal; the figure is single-sourced and is not stated.
