SCOUTING REPORT

Denver took him #14 overall out of Missouri. He had just gone for 10.0 points, 6.7 rebounds and 0.3 assists in 3 games at 17.7 minutes a night. The boards had him 7 places above where he went, at #7. The boards ranged from fourth to tenth on him, a spread of 6 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: he did not appear in an NBA game. No NBA row — and no G League or international row either. He had a SECOND back surgery in July 2018, weeks after Denver drafted him 14th, and did not play anywhere in 2018-19; he debuted a year late in 2019-20. 2025-26: 52 games, 52 starts, 32.5 minutes — 24.2 / 7.1 / 3.0 on 46.3% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, +83.5% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $269.91, today's mark of $495.29 is +83.5%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Denver drafted him fourteenth knowing he would not play that season. He had a microdiscectomy before the draft and a second procedure that July, and the Nuggets treated 2018-19 as a redshirt from the start. He is a GHOST ROW on the 2019 board of this exchange, because the All-Rookie votes he drew were cast for 2019-20, a year after his own class was judged.

He was traded once, on Jun. 30, 2025, to Brooklyn with a future first for Cam Johnson. He is on the five-year rookie-scale extension he signed with Denver in 2021, reported at a $207 million maximum by ESPN and at $145.3 million guaranteed by CBS, so no figure is stated. In 2025-26 he played 52 games at 24.2 a game as Brooklyn's primary scorer before being shut down with a left hamstring injury; the Nets finished 20-62.
