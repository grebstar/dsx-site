SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took him #30 overall out of Villanova. He had just gone for 10.9 points, 8.0 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 40 games at 28.1 minutes a night. He went 13 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #43, drafted 30. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #29 to #60 on him, a spread of 31 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 46 games, 11 starts, 17.5 minutes — 5.9 / 4.2 / 1.0 on 40.2% shooting. That ranked him #22 in the class and marked him at $47.43, +775.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Beijing Shougang — Chinese CBA.

Against a frozen IPO of $5.42, today's mark of $10.64 is +96.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Atlanta took him thirtieth on Jun. 21, 2018 and moved him a year later, to Golden State on Jul. 8, 2019 for Damian Jones and a 2026 second. Then he and Jacob Evans were traded together twice: on Feb. 6, 2020 Golden State sent both, with D'Angelo Russell, to Minnesota for Andrew Wiggins and two picks, and on Nov. 24, 2020 Minnesota sent both, with a 2026 second, to New York for Ed Davis. Both are confirmed from each club's own transaction log. New York waived him on Jan. 7, 2021.

2025-26 was the Chinese CBA. He played for Beijing Shougang at 9.7 / 4.3 on .424 shooting and .427 from three. Whether he stayed on the roster through Beijing's playoff run is not established — a May 2026 playoff report credits the club's import scoring to another player — so this page says regular season and stops.
