SCOUTING REPORT

Minnesota took him #20 overall out of Georgia Tech. He had just gone for 18.2 points, 6.3 rebounds and 2.5 assists in 24 games at 36.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #24, pick 20. The boards ranged from #16 to #60 on him, a spread of 44 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 74 games, 52 starts, 23.7 minutes — 7.7 / 2.9 / 1.2 on 38.6% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, +194.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 78 games, 32 starts, 17.4 minutes — 4.5 / 2.6 / 0.9 on 42.5% shooting. That ranked him #26 in the class and marked him at $33.31, -16.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $39.70, today's mark of $33.31 is -16.1%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished #13 in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Minnesota took him twentieth on Jun. 21, 2018 and he left as a free agent for Phoenix in 2022. One trade: on Jan. 15, 2025 Phoenix sent him and three future seconds to Charlotte for Nick Richards and a second. He signed with Houston as a free agent in July 2025 and with Utah in July 2026 on a two-year, $12 million contract, a figure NBA.com and Sports Illustrated state identically.

2025-26 was 78 games and 32 starts for Houston at 4.5 / 2.6 / 0.9 in a defensive reserve role, on a team that finished 52-30 and lost the first round to the Lakers 2-4.
