SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him #16 overall out of Texas Tech. He had just gone for 11.3 points, 5.0 rebounds and 1.8 assists in 37 games at 28.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #16, pick 16. The boards ranged from eighth to #60 on him, a spread of 52 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 6 games, 2 starts, 18.5 minutes — 6.7 / 2.2 / 1.7 on 41.2% shooting. That ranked him #39 in the class and marked him at $9.48, -88.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Piratas de Quebradillas — Puerto Rico's BSN.

Against a frozen IPO of $84.15, today's mark of $17.77 is -78.9%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Phoenix drafted him sixteenth on Jun. 21, 2018 and traded him to Philadelphia that night for the tenth pick, which became Mikal Bridges, plus a protected 2021 first. Then, at a developmental camp weeks later, he broke the fifth metatarsal in his left foot and had surgery in August. While he was recovering, in September, a severe allergic reaction to previously unknown food allergies required a thoracoscopy and hospitalisation, and the Sixers shut him down for the rest of 2018 with no timetable. He debuted in March 2019.

2025-26 was the G League. He signed an Exhibit 10 with Dallas in October 2025 and played 16 games for the Texas Legends at 12.9 / 4.0; on Dec. 27 his G League rights were traded to the Oklahoma City Blue for a 2026 first and a 2027 second, and he started 30 of 33 games there at 20.9 / 5.2 / 4.5 in 39.4 minutes. He held no NBA contract as of September 2026.
