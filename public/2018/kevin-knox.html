SCOUTING REPORT

New York took him ninth overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 15.6 points, 5.4 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 37 games at 32.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #11, pick 9. The boards ranged from eighth to #22 on him, a spread of 14 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 75 games, 57 starts, 28.8 minutes — 12.8 / 4.5 / 1.1 on 37.0% shooting. That ranked him twelfth in the class and marked him at $131.64, -11.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Windy City Bulls — G League.

Against a frozen IPO of $149.42, today's mark of $23.39 is -84.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished twelfth in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

New York took him ninth on Jun. 21, 2018. Three trades since: to Atlanta in January 2022, with a protected 2022 first, for Cam Reddish and Solomon Hill; through Golden State to Portland in a three-team deal in February 2023; and to Utah in February 2024 for Simone Fontecchio, a second and the draft rights to Gabriele Procida, after which Utah waived him almost immediately.

2025-26 was a G League season. He went unsigned in NBA free agency and joined the Windy City Bulls, Chicago's affiliate, around Nov. 1, 2025 for his eighth professional year. Through his first seven games he was at 21.3 / 6.0 on .565 shooting in 25.6 minutes, including a 37-point game against the Cleveland Charge, and he missed at least one March game for a personal matter. He held no NBA contract as of September 2026.
