SCOUTING REPORT

Houston took him #46 overall out of USC. He had no pre-draft season to price. The market liked him a great deal more than the room did: composite #32, drafted 46. The boards ranged from twelfth to #60 on him, a spread of 48 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 50 games, 31 starts, 19.7 minutes — 5.0 / 2.7 / 3.2 on 39.1% shooting. That ranked him #21 in the class and marked him at $51.92, +166.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 49 games, 24 starts, 23.0 minutes — 12.3 / 3.2 / 2.6 on 40.7% shooting. That ranked him #14 in the class and marked him at $104.27, +434.3% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $19.52, today's mark of $104.27 is +434.3%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Houston took him 46th on Jun. 21, 2018. On Aug. 31, 2018 the Rockets sent his rights to Phoenix with Ryan Anderson for Brandon Knight and Marquese Chriss, and he signed with the Suns on Sep. 21, 2018.

On Jul. 7, 2019 he went to Memphis with Josh Jackson and second-round considerations for Kyle Korver and Jevon Carter. Memphis tendered a qualifying offer on Nov. 17, 2020 and he signed a multi-year deal on Nov. 22, 2020, reported as four years, $35 million. On Jun. 23, 2022 Philadelphia acquired him for Danny Green and the 23rd pick, which Memphis used on David Roddy.

He became a free agent in July 2024 and agreed to a one-year, $12.8 million deal with Golden State. On Nov. 12, 2024 he partially tore his ACL, and surgery was announced Nov. 21, 2024. He signed a new multi-year deal on Oct. 1, 2025, played his first game back on Dec. 4, 2025, and agreed to a two-year, $11 million contract with the Warriors (player option in year two) on Jul. 1, 2026.
