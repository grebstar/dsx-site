SCOUTING REPORT

Philadelphia took him #26 overall out of Wichita State. He had just gone for 14.9 points, 3.2 rebounds and 5.2 assists in 32 games at 31.7 minutes a night. He went 10 picks ahead of his composite, which was #36. The boards ranged from #23 to #47 on him, a spread of 24 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 79 games, 27 starts, 22.8 minutes — 9.1 / 1.7 / 1.5 on 43.1% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, +1632.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 51 games, 12 starts, 23.0 minutes — 9.3 / 1.8 / 1.4 on 43.7% shooting. That ranked him #22 in the class and marked him at $47.43, +258.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $13.22, today's mark of $47.43 is +258.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished eighth in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Philadelphia took him twenty-sixth on Jun. 21, 2018 and traded him inside his rookie season: on Feb. 6, 2019 he went to the Clippers with Wilson Chandler, Mike Muscala and four picks for Tobias Harris, Boban Marjanovic and Mike Scott. Three more since: to Brooklyn in November 2020; to Phoenix on Aug. 6, 2021 for Jevon Carter and the No. 29 pick; and to Washington in June 2023 in the Bradley Beal trade. Washington waived him in July 2024.

New York signed him as a free agent in September 2024 and has re-signed him three times since, most recently on Jul. 6, 2026 for four years and $24 million, a figure RealGM and Bleacher Report state identically. In 2025-26 he played 51 games at 9.3 a night on .392 from three, missing time with a right shoulder sprain, and shot 11-of-12 from three across the Eastern Conference finals sweep of Cleveland. New York beat San Antonio 4-1 for the championship.
