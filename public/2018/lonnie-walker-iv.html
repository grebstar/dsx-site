SCOUTING REPORT

San Antonio took him #18 overall out of Miami (FL). He had just gone for 11.5 points, 2.6 rebounds and 1.9 assists in 32 games at 27.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #15, pick 18. The boards ranged from ninth to #21 on him, a spread of 12 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 17 games, 0 starts, 6.9 minutes — 2.6 / 1.0 / 0.5 on 34.8% shooting. That ranked him #40 in the class and marked him at $8.31, -91.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Maccabi FOX Tel Aviv — EuroLeague and the Israeli BSL.

Against a frozen IPO of $93.47, today's mark of $16.15 is -82.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

San Antonio took him eighteenth on Jun. 21, 2018 and he has never been traded: the Lakers, Boston, Philadelphia and everything after have been signings or expirations. Then he left the league entirely, for Zalgiris Kaunas in October 2024 and Maccabi Tel Aviv in July 2025 after Philadelphia declined his team option.

2025-26 was a EuroLeague season at 15.2 a game, including a Round 15 Most Valuable Player award. On Aug. 5, 2026 Denver signed him to a one-year non-guaranteed Exhibit 10, which is a training-camp invitation rather than a roster spot; local reporting corrected early accounts that implied otherwise. As of September 2026 he is competing for a place, not holding one.
