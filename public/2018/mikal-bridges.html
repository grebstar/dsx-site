SCOUTING REPORT

Philadelphia took him tenth overall out of Villanova. He had just gone for 17.7 points, 5.3 rebounds and 1.9 assists in 40 games at 32.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #9, pick 10. The boards ranged from fifth to #13 on him, a spread of 8 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 82 games, 56 starts, 29.5 minutes — 8.3 / 3.2 / 2.1 on 43.0% shooting. That ranked him eleventh in the class and marked him at $149.42, -24.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 82 games, 82 starts, 32.8 minutes — 14.4 / 3.8 / 3.7 on 49.0% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +64.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $196.79, today's mark of $322.90 is +64.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished eleventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Philadelphia drafted him and traded him within the hour. On Jun. 21, 2018 the Sixers sent him to Phoenix for the sixteenth pick, which became Zhaire Smith, and a protected 2021 first. Two more trades since: on Feb. 9, 2023 Phoenix sent him, Cam Johnson, Jae Crowder and four unprotected firsts to Brooklyn for Kevin Durant, and in June 2024 Brooklyn sent him to New York for Bojan Bogdanovic and five firsts.

He signed a four-year, $150 million extension with the Knicks in late July 2025, a figure ESPN and CBS state identically. In 2025-26 he played all 82 games at 14.4 / 3.8 / 3.7 on .490 shooting and .371 from three. New York finished 53-29, went 16-3 in the playoffs and beat San Antonio 4-1 in the Finals; his role and minutes drew public scrutiny during the run.
