SCOUTING REPORT

Chicago took him seventh overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 13.5 points, 9.1 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 37 games at 26.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #6, pick 7. The boards ranged from fourth to #13 on him, a spread of 9 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 44 games, 44 starts, 25.2 minutes — 10.3 / 7.0 / 1.8 on 48.5% shooting. That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, -73.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: 78 games, 78 starts, 29.3 minutes — 11.8 / 7.4 / 2.0 on 51.2% shooting. That ranked him eleventh in the class and marked him at $149.42, -53.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $322.90, today's mark of $149.42 is -53.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #16 in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Chicago took him seventh on Jun. 21, 2018 and traded him once, on Mar. 25, 2021, sending him and Otto Porter Jr. to Orlando for Nikola Vucevic and Al-Farouq Aminu. He has not been traded since. He signed a three-year, $59 million extension with the Magic in October 2024, a figure ESPN and CBS state identically, running through 2028-29.

2025-26 was all 82 games at 11.1 / 7.4 / 2.0 on .505 shooting. Orlando finished 45-37, eighth in the East, and lost the first round to Detroit 4-3; he played all seven games at 10.2 / 6.9.
