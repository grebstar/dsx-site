SCOUTING REPORT

Utah took him #21 overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 15.5 points, 3.3 rebounds and 4.6 assists in 37 games at 35.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #25, pick 21. The boards ranged from #16 to #43 on him, a spread of 27 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 38 games, 2 starts, 10.9 minutes — 5.6 / 0.6 / 0.7 on 37.6% shooting. That ranked him #32 in the class and marked him at $19.52, -46.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 51 games, 27 starts, 28.8 minutes — 16.5 / 3.0 / 3.8 on 40.3% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, +529.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $36.38, today's mark of $229.10 is +529.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Utah took him twenty-first on Jun. 21, 2018 and traded him a year later. Four trades in all: to Memphis on Jul. 6, 2019 with Jae Crowder, Kyle Korver and the draft rights to Darius Bazley for Mike Conley; to Milwaukee on Aug. 7, 2021 for Sam Merrill and two seconds; to Phoenix on Sep. 27, 2023 in the three-team Damian Lillard trade, the same transaction that sent Deandre Ayton to Portland; and to Charlotte on Jun. 28, 2026, with Royce O'Neale and a 2033 first, for Miles Bridges. Two separate deals pairing him with another member of this class, three years apart.

He signed a four-year, $70 million extension with Phoenix in April 2024, a figure CBS and NBA.com state identically, and it moved with him to Charlotte. In 2025-26 he played 51 games with 27 starts at 16.5 / 3.0 / 3.8 on .403 / .349 / .857, managing a left knee late in the year. Phoenix finished 45-37 and was swept by Oklahoma City in the first round.
