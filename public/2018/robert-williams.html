SCOUTING REPORT

Boston took him #27 overall out of Texas A&M. He had just gone for 10.4 points, 9.2 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 30 games at 25.6 minutes a night. The market liked him a great deal more than the room did: composite #13, drafted 27. The boards ranged from eleventh to #18 on him, a spread of 7 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 32 games, 2 starts, 8.8 minutes — 2.5 / 2.5 / 0.2 on 70.6% shooting. That ranked him #33 in the class and marked him at $17.77, -84.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: 59 games, 1 starts, 17.1 minutes — 6.7 / 7.0 / 1.0 on 70.8% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, -51.3% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $116.93, today's mark of $56.94 is -51.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Boston took him twenty-seventh on Jun. 21, 2018, after pre-draft imaging found popliteal artery entrapment syndrome in both legs. He was traded once, on Oct. 1, 2023, to Portland with Malcolm Brogdon and two first-round picks for Jrue Holiday.

2025-26 was 59 games at 6.7 / 7.0 as a backup centre for a Portland team that went 42-40, beat Phoenix in a play-in game to reach the playoffs for the first time in five years, and lost the first round to San Antonio 4-1. He signed a three-year extension with the Blazers at the end of June 2026; the value is carried by one outlet and is not stated.
