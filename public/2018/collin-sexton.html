SCOUTING REPORT

Cleveland took him eighth overall out of Alabama. He had just gone for 19.2 points, 3.8 rebounds and 3.6 assists in 33 games at 29.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #12, pick 8. The boards ranged from sixth to #22 on him, a spread of 16 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 82 games, 72 starts, 31.8 minutes — 16.7 / 2.9 / 3.0 on 43.0% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, +105.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 68 games, 22 starts, 23.7 minutes — 15.4 / 2.3 / 3.3 on 48.5% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +29.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $131.64, today's mark of $170.70 is +29.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished seventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Cleveland took him eighth on Jun. 21, 2018. He left in the 2022 Donovan Mitchell sign-and-trade to Utah, and has since been traded twice in twelve months: on Jun. 29, 2025 Utah sent him and a 2030 second to Charlotte for Jusuf Nurkic, and on Feb. 4, 2026 Charlotte sent him, Ousmane Dieng and three seconds to Chicago for Coby White and Mike Conley.

He played 68 games across the two teams in 2025-26 and scored 17.5 a game after the move to Chicago, on a Bulls team that lost his first six. He signed with the Lakers in July 2026 on a two-year contract with a player option in the second year; the value is carried by one outlet and is not stated.
