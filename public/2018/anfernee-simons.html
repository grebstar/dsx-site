SCOUTING REPORT

Portland took him #24 overall out of IMG Academy in the High school. He had no pre-draft season to price. He went 6 picks ahead of his composite, which was #30. The boards ranged from #22 to #42 on him, a spread of 20 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 20 games, 1 starts, 7.1 minutes — 3.8 / 0.7 / 0.7 on 44.4% shooting. That ranked him #38 in the class and marked him at $10.64, -54.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 55 games, 5 starts, 24.9 minutes — 14.3 / 2.5 / 2.4 on 44.0% shooting. That ranked him #15 in the class and marked him at $93.47, +299.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $23.39, today's mark of $93.47 is +299.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Portland took him twenty-fourth on Jun. 21, 2018 and kept him seven seasons. Two trades, seven months apart: on Jul. 7, 2025 Portland sent him and a 2026 second to Boston for Jrue Holiday, and on Feb. 5, 2026 Boston sent him and that same second to Chicago for Nikola Vucevic, a 2027 second and a trade exception. Boston was reported to be shopping him within days of acquiring him.

He played 49 games for the Celtics at 14.2 a night, all off the bench, and six for the Bulls at 15.2, 55 in total at 14.3 / 2.5 / 2.4. Boston finished 56-26 and lost the first round to Philadelphia 3-4. He was in the final year of his Portland extension; the figure is carried by one outlet and is not stated.
