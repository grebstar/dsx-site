SCOUTING REPORT

Charlotte took him eleventh overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 14.4 points, 4.1 rebounds and 5.1 assists in 37 games at 33.7 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #14, pick 11. The boards ranged from tenth to #32 on him, a spread of 22 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 82 games, 73 starts, 26.5 minutes — 10.8 / 2.8 / 3.3 on 47.6% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +209.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 68 games, 68 starts, 33.2 minutes — 31.1 / 4.3 / 6.6 on 55.3% shooting. That ranked him first in the class and marked him at $1,000.00, +859.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $104.27, today's mark of $1,000.00 is +859.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished sixth in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees. He made All-NBA First Team in 2025-26 with 500 vote points, which is why he sits where he does at that mark.

Charlotte drafted him eleventh on Jun. 21, 2018 and traded him to the Clippers that night for the twelfth pick, which became Miles Bridges, plus future seconds. He never played a game for Charlotte. On Jul. 6, 2019 the Clippers sent him to Oklahoma City in the Paul George trade, along with multiple first-round picks.

He signed a four-year supermax extension with the Thunder in July 2025; the reported figure is carried by one outlet and is not stated. In 2025-26 he won Most Valuable Player for the second year running. Oklahoma City, the defending champion, reached the Western Conference finals and lost Game 7 to San Antonio 111-103 on May 30, 2026, with 35 points from him in the defeat.
