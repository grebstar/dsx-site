Against a frozen IPO of $43.38, today's mark of $27.93 is -35.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #20 in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Indiana took him twenty-third on Jun. 21, 2018. Two trades: to Washington on Aug. 6, 2021 in a multi-team deal, and to Phoenix on Feb. 10, 2022 for cash and a trade exception. Everything after — Atlanta in 2022, Houston in 2023, and re-signings in 2024 and July 2025 — has been free agency rather than a trade.

2025-26 was 57 games and one start for Houston at 5.5 / 1.0 / 1.1 in 13.7 minutes, on .394 from three. The Rockets finished 52-30 and lost the first round to the Lakers 2-4. He was a free agent again after the season; his 2025-26 salary is carried by one tracker and is not stated.
