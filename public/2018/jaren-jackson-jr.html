SCOUTING REPORT

Memphis took him fourth overall out of Michigan State. He had just gone for 10.9 points, 5.8 rebounds and 1.1 assists in 35 games at 21.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #2, pick 4. The boards ranged from second to seventh on him, a spread of 5 places across 6 of them.

2018-19: 58 games, 56 starts, 26.1 minutes — 13.8 / 4.7 / 1.1 on 50.6% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, -38.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 48 games, 48 starts, 30.3 minutes — 19.4 / 5.7 / 2.0 on 47.6% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, -66.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $808.24, today's mark of $269.91 is -66.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished fourth in the All-Rookie vote among 2018 draftees.

Memphis took him fourth on Jun. 21, 2018 and kept him seven and a half seasons, signing him to a multi-year maximum extension before 2025-26 at a reported $240 million — described as four years by ESPN and as five by Sports Illustrated, so neither shape is stated here.

On Feb. 3, 2026 the Grizzlies traded him, John Konchar, Jock Landale and Vince Williams Jr. to Utah for Taylor Hendricks, Kyle Anderson, Georges Niang, Walter Clayton Jr. and three future firsts. Three games into his Jazz career a post-trade physical found a PVNS growth in his left knee; he had surgery to remove it on Feb. 17, 2026 and did not play again that season, finishing on 48 games across the two teams. He was a two-time All-Star before the move.
