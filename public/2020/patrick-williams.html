SCOUTING REPORT

Chicago took him fourth overall out of Florida State. He had just gone for 9.2 points, 4.0 rebounds and 1.0 assists in 29 games at 22.5 minutes a night. He went 8 picks ahead of his composite, which was #12. The boards ranged from seventh to #27 on him, a spread of 20 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 71 games, 71 starts, 27.9 minutes — 9.2 / 4.6 / 1.4 on 48.3% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, +49.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 72 games, 6 starts, 20.5 minutes — 7.0 / 3.0 / 1.5 on 37.2% shooting. That ranked him #24 in the class and marked him at $39.70, -69.8% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $131.64, today's mark of $39.70 is -69.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished ninth in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees.

He has never been traded; every season row reads Chicago. Two injuries shaped the middle of his career. On Oct. 28, 2021 he suffered a perilunate dislocation of the left wrist with torn ligaments on a drive against New York, on a play that drew a Flagrant 1; surgery carried a four-to-six-month timeline and he finished 2021-22 with 17 games. Foot surgery in February 2024 held him to 43 games that season.

Chicago committed to him anyway in July 2024, on a five-year deal covering 2024-25 through 2028-29 at $18,000,000 a season, fully guaranteed. Whether that deal was a free-agent signing or an extension is reported both ways and is not characterised here. He turned an ankle in a team scrimmage on Oct. 1, 2025 and was listed day-to-day. The season itself was quiet: 72 games but only 6 starts, 20.5 minutes and 7.0 points a night on .372 shooting, for a Chicago team that went 31-51 and missed the postseason.
