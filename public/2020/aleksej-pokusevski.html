SCOUTING REPORT

Minnesota took him #17 overall out of Olympiacos B in the Greek HEBA A2. He had just gone for 10.8 points, 7.9 rebounds and 3.1 assists in 11 games at 23.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #18, pick 17. The boards ranged from eleventh to #27 on him, a spread of 16 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 45 games, 28 starts, 24.2 minutes — 8.2 / 4.7 / 2.2 on 34.1% shooting. That ranked him #18 in the class and marked him at $68.95, +0.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. No NBA row — his last recorded season of any kind was 2023-24.

Against a frozen IPO of $68.95, today's mark of $17.77 is -74.2%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #18 in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees.

The slot reads Minnesota because Minnesota made the pick, and it was gone within two days. In the three-team deal of Nov. 20, 2020, Oklahoma City received the rights to Pokusevski, James Johnson and a 2024 Minnesota second-round pick; Minnesota received Ricky Rubio, the rights to Jaden McDaniels and the rights to Leandro Bolmaro; and New York received the rights to Immanuel Quickley along with Mathias Lessort and a 2023 second-round pick from Minnesota. Four members of this class changed hands in that one transaction.

Oklahoma City waived him on Feb. 23, 2024, Charlotte signed him five days later and waived him on Jul. 6; he has held no NBA contract since Jul. 8, 2024. The zero on his 2025-26 row is Europe, not injury: he spent the season at KK Partizan in Belgrade, playing 27 EuroLeague games at 12.1 minutes for 3.7 points and 2.1 rebounds, 23 Adriatic League games at 5.8 points and a handful of Serbian domestic fixtures. He was named deputy captain for his second year there and won Adriatic League and Serbian League titles during his time at the club. Partizan did not activate the additional contract year, and in early August 2026 he signed with Lokomotiv Kuban of the VTB United League.
