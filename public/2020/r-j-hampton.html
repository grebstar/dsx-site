Milwaukee took him #24 overall out of New Zealand Breakers in the Australian NBL. He had just gone for 8.8 points, 3.9 rebounds and 2.4 assists in 15 games at 20.6 minutes a night. The boards had him 11 places above where he went, at #13. The boards ranged from eighth to #18 on him, a spread of 10 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 51 games, 1 starts, 17.4 minutes — 6.9 / 3.5 / 1.7 on 43.5% shooting. That ranked him #24 in the class and marked him at $39.70, -66.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Changsha Yongsheng — NBL China.

Against a frozen IPO of $116.93, today's mark of $14.64 is -87.5%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Milwaukee made the pick and he never played there. On Nov. 23, 2020, in the four-team trade built around Jrue Holiday, Denver received Hampton from Milwaukee and sent Oklahoma City a 2023 first-round pick; Milwaukee received Jrue Holiday, Sam Merrill and a 2024 first-round pick from New Orleans and sent New Orleans Eric Bledsoe and four first-round picks and Oklahoma City George Hill; and Oklahoma City sent Steven Adams to New Orleans. He and Merrill are the two members of this class inside that deal. Orlando then acquired him on Mar. 25, 2021 with Gary Harris, a conditional 2025 first-rounder and a trade exception, for Aaron Gordon and Gary Clark; Orlando declined his fourth-year rookie-scale option in November 2022.

He has held no NBA contract since Washington released him on Mar. 13, 2024, and no NBA game since 2023-24, when he played eight for Miami. The zero here is China: he was on the roster of the Hunan Changsha Yongsheng of the Chinese NBL, with game logs running from Dec. 21, 2025 into January 2026. His last G League season was 2023-24, 21 games between Sioux Falls and Capital City at 13.3 points, 4.1 rebounds and 3.7 assists. He is 25.
