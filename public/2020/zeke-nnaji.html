Against a frozen IPO of $27.93, today's mark of $25.57 is -8.4%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

No trade has ever moved him: six seasons, one franchise, and a ledger that holds only the selection, the rookie signing, two team options and the four-year rookie-scale extension reported at $32 million and beginning in 2024-25. Two seasons remain on it, at roughly $7.4 million each, the second a player option he is expected to take, with unrestricted free agency listed for 2028. A dislocated shoulder with cartilage damage cost him over a month in early 2023.
