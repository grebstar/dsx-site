SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took him sixth overall out of USC. He had just gone for 16.2 points, 8.6 rebounds and 1.1 assists in 28 games at 30.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 6. The boards ranged from second to seventh on him, a spread of 5 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 50 games, 4 starts, 12.0 minutes — 4.6 / 3.3 / 0.4 on 64.4% shooting. That ranked him #27 in the class and marked him at $30.50, -93.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: 74 games, 63 starts, 31.0 minutes — 15.2 / 7.6 / 3.1 on 48.0% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, -60.3% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $196.79 is -60.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Atlanta made the pick, kept it, and has never traded him; six seasons, one franchise. He signed a four-year rookie-scale extension on Oct. 23, 2023, reported at $62MM, with a player option and a team option inside it and unrestricted free agency listed for 2028; the year-by-year figures were not established from a fetched page and are not stated.

2025-26 was the season the range arrived. He shot .376 from three on 5.2 attempts a game, up from .324 the year before, while starting 63 of 74 games at 31.0 minutes for a 46-36 Atlanta team. The trade-off was inside the arc: his field goal percentage fell to .480 from .567, and from the .611 to .690 range of his earlier bench years. He picked up right knee inflammation in Game 1 of the first round against New York, having played through it for 19 points and 8 rebounds in 37 minutes, was listed questionable for Game 2 and did not miss a playoff game. New York took the series 4-2 and closed it 140-89, a game in which he scored 4 on 2-of-6 shooting.
