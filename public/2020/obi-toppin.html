SCOUTING REPORT

New York took him eighth overall out of Dayton. He had just gone for 20.0 points, 7.5 rebounds and 2.2 assists in 31 games at 31.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #6, pick 8. The boards ranged from second to tenth on him, a spread of 8 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 62 games, 0 starts, 11.0 minutes — 4.1 / 2.2 / 0.5 on 49.8% shooting. That ranked him #31 in the class and marked him at $21.38, -93.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 24 games, 3 starts, 17.7 minutes — 11.6 / 4.4 / 2.3 on 50.3% shooting. That ranked him #22 in the class and marked him at $47.43, -85.3% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $322.90, today's mark of $47.43 is -85.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

New York made the pick and kept it for three seasons. On Jul. 7, 2023 the Knicks sent him to Indiana and received a least-favorable 2028 second-round pick and a least-favorable 2029 second-round pick. He re-signed with the Pacers as a restricted free agent in July 2024 on a four-year deal reported at $60MM, running through 2027-28; its option and guarantee structure was not established from a fetched page and is not stated.

The 24 games are entirely an injury. He played Indiana's first three, averaging 14.0 points and 6.7 rebounds, then hurt his right foot on Oct. 26, 2025 in Minnesota. On Oct. 31 the club announced a partial stress fracture of the fifth metatarsal requiring surgery in New York and at least three months out. He returned on Feb. 26, 2026 against Charlotte to a standing ovation, having missed 56 games, and Rick Carlisle said the minutes restriction would hold for the rest of the season. Indiana finished 19-63.
