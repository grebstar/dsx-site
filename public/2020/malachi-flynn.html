SCOUTING REPORT

Toronto took him #29 overall out of San Diego State. He had just gone for 17.6 points, 4.5 rebounds and 5.1 assists in 32 games at 33.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #27, pick 29. The boards ranged from #18 to #37 on him, a spread of 19 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 47 games, 14 starts, 19.7 minutes — 7.5 / 2.5 / 2.9 on 37.4% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, +86.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Bahcesehir Koleji — Turkish BSL.

Against a frozen IPO of $30.50, today's mark of $11.89 is -61.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Toronto made the pick and kept him for three and a half seasons. On Dec. 30, 2023 New York received Flynn, OG Anunoby, Precious Achiuwa and a trade exception, and Toronto received R.J. Barrett, Immanuel Quickley and a 2024 Detroit second-round pick -- three members of this class in one deal. His last NBA contract was a short Charlotte deal signed Mar. 1, 2025 that ended ten days later.

The zero on his 2025-26 row is Turkey. He signed a one-year contract with Bahcesehir Koleji Istanbul on Jun. 13, 2025, his first European club, and played a full season in the Turkish BSL and the EuroCup: 33 BSL games at 15.7 points and 4.9 assists with two Player of the Week awards, and 21 EuroCup games at 17.6 points, 4.3 rebounds, 3.8 assists and 1.7 steals, earning EuroCup First Team, All-Defensive Team and All-Imports Team selections from Eurobasket. Bahcesehir reached the EuroCup semifinals and was eliminated by Besiktas Gain, a series that ended in an altercation after an unsportsmanlike foul for which he apologised publicly. The club re-signed him in June 2026.
