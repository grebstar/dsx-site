SCOUTING REPORT

Brooklyn took him #19 overall out of Villanova. He had just gone for 16.1 points, 4.7 rebounds and 2.4 assists in 31 games at 33.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #15, pick 19. The boards ranged from eighth to #25 on him, a spread of 17 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 70 games, 53 starts, 27.3 minutes — 12.2 / 4.5 / 1.4 on 40.4% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, +429.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: 72 games, 64 starts, 31.2 minutes — 17.7 / 5.6 / 2.5 on 45.1% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +82.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $93.47, today's mark of $170.70 is +82.6%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished fourth in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees.

The slot reads Brooklyn and he never played there. On Nov. 19, 2020, in a three-team deal, Detroit received the rights to Bey, Rodney McGruder, Dzanan Musa, the rights to Jaylen Hands, a 2021 Toronto second-round pick and cash; the Clippers received the rights to Jay Scrubb from Brooklyn plus Luke Kennard, Justin Patton and four Detroit second-round picks; and Brooklyn received Bruce Brown from Detroit and Reggie Perry and Landry Shamet from the Clippers. Three members of this class -- Bey, Scrubb and Perry -- changed hands in that single transaction.

Detroit sent him to Atlanta on Feb. 9, 2023 for five second-round picks and a trade exception, in the same four-team deal that moved James Wiseman to Detroit. On Mar. 10, 2024 against New Orleans he tore his left ACL with 10:41 left in the fourth quarter, ending that season at 63 games and costing him all of 2024-25. Atlanta declined to extend a qualifying offer and Washington signed him in July 2024 on a three-year deal reported at $20 million; New Orleans then acquired him with Jordan Poole and the rights to Micah Peavy for C.J. McCollum, Kelly Olynyk, the rights to Mojave King and a conditional 2027 second-rounder. The return season was the best of his career: 17.7 points, 5.6 rebounds and 2.5 assists across 72 games and 64 starts.
