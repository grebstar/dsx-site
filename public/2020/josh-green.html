SCOUTING REPORT

Dallas took him #18 overall out of Arizona. He had just gone for 12.0 points, 4.6 rebounds and 2.6 assists in 30 games at 30.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #21, pick 18. The boards ranged from #14 to #38 on him, a spread of 24 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 39 games, 5 starts, 11.4 minutes — 2.6 / 2.0 / 0.7 on 45.2% shooting. That ranked him #36 in the class and marked him at $13.22, -74.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 58 games, 0 starts, 15.7 minutes — 4.3 / 1.8 / 0.8 on 45.9% shooting. That ranked him #28 in the class and marked him at $27.93, -46.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $51.92, today's mark of $27.93 is -46.2%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Dallas made the pick, kept it, and signed him to a three-year rookie-scale extension on Oct. 23, 2023 reported at $41 million, which still governs his contract. In the summer of 2024 the Mavericks moved him: Charlotte received Green, Reggie Jackson and Denver's 2029 and 2030 second-round picks; Dallas received a 2025 Philadelphia second-round pick and cash.

Then he moved twice in eight weeks. Minnesota acquired him together with LaMelo Ball and the rights to Isaiah Evans, sending Charlotte Julius Randle, Naz Reid and a package of picks; the two accounts of that trade itemize the picks differently and describe it as four teams and as two, so those assets are named neither here nor on Ball's page. Utah then acquired Green and cash from Minnesota for John Konchar and Cody Williams, logged as Aug. 29 in one ledger and reported as Aug. 30 in another. He arrives in Utah on an expiring contract described as $14.6 million, into a crowded backcourt, after a season in which he made no starts: 58 games at 15.7 minutes and 4.3 points, though on .420 from three and .893 from the line.
