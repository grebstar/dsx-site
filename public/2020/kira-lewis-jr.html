SCOUTING REPORT

New Orleans took him #13 overall out of Alabama. He had just gone for 18.5 points, 4.8 rebounds and 5.2 assists in 31 games at 37.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #11, pick 13. The boards ranged from fifth to #18 on him, a spread of 13 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 54 games, 0 starts, 16.7 minutes — 6.4 / 1.3 / 2.3 on 38.6% shooting. That ranked him #25 in the class and marked him at $36.38, -75.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. No NBA row — his last recorded season of any kind was 2023-24.

Against a frozen IPO of $149.42, today's mark of $19.52 is -86.9%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

New Orleans made the pick and he was a nineteen-year-old rotation guard as a rookie. On Dec. 8, 2021 against Denver he tore his right ACL and suffered a Grade 2 MCL sprain in the second quarter, which ended his second season at 24 games. On Jan. 17, 2024 he moved twice in one day: Indiana acquired him from New Orleans with a second-round pick for cash, and Indiana then sent him to Toronto in the Pascal Siakam trade alongside Bruce Brown and Jordan Nwora, with Indiana receiving Siakam and a second-round pick routed from New Orleans. Utah acquired him on Feb. 8, 2024 with Otto Porter Jr., a pick and a trade exception for Ochai Agbaji and Kelly Olynyk.

The zero on his 2025-26 row is not an injury. Washington signed him on Sep. 29, 2024 and waived him on Oct. 12; he has held no NBA contract since. The Wisconsin Herd, Milwaukee's G League affiliate, acquired his returning player rights from the Capital City Go-Go on Aug. 28, 2025 for Justin Moore's rights and a 2025 G League draft first-rounder, and he was on their opening-night roster. He played 27 G League games at 26.4 minutes for 11.9 points, 3.7 assists and 3.1 rebounds on .461 shooting and .900 from the line, and appeared with Milwaukee at the 2026 Summer League as a listed Herd alumnus. That is a player working somewhere that is not the NBA, which is the absence the carry rule declines to rescue.
