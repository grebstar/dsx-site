SCOUTING REPORT

Philadelphia took him #21 overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 14.0 points, 4.3 rebounds and 3.2 assists in 31 games at 34.5 minutes a night. The boards had him 7 places above where he went, at #14. The boards ranged from sixth to #28 on him, a spread of 22 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 61 games, 8 starts, 15.3 minutes — 8.0 / 1.7 / 2.0 on 46.2% shooting. That ranked him #14 in the class and marked him at $104.27, +0.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 70 games, 70 starts, 38.0 minutes — 28.3 / 4.1 / 6.6 on 46.2% shooting. That ranked him first in the class and marked him at $1,000.00, +859.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $104.27, today's mark of $1,000.00 is +859.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #14 in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees. He made All-NBA Third Team in 2025-26 with 168 vote points, which is why he sits where he does at that mark.

No trade has ever moved him. Philadelphia's ledger holds the selection, the rookie-scale signing, two team options, a qualifying offer and the five-year deal signed in July 2024 that runs through 2028-29 with three seasons still on it. The total is reported as $203,852,600 in one place and as $204 million in others, and the agreement is dated Jul. 1 by one account and Jul. 7 by another, so neither is stated as fact.

2025-26 was the season the scoring curve finished its climb: from 8.0 points a game as a rookie to 28.3 on 38.0 minutes with 6.6 assists, 4.1 rebounds and 1.9 steals across 70 games, on .462 shooting and .892 from the line. He was named to the All-NBA Third Team, appearing on 96 of 100 ballots and finishing eleventh in the voting, and made his second All-Star team as a starter. He scored 54 in a November overtime win over Milwaukee and had five 40-point games. The selection carried no immediate contract consequence but would make him supermax-eligible the following summer with another. Philadelphia came back to beat Boston in the first round and was swept 4-0 by New York in the semifinals, with Maxey playing through a right pinky injury for 23.7 points a game.
