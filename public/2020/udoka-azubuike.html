SCOUTING REPORT

Utah took him #27 overall out of Kansas. He had just gone for 13.7 points, 10.5 rebounds and 0.9 assists in 31 games at 27.7 minutes a night. He went 21 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #48, drafted 27. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #37 to #60 on him, a spread of 23 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 15 games, 0 starts, 3.8 minutes — 1.1 / 0.9 / 0.0 on 44.4% shooting. That ranked him #52 in the class and marked him at $0.00, -100.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Ness Ziona — Israeli Winner League.

Against a frozen IPO of $1.47, today's mark of $13.22 is +801.8%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

He himself was never traded, but the pick that became him was, the day before the draft: Utah received from New York the first-round pick it spent at 27 on Azubuike and the second-rounder that became Saben Lee, and New York received Ante Tomic and the first-round pick it spent at 23 on Leandro Bolmaro. Utah then made the selection and signed him.

He has held no NBA contract since becoming a free agent from Phoenix on Jul. 1, 2024, and his NBA career stands at 84 games. The zero on this row is Israel: he spent 2025-26 with Ironi Nes Ziona in the Winner League, playing 16 games at 23.3 minutes for 9.4 points and 6.4 rebounds, shooting .842 from the field and .400 from the line, for a club that went 13-19 and sat eleventh through 32 games. Before that he was at Buducnost in the Adriatic League and EuroCup. No injury appears anywhere in his record; this is a player employed outside the NBA, which is exactly the absence the carry rule declines to rescue. At Kansas he was Big 12 Player of the Year, a Consensus All-American Second Team pick and NABC Defensive Player of the Year in 2019-20.
