SCOUTING REPORT

Washington took him ninth overall out of Maccabi Tel Aviv in the Israeli BSL. He had just gone for 12.9 points, 6.3 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 33 games at 27.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #5, pick 9. The boards ranged from second to twelfth on him, a spread of 10 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 54 games, 32 starts, 23.3 minutes — 6.3 / 4.9 / 1.2 on 41.7% shooting. That ranked him #23 in the class and marked him at $43.38, -89.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 66 games, 66 starts, 33.3 minutes — 24.2 / 6.9 / 6.7 on 46.2% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, +24.9% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $396.43, today's mark of $495.29 is +24.9%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Washington made the pick and kept him for four seasons. In the summer of 2024 the Wizards sent him to Portland and received Malcolm Brogdon, the draft rights to Bub Carrington, a 2029 Portland first-round pick and two second-round picks -- Golden State's 2028 and Portland's 2030 -- with a trade exception going back to Portland on one ledger and not on the other. The transaction date is logged as Jul. 6, 2024 in one place and reported as Jun. 26, 2024 in another, so the month is not stated as a fact.

He is playing under the four-year rookie-scale extension signed with Washington in October 2023, reported at $55M fully guaranteed, running through 2027-28 -- which is the shape of the 2025-26 story. He raised his scoring from 16.9 to 24.2 points a game with 6.9 rebounds and 6.7 assists, started all 66 games he played, made his first All-Star team and finished as one of three Most Improved Player finalists, behind Nickeil Alexander-Walker. He received 26 All-NBA vote points without making a team. On Apr. 15, 2026 he scored 41 in a 114-110 play-in win over Phoenix that put Portland in the playoffs for the first time since 2021; San Antonio won the first round 4-1.
