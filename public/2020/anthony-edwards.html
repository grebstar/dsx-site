SCOUTING REPORT

Minnesota took him first overall out of Georgia. He had just gone for 19.1 points, 5.2 rebounds and 2.8 assists in 32 games at 33.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #2, pick 1. The boards ranged from first to fourth on him, a spread of 3 places across 10 of them.

Against a frozen IPO of $808.24, today's mark of $808.24 is +0.0%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished second in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees.

He has never been traded. Minnesota made the pick on Nov. 18, 2020 and every season row on his record reads Minnesota. He signed a five-year designated rookie-scale maximum extension in July 2023, running through 2028-29 with unrestricted free agency after it. The reported value is not stated here: one account puts the guaranteed total at $244,623,120, another describes a $207 million base that reaches $260 million on an All-NBA escalator, and the page will not pick between them.

The 2025-26 season was interrupted three times. A right hamstring strain three minutes into the home opener against Indiana on Oct. 26 cost him four games. Right knee inflammation in mid-March cost at least four more. Then a left-knee bone bruise in Game 4 against Denver kept him out for the rest of that series, including the clincher; he returned for San Antonio and played all six. Minnesota finished 49-33 and sixth in the West, beat Denver 4-2 and lost the conference semifinal 4-2. On Jun. 25, 2026 the Timberwolves acquired LaMelo Ball and Josh Green from Charlotte and sent out Naz Reid and Julius Randle, so he enters 2026-27 beside a new lead ball-handler from his own draft class.
