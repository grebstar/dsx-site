SCOUTING REPORT

Orlando took him #15 overall out of North Carolina. He had just gone for 18.5 points, 5.7 rebounds and 4.0 assists in 22 games at 34.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #17, pick 15. The boards ranged from eighth to #23 on him, a spread of 15 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 47 games, 34 starts, 27.1 minutes — 12.9 / 4.7 / 4.1 on 39.7% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +124.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 35 games, 0 starts, 15.1 minutes — 6.7 / 2.5 / 3.5 on 42.4% shooting. That ranked him #25 in the class and marked him at $36.38, -52.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $75.99, today's mark of $36.38 is -52.1%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished tenth in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees.

Orlando made the pick, signed a rookie-scale extension with him in October 2023 and then used him as salary in the deal for a member of his own draft class. On Jun. 15, 2025 Memphis received Anthony, Kentavious Caldwell-Pope and draft picks; Orlando received Desmond Bane and pick swaps. Memphis bought him out and waived him on Jul. 12, 2025, and Milwaukee signed him four days later on what was reported as a one-year deal whose salary mechanism was never confirmed.

Milwaukee used him as a reserve ball-handler for 35 games at 15.0 minutes, 6.7 points and 3.5 assists on .424 shooting, and then traded him too. On Feb. 5, 2026 Phoenix received Anthony and Amir Coffey from Milwaukee, and Milwaukee received Nigel Hayes-Davis and Nick Richards -- the 42nd pick of this same 2020 draft -- with Richards moving on to Chicago in the same three-team framework. Phoenix waived Anthony on Feb. 27 without playing him. He was a free agent from Mar. 1, and on Aug. 5, 2026 he signed with Melbourne United in the Australian NBL, joining a roster that includes Joe Ingles.
