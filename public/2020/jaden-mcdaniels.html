SCOUTING REPORT

the Lakers took him #28 overall out of Washington. He had just gone for 13.0 points, 5.8 rebounds and 2.1 assists in 31 games at 31.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #26, pick 28. The boards ranged from #19 to #37 on him, a spread of 18 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 63 games, 27 starts, 24.0 minutes — 6.8 / 3.7 / 1.1 on 44.7% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, +251.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 73 games, 73 starts, 31.7 minutes — 14.8 / 4.2 / 2.7 on 51.5% shooting. That ranked him twelfth in the class and marked him at $131.64, +295.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $33.31, today's mark of $131.64 is +295.2%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #13 in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees.

The Lakers made the pick and his rights left immediately: Oklahoma City received Danny Green and the rights to McDaniels, and the Lakers received Dennis Schroder, a transfer logged Nov. 18 by one ledger and Nov. 19 by the other. Two days later he moved again in the three-team deal: Minnesota received the rights to McDaniels, Ricky Rubio and the rights to Leandro Bolmaro; Oklahoma City received the rights to Aleksej Pokusevski, James Johnson and a 2024 Minnesota second-round pick; and New York received the rights to Immanuel Quickley. One ledger also places Mathias Lessort on Oklahoma City's side where the other sends him to New York, so his direction is not asserted.

He signed a rookie-scale extension on Oct. 23, 2023, reported as five years and $136 million and taking effect in 2024-25, with unrestricted free agency listed for 2029; whether that figure is guaranteed or includes incentives was not established and is not stated. In 2025-26 he started all 73 games he played at 31.7 minutes for 14.8 points on .515 from the field and .412 from three, and raised that to 16.3 a game across 12 playoff games. An MRI on Mar. 30, 2026 showed left knee patella tendinopathy and a bone bruise; no surgery was required and the rehab was pointed at a playoff return, which he made. He received no All-Defensive selection.
