SCOUTING REPORT

Boston took him #14 overall out of Vanderbilt. He had just gone for 23.0 points, 4.9 rebounds and 0.9 assists in 14 games at 35.7 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #16, pick 14. The boards ranged from ninth to #30 on him, a spread of 21 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 46 games, 1 starts, 14.5 minutes — 4.7 / 2.8 / 0.5 on 43.8% shooting. That ranked him #28 in the class and marked him at $27.93, -66.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: 45 games, 42 starts, 29.7 minutes — 13.8 / 4.2 / 1.9 on 41.4% shooting. That ranked him #15 in the class and marked him at $93.47, +11.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $84.15, today's mark of $93.47 is +11.1%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

Boston made the pick and kept it for two seasons. On Jul. 9, 2022 the Celtics sent him to Indiana in the Malcolm Brogdon trade: Indiana received Nesmith, Daniel Theis, Nik Stauskas, Malik Fitts, Juwan Morgan and a 2023 first-round pick; Boston received Brogdon. Indiana has kept extending him -- a rookie-scale extension on Oct. 23, 2023, then a veteran extension on Oct. 20, 2025 reported at two years and $40.4 million with a trade kicker, taking the deal through 2028-29.

Availability was the whole of his 2025-26. He missed 19 consecutive games from mid-November to late December with a left knee sprain, sprained his right ankle against Washington on Feb. 19 and was ruled out at least a week beyond Feb. 22, then played eight straight games from Mar. 13 to Mar. 27 and his season ended; the cause of that final absence was not established from a fetched page. He finished with 45 games and 42 starts at 29.7 minutes, the heaviest minutes load of his career, shooting .379 from three but .414 from the field, down from .507. Indiana went 19-63.
