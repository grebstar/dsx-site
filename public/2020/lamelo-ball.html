SCOUTING REPORT

Charlotte took him third overall out of Illawarra Hawks in the Australian NBL. He had just gone for 17.0 points, 7.4 rebounds and 6.8 assists in 12 games at 31.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #1, pick 3. The boards ranged from first to sixth on him, a spread of 5 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 51 games, 31 starts, 28.8 minutes — 15.7 / 5.9 / 6.1 on 43.6% shooting. That ranked him first in the class and marked him at $1,000.00, +0.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 72 games, 69 starts, 28.0 minutes — 20.1 / 4.8 / 7.1 on 40.7% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, -60.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $1,000.00, today's mark of $396.43 is -60.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished first in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees.

Charlotte made the pick and he played all six of his seasons there before a trade finally moved him. On Jun. 25, 2026 the Hornets sent Ball and Josh Green to Minnesota and received Naz Reid and a 2033 first-round pick. The accompanying pick swaps and second-rounders are itemized two ways across NBA.com and CBS Sports, and the wider deal is described as four teams by one and as two by the other, so those assets are named on neither this page nor Green's. The players, whose direction both accounts agree on, are named in full on both.

He signed a five-year designated rookie-scale maximum extension with Charlotte in July 2023, running through 2028-29, and it travels with him to Minnesota. The dollar figure is not stated: one account gives $203,852,600, another describes a deal worth up to $260 million. Availability has been the running theme -- a fractured right wrist cost him 21 games as a rookie, and recurring right ankle problems held him to 36, 22 and 47 games from 2022-23 through 2024-25. 2025-26 was the answer to that: 72 games, his heaviest load since 2021-22, on a 44-38 Charlotte team that won a play-in game over Miami in overtime and was eliminated at Orlando.
