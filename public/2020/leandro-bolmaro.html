SCOUTING REPORT

New York took him #23 overall out of FC Barcelona II in the Spanish Segunda FEB. He had just gone for 14.9 points, 2.7 rebounds and 3.6 assists in 9 games at 26.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #24, pick 23. The boards ranged from tenth to #35 on him, a spread of 25 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: he did not appear in an NBA game. Spain — BR lists the season as other pro league. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. EA7 Emporio Armani Milan — Italian LBA.

Against a frozen IPO of $39.70, today's mark of $16.15 is -59.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

His rights moved twice inside four days and he did not play a minute of NBA basketball for either club. First, on Nov. 17, 2020, New York sent Utah the first-round pick that became Udoka Azubuike and the second-rounder that became Saben Lee, and received Ante Tomic and the first-round pick New York then spent at 23 on Bolmaro. Then, on Nov. 20, in the three-team deal, Minnesota received the rights to Bolmaro, the rights to Jaden McDaniels and Ricky Rubio; Oklahoma City received the rights to Aleksej Pokusevski, James Johnson and a 2024 Minnesota second-round pick; and New York received the rights to Immanuel Quickley plus Mathias Lessort and a 2023 second-round pick.

The 2022 Rudy Gobert trade sent him to Utah with Malik Beasley, Patrick Beverley, Jarred Vanderbilt and picks; Utah waived him on Feb. 16, 2023 and he has not held an NBA contract since. His NBA career is 49 games at 1.1 points. He has been an established European since: Bayern Munich, then Olimpia Milano on a multi-year deal from July 2024. In 2025-26 he played 29 EuroLeague games at 9.8 points, 3.4 rebounds and 2.9 assists and 20 Italian league games at 9.6 points, and Milano beat Brescia 91-86 in Game 4 to take the Italian semifinal 3-1 and reach the final. In August 2026 he rejoined Argentina for 2027 World Cup qualifying.
