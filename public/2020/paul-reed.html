SCOUTING REPORT

Reed's last college season at DePaul was a 15.1 point, 10.7 rebound, 1.6 assist year in 31.7 minutes over 29 games, with 2.6 blocks and 1.9 steals, 51.6% shooting, 73.8% at the line and 0.6 made threes a game. He had 18 double-doubles and 74 blocks, a DePaul record for a junior, and was named second-team All-Big East.

Philadelphia took him 58th on Nov. 18, 2020, with a pick that had come from the Lakers via Orlando. Eight of the ten boards ranked him, between 27th and 47th, and the other two left him off, for a composite rank of 39th.

Reed started on a two-way deal, played 26 games as a rookie and won G League MVP and Rookie of the Year in Delaware before his March 2021 conversion. He played 38 games in 2021-22 and 69 (two starts) at 10.9 minutes in 2022-23, when his four late free throws helped Philadelphia win Game 1 of the conference semifinals at Boston on May 2, 2023. In 2023-24 he played all 82 games and made 24 starts at 19.4 minutes, averaging 7.3 points and 6.0 rebounds, with a career-high 30 points against Denver on Jan. 27, 2024. Detroit claimed him in July 2024, and he played 45 games at 9.7 minutes in 2024-25; in 2025-26 he played 65 games (11 starts) on 61.7% shooting for a 60-22 team.

Philadelphia converted his two-way deal to a three-year, $3.9 million contract in March 2021, then in July 2023 matched Utah's three-year, $23 million offer sheet, which guaranteed only the first year. The 76ers waived him on July 6, 2024 to sign Caleb Martin, and Detroit claimed him three days later. The Pistons waived him on Dec. 14, 2024, before his $7.7 million salary became guaranteed, re-signed him two days later at the minimum, and in summer 2025 brought him back on a two-year, $11 million deal with a non-guaranteed second year.

Detroit fully guaranteed his $5,602,689 salary for 2026-27 on Sep. 2, 2026, and he is set for unrestricted free agency in 2027. With Duren out for at least two weeks with a hamstring strain, Reed has started since Oct. 25, 2026 and is averaging 14.2 points and 7.6 rebounds through five games.
