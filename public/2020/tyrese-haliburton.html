SCOUTING REPORT

Sacramento took him twelfth overall out of Iowa State. He had just gone for 15.2 points, 5.9 rebounds and 6.5 assists in 22 games at 36.7 minutes a night. The boards had him 5 places above where he went, at #7. The boards ranged from fourth to #13 on him, a spread of 9 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 58 games, 20 starts, 30.1 minutes — 13.0 / 3.0 / 5.3 on 47.2% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +130.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Did not play — out for the season with an Achilles injury.

Against a frozen IPO of $269.91, today's mark of $623.16 is +130.9%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished third in the All-Rookie vote among 2020 draftees.

Sacramento made the pick and traded him fourteen months later. On Feb. 8, 2022 Indiana received Haliburton, Buddy Hield, Tristan Thompson and a trade exception; Sacramento received Domantas Sabonis, Justin Holiday, Jeremy Lamb, a conditional 2023 Indiana second-round pick and a trade exception. He signed a five-year designated rookie-scale maximum extension with Indiana in July 2023, running from 2024-25 through 2028-29. The dollar figure is not stated: it is reported flatly as $260 million in one place and as an explicitly conditional 'up to $260 million' in another, contingent on the deal starting at 30% of the cap.

He tore his right Achilles tendon early in Game 7 of the 2025 NBA Finals on Jun. 22, 2025, having scored nine points on three three-pointers in the first seven minutes. An MRI confirmed the tear and Dr. Martin O'Malley operated at the Hospital for Special Surgery, with a stated timetable of roughly twelve months. He did not play in 2025-26; Indiana went 19-63 without him. By January he was scrimmaging four-on-four and had gone from 180 pounds on Game 7 day to 210, weight he intends to keep for a more physical style. In late June 2026 he said he was operating like a healthy NBA player, while general manager Chad Buchanan cautioned that it is not going to be instantly back to Tyrese.
