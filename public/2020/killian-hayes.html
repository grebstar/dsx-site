SCOUTING REPORT

Detroit took him seventh overall out of Ratiopharm Ulm in the German BBL. He had just gone for 11.6 points, 3.1 rebounds and 5.3 assists in 20 games at 25.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #9, pick 7. The boards ranged from second to #19 on him, a spread of 17 places across 10 of them.

2020-21: 26 games, 18 starts, 25.8 minutes — 6.8 / 2.7 / 5.3 on 35.3% shooting. That ranked him #21 in the class and marked him at $51.92, -73.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 23 games, 3 starts, 17.7 minutes — 5.5 / 2.3 / 3.5 on 30.4% shooting. That ranked him #26 in the class and marked him at $33.31, -83.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $196.79, today's mark of $33.31 is -83.1%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

He has never been traded as an NBA player. Detroit made the pick, and every team change since has been a waiver or a signing. The rookie year turned on one play: seven games in, on Jan. 4, 2021 against Milwaukee, he tore the labrum in his right hip, an MRI confirmed it the next day, and he finished with 26 games.

The 23 games on this page came late and from below. He signed with Cleveland on Sep. 26, 2025 and was waived on Oct. 15. The Cleveland Charge had acquired his G League rights by trade on Sep. 23, and he joined them on Oct. 24, playing 28 G League games at 31.8 minutes for 23.1 points and 8.1 assists. Sacramento called him up on a ten-day contract on Feb. 23, 2026, signed a second ten-day on Mar. 5 and converted him to a two-year deal on Mar. 15 with the second season non-guaranteed. The Kings went 22-60, declined to keep him in late June, and he was an unrestricted free agent from Jul. 1, 2026 with no signing recorded since.
