SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him #29 overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 14.1 points, 4.6 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 33 games at 31.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #32, pick 29. The boards ranged from #18 to #60 on him, a spread of 42 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 52 games, 0 starts, 10.3 minutes — 3.7 / 1.7 / 0.4 on 45.5% shooting. That ranked him #26 in the class and marked him at $33.31, +70.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2016-17 (New Orleans/Brooklyn) -- his last recorded season was 2024-25 with Jiangsu (Chinese CBA); no 2025-26 club found.

Against a frozen IPO of $19.52, today's mark of $25.57 is +31.0%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Oklahoma City took him 29th and traded his rights that night to Golden State, with cash, for the rights to Andre Roberson (No. 26). Golden State sent him on, with Malcolm Lee, to Phoenix for the rights to Nemanja Nedovic (No. 30). He signed with the Suns on Jul. 12, 2013, and they exercised his third- and fourth-year options.

Phoenix waived him on Oct. 24, 2016. New Orleans signed him Nov. 7 and waived him Nov. 20 after three games; the Greensboro Swarm claimed him, and Brooklyn called him up on a 10-day contract on Mar. 15, 2017 and signed him for the rest of the season on Apr. 4 (12 games, 7.9 points on .557 shooting). Brooklyn waived him Jul. 28, 2017; Portland signed him to a camp deal Sep. 11 and waived him Oct. 14, 2017.

Since then he has played in the G League (Northern Arizona, Maine) and abroad: clubs in China, Turkey, Germany, France, Ukraine, Puerto Rico, Israel and Lebanon, and in 2024-25 Jiangsu (CBA) and El Calor de Cancun (Mexico).
