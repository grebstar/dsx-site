SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him twelfth overall out of Pittsburgh. He had just gone for 7.2 points, 6.3 rebounds and 0.6 assists in 32 games at 23.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #11, pick 12. The boards ranged from third to #30 on him, a spread of 27 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 81 games, 20 starts, 14.8 minutes — 3.3 / 4.1 / 0.5 on 50.3% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +14.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: 32 games, 11 starts, 22.8 minutes — 5.8 / 8.6 / 1.5 on 50.4% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, +80.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $149.42, today's mark of $269.91 is +80.6%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished tenth in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Oklahoma City took him twelfth, kept him seven seasons and signed him to a rookie-scale extension on Oct. 31, 2016. On Nov. 23, 2020 he went to New Orleans in the multi-team trade that sent Jrue Holiday out of New Orleans, and on Aug. 7, 2021 the Pelicans sent him and Eric Bledsoe to Memphis for Jonas Valanciunas and draft picks. He signed an extension with the Grizzlies on Oct. 1, 2022.

Knee surgery in October 2023 cost him all of 2023-24. On Feb. 1, 2024, while he was out, Memphis traded him to Houston for Victor Oladipo, the second pick of the 2013 draft, and three second-round picks. He returned for 58 games (3 starts) in 2024-25 and signed a three-year, $39 million extension with the Rockets on Jun. 14, 2025.
