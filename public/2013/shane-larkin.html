Against a frozen IPO of $116.93, today's mark of $51.92 is -55.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Atlanta took him 18th on Jun. 27, 2013 and sent his rights to Dallas that night for Jared Cunningham, the draft rights to Lucas Nogueira and Mike Muscala, and cash. He signed with the Mavericks on Jul. 29, 2013. On Jun. 25, 2014 Dallas traded him to New York with Jose Calderon, Samuel Dalembert, Wayne Ellington and two 2014 second-round picks for Tyson Chandler and Raymond Felton. He signed with Brooklyn on Jul. 9, 2015 and played 78 games there, then left for Baskonia in Spain for 2016-17 and returned to the NBA with Boston (signed Jul. 31, 2017; 54 games).

He joined Anadolu Efes for 2018-19 and stayed eight seasons, the last four as captain: EuroLeague titles in 2021 and 2022, All-EuroLeague First Team in 2022 and Second Team in 2021. He obtained Turkish citizenship in 2019-20 and had 13 points, six rebounds and nine assists for Turkey in the EuroBasket 2025 final, an 88-83 loss to Germany and Dennis Schroder. Efes gave him a four-year deal in May 2024, but announced his departure on Jun. 30, 2026; he signed with Fenerbahce on Jul. 13, 2026.
