SCOUTING REPORT

Portland took him tenth overall out of Lehigh. He had just gone for 23.9 points, 5.0 rebounds and 2.9 assists in 12 games at 31.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #8, pick 10. The boards ranged from fifth to #14 on him, a spread of 9 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 38 games, 0 starts, 12.5 minutes — 5.3 / 1.3 / 0.7 on 41.6% shooting. That ranked him #19 in the class and marked him at $62.59, -72.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 76 games, 60 starts, 29.8 minutes — 18.7 / 3.3 / 3.9 on 45.5% shooting. That ranked him second in the class and marked him at $808.24, +252.8% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $229.10, today's mark of $808.24 is +252.8%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #19 in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Portland took him tenth and kept him for eight and a half seasons. He signed a rookie-scale extension on Jul. 27, 2016, after his Most Improved Player season, and a veteran extension on Jul. 30, 2019. On Feb. 8, 2022 the Blazers traded him to New Orleans with Larry Nance Jr. and Tony Snell, the 20th pick of the same 2013 draft, for Josh Hart, Nickeil Alexander-Walker, Tomas Satoransky, Didi Louzada and draft picks. He signed another extension with the Pelicans on Sep. 26, 2022.

New Orleans agreed on Jun. 24, 2025 to send him and Kelly Olynyk, the 13th pick of his class, to Washington for Jordan Poole, Saddiq Bey and the draft rights to Micah Peavy; the deal was completed Jul. 6, 2025. Washington traded him to Atlanta with Corey Kispert for Trae Young, a deal reported Jan. 7 and completed Jan. 9, 2026. On Jun. 21, 2026 he agreed to a one-year, $21 million deal to stay with the Hawks, which RealGM records as a veteran extension signed Jun. 22.
