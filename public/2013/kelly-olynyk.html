SCOUTING REPORT

Dallas took him #13 overall out of Gonzaga. He had just gone for 17.8 points, 7.3 rebounds and 1.7 assists in 32 games at 26.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #14, pick 13. The boards ranged from twelfth to #26 on him, a spread of 14 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 70 games, 9 starts, 20.0 minutes — 8.7 / 5.2 / 1.6 on 46.6% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +209.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 42 games, 0 starts, 8.6 minutes — 3.2 / 1.8 / 1.2 on 49.0% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, +119.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $104.27, today's mark of $229.10 is +119.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished sixth in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Dallas drafted him thirteenth and traded his rights that night to Boston for the rights to Lucas Nogueira (16th) and two 2014 second-round picks. After four Celtics seasons he signed with Miami on Jul. 7, 2017. On Mar. 25, 2021 the Heat sent him to Houston with Avery Bradley and a 2022 first-round swap for Victor Oladipo, the 2013 draft's second pick; he averaged 19.0 points in 27 Rockets games.

He signed with Detroit on Aug. 6, 2021, went to Utah for Bojan Bogdanovic on Sep. 22, 2022, and on Feb. 8, 2024 went to Toronto with Ochai Agbaji for Kira Lewis Jr., Otto Porter Jr. (third pick in 2013) and a 2024 first. Toronto sent him to New Orleans in the Brandon Ingram trade on Feb. 6, 2025. On Jul. 6, 2025 the Pelicans moved him to Washington with CJ McCollum (tenth pick in 2013) for Jordan Poole and Saddiq Bey, and three days later the Wizards traded him to San Antonio for Malaki Branham, Blake Wesley and a second.
