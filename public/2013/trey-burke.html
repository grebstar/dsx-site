SCOUTING REPORT

Minnesota took him ninth overall out of Michigan. He had just gone for 18.6 points, 3.2 rebounds and 6.7 assists in 39 games at 35.3 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #7, pick 9. The boards ranged from fourth to #13 on him, a spread of 9 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 70 games, 68 starts, 32.3 minutes — 12.8 / 3.0 / 5.7 on 38.0% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +130.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Dallas) -- played for Astros de Jalisco (Mexico) from January to June 2026.

Against a frozen IPO of $269.91, today's mark of $75.99 is -71.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished third in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Minnesota drafted him ninth and traded his rights on draft night to Utah for the rights to two other members of the class, Shabazz Muhammad (14th) and Gorgui Dieng (21st). Utah sent him to Washington for a 2021 second-round pick on Jul. 7, 2016. After 26 G League games for Westchester at 26.6 points, New York signed him on Jan. 14, 2018.

On Jan. 31, 2019 the Knicks traded him to Dallas with Tim Hardaway Jr., the 24th pick of his class, Kristaps Porzingis and Courtney Lee for DeAndre Jordan, Wesley Matthews, Dennis Smith Jr. and two first-round picks. After a half-season in Philadelphia he rejoined Dallas for the 2020 restart and signed a three-year, $10 million deal there that fall. Dallas traded him to Houston on Jun. 24, 2022 in the Christian Wood deal; Houston sent him to Oklahoma City on Sep. 30, and the Thunder waived him on Oct. 17, 2022.

Since then he has played for the Stockton Kings (2022-23) and Mexico City Capitanes (2023-25) in the G League, Guangdong Dongguan in China and Leones de Ponce in Puerto Rico (2025), and Astros de Jalisco in Mexico (2025-26).
