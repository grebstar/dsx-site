SCOUTING REPORT

Indiana took him #23 overall out of Arizona. He had just gone for 13.4 points, 5.3 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 35 games at 33.0 minutes a night. He went 21 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #44, drafted 23. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #29 to #60 on him, a spread of 31 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 28 games, 0 starts, 8.1 minutes — 1.7 / 1.5 / 0.4 on 42.5% shooting. That ranked him #34 in the class and marked him at $16.15, +254.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Atlanta) -- traded to New York and waived in January 2022; no club found since.

Against a frozen IPO of $4.55, today's mark of $36.38 is +698.9%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Indiana took him 23rd and picked up his third-year option, then declined his fourth-year option; he signed a four-year, $52 million deal with New Orleans in July 2016 (signed Jul. 21). In August 2017 he tore his left proximal hamstring in a workout and had surgery, and he played 12 games that season.

On Jul. 7, 2019 he moved twice: New Orleans sent him to Atlanta in the trade that also sent the draft rights to De'Andre Hunter there, and Atlanta sent him with Miles Plumlee to Memphis for Chandler Parsons. On Feb. 6, 2020 Memphis traded him with Jae Crowder and Andre Iguodala to Miami in a three-team deal in which Memphis received Gorgui Dieng from Minnesota and Justise Winslow and Dion Waiters from Miami, and Minnesota received James Johnson. He signed with Atlanta on Nov. 25, 2020 and again on Aug. 5, 2021. After his December 2021 hamstring tear, Atlanta traded him on Jan. 13, 2022 to New York with Cam Reddish, a 2025 second and cash for Kevin Knox II and a protected first; the Knicks waived him on Jan. 19, 2022.
