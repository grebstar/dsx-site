SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him #30 overall out of Lietuvos Rytas in the Lithuanian LKL + VTB United League + EuroLeague. He had just gone for 10.5 points, 2.4 rebounds and 3.0 assists in 45 games at 21.9 minutes a night. He went 17 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #47, drafted 30. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #32 to #60 on him, a spread of 28 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 24 games, 0 starts, 5.9 minutes — 1.1 / 0.6 / 0.5 on 20.5% shooting. That ranked him #40 in the class and marked him at $8.31, +282.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2013-14 (Golden State) -- spent 2025-26 with AS Monaco (EuroLeague / LNB Elite).

Against a frozen IPO of $2.17, today's mark of $23.39 is +975.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Phoenix took him 30th and traded his rights on draft night to Golden State for Malcolm Lee and the rights to Archie Goodwin (No. 29), whom the Warriors had acquired that night from Oklahoma City for the rights to Andre Roberson (No. 26). He signed with the Warriors on Jul. 9, 2013, and played five Las Vegas Summer League games for them in July 2014.

After his release on Nov. 11, 2014 he stayed in Europe: Valencia (2014-15), Unicaja Malaga (2015-2018), Olimpia Milano (2018-2020), Panathinaikos (2020-2022), where he averaged 15.7 points in 2020-21, Crvena Zvezda (2022-2025), the club where he started his career, and AS Monaco (2025-26).
