SCOUTING REPORT

the Clippers took him #25 overall out of North Carolina. He had just gone for 13.9 points, 6.5 rebounds and 2.9 assists in 35 games at 31.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #27, pick 25. The boards ranged from #19 to #60 on him, a spread of 41 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 43 games, 0 starts, 9.2 minutes — 2.7 / 1.3 / 0.3 on 35.5% shooting. That ranked him #22 in the class and marked him at $47.43, +55.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2023-24 (Houston) -- an unrestricted free agent since July 2024; no club found.

Against a frozen IPO of $30.50, today's mark of $33.31 is +9.2%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished #23 in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

The Clippers took him 25th. On Jan. 15, 2015 a three-team trade sent him to Phoenix, Austin Rivers from Boston to the Clippers, and Chris Douglas-Roberts and Shavlik Randolph to Boston. Phoenix sent him to Detroit on Jul. 9, 2015 with Marcus Morris and Danny Granger for a 2020 second. He re-signed with Detroit on Jul. 14, 2017, then started 52 games in 2017-18 at 11.3 points and 44.5% from three.

Detroit traded him to the Lakers on Feb. 6, 2019 for Svi Mykhailiuk and a 2021 second. A two-year, $21 million agreement with the Knicks in July 2019 was revised below the $4.7 million exception after an issue surfaced in his physical; he had cervical disc surgery that month and played 29 games in 2019-20. He signed a three-year, $30.5 million deal with Dallas in August 2021 and was the NBA's 2022 Social Justice Champion. Dallas traded him to San Antonio on Jul. 12, 2023 in the Grant Williams sign-and-trade; the Spurs waived him on Sep. 30, 2023 and he signed a one-year minimum deal with Houston on Oct. 4, 2023.
