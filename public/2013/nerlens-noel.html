SCOUTING REPORT

New Orleans took him sixth overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 10.5 points, 9.5 rebounds and 1.6 assists in 24 games at 31.9 minutes a night. The boards had him 5 places above where he went, at #1. The boards ranged from first to fourth on him, a spread of 3 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: he did not appear in an NBA game. missed the entire 2013-14 season recovering from the knee injury that ended his Kentucky season on Feb. 12, 2013. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2022-23 (Detroit/Brooklyn) -- signed with Sacramento in July 2023 and was waived that September; unsigned since.

Against a frozen IPO of $1,000.00, today's mark of $93.47 is -90.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

New Orleans took him sixth and traded his rights on draft night to Philadelphia, with a 2014 first-round pick, for Jrue Holiday and the rights to Pierre Jackson, the 42nd pick of the same draft; the deal was finalized Jul. 10, 2013. On Feb. 23, 2017 Philadelphia traded him to Dallas for Justin Anderson, Andrew Bogut and a conditional first-round pick.

Dallas made him a restricted free agent in 2017 and he signed a one-year deal to stay on Aug. 25, 2017. A lawsuit he filed in August 2021 against his former agent Rich Paul and Klutch Sports alleged Paul had advised him to turn down a reported four-year, $70 million Dallas offer that summer; the two sides settled in January 2023. He played 2018-20 in Oklahoma City and 2020-22 in New York, re-signing there for three years and $32 million in August 2021.

New York traded him to Detroit with Alec Burks on Jul. 11, 2022. The Pistons bought him out in February 2023, he played three games for Brooklyn in March, and Sacramento signed him on Jul. 21, 2023 and waived him on Sep. 12, 2023, his last NBA transaction.
