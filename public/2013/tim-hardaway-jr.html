New York took him #24 overall out of Michigan. He had just gone for 14.5 points, 4.7 rebounds and 2.4 assists in 38 games at 34.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #25, pick 24. The boards ranged from #20 to #60 on him, a spread of 40 places across 16 of them.

Against a frozen IPO of $36.38, today's mark of $396.43 is +989.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished fifth in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

New York took him 24th and traded him on Jun. 25, 2015 to Atlanta for the rights to Jerian Grant. After the Hawks tendered a qualifying offer in 2017, he signed a multi-year offer sheet with the Knicks on Jul. 6, 2017 and joined them on Jul. 8.

On Jan. 31, 2019 New York sent him to Dallas with Trey Burke (the ninth pick in the same draft), Kristaps Porzingis and Courtney Lee for DeAndre Jordan, Wesley Matthews, Dennis Smith Jr. and two first-round picks. He exercised his player option in November 2020 and re-signed with Dallas on a four-year deal on Aug. 9, 2021; he fractured the fifth metatarsal in his left foot in January 2022 and played 42 games that season. On Jul. 6, 2024 Dallas traded him and three second-round picks to Detroit for Quentin Grimes. He signed a one-year minimum deal with Denver on Jul. 10, 2025 and a one-year, $6.5 million deal with Miami on Jul. 6, 2026.
