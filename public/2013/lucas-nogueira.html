SCOUTING REPORT

Boston took him #16 overall out of Estudiantes in the Spanish ACB + Copa del Rey. He had just gone for 5.5 points, 3.5 rebounds and 0.3 assists in 35 games at 13.6 minutes a night. He went 12 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #28, drafted 16. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from ninth to #60 on him, a spread of 51 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: he did not appear in an NBA game. stayed at Estudiantes (Liga ACB) for 2013-14 as a draft-and-stash; his NBA debut came in 2014-15. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2017-18 (Toronto) -- his last recorded professional games were with the Guelph Nighthawks (CEBL, Canada) in 2022.

Against a frozen IPO of $27.93, today's mark of $56.94 is +103.9%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Boston took him 16th on Jun. 27, 2013 and moved him twice that night. The Celtics sent his rights and two 2014 second-round picks to Dallas for the rights to Kelly Olynyk; Dallas then sent his rights, Jared Cunningham, the rights to Mike Muscala and cash to Atlanta for the rights to Shane Larkin. Atlanta removed his cap hold on Oct. 2, 2013 and he stayed at Estudiantes; in December 2013 he was reported to need two months of rest for tendinitis in both knees.

On Jun. 30, 2014 Atlanta traded his rights and Lou Williams to Toronto for John Salmons and a 2015 second-round pick. He signed with the Raptors on Jul. 30, 2014, and Toronto exercised his team options on Sep. 29, 2015 and Oct. 23, 2016, with D-League stints at Fort Wayne and Raptors 905 along the way. His busiest season was 2016-17: 57 games at 4.4 points and 4.3 rebounds on .660 shooting.

He became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2018 after 141 NBA games, all for Toronto. He then played 13 games for Fuenlabrada in Spain (2018-19), 10 for Basquete Cearense in Brazil (2020-21), and 12 in 2021-22 split between Sao Paulo FC and the Guelph Nighthawks.
