SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him fifth overall out of Maryland. He had just gone for 11.9 points, 7.8 rebounds and 1.0 assists in 38 games at 26.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #5, pick 5. The boards ranged from first to #13 on him, a spread of 12 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 42 games, 3 starts, 8.6 minutes — 2.0 / 2.4 / 0.1 on 42.3% shooting. That ranked him #23 in the class and marked him at $43.38, -89.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2024-25 (Sacramento/LA Lakers) -- waived by New York from a training-camp deal in October 2025, then signed with Real Madrid (Liga ACB / EuroLeague) and played the 2025-26 season there.

Against a frozen IPO of $396.43, today's mark of $104.27 is -73.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #21 in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Phoenix took him fifth and kept him five seasons, re-signing him for 2017-18 after making him a restricted free agent. He signed with Atlanta in August 2018, and on Feb. 6, 2020 the Hawks traded him and Jabari Parker to Sacramento for Dewayne Dedmon and two second-round picks. He opened 2020-21 in Toronto (waived Jan. 19, 2021) and finished it in Washington, then returned to Sacramento on a multi-year deal in August 2021 and re-signed there in 2023 and 2024.

Sacramento traded him to Washington on Feb. 6, 2025; the Wizards waived him two days later, and after first agreeing to join Indiana he signed with the Lakers on Feb. 11. A Knicks camp deal in September 2025 ended in his release in October, and he left for Real Madrid. RealGM lists a further Real Madrid signing dated Sep. 14, 2026.
