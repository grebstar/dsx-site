SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took him #17 overall out of Phantoms Braunschweig in the German BBL. He had just gone for 12.0 points, 2.5 rebounds and 3.2 assists in 32 games at 25.3 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #19, pick 17. The boards ranged from eighth to #60 on him, a spread of 52 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 49 games, 0 starts, 13.1 minutes — 3.7 / 1.2 / 1.9 on 38.3% shooting. That ranked him #24 in the class and marked him at $39.70, -36.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 70 games, 17 starts, 24.2 minutes — 10.8 / 2.7 / 4.9 on 40.5% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, +691.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $62.59, today's mark of $495.29 is +691.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Atlanta took him 17th and signed him on Jul. 10, 2013. On Jul. 25, 2018 he went to Oklahoma City in a three-team trade that sent Carmelo Anthony and a protected 2022 first to Atlanta and moved fellow 2013 draftee Mike Muscala from Atlanta to Philadelphia. The Thunder traded him to the Lakers on Nov. 19, 2020 for Danny Green and the rights to Jaden McDaniels.

Since then: Boston (signed Aug. 13, 2021), Houston (traded Feb. 10, 2022), the Lakers again (Sep. 16, 2022), Toronto (Jul. 12, 2023), Brooklyn (traded Feb. 8, 2024), Golden State (Dec. 15, 2024) and Detroit (Feb. 6, 2025). On Jul. 7, 2025 he went to Sacramento in a sign-and-trade with Detroit, on a reported three-year, $45 million deal. The Kings sent him to Cleveland on Feb. 1, 2026, and Cleveland traded him to Charlotte for Tre Mann on Aug. 20, 2026 — his 12th NBA franchise.

In January 2026 the NBA suspended him three games for attempting to strike Luka Doncic in a tunnel after a Dec. 28, 2025 Kings-Lakers game. For Germany he was MVP of the 2023 FIBA World Cup and EuroBasket 2025, with 16 points and 12 assists in the 88-83 final win over Turkey and Shane Larkin.
