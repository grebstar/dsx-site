SCOUTING REPORT

Utah took him #14 overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 17.9 points, 5.2 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 32 games at 30.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #15, pick 14. The boards ranged from ninth to #60 on him, a spread of 51 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 37 games, 0 starts, 7.8 minutes — 3.9 / 1.4 / 0.2 on 46.0% shooting. That ranked him #21 in the class and marked him at $51.92, -44.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2017-18 (Minnesota/Milwaukee) -- played for Beirut Club (Lebanon) in February 2026, then Piratas de La Guaira (Venezuela) in March 2026.

Against a frozen IPO of $93.47, today's mark of $62.59 is -33.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #22 in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Utah drafted him fourteenth and traded his rights that night to Minnesota, along with the rights to Gorgui Dieng (21st), for the rights to Trey Burke (ninth). He spent four seasons with the Timberwolves, playing all 82 games in 2015-16. Minnesota withdrew its qualifying offer in July 2017, re-signed him to a one-year, $1.6 million deal that September, and waived him after a buyout in March 2018. He finished 2017-18 with Milwaukee, signing on Mar. 4, and the Bucks waived him in preseason on Oct. 11, 2018.

He has played abroad since: 29.8 points a game for Shanxi Zhongyu in the Chinese CBA in 2018-19, then Shenzhen, the Philippines' San Miguel Beermen, Beirut Club and Guangdong in 2022-23, and Venezuela's Trotamundos and Lebanon's Sagesse in 2024-25, with G League stints at Grand Rapids in 2021-22 and Stockton in 2023-24.
