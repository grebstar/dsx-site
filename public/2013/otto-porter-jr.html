SCOUTING REPORT

Washington took him third overall out of Georgetown. He had just gone for 16.2 points, 7.5 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 31 games at 35.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #2, pick 3. The boards ranged from third to sixth on him, a spread of 3 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 37 games, 0 starts, 8.6 minutes — 2.1 / 1.5 / 0.3 on 36.3% shooting. That ranked him #33 in the class and marked him at $17.77, -97.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2023-24 (Toronto) -- retired in March 2024.

Against a frozen IPO of $808.24, today's mark of $131.64 is -83.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Washington took him third. In July 2017 he signed a four-year, $106.5 million offer sheet with Brooklyn as a restricted free agent, and the Wizards matched it. On Feb. 6, 2019 Washington traded him to Chicago for Jabari Parker, Bobby Portis and a conditional second-round pick. On Mar. 25, 2021 the Bulls sent him to Orlando with Wendell Carter Jr. and two conditional firsts in the Nikola Vucevic trade.

He signed with Golden State in August 2021 and won the 2022 championship there in 63 regular-season games. He moved to Toronto on a two-year deal in July 2022; left foot surgery ended his 2022-23 season after eight games. On Feb. 8, 2024 the Raptors traded him to Utah with Kira Lewis Jr. and a 2024 first-round pick for Kelly Olynyk and Ochai Agbaji. Utah waived him on Mar. 11, 2024, and he retired.
