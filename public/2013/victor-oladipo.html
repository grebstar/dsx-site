SCOUTING REPORT

Orlando took him second overall out of Indiana. He had just gone for 13.6 points, 6.3 rebounds and 2.1 assists in 36 games at 28.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #3, pick 2. The boards ranged from first to seventh on him, a spread of 6 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 80 games, 44 starts, 31.1 minutes — 13.8 / 4.1 / 4.1 on 41.9% shooting. That ranked him second in the class and marked him at $808.24, +29.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2022-23 (Miami) -- spent 2025-26 with the Wisconsin Herd (G League) without an NBA contract.

Against a frozen IPO of $623.16, today's mark of $149.42 is -76.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished second in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Orlando took him second. On Jun. 23, 2016 he went to Oklahoma City with Ersan Ilyasova and the draft rights to Domantas Sabonis for Serge Ibaka, and a year later, on Jul. 6, 2017, to Indiana with Sabonis for Paul George. In Indiana he was the 2018 Most Improved Player, an All-Star in 2018 and 2019, and All-NBA Third Team in 2018, before a ruptured right quadriceps tendon in January 2019.

On Jan. 16, 2021 he was part of the James Harden trade that sent him to Houston, and on Mar. 25, 2021 Houston sent him to Miami for Kelly Olynyk, Avery Bradley and a 2022 first-round swap. He re-signed with Miami in 2021 and 2022, then tore his left patellar tendon in Game 3 of Miami's April 2023 first-round series against Milwaukee. Miami traded him to Oklahoma City on Jul. 6, 2023; the Thunder sent him to Houston on Oct. 17, 2023 in the Kevin Porter Jr. deal; and on Feb. 1, 2024 Houston traded him and three second-round picks to Memphis for Steven Adams. Memphis waived him on Feb. 8, 2024.
