SCOUTING REPORT

Detroit took him eighth overall out of Georgia. He had just gone for 18.5 points, 7.1 rebounds and 1.8 assists in 32 games at 33.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #10, pick 8. The boards ranged from fifth to #30 on him, a spread of 25 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 80 games, 41 starts, 19.8 minutes — 5.9 / 2.0 / 0.7 on 39.6% shooting. That ranked him #15 in the class and marked him at $93.47, -45.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: 51 games, 14 starts, 21.3 minutes — 8.4 / 2.5 / 2.7 on 41.0% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +89.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $170.70, today's mark of $322.90 is +89.2%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #15 in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Detroit took him eighth and kept him four seasons. After the Pistons withdrew his qualifying offer on Jul. 7, 2017, he signed with the Lakers on Jul. 13 and re-signed with them in 2018, 2019 and 2020, winning the 2020 championship. On Aug. 6, 2021 he went to Washington with Kyle Kuzma, Montrezl Harrell and others in the Russell Westbrook trade.

Washington sent him to Denver with Ish Smith on Jul. 6, 2022 for Will Barton and Monte Morris; he signed an extension on Jul. 16, 2022 and won a second title with the Nuggets in 2023. He signed a three-year, $66 million deal with Orlando in July 2024, and on Jun. 15, 2025 Orlando traded him to Memphis, with Cole Anthony and first-round draft compensation, for Desmond Bane.

He exercised his $21.6 million player option for 2026-27 in June 2026, then agreed to a buyout. Memphis waived him on Jul. 25, 2026 and he signed a one-year deal with Philadelphia on Aug. 5.
