SCOUTING REPORT

Cleveland took him first overall out of UNLV. He had just gone for 16.1 points, 8.1 rebounds and 1.0 assists in 35 games at 27.1 minutes a night. He went 5 picks ahead of his composite, which was #6. The boards ranged from third to eleventh on him, a spread of 8 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 52 games, 0 starts, 12.8 minutes — 4.2 / 3.0 / 0.3 on 35.6% shooting. That ranked him #18 in the class and marked him at $68.95, -78.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2016-17 (Brooklyn) -- played in Taiwan (Hsinchu Lioneers 2022-23, Formosa Dreamers 2024-25) and signed with Al-Najma (Bahrain) in April 2025; no 2025-26 club found.

Against a frozen IPO of $322.90, today's mark of $170.70 is -47.1%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #18 in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Cleveland took him first and signed him on Aug. 15, 2013. On Aug. 23, 2014 he went to Minnesota with Andrew Wiggins in the three-team trade that sent Kevin Love to Cleveland; Thaddeus Young came to Minnesota from Philadelphia, which received Luc Mbah a Moute, Alexey Shved and a Miami first-round pick. Minnesota waived him after a buyout on Sep. 23, 2015. He signed with Toronto (Sep. 27, 2015; waived Mar. 1, 2016) and Brooklyn (Jul. 14, 2016; waived Jan. 9, 2017), then finished 2016-17 at Fenerbahce in Turkey.

Camp deals with Phoenix (2017) and Houston (2019) ended in waivers before the regular season; in between he played G League ball for Northern Arizona, Maine and Agua Caliente. Overseas he signed with Santurce in Puerto Rico (May 2021) and Hapoel Jerusalem (Aug. 2021), played 2022-23 in Taiwan with the Hsinchu Lioneers, and signed with Goyang Sono in Korea in July 2023 but left in September before playing an official game. He returned to Taiwan with the Formosa Dreamers in 2024-25 and signed in Bahrain in April 2025.
