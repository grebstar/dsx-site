SCOUTING REPORT

Cleveland took him #19 overall out of Triumph Lyubertsy in the VTB United League + Russian PBL + EuroCup. He had just gone for 16.4 points, 4.3 rebounds and 2.1 assists in 41 games at 31.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #20, pick 19. The boards ranged from #13 to #60 on him, a spread of 47 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 22 games, 1 starts, 7.1 minutes — 1.7 / 0.7 / 0.3 on 34.3% shooting. That ranked him #38 in the class and marked him at $10.64, -81.3% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2015-16 (Brooklyn) -- spent 2025-26 with Zenit St. Petersburg (VTB United League).

Against a frozen IPO of $56.94, today's mark of $47.43 is -16.7%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

Cleveland took him 19th and signed him on Aug. 15, 2013. On Jul. 10, 2014 the Cavaliers traded him and Jarrett Jack to Brooklyn for Marcus Thornton and the draft rights to Edin Bavcic and Ilkan Karaman, part of a three-team deal with Boston that cleared cap space in Cleveland. The Nets exercised his team option on Oct. 24, 2014. After a knee injury ended 2014-15, he played 40 games in 2015-16 and became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2016.

He signed with Zenit St. Petersburg on Jul. 2, 2016 and averaged 15.1 points in 2016-17 and 15.0 in 2017-18, winning VTB United League Player of the Month for January in both seasons. He moved to Khimki in the EuroLeague on Jun. 28, 2019 for two seasons, returned to Zenit for 2021-22, and re-signed there on Jun. 21, 2024.
