Denver took him #27 overall out of Cholet in the French Pro A + EuroCup. He had just gone for 8.1 points, 5.0 rebounds and 0.4 assists in 30 games at 21.4 minutes a night. The boards had him 11 places above where he went, at #16. The boards ranged from fourth to #34 on him, a spread of 30 places across 16 of them.

Against a frozen IPO of $84.15, today's mark of $623.16 is +640.6%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Denver took him 27th and traded his rights on draft night to Utah for the rights to Erick Green (No. 46) and cash. He signed with the Jazz on Jul. 3, 2013, signed a rookie-scale extension on Oct. 31, 2016 and a veteran extension on Dec. 20, 2020. In Utah he won Defensive Player of the Year in 2018, 2019 and 2021, made three All-Star teams (2020-2022) and four All-NBA teams.

On Jul. 6, 2022 Utah traded him to Minnesota for Malik Beasley, Patrick Beverley, Leandro Bolmaro, Jarred Vanderbilt, the draft rights to Walker Kessler, Minnesota's 2023, 2025 and 2027 first-round picks, a 2026 first-round swap and a conditional 2029 first. He won a fourth Defensive Player of the Year award in 2024, and in October 2024 agreed to a three-year, $110 million extension with a third-year player option, declining his 2025-26 option to do it.
