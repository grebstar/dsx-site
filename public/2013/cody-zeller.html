SCOUTING REPORT

Charlotte took him fourth overall out of Indiana. He had just gone for 16.5 points, 8.0 rebounds and 1.3 assists in 36 games at 29.5 minutes a night. He went 8 picks ahead of his composite, which was #12. The boards ranged from seventh to #19 on him, a spread of 12 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 82 games, 3 starts, 17.3 minutes — 6.0 / 4.3 / 1.1 on 42.6% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, +49.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2023-24 (New Orleans) -- traded to Atlanta and then Houston, and waived by Houston in March 2025; unsigned since.

Against a frozen IPO of $131.64, today's mark of $116.93 is -11.2%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished ninth in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Charlotte took him fourth and kept him eight seasons, signing him to a rookie-scale extension on Oct. 31, 2016. He left as a free agent in 2021 for Portland, which waived him in February 2022. After a Utah camp deal ended in October 2022, he signed with Miami in February 2023 and played 21 playoff games for the Heat that spring.

He signed with New Orleans for 2023-24. On Jul. 6, 2024 he re-signed with the Pelicans as part of a sign-and-trade to Atlanta in the Dejounte Murray deal, with Dyson Daniels, Larry Nance Jr., E.J. Liddell and two first-round picks going to the Hawks. Atlanta traded him and a 2028 second-round pick to Houston on Feb. 6, 2025 for the draft rights to Alpha Kaba, a move that took the Hawks under the luxury tax. Houston waived him on Mar. 2, 2025 to convert Nate Williams to a standard contract.
