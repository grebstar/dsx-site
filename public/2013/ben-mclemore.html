SCOUTING REPORT

Sacramento took him seventh overall out of Kansas. He had just gone for 15.9 points, 5.2 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 37 games at 32.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 7. The boards ranged from first to #13 on him, a spread of 12 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 82 games, 55 starts, 26.7 minutes — 8.8 / 2.9 / 1.0 on 37.6% shooting. That ranked him eleventh in the class and marked him at $149.42, -69.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Portland) -- convicted by an Oregon jury in July 2025 of first-degree rape and related charges and sentenced to 100 months in prison; he maintains his innocence.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $84.15 is -83.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished eleventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Sacramento took him seventh and kept him four seasons. He signed with Memphis in July 2017; on Jul. 17, 2018 Memphis traded him back to Sacramento for Garrett Temple, and the Kings waived him in February 2019. He then played for Houston, the Lakers and Portland (2021-22), and after that in China (Shandong), Greece (AEK Athens), Spain (Rio Breogan) and, in 2024-25, Turkey (Merkezefendi), averaging 12.3 points there.

On Apr. 9, 2024 he was arrested on a Clackamas County grand jury indictment charging first-degree rape, first-degree unlawful sexual penetration and two counts of second-degree sexual abuse, from an Oct. 3, 2021 party at then-teammate Robert Covington's Lake Oswego home. He denied the allegations and was released on bail. On Jul. 3, 2025 a jury found him guilty of rape, unlawful sexual penetration and one count of sexual abuse, and not guilty on the second sexual abuse count; he had testified the encounter was consensual. He was sentenced on Jul. 9, 2025 to 100 months in prison, and later that month said he maintains his innocence and that "this fight is not over."
