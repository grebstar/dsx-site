He was priced at $0.00 at IPO — below the replacement line at composite rank 50 — so there is no percentage to quote from zero. He is carried as FROM UNLISTED. He finished twelfth in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

The Lakers took him 48th and signed him to a one-year, non-guaranteed deal on Sep. 20, 2013. In the summer of 2014 they tendered a qualifying offer, withdrew it on Jul. 21, 2014 and re-signed him to a multi-year deal the same day. He played 36 games in 2015-16 and became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2016.

Atlanta signed him to a camp deal on Sep. 19, 2016 and waived him Oct. 19; Boston signed and waived him days later, and after an allocation to the Maine Red Claws, Atlanta signed him on Oct. 31, 2016, waived him Jan. 6, 2017 and brought him back Feb. 24 for the rest of the season (16 games). The Hawks traded him to Houston for cash on Jun. 28, 2017, and the Rockets waived him Jul. 7.

He signed with Real Betis in Spain in September 2017 (13.4 points in 34 ACB games), then moved to Japan's B.League, averaging 20.1, 22.4 and 21.0 points in his first three seasons with the Sun Rockers.
