SCOUTING REPORT

Utah took him #21 overall out of Louisville. He had just gone for 9.8 points, 9.4 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 33 games at 31.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #18, pick 21. The boards ranged from twelfth to #60 on him, a spread of 48 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 60 games, 15 starts, 13.6 minutes — 4.8 / 5.0 / 0.7 on 49.8% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, +232.3% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2022-23 (San Antonio) -- retired as a player in December 2023 and moved into the Spurs' front office.

Against a frozen IPO of $68.95, today's mark of $39.70 is -42.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished eighth in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Utah took him 21st on Jun. 27, 2013 and traded him the same night: his rights and those of Shabazz Muhammad (the 14th pick) went to Minnesota for the rights to Trey Burke (the ninth pick). He signed on Jul. 13, 2013, Minnesota picked up both of his team options, and on Oct. 31, 2016 he agreed to a four-year, $64 million rookie-scale extension.

On Feb. 6, 2020 he was part of a three-team trade: Minnesota sent him to Memphis and received James Johnson from Miami; Memphis also received Justise Winslow and Dion Waiters from Miami and sent Jae Crowder, Solomon Hill and Andre Iguodala to Miami. Memphis waived him on Mar. 26, 2021. He signed with San Antonio three days later, with Atlanta on Aug. 9, 2021, and with San Antonio again on Aug. 9, 2022; the Spurs waived him on Jan. 5, 2023, brought him back on two 10-day contracts (Jan. 8 and Jan. 20) and re-signed him on Feb. 10, 2023. He retired in December 2023 and joined the Spurs' front office.
