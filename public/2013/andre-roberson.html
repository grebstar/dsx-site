SCOUTING REPORT

Minnesota took him #26 overall out of Colorado. He had just gone for 10.9 points, 11.2 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 31 games at 33.4 minutes a night. He went 24 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #50, drafted 26. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #27 to #60 on him, a spread of 33 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 40 games, 16 starts, 10.0 minutes — 1.9 / 2.4 / 0.4 on 48.5% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, up from an unlisted IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2020-21 (Brooklyn) -- spent 2025-26 with Zenit St. Petersburg (VTB United League).

He was priced at $0.00 at IPO — below the replacement line at composite rank 50 — so there is no percentage to quote from zero. He is carried as FROM UNLISTED. He finished #20 in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Minnesota took him 26th on Jun. 27, 2013 and sent his rights that night, with Malcolm Lee, to Golden State for a 2014 second-round pick and cash. Golden State then sent Roberson's rights to Oklahoma City for the rights to Archie Goodwin (No. 29) plus cash, and moved Goodwin and Lee on to Phoenix for the rights to Nemanja Nedovic (No. 30). Roberson signed with the Thunder on Jul. 8, 2013.

He made the All-Defensive Second Team in 2017 and re-signed on a three-year, $30 million deal that July (signed Jul. 14, 2017). On Jan. 27, 2018 he ruptured his left patellar tendon against Detroit; he did not play in 2018-19 and appeared in 7 games in 2019-20. A free agent from November 2020, he had two short stints with Brooklyn in February and March 2021 (5 games) and was released Mar. 8, 2021.

After 14 G League games for the Oklahoma City Blue in 2022-23, he signed with Cholet in France in September 2024, also played for ASVEL that season, and signed with Zenit Saint Petersburg on Jul. 31, 2025.
