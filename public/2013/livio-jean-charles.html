SCOUTING REPORT

San Antonio took him #28 overall out of ASVEL in the French Pro A + Leaders Cup. He had just gone for 3.4 points, 2.8 rebounds and 0.5 assists in 30 games at 14.0 minutes a night. He went 11 picks ahead of his composite, which was #39. The boards ranged from #25 to #60 on him, a spread of 35 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: he did not appear in an NBA game. missed 2013-14 after surgery on his right knee, injured playing for France at the FIBA U20 European Championship in July 2013. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. has never played in the NBA -- cut by San Antonio in 2016 camp; spent 2025-26 with CSKA Moscow (VTB United League).

Against a frozen IPO of $9.48, today's mark of $27.93 is +194.6%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

San Antonio took him 28th and kept him in France. On Jul. 9, 2015 the league allowed the Spurs to remove his cap hold for 2015-16, which barred them from signing him until the following July. They signed him on Jul. 22, 2016 and waived him Oct. 22, 2016 after training camp; he played 45 D-League games for the Austin Spurs that season (10.0 points, 5.6 rebounds) before returning to ASVEL on Mar. 30, 2017.

He was back with Austin for 45 more games in 2017-18 until the G League terminated his contract on Mar. 21, 2018, then finished that season with Unicaja in Spain. He returned to ASVEL for 2018-19 and 2019-20, spent 2020-21 and 2021-22 with Olympiacos, and has played for CSKA Moscow since 2022-23. He played Summer League for San Antonio in 2015, 2016 and 2017.
