SCOUTING REPORT

Milwaukee took him #15 overall out of Filathlitikos in the Greek A2. He had no pre-draft season to price. He played 2012-13 for Filathlitikos in the Greek second division, a league RealGM does not carry, so there is no pre-draft line this board accepts. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #17, pick 15. The boards ranged from ninth to #60 on him, a spread of 51 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 77 games, 23 starts, 24.6 minutes — 6.8 / 4.4 / 1.9 on 41.4% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, +255.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: 36 games, 36 starts, 28.9 minutes — 27.6 / 9.8 / 5.4 on 62.4% shooting. That ranked him first in the class and marked him at $1,000.00, +1215.9% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $75.99, today's mark of $1,000.00 is +1215.9%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished seventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Milwaukee took him fifteenth out of Filathlitikos in Greece's second division and signed him on Jul. 31, 2013. He signed a rookie-scale extension on Sep. 20, 2016 and veteran extensions on Dec. 15, 2020 and Oct. 23, 2023. Across 13 seasons and 895 regular-season games, all with the Bucks, he was named MVP in 2019 and 2020, Defensive Player of the Year in 2020 and Most Improved Player in 2017, won the 2021 championship and Finals MVP, and made 10 All-Star teams and nine All-NBA teams, seven of them First Team.

The Bucks agreed on Jun. 22, 2026 to trade him and Bobby Portis to Miami for Tyler Herro, Kel'el Ware, Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kasparas Jakucionis, the rights to No. 13 pick Nate Ament, 2031 and 2033 first-round picks, a 2030 swap and a 2033 second; the deal became official on Jul. 6. His contract carries a 2027 player option.
