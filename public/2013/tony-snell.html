SCOUTING REPORT

Chicago took him #20 overall out of New Mexico. He had just gone for 12.5 points, 2.6 rebounds and 2.9 assists in 35 games at 31.2 minutes a night. He went 10 picks ahead of his composite, which was #30. The boards ranged from #22 to #60 on him, a spread of 38 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 77 games, 12 starts, 16.0 minutes — 4.5 / 1.6 / 0.9 on 38.4% shooting. That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, +259.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Portland/New Orleans) -- spent 2025-26 with Boulazac (French LNB Elite).

Against a frozen IPO of $23.39, today's mark of $43.38 is +85.5%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #16 in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Chicago took him 20th and signed him on Jul. 10, 2013. On Oct. 17, 2016 the Bulls traded him to Milwaukee straight up for fellow 2013 first-rounder Michael Carter-Williams. He started all 80 games he played that season, then re-signed with the Bucks on a reported four-year, $46 million deal on Jul. 31, 2017. Milwaukee sent him and the draft rights to Kevin Porter Jr. to Detroit for Jon Leuer on Jun. 20, 2019, and the Pistons traded him and Khyri Thomas to Atlanta for Dewayne Dedmon on Nov. 20, 2020; he shot .569 from three in 47 games for the Hawks.

He signed with Portland on Aug. 10, 2021, and on Feb. 8, 2022 was traded to New Orleans with fellow 2013 draftee CJ McCollum and Larry Nance Jr. for Nickeil Alexander-Walker, Josh Hart, Tomas Satoransky, Didi Louzada and draft picks. He became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2022. He played in the G League for the Maine Celtics (2022-23 and 2023-24) and was reported to have gone unsigned past the Feb. 2, 2024 cutoff for a 10th year of NBA service credit; Sioux Falls acquired his G League rights in October 2024. He signed with Boulazac on Aug. 23, 2025 and with Cholet on Jun. 19, 2026.
