SCOUTING REPORT

Philadelphia took him eleventh overall out of Syracuse. He had just gone for 11.9 points, 5.0 rebounds and 7.3 assists in 40 games at 35.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #9, pick 11. The boards ranged from eighth to #32 on him, a spread of 24 places across 16 of them.

2013-14: 70 games, 70 starts, 34.5 minutes — 16.7 / 6.2 / 6.3 on 40.5% shooting. That ranked him first in the class and marked him at $1,000.00, +408.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2022-23 (Orlando) -- his last recorded games were with the Mexico City Capitanes (G League) in late 2023; his retirement was reported in October 2024.

Against a frozen IPO of $196.79, today's mark of $68.95 is -65.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished first in the All-Rookie vote among 2013 draftees.

Philadelphia took him eleventh and traded him 20 months later. On Feb. 19, 2015, in a three-team deadline deal, he went to Milwaukee with Tyler Ennis and Miles Plumlee while Brandon Knight went to Phoenix and the 76ers received the Lakers' protected 2015 first-round pick. His 2015-16 season ended in March with surgery for a torn labrum in his left hip. On Oct. 17, 2016 the Bucks traded him to Chicago straight up for Tony Snell, a fellow 2013 first-rounder.

He signed with Charlotte on Jul. 7, 2017 and Houston on Jul. 6, 2018; the Rockets traded him to Chicago on Jan. 7, 2019, and the Bulls waived him the same day. Orlando signed him to 10-day contracts in March 2019 and kept him into 2021-22, waiving him on Feb. 10, 2022 while he recovered from left ankle surgery. He re-signed with the Magic on Feb. 26, 2023, played four games, and became a free agent that July.
