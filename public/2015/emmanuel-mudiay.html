SCOUTING REPORT

Denver took him seventh overall out of Guangdong Southern Tigers in the CBA China. He had just gone for 18.0 points, 6.3 rebounds and 5.9 assists in 12 games at 31.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #6, pick 7. The boards ranged from third to eighth on him, a spread of 5 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 68 games, 66 starts, 30.4 minutes — 12.8 / 3.4 / 5.5 on 36.4% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, -16.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Sacramento) -- signed with Al Ahly Tripoli (Libya) in January 2026, then played for Piratas de Quebradillas (Puerto Rico BSN) from March to June 2026.

Against a frozen IPO of $322.90, today's mark of $75.99 is -76.5%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished seventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Denver took him seventh after a season in China with Guangdong, exercised both team options, and moved him on Feb. 8, 2018 in a three-team trade: New York received Mudiay, Dallas received Doug McDermott, and Denver received Devin Harris and a second-round consideration. He started 42 games for the Knicks in 2018-19 at 14.8 points, but New York declined to tender a qualifying offer that June. He signed with Utah on Jul. 19, 2019 and played 54 games.

He did not play in the NBA in 2020-21. He appeared in 11 games for Lithuania's Zalgiris before leaving on Nov. 3, 2021, took a 10-day hardship contract with Sacramento on Dec. 22, 2021 (two games), and was signed and waived by Minnesota on Oct. 15, 2022 before 40 games for the Iowa Wolves at 16.7 points. Since June 2023 he has played in Puerto Rico's BSN, first for Cangrejeros de Santurce and, from February 2024, Piratas de Quebradillas. He was named 2025 BSN MVP after averaging 23.6 points and 5.9 assists, per Primera Hora.
