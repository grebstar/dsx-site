Miami took him #40 overall out of Tennessee. He had just gone for 16.0 points, 4.5 rebounds and 3.6 assists in 32 games at 36.3 minutes a night. He went 6 picks ahead of his composite, which was #46. The boards ranged from #39 to #60 on him, a spread of 21 places across 12 of them.

Against a frozen IPO of $2.89, today's mark of $6.38 is +120.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished eleventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Miami took him 40th and signed him Aug. 3, 2015, then extended him in September 2017. He started all 81 of his games in 2017-18 and all 73 in 2018-19 (16.6 points). On Jul. 6, 2019 the Heat sent him to Philadelphia in the sign-and-trade for Jimmy Butler. Philadelphia moved him to Dallas for Seth Curry on Nov. 19, 2020; Dallas sent him to Boston for Moses Brown on Jul. 31, 2021; Boston traded him to San Antonio with Romeo Langford and a first-round pick for Derrick White on Feb. 10, 2022; and the Spurs sent him to New Orleans for Devonte' Graham and four second-round picks on Feb. 9, 2023.

He returned to Miami as a free agent in July 2023 on a two-year deal. Surgery on a dislocated right shoulder in March 2024 ended his 2023-24 season, and he played 8 games in 2024-25 before Miami traded him to Utah with K.J. Martin on Feb. 6, 2025; the Jazz waived him three days later. His NBA career closed at 554 regular-season games for six franchises.
