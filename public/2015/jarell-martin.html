SCOUTING REPORT

Memphis took him #25 overall out of LSU. He had just gone for 16.9 points, 9.2 rebounds and 1.8 assists in 33 games at 35.1 minutes a night. He went 5 picks ahead of his composite, which was #30. The boards ranged from #25 to #60 on him, a spread of 35 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 27 games, 0 starts, 14.1 minutes — 5.7 / 3.2 / 0.6 on 46.6% shooting. That ranked him #26 in the class and marked him at $33.31, +42.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2018-19 (Orlando) -- spent 2025-26 with the Koshigaya Alphas (Japan B.League) after a 2024-25 season with the Adelaide 36ers (Australian NBL).

Against a frozen IPO of $23.39, today's mark of $25.57 is +9.4%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

Memphis took him 25th, signed him on Jul. 9, 2015 and picked up both rookie-scale options. On Jul. 23, 2018 the Grizzlies traded him and cash to Orlando for Dakari Johnson (the 48th pick in the same 2015 class) and the draft rights to Tyler Harvey (51st), a move reported as taking Memphis under the luxury tax. He played 42 games for the Magic, signed with Cleveland in August 2019 and was waived before the season.

He has played abroad since: Shenzhen in China (2019-20, then the Rio Grande Valley Vipers in the G League), the Sydney Kings from December 2020, where he won the NBL22 title with 29 points and 17 rebounds in the deciding game of the grand final, Maccabi Tel Aviv (2022-23), Galatasaray (2023-24), Adelaide and Suwon KT (2024-25), and Koshigaya in Japan (2025-26).
