SCOUTING REPORT

Charlotte took him ninth overall out of Wisconsin. He had just gone for 18.8 points, 8.2 rebounds and 2.6 assists in 39 games at 33.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #10, pick 9. The boards ranged from seventh to #15 on him, a spread of 8 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 81 games, 3 starts, 21.1 minutes — 7.5 / 4.1 / 1.2 on 41.0% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +0.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2022-23 (Atlanta/Houston) -- his last NBA contract was a Phoenix camp deal waived in October 2024; joined Alvark Tokyo (Japan B.League) in December 2025 and appeared in 3 games.

Against a frozen IPO of $170.70, today's mark of $62.59 is -63.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished tenth in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Charlotte took him ninth and kept him four seasons (282 games), exercising both team options. He signed with Phoenix on Jul. 17, 2019. After becoming a free agent in November 2020 he signed with Sacramento on Nov. 30, was waived on Dec. 19, and was claimed back by Phoenix two days later. He re-signed with the Suns in August 2021, was waived on Apr. 7, 2022, and signed with Atlanta on Jul. 11, 2022. On Feb. 9, 2023 the Hawks traded him to Houston, with Justin Holiday and two second-round picks, for Bruno Fernando and Garrison Mathews.

He signed a one-year deal with Partizan in Serbia in August 2023 and played 63 games across the EuroLeague, ABA League and domestic competitions at 9.1 points. Phoenix brought him to camp on Sep. 26, 2024 and waived him on Oct. 19. Raptors 905 claimed him off waivers in December 2024, and he was on Team USA's roster for AmeriCup qualifying games that November. Alvark Tokyo announced his signing on Dec. 5, 2025.
