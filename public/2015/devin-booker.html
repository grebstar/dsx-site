SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him #13 overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 10.0 points, 2.0 rebounds and 1.1 assists in 38 games at 21.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #12, pick 13. The boards ranged from sixth to #28 on him, a spread of 22 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 76 games, 51 starts, 27.7 minutes — 13.8 / 2.5 / 2.6 on 42.3% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +373.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 64 games, 64 starts, 33.5 minutes — 26.1 / 3.9 / 6.0 on 45.6% shooting. That ranked him first in the class and marked him at $1,000.00, +659.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $131.64, today's mark of $1,000.00 is +659.6%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished third in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Phoenix took him thirteenth on Jun. 25, 2015 and he has never played for another NBA team: 737 regular-season games, all for the Suns, through 2025-26. He signed a rookie scale extension on Jul. 7, 2018 and a veteran extension on Jul. 6, 2022. In between, the Suns reached the 2021 Finals and lost to Milwaukee.

On Jul. 9, 2025 he agreed to a two-year, $145 million extension that runs through 2029-30 (RealGM records the signing on Jul. 10, 2025). His honors to date: five All-Star selections (2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2026), All-NBA First Team in 2022, All-NBA Third Team in 2024, the 2018 Three-Point Contest title, and two Olympic gold medals.
