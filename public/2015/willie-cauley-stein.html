SCOUTING REPORT

Sacramento took him sixth overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 8.9 points, 6.4 rebounds and 1.0 assists in 39 games at 25.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #8, pick 6. The boards ranged from fifth to #13 on him, a spread of 8 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 66 games, 39 starts, 21.4 minutes — 7.0 / 5.3 / 0.6 on 56.3% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, -14.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Dallas/Philadelphia) -- played in Italy (Varese, 2023-24) and briefly in China (Nanjing, 2024-25); no professional club found for 2025-26.

Against a frozen IPO of $229.10, today's mark of $84.15 is -63.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished ninth in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Sacramento took him sixth and kept him four seasons, exercising both team options. The Kings tendered a qualifying offer in June 2019 and withdrew it on Jul. 1, 2019, making him an unrestricted free agent; he signed with Golden State on Jul. 8. On Jan. 25, 2020 the Warriors traded him to Dallas for a 2020 second-round pick. He sat out the 2020 restart in Orlando with a baby due in July, re-signed with Dallas on Dec. 1, 2020, and was waived on Jan. 15, 2022.

A 10-day contract with Philadelphia followed in February 2022 (two games). He then had a training-camp deal and two later contracts with Houston in 2022-23 without an NBA appearance, playing 29 games for the Rio Grande Valley Vipers in between. He signed with Varese in Italy on Jul. 31, 2023, grabbed 20 rebounds in an October 2023 FIBA Europe Cup game, a new single-game mark for that competition, and left the club in December. He joined Puerto Rico's Indios de Mayaguez in early 2024 and signed with China's Nanjing Monkey Kings in October 2024, leaving after two games in November.
