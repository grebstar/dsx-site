SCOUTING REPORT

Washington took him #19 overall out of Notre Dame. He had just gone for 16.5 points, 3.0 rebounds and 6.7 assists in 38 games at 37.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #18, pick 19. The boards ranged from eleventh to #21 on him, a spread of 10 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 76 games, 6 starts, 16.6 minutes — 5.6 / 1.9 / 2.3 on 39.4% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, -17.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2019-20 (Washington) -- spent 2025-26 with Panathinaikos (Greek league / EuroLeague), his third season there.

Against a frozen IPO of $68.95, today's mark of $36.38 is -47.2%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #20 in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Washington drafted him 19th on Jun. 25, 2015 and traded his rights that night, with 2016 and 2019 second-round picks, to Atlanta for the rights to No. 15 pick Kelly Oubre Jr.; the Hawks then sent Grant's rights to New York for Tim Hardaway Jr. On Jun. 22, 2016 the Knicks traded him to Chicago with Jose Calderon and Robin Lopez for Derrick Rose, Justin Holiday and a 2017 second. On Jul. 7, 2018 he went to Orlando in a three-team deal with Charlotte that also sent Timofey Mozgov to Orlando and Bismack Biyombo to Charlotte.

After the 2018-19 season he signed with Qingdao in China (August 2019), left in November, and was claimed by the G League's Capital City Go-Go (39 games, 16.3 points). Washington signed him as a substitute player for the 2020 Orlando restart, where he played six games. Houston signed him Nov. 30, 2020 and waived him Dec. 16. He then moved to Europe: Promitheas Patras (2020-21), Olimpia Milan (2021-22), Turk Telekom (2022-23, EuroCup MVP) and Panathinaikos since 2023, winning the EuroLeague title with the Greek club in May 2024.
