SCOUTING REPORT

Detroit took him eighth overall out of Arizona. He had just gone for 13.8 points, 6.5 rebounds and 1.7 assists in 38 games at 28.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #9, pick 8. The boards ranged from fifth to twelfth on him, a spread of 7 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 73 games, 6 starts, 23.1 minutes — 8.1 / 4.2 / 1.6 on 37.5% shooting. That ranked him twelfth in the class and marked him at $131.64, -33.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2022-23 (San Antonio) -- spent 2025-26 with Nagasaki Velca of the Japanese B.League.

Against a frozen IPO of $196.79, today's mark of $68.95 is -65.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished twelfth in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Detroit took him eighth and exercised both team options, then sent him to New Orleans on Feb. 7, 2019 in the deal built around Nikola Mirotic, with Jason Smith and four second-round picks also going to the Pelicans. He signed with Toronto on Jul. 10, 2019 and picked up his player option for 2020-21. After a Chicago camp deal ended in October 2021 he joined the South Bay Lakers, took a 10-day with the Bulls in December, then signed a run of 10-day deals with the Lakers from Dec. 24, 2021 that became a multi-year contract on Jan. 27, 2022.

On Aug. 25, 2022 the Lakers traded him to Utah with Talen Horton-Tucker for Patrick Beverley; Utah waived him in October. San Antonio signed him on Dec. 13, 2022 and waived him on Feb. 12, 2023. He played 34 G League games for Stockton in 2023-24, signed with Turkey's Anadolu Efes on Jul. 2, 2024, was granted leave for family reasons in late January 2025 and left the club in February, finishing the season with the South Bay Lakers. Nagasaki Velca announced his signing in July 2025.
