SCOUTING REPORT

Portland took him #23 overall out of Arizona. He had just gone for 11.2 points, 6.8 rebounds and 1.6 assists in 38 games at 28.7 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #23, pick 23. The boards ranged from #16 to #27 on him, a spread of 11 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 29 games, 17 starts, 21.2 minutes — 5.8 / 5.3 / 1.5 on 45.7% shooting. That ranked him #18 in the class and marked him at $68.95, +59.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2020-21 (Portland) -- under contract to TNT Tropang 5G in the Philippines, he played for Meralco in the East Asia Super League until he tore his right Achilles tendon in December 2025.

Against a frozen IPO of $43.38, today's mark of $27.93 is -35.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #18 in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Portland drafted him 23rd and traded his rights to Brooklyn on Jun. 26, 2015, with Steve Blake, for Mason Plumlee and the draft rights to Pat Connaughton, the 41st pick in the same class. The Nets picked up both of his rookie-scale options and then declined to tender him a qualifying offer in July 2019. He signed a one-year deal with Toronto on Jul. 17, 2019, was signed and waived by Minnesota in December 2020, and finished 2020-21 in Portland on two 10-day contracts and a rest-of-season deal signed Apr. 28, 2021.

He then went abroad: Besiktas in Turkey (September 2021), Atleticos de San German in Puerto Rico, Jeonju KCC in Korea (September 2022 to February 2023), then TNT in the Philippine Basketball Association as a replacement import in the 2023 Governors' Cup. By April 2025 he had led TNT to three PBA titles and won three Best Import awards, and signed a 12-month exclusive contract with the club.
