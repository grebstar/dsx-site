SCOUTING REPORT

Milwaukee took him #17 overall out of UNLV. He had just gone for 17.8 points, 4.8 rebounds and 1.6 assists in 23 games at 32.3 minutes a night. He went 8 picks ahead of his composite, which was #25. The boards ranged from #15 to #60 on him, a spread of 45 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 70 games, 6 starts, 14.3 minutes — 3.1 / 1.3 / 0.6 on 30.5% shooting. That ranked him #28 in the class and marked him at $27.93, -23.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2017-18 (Milwaukee/Brooklyn/Orlando) -- played for Lavrio (Greece) in 2024-25 and Kesatria Bengawan Solo (Indonesia IBL) in 2025-26.

Against a frozen IPO of $36.38, today's mark of $43.38 is +19.2%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

Milwaukee took him 17th on Jun. 25, 2015 and signed him Jul. 17. The Bucks picked up his third-year option in October 2016 but declined the fourth-year option the next fall (reported Nov. 1, 2017). On Feb. 5, 2018 they traded him and a conditional 2018 second-round pick to Brooklyn for Tyler Zeller; three days later Brooklyn sent him to New Orleans for Dante Cunningham, and the Pelicans waived him on Feb. 10. He signed two 10-day contracts with Orlando (Feb. 20 and Mar. 2, 2018), playing five games before Orlando ended the second one on Mar. 7. Dallas signed him on Oct. 8, 2018 and waived him three days later; he spent 2018-19 in the G League with the Texas Legends and Delaware Blue Coats (51 games, 15.5 points).

He has played abroad since 2019: Igokea (Bosnia, Liga ABA) in 2019-20; Buducnost and then Prometey in Ukraine in 2020-21, where he was Ukrainian Superleague Finals MVP; Dnipro and GS Lavrio in 2021-22; the G League's Cleveland Charge in 2022-23 (39 games); Apollon Patras in Greece in 2023-24; and Lavrio again in 2024-25.
