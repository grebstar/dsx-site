Brooklyn took him 41st, and on draft day, Jun. 25, 2015, the Nets sent his rights with center Mason Plumlee to Portland, per the Trail Blazers' announcement. He played three seasons there.

In 2018 he signed with Milwaukee as a free agent, coming off 82 games for Portland, and he won the 2021 title with the Bucks. In July 2025 he went to Charlotte, which acquired him along with two second-round picks. The Hornets waived him Feb. 4, 2026 and signed him again in February.
