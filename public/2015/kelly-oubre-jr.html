SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took him #15 overall out of Kansas. He had just gone for 9.3 points, 5.0 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 36 games at 21.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #15, pick 15. The boards ranged from twelfth to #23 on him, a spread of 11 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 63 games, 9 starts, 10.7 minutes — 3.7 / 2.1 / 0.2 on 42.7% shooting. That ranked him #29 in the class and marked him at $25.57, -72.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 50 games, 41 starts, 31.5 minutes — 14.1 / 5.0 / 1.6 on 46.7% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, +188.8% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $93.47, today's mark of $269.91 is +188.8%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Atlanta drafted him fifteenth on Jun. 25, 2015 and traded his rights that night to Washington for the rights to Jerian Grant, the nineteenth pick, plus Washington's 2016 and 2019 second-round picks. On Dec. 17, 2018 the Wizards sent him and Austin Rivers to Phoenix for Trevor Ariza; he re-signed with the Suns on Jul. 16, 2019.

He was part of the Nov. 16, 2020 trade that sent Chris Paul from Oklahoma City to Phoenix, landing in Oklahoma City, and six days later the Thunder moved him to Golden State for a conditional first and two seconds. He signed with Charlotte on Aug. 7, 2021, with Philadelphia on Sep. 26, 2023, re-signed there on Jul. 7, 2024 and picked up an $8.4 million player option for 2025-26. After that season he agreed to a two-year, $17 million deal with Indiana, his sixth NBA team, signed Jul. 7, 2026. Career through 2025-26: 705 regular-season games, 352 starts.
