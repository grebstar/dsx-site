Golden State took him #30 overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 11.6 points, 9.2 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 36 games at 30.9 minutes a night. The boards had him 10 places above where he went, at #20. The boards ranged from twelfth to #27 on him, a spread of 15 places across 12 of them.

Against a frozen IPO of $56.94, today's mark of $149.42 is +162.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Golden State drafted him 30th and signed him Jul. 7, 2015. After two hip surgeries in his first year, the Warriors exercised his third-year option in October 2016 but declined his fourth-year option on Oct. 31, 2017. He re-signed anyway — one year in July 2018, three years in July 2019 (exercising the final-year player option in June 2021), and three more in July 2022 — and was part of title teams in 2017, 2018 and 2022. He played 82 games in both 2021-22 and 2022-23, starting 150 of them.

After ten seasons and 599 regular-season games with the Warriors, he agreed to a two-year, $16 million deal with New Orleans on Jun. 30, 2025. The Pelicans declined the second-year option, he became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2026, and he signed with the Los Angeles Lakers on Jul. 12, 2026. He has not been traded in his career.
