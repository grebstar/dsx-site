SCOUTING REPORT

Indiana took him eleventh overall out of Texas. He had just gone for 10.1 points, 6.5 rebounds and 0.6 assists in 34 games at 22.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #11, pick 11. The boards ranged from tenth to #18 on him, a spread of 8 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 60 games, 30 starts, 22.8 minutes — 10.3 / 5.5 / 0.7 on 49.8% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, +53.3% against his IPO. 2025-26: 71 games, 71 starts, 26.9 minutes — 11.9 / 5.3 / 1.5 on 44.0% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, +165.3% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $149.42, today's mark of $396.43 is +165.3%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished eighth in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Indiana took him eleventh on Jun. 25, 2015 and kept him for ten seasons and 642 regular-season games. He signed a rookie scale extension on Oct. 15, 2018, and on Jan. 28, 2023 agreed to a two-year, $60 million renegotiation-and-extension that raised his 2022-23 salary from $18 million to $35 million and ran his deal through 2024-25. He was part of the Team Pacers group that won the 2024 Skills Challenge, and started for the 2024-25 Pacers team that reached the NBA Finals.

He became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2025 and that day agreed to a four-year, $107 million contract with Milwaukee, with a player option for 2028-29; the Bucks cleared the room by waiving and stretching Damian Lillard's contract. The signing was completed on Jul. 6, 2025. He has not been traded in his career.
