Orlando took him fifth, exercised his third-year option and declined his fourth on Oct. 31, 2017. He left as a free agent in July 2018 on a one-year, $6.5 million deal with New York, then signed a multi-year contract with Portland on Jul. 3, 2019. He then went to Europe: Panathinaikos (February 2021), UNICS Kazan (2021-22, VTB United League MVP) and Real Madrid from 2022, with an All-EuroLeague Second Team selection in 2024 and the Spanish ACB regular-season MVP award in 2026. He agreed to a one-year, $2.8 million deal with Cleveland in late July 2026 and signed on Jul. 31, after Real Madrid pushed back over the timing of his expired NBA opt-out clause.
