SCOUTING REPORT

Houston took him #18 overall out of Wisconsin. He had just gone for 13.9 points, 5.5 rebounds and 1.2 assists in 40 games at 31.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #17, pick 18. The boards ranged from #13 to #31 on him, a spread of 18 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 3 games, 0 starts, 2.0 minutes — 0.0 / 0.3 / 0.0, no field-goal attempt recorded. That ranked him #40 in the class and marked him at $8.31, -89.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Toronto) -- spent 2025-26 with Joventut Badalona (Liga ACB), his second season there.

Against a frozen IPO of $75.99, today's mark of $39.70 is -47.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Houston took him 18th on Jun. 25, 2015. On Jun. 28, 2017 he was part of the package the Rockets sent to the Clippers for Chris Paul, alongside fellow 2015 draftees Montrezl Harrell and Darrun Hilliard, Patrick Beverley, Lou Williams, DeAndre Liggins, Kyle Wiltjer, a conditional 2018 first-round pick and cash. The Clippers traded him to Cleveland on Aug. 7, 2018 with the draft rights to Renaldas Seibutis for the rights to Vladimir Veremeenko, and on Dec. 7, 2018 Cleveland sent him to Washington in a three-team trade with Milwaukee in which Jason Smith went to the Bucks. Washington renounced its rights to him in July 2019.

He then played abroad: Lokomotiv Kuban (2019-20), Turk Telekom (2020-21), a training-camp deal with Toronto that produced one game before the Raptors waived him on Nov. 6, 2021, Bahcesehir Koleji (2021-22) and the London Lions (2022-24), where he was the 2023 BBL Player of the Year, made the 2023 All-EuroCup Second Team and was the 2024 BBL Finals MVP. He joined Joventut Badalona in November 2024 and re-signed in 2025.
