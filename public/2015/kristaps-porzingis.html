New York took him fourth overall out of Baloncesto Sevilla in the Spanish ACB + EuroCup. He had just gone for 11.0 points, 4.6 rebounds and 0.5 assists in 50 games at 21.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 4. The boards ranged from third to eleventh on him, a spread of 8 places across 12 of them.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $495.29 is +0.0%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished second in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

New York took him fourth and traded him on Jan. 31, 2019, during a 2018-19 season in which he did not play, to Dallas with Tim Hardaway Jr., Courtney Lee and Trey Burke for DeAndre Jordan, Wesley Matthews, Dennis Smith Jr. and two first-round picks. Dallas re-signed him in July 2019 and traded him to Washington on Feb. 10, 2022 for Spencer Dinwiddie and Davis Bertans.

On Jun. 23, 2023 he went to Boston in a three-team trade: Washington received Danilo Gallinari and Mike Muscala from Boston and 2015 classmate Tyus Jones from Memphis, while Marcus Smart went from Boston to Memphis. He won the 2024 championship with the Celtics. Boston sent him to Atlanta in a three-team deal with Brooklyn, agreed in June 2025 and completed Jul. 7, 2025, and Atlanta traded him to Golden State on Feb. 5, 2026. He agreed to a two-year, $40 million deal with the Warriors in June 2026, then was ruled out indefinitely at the start of training camp in September with an undisclosed health issue.
