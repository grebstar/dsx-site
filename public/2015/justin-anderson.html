SCOUTING REPORT

Dallas took him #21 overall out of Virginia. He had just gone for 12.2 points, 4.0 rebounds and 1.7 assists in 26 games at 27.8 minutes a night. He went 5 picks ahead of his composite, which was #26. The boards ranged from #18 to #60 on him, a spread of 42 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 55 games, 9 starts, 11.8 minutes — 3.8 / 2.4 / 0.5 on 40.6% shooting. That ranked him #19 in the class and marked him at $62.59, +87.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Cleveland/Indiana) -- spent 2025-26 with Dubai Basketball (Adriatic ABA League / EuroLeague).

Against a frozen IPO of $33.31, today's mark of $30.50 is -8.4%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished #19 in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Dallas took him 21st and signed him on Jul. 31, 2015. On Feb. 23, 2017 he went to Philadelphia with Andrew Bogut and a protected 2017 first-round pick for Nerlens Noel. On Jul. 25, 2018 he was part of a three-team trade that sent him from Philadelphia to Atlanta, where he joined Carmelo Anthony (from Oklahoma City); Dennis Schroder went to the Thunder and Mike Muscala to the 76ers. He became a free agent in July 2019.

After that came short stints: a camp deal with Washington (waived Oct. 16, 2019), a 10-day contract with Brooklyn in January 2020 and a substitute contract with the Nets that July, a camp deal with Philadelphia in November 2020, and 10-day and hardship deals with Cleveland and Indiana in 2021-22, between G League seasons with Raptors 905, Long Island and Fort Wayne (All-NBA G League First Team in 2022). He went to Spain in October 2023 — Rio Breogan, then Valencia, then Barcelona for 2024-25 — and signed with Dubai on Jul. 17, 2025.
