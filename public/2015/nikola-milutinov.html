SCOUTING REPORT

San Antonio took him #26 overall out of Partizan in the Adriatic ABA + Serbian KLS + EuroCup. He had just gone for 9.8 points, 7.6 rebounds and 1.5 assists in 57 games at 26.0 minutes a night. He went 16 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #42, drafted 26. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #26 to #60 on him, a spread of 34 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: he did not appear in an NBA game. stayed at Olympiacos (Greek league / EuroLeague) for 2015-16 as a draft-and-stash. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. has never played in the NBA -- spent 2025-26 with Olympiacos (Greek league / EuroLeague).

Against a frozen IPO of $6.38, today's mark of $23.39 is +266.5%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

San Antonio drafted him 26th on Jun. 25, 2015 and never brought him over. He signed with Olympiacos that July, and in October 2017 extended with the club through 2020 on a deal that, per Eurohoops, included NBA buyout windows in the summers of 2018 and 2019; neither was used. On Aug. 6, 2021 the Spurs sent his draft rights to Brooklyn, with a 2024 second-round pick, a 2025 second-round swap and a trade exception, in the sign-and-trade that moved Spencer Dinwiddie out of Brooklyn. RealGM still lists the Nets as holding his rights.

In Europe he moved to CSKA Moscow in 2020 and played three seasons there (VTB United League MVP in 2023), returned to Olympiacos in June 2023, and re-signed with the Greek club in June 2025. He opened 2026-27 with Olympiacos and appeared in one EuroLeague game before missing the club's Sep. 29 trip to Kaunas.
