SCOUTING REPORT

Brooklyn took him #29 overall out of Syracuse. He had just gone for 9.3 points, 6.9 rebounds and 1.1 assists in 16 games at 28.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #28, pick 29. The boards ranged from #19 to #60 on him, a spread of 41 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 24 games, 4 starts, 15.1 minutes — 4.7 / 2.8 / 0.4 on 40.4% shooting. That ranked him #25 in the class and marked him at $36.38, +30.3% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2017-18 (Washington) -- spent 2025-26 with the Taoyuan Taiwan Beer Leopards (Taiwan TPBL), then TNT (Philippine PBA) and Vaqueros de Bayamon (Puerto Rico BSN).

Against a frozen IPO of $27.93, today's mark of $19.52 is -30.1%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Brooklyn drafted him 29th and signed him Jul. 1, 2015. On Feb. 22, 2017 the Nets traded him with Bojan Bogdanovic to Washington for Andrew Nicholson, veteran guard Marcus Thornton (the 2009 draftee from LSU, not the 2015 pick of that name) and a 2017 first-round pick. Washington declined his 2018-19 option on Oct. 31, 2017, and his NBA career ended at 59 games. Detroit signed and waived him in the 2018 preseason.

Since then he has played across Asia and the Americas: the San Miguel Beermen (PBA), Brujos de Guayama (BSN) and Shanxi (CBA) in 2018-19, Anyang KGC in Korea, a short fall 2020 stint with Lietuvos Rytas in Lithuania, and Gigantes de Carolina in Puerto Rico. In 2025-26 he played in Taiwan, then for TNT in the PBA Commissioner's Cup, and in July 2026 Bayamon signed him to replace Jae Crowder for the BSN playoffs.
