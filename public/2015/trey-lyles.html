SCOUTING REPORT

Utah took him twelfth overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 8.7 points, 5.2 rebounds and 1.1 assists in 36 games at 23.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #14, pick 12. The boards ranged from eleventh to #22 on him, a spread of 11 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 80 games, 33 starts, 17.3 minutes — 6.1 / 3.7 / 0.7 on 43.8% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, +12.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2024-25 (Sacramento) -- signed a one-year deal with Real Madrid (Liga ACB / EuroLeague) and played the 2025-26 season there.

Against a frozen IPO of $104.27, today's mark of $51.92 is -50.2%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #13 in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Utah took him twelfth on Jun. 25, 2015 and moved him two years later: on Jun. 22, 2017 he went to Denver with the draft rights to Tyler Lydon for the draft rights to Donovan Mitchell. Denver tendered and then withdrew a qualifying offer in July 2019, and he signed with San Antonio on Jul. 12, 2019. He moved on to Detroit as a free agent on Aug. 6, 2021.

On Feb. 10, 2022 he was traded to Sacramento with Donte DiVincenzo and Josh Jackson in the four-team deal that sent Marvin Bagley III to Detroit. He re-signed with the Kings on Jul. 7, 2023 and played three more seasons there, ending with 69 games in 2024-25 — 650 NBA regular-season games in ten seasons across five franchises.

He became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2025 and signed with Real Madrid on Sep. 5, 2025, playing 74 games across the EuroLeague, Liga ACB and Spanish domestic cups at 12.5 / 4.7 on .496 shooting and .428 from three. He agreed to a one-year deal with Minnesota in July 2026; RealGM records the signing on Jul. 10, 2026.
