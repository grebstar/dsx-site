SCOUTING REPORT

Philadelphia took him third overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 17.3 points, 8.5 rebounds and 1.3 assists in 38 games at 30.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #2, pick 3. The boards ranged from first to third on him, a spread of 2 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 53 games, 48 starts, 30.0 minutes — 17.5 / 7.0 / 1.2 on 50.8% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, -38.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2024-25 (Indiana) -- signed with Levanga Hokkaido of the Japanese B.League in July 2025 and played the 2025-26 season there.

Against a frozen IPO of $808.24, today's mark of $104.27 is -87.1%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished fourth in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Philadelphia took him third and declined his fourth-year option on Oct. 31, 2017. On Dec. 7, 2017 he went to Brooklyn with Nik Stauskas and a 2019 second-round pick for Trevor Booker, joining 2015 No. 2 pick D'Angelo Russell on the Nets. He signed with New Orleans in August 2018 (two seasons) and Detroit in December 2020. Detroit traded him to Brooklyn on Sep. 4, 2021 in the DeAndre Jordan deal; the Nets waived him five days later, and Atlanta waived him after camp that October.

Since then: Zhejiang Guangsha in China (signed Jan. 2022), 15 G League games for the Mexico City Capitanes in 2022-23 at 20.5 a night, a 2023-24 season split among Casademont Zaragoza, Zhejiang and Capitanes de Arecibo, and 41 games for Indiana's G League affiliate in 2024-25 at 18.2 points and 9.4 rebounds. Indiana signed him to a 10-day contract on Feb. 11, 2025; he played one game. He signed with Levanga Hokkaido in July 2025.
