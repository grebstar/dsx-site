SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him #14 overall out of Murray State. He had just gone for 20.2 points, 3.7 rebounds and 6.0 assists in 35 games at 32.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #13, pick 14. The boards ranged from ninth to #21 on him, a spread of 12 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 57 games, 1 starts, 12.2 minutes — 5.0 / 1.5 / 1.9 on 41.0% shooting. That ranked him #21 in the class and marked him at $51.92, -55.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 22 games, 1 starts, 17.0 minutes — 7.4 / 2.0 / 2.6 on 37.6% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, +68.3% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $116.93, today's mark of $196.79 is +68.3%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #21 in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Oklahoma City took him fourteenth on Jun. 25, 2015 and traded him on Feb. 23, 2017 to Chicago with Joffrey Lauvergne and Anthony Morrow for Taj Gibson, Doug McDermott and a 2018 second. The Bulls waived him on Jan. 3, 2019; he played on two 10-day contracts with Cleveland that month, was waived by Toronto before the 2019-20 season, played two games for Shanxi in China, and joined the G League's Texas Legends on a waiver claim in January 2020 (15 games, 23.2 points).

Phoenix signed him on Jun. 30, 2020 for the season restart; he played 60 games for the Suns team that reached the 2021 Finals and re-signed on Aug. 6, 2021. Phoenix traded him to San Antonio on Jul. 17, 2023, the Spurs waived him in September, and he signed with Milwaukee on Oct. 1, 2023. On Feb. 8, 2024 the Bucks traded him to Philadelphia for Patrick Beverley. He signed with the Knicks on Jul. 15, 2024 and played 72 games.

2025-26 ran through an Indiana camp deal (waived Oct. 18, 2025), Partizan and a return to Philadelphia. RealGM reported on Aug. 20, 2026 that Sacramento would host him for a workout.
