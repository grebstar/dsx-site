Miami took him tenth overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 12.6 points, 6.5 rebounds and 2.1 assists in 39 games at 29.1 minutes a night. The boards had him 5 places above where he went, at #5. The boards ranged from second to tenth on him, a spread of 8 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 78 games, 8 starts, 28.6 minutes — 6.4 / 5.2 / 1.5 on 42.2% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, +0.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2022-23 (Portland) -- played in the G League for Raptors 905 (2023-24) and the Wisconsin Herd (2024-25) after a Milwaukee camp deal ended in October 2024; no professional club found for 2025-26.

Against a frozen IPO of $396.43, today's mark of $56.94 is -85.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished fifth in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Miami took him tenth, exercised both team options and in October 2018 signed him to a three-year, $39 million extension with a team option in the final year. On Feb. 6, 2020 the Heat traded him to Memphis with Gorgui Dieng and Dion Waiters for Jae Crowder, Solomon Hill and Andre Iguodala. A hip injury in a July 2020 intra-squad scrimmage ended his 2019-20 season before he played for Memphis; he appeared in 26 games the next season, and the Grizzlies declined his $13 million option in August 2021.

He signed with the Clippers on Aug. 7, 2021. On Feb. 4, 2022 the Clippers traded him to Portland with Eric Bledsoe, Keon Johnson and a 2025 second-round pick for Robert Covington and Norman Powell, the 46th pick of the same 2015 draft. Left ankle surgery on Mar. 29, 2023 ended his second Portland season after 29 games. Toronto signed and waived him in October 2023 and gave him a 10-day contract in February 2024 without an appearance; Milwaukee signed and waived him in October 2024.
