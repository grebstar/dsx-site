Boston took him #28 overall out of Georgia State. He had just gone for 19.7 points, 4.7 rebounds and 3.6 assists in 35 games at 37.0 minutes a night. The boards had him 7 places above where he went, at #21. The boards ranged from #16 to #25 on him, a spread of 9 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 36 games, 0 starts, 8.8 minutes — 2.7 / 1.0 / 0.4 on 36.7% shooting. That ranked him #33 in the class and marked him at $17.77, -65.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2018-19 (Boston) -- stopped playing after a 2023-24 G League season with the Greensboro Swarm and was an assistant coach at Tulane in 2025-26.

Against a frozen IPO of $51.92, today's mark of $21.38 is -58.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Boston drafted him 28th and signed him on Jul. 27, 2015, then waived him at the end of the next preseason. After Chicago let him go in December 2016 he spent most of three seasons in the G League, around two-way stints with Houston (Jan. 14 to Aug. 17, 2018; 5 games) and back with Boston (Jan. 10, 2019; 1 game, 17 points in 26 minutes). Atlanta signed and waived him in the 2018 preseason.

He played in Turkey for Turk Telekom (2019-20) and Galatasaray (2020-21) and appeared once for the Sydney Kings in 2021-22. Charlotte signed him on Sep. 29, 2023 and waived him Oct. 21; he spent that season with the Greensboro Swarm. His NBA total is 45 regular-season games over four seasons with Boston, Chicago and Houston.
