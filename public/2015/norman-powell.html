Milwaukee took him #46 overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 16.4 points, 4.7 rebounds and 2.1 assists in 36 games at 34.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #45, pick 46. The boards ranged from #39 to #60 on him, a spread of 21 places across 12 of them.

2015-16: 49 games, 24 starts, 14.8 minutes — 5.6 / 2.3 / 1.0 on 42.4% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +16489.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $3.76, today's mark of $623.16 is +16489.0%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #17 in the All-Rookie vote among 2015 draftees.

Milwaukee took him 46th, and he was dealt the same night. On Jun. 25, 2015 the Raptors announced they had acquired a future first-round pick and the rights to Powell from the Bucks, the same release in which Toronto took Delon Wright (Utah) at No. 20.

He spent six seasons in Toronto and won the 2019 championship. On Mar. 25, 2021 he was traded to Portland for Gary Trent Jr. On Feb. 4, 2022 Portland sent him and Robert Covington to the Clippers for Eric Bledsoe, Justise Winslow, Keon Johnson and a 2025 second-round pick. He later moved to Miami and was an All-Star reserve for the Heat in 2026; Basketball-Reference lists him with the Chicago Bulls.
