Against a frozen IPO of $13.22, today's mark of $16.15 is +22.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

The Clippers took him 28th and signed him on Jul. 11, 2014. They exercised his third-year option on Oct. 27, 2015, then traded him on Jul. 15, 2016 to Orlando, with cash, for fellow 2014 draftee Devyn Marble (taken 56th) and a 2020 second-round pick; the Clippers waived Marble the same day. Orlando waived Wilcox on Apr. 3, 2017 after 22 games.

He then signed two-way contracts with Portland (Aug. 9, 2017) and Indiana (Aug. 3, 2018). Right knee arthroscopy in October 2017 was expected to cost him six to eight weeks, and a torn right Achilles tendon suffered in an offseason workout, reported Sep. 21, 2018, ruled him out of 2018-19; Indiana ended the two-way deal on Oct. 19, 2018. He signed with the Pacers again on Jul. 1, 2019, was waived on Oct. 16, 2019, and played 42 G League games for Fort Wayne that season at 12.7 points. No later professional appearance appears in his RealGM record.
