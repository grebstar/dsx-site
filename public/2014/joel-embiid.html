Philadelphia took him third overall out of Kansas. He had just gone for 11.2 points, 8.1 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 28 games at 23.1 minutes a night. Philadelphia took him third on Jun. 26, 2014 and he has played his whole career there. After two lost seasons he debuted in 2016-17, signed a rookie-scale extension on Oct. 9, 2017, and added veteran extensions on Aug. 17, 2021 and Sep. 19, 2024; his current deal carries a player option for 2028-29.

He was the 2023 MVP and an All-NBA First Team pick that year, made the All-NBA Second Team in 2018, 2019, 2021 and 2022, and was an All-Star seven straight years (2018-2024). Availability has been the recurring issue: 39 games in 2023-24, 19 in 2024-25 and 38 in 2025-26, 96 combined.

In the 2026 offseason Philadelphia traded Paul George and four draft picks to Boston for Jaylen Brown and signed LeBron James. In August 2026 team president Mike Gansey said Embiid was healthy and working out through a full summer.
