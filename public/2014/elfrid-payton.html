SCOUTING REPORT

Philadelphia took him tenth overall out of Louisiana. He had just gone for 19.2 points, 6.0 rebounds and 5.9 assists in 35 games at 35.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #11, pick 10. The boards ranged from eighth to #20 on him, a spread of 12 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 82 games, 63 starts, 30.4 minutes — 8.9 / 4.3 / 6.5 on 42.5% shooting. That ranked him second in the class and marked him at $808.24, +440.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2024-25 (New Orleans/Charlotte) -- New Orleans declined his 2025-26 option; he played 29 regular-season games for the Austin Spurs (G League) and signed with Sacramento on Sept. 16, 2026 for 2026-27.

Against a frozen IPO of $149.42, today's mark of $43.38 is -71.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished second in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Philadelphia took him tenth on Jun. 26, 2014 and traded his rights that night to Orlando for the rights to Dario Saric, the 12th pick, plus a 2015 second-round pick and a conditional 2017 first. He started 234 games over three and a half seasons for the Magic before a Feb. 8, 2018 trade to Phoenix for a 2018 second-round pick.

He then moved through New Orleans (July 2018), New York (July 2019; waived and re-signed in November 2020) and Phoenix again (August 2021). After 2021-22 he went to the G League and Puerto Rico's BSN; with the Indiana Mad Ants in 2023-24 he averaged 11.9 points and 9.0 assists and made the All-NBA G League Third Team.

In 2024-25 he returned to the NBA on short-term deals with New Orleans and Charlotte, playing 24 games, and signed a multi-year deal with the Pelicans on Apr. 11, 2025; they declined his option in June. He spent 2025-26 in the G League, and Sacramento signed him to an Exhibit 10 contract on Sep. 16, 2026.
