SCOUTING REPORT

Chicago took him #16 overall out of Cedevita in the Adriatic ABA + EuroCup + Croatian A-1. He had just gone for 10.9 points, 5.0 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 58 games at 16.3 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #14, pick 16. The boards ranged from eleventh to #60 on him, a spread of 49 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 62 games, 27 starts, 17.8 minutes — 6.9 / 6.2 / 0.8 on 44.6% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +209.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 41 games, 36 starts, 26.4 minutes — 10.9 / 10.4 / 4.8 on 50.3% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +209.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $104.27, today's mark of $322.90 is +209.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished sixth in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Chicago picked him 16th and traded him on draft night, Jun. 26, 2014: the Bulls sent his rights and the rights to No. 19 Gary Harris, plus a 2015 second-round pick, to Denver for the rights to No. 11 Doug McDermott and Anthony Randolph. On Feb. 13, 2017 Denver traded him to Portland with a first-round pick and a 2018 second-round swap for Mason Plumlee and cash. He re-signed with the Blazers as a restricted free agent in July 2018 and again in July 2022 (four years, $70 million).

On Sep. 27, 2023 he went to Phoenix with Grayson Allen, Keon Johnson and Nassir Little for Deandre Ayton and Toumani Camara. On Feb. 6, 2025 the Suns sent him and a 2026 first-round pick to Charlotte for Cody Martin and Vasilije Micic, the 52nd pick of his own 2014 class. Charlotte moved him to Utah on Jun. 29, 2025 for Collin Sexton and a 2030 second-round pick. He became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2026 and re-signed with the Jazz on Jul. 9, 2026.
