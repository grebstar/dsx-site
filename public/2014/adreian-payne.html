SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took him #15 overall out of Michigan State. He had just gone for 16.4 points, 7.3 rebounds and 1.3 assists in 31 games at 28.1 minutes a night. He went 6 picks ahead of his composite, which was #21. The boards ranged from #13 to #60 on him, a spread of 47 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 32 games, 22 starts, 23.1 minutes — 6.7 / 5.1 / 0.9 on 41.4% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, +9.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2017-18 (Orlando) -- died on May 9, 2022, at 31; he last played for Juventus Utena (Lithuania LKL) in 2021-22.

Against a frozen IPO of $51.92, today's mark of $36.38 is -29.9%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Atlanta took him fifteenth on Jun. 26, 2014 and traded him on Feb. 10, 2015 to Minnesota for a conditional first-round pick. The Wolves exercised his third-year option in October 2015. In February 2017 he was ruled out indefinitely with thrombocytopenia, a low platelet count; he played 18 games that season. He signed a two-way contract with Orlando on Aug. 21, 2017, played five games for the Magic and 17 for Lakeland, and the contract was ended on Jan. 27, 2018. He finished with 107 NBA games.

He then played overseas: Panathinaikos (signed February 2018), Nanjing Tongxi in China, Panathinaikos again in January 2019, ASVEL in France (March 2019 through 2019-20), Ormanspor Genc Ankara in Turkey (2021) and Juventus Utena in Lithuania (2021-22).

He died on May 9, 2022, at 31. FOX 35 Orlando reported that he was shot early that morning on Egret Shores Drive in Orlando and that a 29-year-old man was arrested at the scene on a first-degree murder charge.
