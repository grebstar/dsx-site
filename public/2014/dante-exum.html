SCOUTING REPORT

Utah took him fifth overall out of Australian Institute of Sport in the AIS. He had no pre-draft season to price. He spent 2013-14 in the Australian Institute of Sport programme, which is not a tracked league, so there is no pre-draft line to measure. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 5. The boards ranged from third to sixth on him, a spread of 3 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 82 games, 41 starts, 22.2 minutes — 4.8 / 1.6 / 2.4 on 34.9% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, -53.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26 -- re-signed with Dallas on Aug. 30, 2025, but right-knee soreness from training camp ended in season-ending knee surgery (reported Nov. 20, 2025); traded to Washington on Feb. 5, 2026 and waived three days later.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $131.64 is -73.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished eighth in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Utah took him fifth and kept him five and a half seasons, re-signing him as a restricted free agent on Jul. 6, 2018. On Dec. 23, 2019 the Jazz traded him to Cleveland, with 2022 and 2023 second-round picks, for fellow 2014 draftee Jordan Clarkson. On Jan. 16, 2021 the Cavaliers sent him to Houston in the James Harden trade; the Rockets re-signed him in September 2021 and waived him on Oct. 16, 2021.

He then played two seasons in Europe: FC Barcelona from December 2021 (49 games) and Partizan in 2022-23 (74 games at 12.2 points). Dallas signed him on Jul. 14, 2023, and he shot .491 from three in 55 games in 2023-24. In 2024-25 right-wrist surgery in October and a broken left hand in March limited him to 20 games.

He re-signed with Dallas on a one-year minimum deal on Aug. 30, 2025 but did not play. On Feb. 5, 2026 he was traded to Washington with Anthony Davis, Jaden Hardy and D'Angelo Russell for Khris Middleton, Marvin Bagley III, Malaki Branham, A.J. Johnson and draft picks. The Wizards waived him on Feb. 8, 2026, and he has been a free agent since.
