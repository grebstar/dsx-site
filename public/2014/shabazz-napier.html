SCOUTING REPORT

Charlotte took him #24 overall out of UConn. He had just gone for 18.0 points, 5.9 rebounds and 4.9 assists in 40 games at 35.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #20, pick 24. The boards ranged from #19 to #60 on him, a spread of 41 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 51 games, 10 starts, 19.8 minutes — 5.1 / 2.2 / 2.5 on 38.2% shooting. That ranked him #15 in the class and marked him at $93.47, +64.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2019-20 (Minnesota/Washington) -- played for Bayern Munich (German BBL / EuroLeague) through June 2025; no 2025-26 games recorded.

Against a frozen IPO of $56.94, today's mark of $19.52 is -65.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #15 in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Charlotte drafted him 24th, and on Jun. 27, 2014 the Hornets sent his rights to Miami for the rights to fellow 2014 picks P.J. Hairston (26th) and Semaj Christon (55th), a 2019 second and cash. Miami traded him to Orlando on Jul. 27, 2015, Orlando sent him to Portland for cash on Jul. 7, 2016, and he signed with Brooklyn on Jul. 17, 2018.

In July 2019 he went to Golden State in the Kevin Durant sign-and-trade and on to Minnesota a day later. On Feb. 5, 2020 a four-team trade sent him and Noah Vonleh (2014's ninth pick) to Denver; the same deal moved Clint Capela (2014's 25th pick) from Houston to Atlanta. A day later Denver sent him to Washington for 2014 draftee Jordan McRae. He became a free agent in November 2020 after 345 NBA games.

He then played for Zenit Saint Petersburg, the G League's Mexico City Capitanes, Olimpia Milano, Crvena Zvezda, Milan again and, from September 2024, Bayern Munich, where he was 2025 BBL Finals MVP. He left Bayern on Aug. 17, 2025.
