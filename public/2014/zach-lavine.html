SCOUTING REPORT

Minnesota took him #13 overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 9.4 points, 2.5 rebounds and 1.8 assists in 37 games at 24.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #12, pick 13. The boards ranged from tenth to #28 on him, a spread of 18 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 77 games, 40 starts, 24.7 minutes — 10.1 / 2.8 / 3.6 on 42.2% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, +201.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 39 games, 37 starts, 31.4 minutes — 19.2 / 2.8 / 2.3 on 47.9% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, +105.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $131.64, today's mark of $269.91 is +105.0%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished fifth in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Minnesota took him thirteenth on Jun. 26, 2014. He tore the ACL in his left knee in a game against Detroit on Feb. 3, 2017, ending a season in which he was scoring 18.9 a night. On Jun. 22, 2017 the Wolves traded him to Chicago with Kris Dunn and the rights to Lauri Markkanen for Jimmy Butler and the rights to Justin Patton. As a restricted free agent he signed an offer sheet with Sacramento on Jul. 6, 2018; the Bulls matched it two days later. He made the All-Star team in 2021 and 2022 and re-signed with Chicago on Jul. 7, 2022.

On Feb. 3, 2025 he was traded to Sacramento, with Sidy Cissoko and a package of first- and second-round picks, in a three-team deal with San Antonio in which De'Aaron Fox went to the Spurs and Kevin Huerter to Chicago. He exercised his $48.9 million player option for 2026-27 in June 2026; in August the Kings were reported to have no interest in a buyout.
