SCOUTING REPORT

Boston took him #17 overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 14.3 points, 4.3 rebounds and 1.7 assists in 40 games at 32.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #15, pick 17. The boards ranged from eleventh to #60 on him, a spread of 49 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 31 games, 0 starts, 10.7 minutes — 3.4 / 1.4 / 0.4 on 35.3% shooting. That ranked him #18 in the class and marked him at $68.95, -26.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2017-18 (Philadelphia) -- his last recorded games were with Varese (Italy) in January 2024.

Against a frozen IPO of $93.47, today's mark of $33.31 is -64.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #18 in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Boston drafted him 17th and kept him three seasons, exercising his third-year option in October 2015 and sending him to the Maine Red Claws on repeated assignments. He became a free agent in July 2017. He signed a camp deal with Milwaukee on Sep. 6, 2017, was waived Oct. 5, and joined the Bucks' G League team, the Wisconsin Herd. On Jan. 5, 2018 Philadelphia signed him to a two-way contract; he played six games for the 76ers before it ended on Mar. 26, 2018.

After another Milwaukee camp stint in September 2018 and a second Herd season, he went overseas: Maccabi Haifa in Israel (20.5 points a game in 2019-20), then, after a December 2020 camp deal with the Knicks that sent him to Westchester, Hapoel Tel Aviv from March 2021 until January 2023. He moved to Greece's Kolossos Rodou in January 2023, then to Italy with Treviso (July to December 2023) and Varese, which he left on Feb. 1, 2024.
