SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him #18 overall out of Syracuse. He had just gone for 12.9 points, 3.4 rebounds and 5.5 assists in 34 games at 35.7 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #16, pick 18. The boards ranged from ninth to #60 on him, a spread of 51 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 33 games, 1 starts, 12.5 minutes — 3.7 / 1.1 / 2.2 on 36.1% shooting. That ranked him #24 in the class and marked him at $39.70, -52.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2017-18 (LA Lakers) -- played 21 games for Hapoel Tel Aviv (Israeli BSL / EuroLeague) from September 2025 to January 2026.

Against a frozen IPO of $84.15, today's mark of $30.50 is -63.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Phoenix drafted him 18th and traded him eight months later: on Feb. 19, 2015 he went to Milwaukee with Michael Carter-Williams and Miles Plumlee in a three-team deal with Philadelphia that sent Brandon Knight and Kendall Marshall to the Suns. The Bucks traded him to Houston for Michael Beasley on Sep. 22, 2016, and the Rockets sent him to the Lakers with the rights to Brad Newley on Feb. 23, 2017 for Marcelinho Huertas. He re-signed with the Lakers in July 2017; they released him on Jun. 28, 2018.

He played 2018-19 with Fenerbahce, signed and was waived by Toronto in October 2019 and spent that season with Raptors 905 (17.2 points, 7.5 assists in 42 games). From 2020 he played in Turkey (Turk Telekom, Tofas), Italy (Napoli, then Umana Venezia in 2024-25, 14.2 points on .436 from three) and Israel with Hapoel Tel Aviv, which he rejoined on Jul. 10, 2025.
