SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him #29 overall out of Stanford. He had just gone for 11.2 points, 8.2 rebounds and 1.2 assists in 36 games at 35.2 minutes a night. He went 19 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #48, drafted 29. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #47 to #60 on him, a spread of 13 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: he did not appear in an NBA game. spent 2014-15 with the Oklahoma City Blue (D-League) instead of signing his rookie contract, then signed with Oklahoma City in July 2015. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2017-18 (Oklahoma City) -- retired in October 2022 after a 2021-22 G League season.

Against a frozen IPO of $1.47, today's mark of $14.64 is +898.2%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Oklahoma City took him 29th and he agreed not to sign his rookie-scale contract for a year. RealGM, citing SB Nation, reported on Jul. 24, 2014 that his agent had proposed the arrangement before the draft: he would spend 2014-15 with the Thunder's D-League affiliate, the Oklahoma City Blue, for roughly $25,000, and his four-year rookie deal would start later. He signed it on Jul. 30, 2015. The Thunder exercised his third-year option on Oct. 29, 2016 and declined the fourth-year option in October 2017.

After 76 NBA games, all for Oklahoma City, he signed an Exhibit 10 deal with San Antonio on Oct. 9, 2018 and was waived two days later. He played for the Austin Spurs in 2018-19, signed with Bayern Munich on Jul. 24, 2019 and left in March 2020, then played G League seasons with Rio Grande Valley and Cleveland before retiring on Oct. 24, 2022.
