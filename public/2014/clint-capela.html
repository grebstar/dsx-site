SCOUTING REPORT

Houston took him #25 overall out of Chalon in the French Pro A + EuroCup. He had just gone for 9.8 points, 6.8 rebounds and 1.0 assists in 43 games at 21.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #24, pick 25. The boards ranged from #18 to #60 on him, a spread of 42 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 12 games, 0 starts, 7.5 minutes — 2.7 / 3.0 / 0.2 on 48.3% shooting. That ranked him #31 in the class and marked him at $21.38, -46.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 75 games, 3 starts, 12.3 minutes — 3.8 / 4.6 / 0.7 on 52.0% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, +194.5% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $39.70, today's mark of $116.93 is +194.5%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Houston took him 25th out of Chalon-sur-Saône in France and signed him on Jul. 23, 2014. He became a restricted free agent in 2018 and re-signed on Jul. 27, 2018 for five years and $85 million, per RealGM.

On Feb. 5, 2020 the Rockets sent him and Nene to Atlanta in a four-team trade that brought Robert Covington to Houston; the same deal moved fellow 2014 draftees Shabazz Napier and Noah Vonleh from Minnesota to Denver. In Atlanta he won the 2020-21 NBA rebounding title at 14.3 a game, per Basketball-Reference, and signed a two-year, $46 million extension on Sep. 1, 2021.

After five seasons with the Hawks he became a free agent in July 2025 and returned to Houston on a three-year, $21.5 million deal, completed Jul. 6, 2025 as a sign-and-trade within the seven-team Kevin Durant trade.
