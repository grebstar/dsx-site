SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him #14 overall out of NC State. He had just gone for 24.9 points, 7.1 rebounds and 1.1 assists in 35 games at 35.4 minutes a night. He went 8 picks ahead of his composite, which was #22. The boards ranged from #16 to #60 on him, a spread of 44 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 40 games, 1 starts, 15.4 minutes — 6.1 / 2.1 / 0.6 on 52.8% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, +146.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2023-24 (Minnesota) -- waived by New York in camp in October 2024; played for the Westchester Knicks (G League) in February and March 2026 and joined Paris Basketball for 2026-27.

Against a frozen IPO of $47.43, today's mark of $39.70 is -16.3%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished #13 in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Phoenix took him fourteenth on Jun. 26, 2014 and gave him a rookie scale extension on Sep. 26, 2017. On Jul. 6, 2019 the Suns traded him to Indiana, with three second-round picks, for cash. He scored 53 points against Philadelphia on Aug. 1, 2020 in the Orlando restart and made the All-Seeding Games First Team. Four games into 2020-21 he needed surgery for a stress fracture in his left foot, and he did not play again until Dec. 2, 2022.

That return came with Brooklyn, which had signed him to a one-year deal in July 2022. On Feb. 9, 2023 the Nets traded him to Phoenix with Kevin Durant for Mikal Bridges, Cameron Johnson, Jae Crowder and four first-round picks plus a swap. He signed two 10-day contracts with Minnesota in March 2024 and then a deal for the rest of that season. The Knicks signed him to an Exhibit 10 contract on Oct. 3, 2024 and waived him in camp; he spent 2024-25 with Westchester at 23.7 points a game.
