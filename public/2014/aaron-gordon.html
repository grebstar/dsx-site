SCOUTING REPORT

Orlando took him fourth overall out of Arizona. He had just gone for 12.4 points, 8.0 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 38 games at 31.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #7, pick 4. The boards ranged from fifth to #19 on him, a spread of 14 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 47 games, 8 starts, 17.0 minutes — 5.2 / 3.6 / 0.7 on 44.7% shooting. That ranked him eleventh in the class and marked him at $149.42, -44.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 36 games, 33 starts, 27.9 minutes — 16.2 / 5.8 / 2.7 on 49.7% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, -15.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $269.91, today's mark of $229.10 is -15.1%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished eleventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Orlando took him fourth and kept him nearly seven seasons, re-signing him as a restricted free agent on Jul. 6, 2018. He competed in the Slam Dunk Contest in 2016, 2017 and 2020.

On Mar. 25, 2021 the Magic traded him to Denver with Gary Clark for fellow 2014 draftee Gary Harris, R.J. Hampton and a conditional 2025 first-round pick. He signed an extension with the Nuggets on Sep. 27, 2021 and started 68 games for the 2023 championship team.

On Oct. 21, 2024 he opted into his 2025-26 salary and agreed to a four-year extension reported at $133 million, with a player option for 2028-29. In June 2026 The Athletic reported Denver was open to trade offers but had not received one that met its valuation.
