Denver took him eleventh overall out of Creighton. He had just gone for 26.7 points, 7.0 rebounds and 1.6 assists in 35 games at 33.7 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #9, pick 11. The boards ranged from eighth to #20 on him, a spread of 12 places across 12 of them.

That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, -57.2% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $196.79, today's mark of $84.15 is -57.2%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Denver took him eleventh on Jun. 26, 2014 and traded him that night: Chicago received his rights and Anthony Randolph for the rights to Jusuf Nurkic (16th) and Gary Harris (19th), both 2014 draftees, plus a 2015 second-round pick. On Feb. 23, 2017 the Bulls sent him to Oklahoma City with Taj Gibson and a 2018 second for Cameron Payne, Joffrey Lauvergne and Anthony Morrow. On Sep. 25, 2017 he went to New York with Enes Kanter (now Enes Freedom) in the Carmelo Anthony trade, and on Feb. 8, 2018 the Knicks moved him to Dallas in a deal involving Devin Harris and a swap of 2018 seconds.

He signed a multi-year deal with Indiana on Jul. 6, 2018, posted 13.6 points a game in 2020-21, and joined San Antonio in an Aug. 7, 2021 sign-and-trade. The Spurs traded him back to Indiana on Feb. 8, 2024 with Furkan Korkmaz in the deal that moved Buddy Hield. He signed a one-year deal with Sacramento on Oct. 16, 2024 and re-signed for 2025-26; he has been an unrestricted free agent since Jul. 1, 2026.
