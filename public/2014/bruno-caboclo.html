SCOUTING REPORT

Toronto took him #20 overall out of Pinheiros in the Brazilian NBB + Liga de las Americas + Intercontinental Cup. He had just gone for 5.0 points, 2.8 rebounds and 0.2 assists in 21 games at 12.8 minutes a night. He went 33 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #53, drafted 20. Somebody in that room saw it differently. All 12 boards had him at #60. There was no disagreement to price.

2014-15: 8 games, 0 starts, 2.9 minutes — 1.3 / 0.3 / 0.0 on 33.3% shooting. That ranked him #41 in the class and marked him at $7.28, up from an unlisted IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2020-21 (Houston) -- joined Dubai Basketball (Adriatic ABA League / EuroLeague) in January 2026.

He was priced at $0.00 at IPO — below the replacement line at composite rank 50 — so there is no percentage to quote from zero. He is carried as FROM UNLISTED.

Toronto drafted him 20th out of Pinheiros and kept him three and a half seasons, mostly in the D-League. On Feb. 8, 2018 the Raptors traded him to Sacramento for Malachi Richardson. After a camp deal with Houston that ended in an Oct. 13, 2018 waiver and a stint with the Rockets' G League team, Memphis signed him to 10-day deals in January 2019 and a multi-year contract on Feb. 13, 2019; he started 19 of 34 games that season at 8.3 points. Memphis traded him to Houston on Feb. 6, 2020 for Jordan Bell and a second-round swap; the Rockets waived him Jan. 14, 2021.

Overseas he played for Limoges, then Sao Paulo (2022 Brazilian NBB MVP and BCL Americas Finals MVP), Ratiopharm Ulm (All-EuroCup Second Team, 2023), Partizan (2023-24) and Hapoel Tel Aviv (from August 2024), with a Boston camp deal in 2022 that ended in a Sep. 20 waiver. He was MVP of Brazil's 2024 Olympic qualifier in Latvia.
