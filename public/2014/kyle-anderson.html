SCOUTING REPORT

San Antonio took him #30 overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 14.6 points, 8.8 rebounds and 6.5 assists in 36 games at 33.2 minutes a night. The market liked him a great deal more than the room did: composite #18, drafted 30. The boards ranged from fourth to #60 on him, a spread of 56 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 33 games, 8 starts, 10.8 minutes — 2.2 / 2.2 / 0.8 on 34.8% shooting. That ranked him #27 in the class and marked him at $30.50, -55.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: 43 games, 5 starts, 19.8 minutes — 6.2 / 3.5 / 2.9 on 53.0% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +147.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $68.95, today's mark of $170.70 is +147.6%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

San Antonio took him 30th and signed him on Jul. 10, 2014. After four seasons, Memphis signed him to a restricted free-agent offer sheet on Jul. 6, 2018; the Spurs did not match, and he joined the Grizzlies on a four-year, $37.2 million deal. He signed with Minnesota on Jul. 7, 2022, and in July 2024 went to Golden State in a three-year, $27 million sign-and-trade that used the exception from Klay Thompson's move to Dallas.

On Feb. 6, 2025 the Warriors sent him to Miami with fellow 2014 draftee Andrew Wiggins (the No. 1 pick), Davion Mitchell and a conditional 2025 first in the Jimmy Butler trade. Miami moved him to Utah with Kevin Love on Jul. 7, 2025 for John Collins, and Utah sent him to Memphis on Feb. 3, 2026 in the Jaren Jackson Jr. trade. The Grizzlies waived him after a buyout on Feb. 26, 2026, and he re-signed with Minnesota on Mar. 1. He agreed to a one-year, $3.9 million deal with Toronto in July 2026.
