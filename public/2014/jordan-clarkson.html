SCOUTING REPORT

Washington took him #46 overall out of Missouri. He had just gone for 17.5 points, 3.8 rebounds and 3.4 assists in 35 games at 35.1 minutes a night. The market liked him a great deal more than the room did: composite #32, drafted 46. The boards ranged from #23 to #60 on him, a spread of 37 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 59 games, 38 starts, 25.0 minutes — 11.9 / 3.2 / 3.5 on 44.8% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +3093.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 72 games, 1 starts, 17.8 minutes — 8.6 / 1.8 / 1.3 on 45.1% shooting. That ranked him eleventh in the class and marked him at $149.42, +665.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $19.52, today's mark of $149.42 is +665.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished third in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Washington took him 46th and sold his rights to the Lakers for cash on draft night; he signed on Aug. 25, 2014 and re-signed as a restricted free agent on Jul. 7, 2016. On Feb. 8, 2018 the Lakers traded him and Larry Nance Jr. to Cleveland for Isaiah Thomas, Channing Frye and the Cavaliers' 2018 first-round pick.

On Dec. 23, 2019 Cleveland sent him to Utah for fellow 2014 draftee Dante Exum (the No. 5 pick) and two second-round picks. He won Sixth Man of the Year for 2020-21, re-signed for four years and $52 million in November 2020, and added a three-year, $55 million renegotiate-and-extend deal in July 2023. A left-foot plantar fasciitis procedure ended his 2024-25 season in March. Utah bought him out and waived him on Jul. 1, 2025; he signed a one-year minimum deal with the Knicks, played 72 regular-season and 18 playoff games for the 2026 champions, and re-signed with New York on Jul. 10, 2026 for one year and $3.9 million.
