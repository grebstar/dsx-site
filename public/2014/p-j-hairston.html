SCOUTING REPORT

Miami took him #26 overall out of Texas Legends in the NBA D-League. He had just gone for 21.8 points, 3.5 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 26 games at 32.3 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #23, pick 26. The boards ranged from twelfth to #60 on him, a spread of 48 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 45 games, 2 starts, 15.3 minutes — 5.6 / 2.0 / 0.5 on 32.3% shooting. That ranked him #23 in the class and marked him at $43.38, +0.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2015-16 (Charlotte/Memphis) -- his last recorded games were with the Rio Grande Valley Vipers (D-League) in January 2017.

Against a frozen IPO of $43.38, today's mark of $17.77 is -59.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Miami took him 26th after a pre-draft season with the D-League's Texas Legends (North Carolina had said in December 2013 it would not seek his reinstatement). On Jun. 27, 2014 the Heat sent his rights, the rights to fellow 2014 pick Semaj Christon, a 2019 second-round pick and cash to Charlotte for the rights to Shabazz Napier. He signed with the Hornets on Aug. 22, 2014.

On Feb. 16, 2016 Charlotte sent him and second-round picks to Memphis in a three-team trade for Courtney Lee, with Chris Andersen also going from Miami to Memphis. He became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2016 and Memphis renounced his rights on Jul. 7. He signed a training-camp deal with Houston on Sep. 30, 2016, was waived Oct. 22, and played 10 D-League games for the Rio Grande Valley Vipers at 11.8 points. WRAL reported on Jul. 13, 2018 that he had announced on Instagram he was walking away from basketball.
