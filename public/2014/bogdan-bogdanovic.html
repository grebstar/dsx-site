SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him #27 overall out of Partizan in the Adriatic ABA + EuroLeague + Serbian KLS. He had just gone for 14.9 points, 3.7 rebounds and 3.7 assists in 66 games at 30.2 minutes a night. He went 7 picks ahead of his composite, which was #34. The boards ranged from #25 to #60 on him, a spread of 35 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: he did not appear in an NBA game. stayed at Fenerbahce (Turkish BSL / EuroLeague) for 2014-15 as a draft-and-stash. 2025-26: 23 games, 3 starts, 19.7 minutes — 7.4 / 2.6 / 2.2 on 38.8% shooting. That ranked him #14 in the class and marked him at $104.27, +545.8% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $16.15, today's mark of $104.27 is +545.8%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Phoenix took him 27th on Jun. 26, 2014 and never signed him. On Jul. 9, 2014 the league let the Suns remove his cap hold, and on Jul. 22 he signed with Fenerbahce, where he stayed three seasons. On Jun. 23, 2016 Phoenix traded his rights to Sacramento, with the rights to Skal Labissiere and George Papagiannis and a 2020 second-round pick, for the rights to Marquese Chriss. He signed with the Kings on Jul. 13, 2017 and made the All-Rookie Second Team in 2018.

Atlanta signed him to an offer sheet on Nov. 22, 2020; Sacramento did not match and he joined the Hawks on Nov. 24, adding a four-year, $68 million extension in March 2023. On Feb. 6, 2025 Atlanta traded him to the Clippers, with three second-round picks, for Bones Hyland and Terance Mann. After a 23-game 2025-26, the Clippers declined his $16 million option on Jun. 29, 2026; he agreed to a one-year, veteran's-minimum deal with Houston and signed on Jul. 8, 2026.
