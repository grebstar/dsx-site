SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him #21 overall out of Michigan. He had just gone for 9.5 points, 8.3 rebounds and 1.5 assists in 8 games at 24.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #25, pick 21. The boards ranged from #24 to #60 on him, a spread of 36 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 32 games, 2 starts, 15.2 minutes — 6.3 / 5.2 / 0.4 on 53.3% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +369.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2015-16 (Oklahoma City) -- waived by Oklahoma City in October 2016; no professional games recorded since.

Against a frozen IPO of $36.38, today's mark of $25.57 is -29.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished tenth in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Oklahoma City took him 21st on Jun. 26, 2014 and signed him on Jul. 3. The Thunder exercised his third-year option on Oct. 22, 2015, and across two seasons he was assigned to the Oklahoma City Blue more than a dozen times. RealGM, relaying The Vertical, reported in September 2016 that he had left the team for the final weeks of 2015-16 for what the franchise termed “personal reasons.”

On Jul. 8, 2016 the NBA suspended him five games without pay for violating the NBA/NBPA Anti-Drug Program. On Sep. 13, 2016 the league added 10 games for non-compliance with the drug policy; the report said this was not believed to be another positive test but a failure to follow the program's procedural guidelines. That made 15 games to open 2016-17. On Oct. 24, 2016 the Thunder waived him, owing $1.526 million for the season, a move that kept fellow 2014 draftee Semaj Christon on the roster. He became a free agent on Oct. 26, 2016 and no professional game has been recorded for him since.
