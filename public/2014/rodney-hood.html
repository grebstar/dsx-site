SCOUTING REPORT

Utah took him #23 overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 16.1 points, 3.9 rebounds and 2.1 assists in 35 games at 32.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #19, pick 23. The boards ranged from twelfth to #60 on him, a spread of 48 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 50 games, 21 starts, 21.3 minutes — 8.7 / 2.3 / 1.7 on 41.4% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, +331.3% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Milwaukee/LA Clippers) -- his last recorded games were with the Memphis Hustle (G League) in March 2024.

Against a frozen IPO of $62.59, today's mark of $21.38 is -65.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished seventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Utah took him 23rd and kept him three and a half seasons, then sent him to Cleveland on Feb. 8, 2018 with George Hill in a three-team deadline deal with Sacramento that moved Jae Crowder and Derrick Rose to Utah. He re-signed with the Cavaliers on his qualifying offer in September 2018, and on Feb. 4, 2019 Cleveland traded him to Portland for fellow 2014 draftee Nik Stauskas, Wade Baldwin IV and two second-round picks.

Portland re-signed him on a two-year, $16 million deal in July 2019. He tore his left Achilles tendon on Dec. 6, 2019 and re-signed on a two-year, $21 million deal in November 2020. On Mar. 25, 2021 the Blazers traded him to Toronto with Gary Trent Jr. for Norman Powell; the Raptors waived him on Aug. 3, 2021 and he signed with Milwaukee three days later. On Feb. 10, 2022 the Bucks sent him to the Clippers with Semi Ojeleye in a four-team trade for Serge Ibaka. He became a free agent in July 2022, was claimed by the Memphis Hustle in February 2024, and retired on Nov. 21, 2024 after 448 NBA games.
