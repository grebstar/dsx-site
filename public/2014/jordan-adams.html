SCOUTING REPORT

Memphis took him #22 overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 17.4 points, 5.3 rebounds and 2.3 assists in 36 games at 30.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #26, pick 22. The boards ranged from sixth to #60 on him, a spread of 54 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 30 games, 0 starts, 8.3 minutes — 3.1 / 0.9 / 0.5 on 40.7% shooting. That ranked him #28 in the class and marked him at $27.93, -16.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2015-16 (Memphis) -- has played in Indonesia's IBL for Dewa United Banten since 2024 (Latinbasket lists him with the club in 2026).

Against a frozen IPO of $33.31, today's mark of $23.39 is -29.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Memphis took him 22nd on Jun. 26, 2014 and signed him on Jul. 6. The Grizzlies exercised his third-year option on Oct. 27, 2015, then waived him on Oct. 24, 2016. The Oklahoma City Blue claimed him that November; he later played four Las Vegas Summer League games for Portland in July 2017, two G League games for the Rio Grande Valley Vipers in early 2019 (claimed Feb. 28, waived Mar. 21), and was drafted and waived by the Windy City Bulls in October 2019.

From there he played in Latin America: Venados de Mazatlán (Mexico's CIBACOPA), Libertadores de Querétaro (LNBP), Comunicaciones Mercedes in Argentina's Liga A (he left in February 2023), and Gladiadores de Anzoátegui in Venezuela, where he averaged 17.4 points in 42 games. He signed with Dewa United Banten of the Indonesian Basketball League in December 2023, was brought in by the PBA's San Miguel Beermen as a Governors' Cup import in August 2024, and returned to Dewa United for the 2025 IBL season.
