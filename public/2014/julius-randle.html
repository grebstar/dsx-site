The Lakers took him seventh, and after the lost rookie year he spent three more seasons there. In July 2018 the Lakers withdrew his qualifying offer and renounced his rights, making him an unrestricted free agent; he signed with New Orleans on Jul. 8, 2018 and averaged 21.4 points in 73 games.

He signed with New York on Jul. 8, 2019. As a Knick he was named Most Improved Player and All-NBA Second Team in 2021, made All-NBA Third Team in 2023, made three All-Star teams (2021, 2023, 2024), and signed a veteran extension on Aug. 26, 2021. On Oct. 2, 2024 New York traded him to Minnesota, with Donte DiVincenzo and Keita Bates-Diop, in the deal that sent Karl-Anthony Towns to the Knicks.

Minnesota re-signed him on Jul. 15, 2025 on a three-year, $100 million contract. On Jun. 22, 2026 RealGM reported that the Wolves were trading him to Brooklyn, with Nic Claxton going from the Nets to Chicago; the deal was completed on Jul. 10, 2026, with the draft rights to Joshua Jefferson also going to Brooklyn. The Nets' release called it a four-team trade.
