SCOUTING REPORT

Sacramento took him eighth overall out of Michigan. He had just gone for 17.5 points, 2.9 rebounds and 3.3 assists in 36 games at 35.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #10, pick 8. The boards ranged from ninth to #14 on him, a spread of 5 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 73 games, 1 starts, 15.4 minutes — 4.4 / 1.2 / 0.9 on 36.5% shooting. That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, -50.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Miami/Boston) -- traded to Indiana and waived in July 2022; no professional games recorded since.

Against a frozen IPO of $170.70, today's mark of $51.92 is -69.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished #16 in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Sacramento took him eighth and traded him after one season: the deal agreed Jul. 1, 2015 and completed Jul. 9 sent him, Carl Landry and Jason Thompson to Philadelphia, with a protected first and two first-round swap rights, for two draft-rights stashes, clearing cap space for the Kings. On Dec. 7, 2017 the Sixers sent him and Jahlil Okafor to Brooklyn for Trevor Booker.

After a year in Portland, he was traded on Feb. 4, 2019 to Cleveland, with Wade Baldwin IV and two seconds, for fellow 2014 draftee Rodney Hood. Three days later he was routed through Houston to Indiana, which waived him; Cleveland re-signed him on Feb. 11, 2019. He spent 2019-20 with Baskonia in Spain (40 EuroLeague and ACB games) before leaving in February 2020.

He returned through the G League, scoring 57 and 43 points in back-to-back Grand Rapids Gold games in March 2022, and signed with Boston on Mar. 4, 2022 after a 10-day stint with Miami. Boston included him in the Jul. 9, 2022 trade for Malcolm Brogdon; Indiana waived him on Jul. 14, 2022.
