SCOUTING REPORT

Milwaukee took him second overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 19.1 points, 8.7 rebounds and 1.2 assists in 35 games at 30.7 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #2, pick 2. The boards ranged from first to third on him, a spread of 2 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 25 games, 25 starts, 29.5 minutes — 12.3 / 5.5 / 1.7 on 49.0% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, -75.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. did not play in the NBA in 2025-26; last appeared in the NBA in 2021-22 (Boston) -- played for Partizan (Adriatic ABA League / EuroLeague) from September 2025 to January 2026, then Joventut Badalona (Liga ACB) from March to June 2026.

Against a frozen IPO of $808.24, today's mark of $56.94 is -93.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished ninth in the All-Rookie vote among 2014 draftees.

Milwaukee took him second and kept him four seasons, through two tears of the ACL in his left knee, in 2014 and 2017. He averaged 20.1 points in 51 games in 2016-17 before the second tear. The Bucks withdrew his qualifying offer on Jul. 14, 2018 and he signed with Chicago the same day. On Feb. 6, 2019 the Bulls traded him to Washington with Bobby Portis for Otto Porter.

He signed with Atlanta on Jul. 11, 2019, and on Feb. 6, 2020 was traded to Sacramento with Alex Len for Dewayne Dedmon and two second-round picks. The Kings waived him on Mar. 25, 2021; Boston signed him on Apr. 16, 2021, waived him on Oct. 17, re-signed him two days later and waived him for good on Jan. 7, 2022.

He moved to FC Barcelona in August 2023 and played two seasons there (11.5 and 13.4 points a game), making the All-Liga ACB Second Team in 2025. After a mutual parting in June 2025 he signed a two-year contract with Partizan that included NBA outs. Partizan loaned him to Joventut Badalona in February 2026; RealGM lists him back with Partizan from Aug. 16, 2026, and Hoops Rumors reported his contract runs through 2026-27.
