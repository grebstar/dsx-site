Philadelphia took him 39th and signed him on Sep. 29, 2014. Two games into 2016-17, on Nov. 1, 2016, the Sixers traded him to Oklahoma City for Ersan Ilyasova and a conditional first-round pick. He re-signed with the Thunder on Jul. 10, 2018 and was traded to Denver on Jul. 8, 2019 for a conditional 2020 first.

On Nov. 22, 2020 he left Denver in a sign-and-trade to Detroit, where he averaged 22.3 points in 2020-21. The Pistons sent him to Portland on Jul. 6, 2022 for a conditional 2025 first (via Milwaukee) and second-round picks, and he agreed to a five-year, $160 million deal with the Blazers on Jun. 30, 2023. On Jun. 29, 2026 Portland traded him and Kris Murray to Memphis for Ja Morant; he is due $34.2 million for 2026-27 and holds a $37.2 million player option for 2027-28.
