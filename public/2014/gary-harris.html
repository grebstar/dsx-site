SCOUTING REPORT

Chicago took him #19 overall out of Michigan State. He had just gone for 16.7 points, 4.0 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 35 games at 32.3 minutes a night. The boards had him 6 places above where he went, at #13. The boards ranged from seventh to #60 on him, a spread of 53 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: 55 games, 6 starts, 13.1 minutes — 3.4 / 1.2 / 0.5 on 30.4% shooting. That ranked him #25 in the class and marked him at $36.38, -68.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: 48 games, 2 starts, 13.8 minutes — 2.7 / 1.3 / 1.1 on 44.2% shooting. That ranked him #17 in the class and marked him at $75.99, -35.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $116.93, today's mark of $75.99 is -35.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Chicago took him 19th and traded him on draft night, Jun. 26, 2014, to Denver along with the rights to No. 16 Jusuf Nurkic and a 2015 second-round pick, for the rights to No. 11 Doug McDermott and Anthony Randolph. He signed a rookie-scale extension with the Nuggets on Oct. 12, 2017.

On Mar. 25, 2021 Denver sent him to Orlando with R.J. Hampton and a conditional 2025 first-round pick for Aaron Gordon, the fourth pick of his own 2014 class, and Gary Clark. He signed an extension with the Magic on Jun. 30, 2022 and re-signed as a free agent on Jul. 6, 2024. Orlando declined his option on Jun. 15, 2025, and he signed a two-year deal with Milwaukee on Jul. 8, 2025.

He exercised his player option on Jun. 20, 2026. On Jul. 8, 2026 he went to Detroit with John Collins and Taurean Prince in a trade the Pistons announced as a six-team deal, with Caris LeVert going to Milwaukee. Detroit waived him on Sep. 27, 2026, and he became a free agent on Sep. 29.
