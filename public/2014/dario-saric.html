SCOUTING REPORT

Orlando took him twelfth overall out of Cibona in the Adriatic ABA + Croatian A-1 + EuroCup. He had just gone for 16.9 points, 9.4 rebounds and 3.3 assists in 57 games at 32.4 minutes a night. He went 5 picks ahead of his composite, which was #17. The boards ranged from tenth to #60 on him, a spread of 50 places across 12 of them.

2014-15: he did not appear in an NBA game. stayed at Anadolu Efes (Turkish BSL / EuroLeague) for 2014-15 as a draft-and-stash. 2025-26: 5 games, 0 starts, 8.2 minutes — 1.0 / 1.2 / 0.4 on 16.7% shooting. That ranked him #19 in the class and marked him at $62.59, -17.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $75.99, today's mark of $62.59 is -17.6%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

Orlando took him twelfth on Jun. 26, 2014 and traded his rights that night to Philadelphia, with a 2015 second and a conditional 2017 first, for the rights to Elfrid Payton, the tenth pick of the same draft. After two seasons in Istanbul he signed with the 76ers on Jul. 15, 2016 and made the 2016-17 All-Rookie First Team. On Nov. 12, 2018 he went to Minnesota with Robert Covington and Jerryd Bayless for Jimmy Butler, and on Jul. 6, 2019 to Phoenix with the rights to Cameron Johnson for the rights to Jarrett Culver.

Phoenix re-signed him in November 2020. He tore the ACL in his right knee in Game 1 of the 2021 Finals and missed all of 2021-22. The Suns traded him to Oklahoma City for Darius Bazley on Feb. 9, 2023; he then signed with Golden State (Jul. 2023) and Denver (Jul. 2024, two years). The Nuggets traded him to Sacramento for Jonas Valanciunas, completed Jul. 13, 2025. After the two February 2026 trades and his release by Detroit, he returned to Anadolu Efes.
