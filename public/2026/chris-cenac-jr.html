SCOUTING REPORT

The production is the weakest part of his case and there's no dressing it up. 9.5 points, 7.9 rebounds, 0.7 assists, 0.8 steals and 0.5 blocks on 48.5% from the field and 62.1% from the line at Houston. The nine-cat composite comes out at −3.77, among the lowest on this board. Points are low, blocks are low for a big, assists are essentially zero, and the free throw shooting is a drag.

What is genuinely good: 7.9 rebounds in 24.8 minutes is a strong rate, 0.8 made threes from a big is a real skill flag at 19, and the 0.9 turnovers is exceptional discipline. He is 19.5 years old, one of the six youngest players on this board, and he was a high-pedigree recruit whose college role never matched his billing.

Boston is a real part of the thesis. The Celtics have been among the league's better organizations at converting late-first-round picks into useful players, and they have the luxury of a multi-year runway. This is a development situation rather than an opportunity situation, and his Pathway score of 15 reflects that honestly — he will not play meaningfully in 2026-27.

The read. At $47.43, twenty-second on the board. The bull case is that a 19-year-old with 7.9 rebounds per 25 minutes and a functional three-point stroke, in a good organization, looks completely different at 22. The bear case is that his freshman numbers are simply what he is. Sporting News has him eighth in this class and Angel twentieth, so those two are the evaluators meaningfully in the bull case; CBS is partway there at eighteenth. As a late second-round or third-round rookie pick, that asymmetry is worth owning.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 25th, 8th and 18th in this class. Sporting News is the high mark at 8th and Yahoo the low at 25th.
