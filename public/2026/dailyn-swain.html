SCOUTING REPORT

The college season stands up. 17.3 points, 7.5 rebounds, 3.6 assists and 1.6 steals in 32.8 minutes on 54.2% from the field and 81.5% from the line across 36 games at Texas. Wings who rebound at 7.5 and steal at 1.6 while shooting 54% are genuinely scarce, and his nine-cat composite of +1.61 ranks twentieth in the class. Chicago was drawn to the mix of physical tools, downhill scoring, rebounding and defense.

The two problems are shooting and turnovers. 0.9 made threes per game is a dead category and, for a wing, a structural one — NBA defenses will sag off him. 2.7 turnovers is high for a secondary creator. Those two together explain why the composite has him twenty-first despite the raw production.

Summer League was genuinely poor and there is no way to spin it: three-of-ten in his debut, then zero-for-nine. Coverage labeled him the most disappointing lottery selection in Las Vegas. Two games is a tiny sample and July results are weakly predictive, but for a player whose value already rested on shot-making questions, it was the worst possible sample to produce.

The read. At $51.92 he is priced twenty-first and is the clearest post-Vegas buy-the-dip candidate on the board. Dizzle and Roto both had him fourteenth; Angel, Yahoo and Sporting News had him twenty-first and CBS twenty-fifth. He landed on a Bulls roster that also took Caleb Wilson, which complicates the frontcourt minutes but says something about how Chicago values his versatility. HOLD with an upgrade path: if the three-point volume ever reaches 1.5 a game, the profile is top-100.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 21st, 21st and 25th in this class.
