SCOUTING REPORT

The line is genuinely excellent. 19.9 points, 5.1 rebounds, 3.9 assists, 1.1 steals and 1.8 made threes in 36.5 minutes on 55.4% from the field and 82.9% from the line, with 1.3 turnovers. Fifty-five percent shooting from a lead guard at nearly 20 points a game is close to unheard of, and his nine-cat composite of +1.57 ranks twenty-first in the class — ahead of seventeen players drafted before him.

Houston took him with the first pick of the second round and, unlike most of the names in this range, he has a plausible route to real minutes: he is physically and mentally NBA-ready in a way that a 19-year-old isn't, and second-unit guard minutes are the easiest to earn on merit. His Pathway score of 44 is high for a second-rounder.

The age is the whole discount and it is real. He is 22.9 years old, which in a dynasty format means you are buying most of what he will ever be. By the time this rookie class's teenagers hit their primes, Thornton will be 27 and flat. The model's Long-Term Starter Upside score docks him accordingly, and it should.

The read. At $16.14, thirty-fourth on the board. All six boards list him and none ranks him better than twenty-seventh; Sporting News and CBS are cooler still, with Sporting News forty-seventh and CBS fortieth in this class. This is a straightforward trade: you are getting genuinely good, immediately usable production — efficient scoring, real assists, low turnovers, positive in both percentage categories — in exchange for zero appreciation. On a contending roster in a 10-team league, that is a very reasonable third-round rookie pick. On a rebuild, skip it.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 32nd, 47th and 40th in this class. Yahoo is the high mark at 32nd and Sporting News the low at 47th.
