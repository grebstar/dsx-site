SCOUTING REPORT

The raw line looks alarming and largely shouldn't. 7.1 points, 2.2 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 14.8 minutes over 72 games, on 43.6% shooting with 1.0 made three and 81.1% from the line. Because our class-wide nine-category model scores per-game production, he grades at −6.69 — by far the lowest figure on this board. That number is close to meaningless. He is a 20-year-old playing limited minutes in a professional league against grown men, not a college freshman with the ball in his hands for 35 minutes a night.

On a per-36 basis he projects to roughly 17 points and 6.6 assists, which is a completely different picture, and the 81.1% free throw rate suggests genuine touch. Seventy-two games is also the largest sample of anyone on this board by a factor of two, and durability against professional competition at 20 is not nothing.

The uncertainty that was the whole thesis has resolved, and it resolved well. De Larrea signed with Dallas rather than staying at Valencia, and he's already seeing real run — 8.0 points, 4.8 rebounds and 8.8 assists across four Summer League games running the Mavericks' offense, with early regular-season minutes following. The roster-clog risk that made him a zero in a 25-man format is gone.

The read. At $36.38 he is priced twenty-fifth, and all six boards list him. Angel is the outlier bull at seventeenth and Yahoo has him twentieth; Dizzle and Roto both have him twenty-third, and CBS is the outlier bear at forty-first in this class. Those composite ranks predate the signing news — the board is likely underpricing him right now, and worth checking in on again once the sources catch up. He's no longer a pure speculative stash; he's a rotation player on an NBA roster.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 20th, 26th and 41st in this class. Yahoo is the high mark at 20th and CBS the low at 41st.
