SCOUTING REPORT

The freshman season deserves its reputation. 23.5 points, 6.4 assists and 2.5 made threes in 35.1 minutes on 48.4% from the field, 44.0% from three and 80.6% from the line. Volume scoring at that efficiency, with real playmaking attached, from a 19-year-old, is a first-overall-pick statistical profile. Angel and CBS ranked him fifth in the class, and Sacramento took him seventh.

Then Las Vegas happened, and the reporting on it was unusually pointed. The observation that landed hardest is that so much of the case for him as a franchise player rests specifically on efficiency, and efficiency is exactly what disappeared. Coverage also noted the two versions of him on tape: clips of him using his length to make real defensive plays, and clips of him disengaged when the ball wasn't in his hands. He said himself he wasn't pleased with how he played.

The opportunity is nevertheless outstanding. Sacramento is heading into a true rebuild and is expected to move on from its veteran core. Among lottery guards in this class, nobody has a clearer runway to immediate volume — which is why his Pathway to Early Minutes score is the third highest on the board despite going seventh. He averaged 19.0 points and 5.3 assists in his first three Vegas games; the counting stats were there even while the percentages weren't.

The read. At $396.43 he is ranked fifth on the composite, on boards that run from fifth (Angel and CBS) to seventh (Dizzle and Roto). This is the widest-outcome-band asset in the class. If the shooting was real and Vegas was noise, he is a top-40 dynasty player inside three years on a team that will feed him. If the efficiency was a college artifact, you own a high-usage guard who actively hurts your field goal percentage — the single most expensive way to lose a nine-cat matchup.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 6th, 6th and 5th in this class.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 48.4/44.0/80.6 as a freshman — one of the best shooting lines by a high-major freshman guard this century
  • 23.5 points and 6.4 assists in 35.1 minutes; elite usage already proven
  • Sacramento is rebuilding and expected to move veterans — the runway is real
  • Angel and CBS ranked him fifth in the class, two spots above his draft slot

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 29.4% FG, 23.1% 3PT and 62.5% FT through four Summer League games
  • The entire franchise thesis rests on efficiency, the one thing that just broke
  • Documented engagement concerns when he isn't directly involved in the play
  • Wide outcome band: the case swings on whether the efficiency was real or a college artifact
