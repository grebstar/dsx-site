SCOUTING REPORT

The production is unspectacular and that is somewhat the point. 16.1 points, 4.9 rebounds, 2.4 assists, 1.5 steals and 1.8 made threes in 29.8 minutes on 49.1% shooting and 80.5% from the line, with 1.5 turnovers. Nothing there jumps off the page and nothing there is a hole. Five categories positive, zero categories actively hurting you, in a class where most guards are trading efficiency for volume.

Then Las Vegas, where he separated. He was widely regarded as the best lottery guard on the floor, and the specific praise is exactly what a nine-cat manager wants to hear: efficient three-level scoring, comfort both on and off the ball, and active hands in passing lanes. He earned Summer League First Team honors alongside Boozer, Lendeborg, Caleb Wilson and Meleek Thomas.

The situation is the multiplier. Milwaukee is a genuinely open roster now. Some analysts noted it may take a year for the Bucks to sort out the post-Giannis hierarchy, and that is fair — but "may take a year" on a 20-year-old is a feature in dynasty, not a bug. He is not being asked to produce immediately into a crowded depth chart; he is being asked to grow into one that is being rebuilt around him.

The read. At $196.79 he is the ninth listing on the board and one of the better risk-adjusted buys in the first round. His bid-ask spread is four spots, his beta is a modest 0.83, and both the fundamentals and the situation point the same direction. He was the one lottery guard whose Summer League performance improved his standing, and the board has not fully repriced it.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 9th, 12th and 10th in this class.
