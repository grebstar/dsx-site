SCOUTING REPORT

Let's be precise about the production, because it is the crux. 16.7 points, 6.3 rebounds, 2.3 assists, 1.0 steal and 1.3 made threes in 29.7 minutes — on 39.9% shooting with 2.3 turnovers. The rebounding is genuinely good for a wing and the free throw rate at 79.0% is a positive. Everything else is either neutral or a drag. His nine-cat composite of −1.51 is the lowest of any player in the top twenty-two on this board.

What he has is the frame and the age. 19.6 years old, long, mobile, and the flashes are the kind that make evaluators reach for enormous comparisons. Milwaukee took him with the pick acquired in the Giannis trade, which means he arrives on a roster with no urgency and a genuine developmental mandate. That is the right environment for this profile.

The gap between the boards is instructive, and it runs along board type. Sporting News and CBS both have him ninth in this class and Yahoo thirteenth, while Dizzle and Roto both have him twenty-first and Angel twenty-second. Scouts and points-league managers rate him considerably higher than category-league managers, which is the correct distinction: 16.7 points on 39.9% shooting is a fine points-league line and a genuinely damaging nine-cat one.

The read. At $116.93 he is the thirteenth listing on the board — a price set mostly by Sporting News and CBS, and well above where the category-league boards would put him. The bull case is that a 19-year-old with that frame improves his shot and becomes a five-category wing; that outcome would make this pick look like theft. The bear case is that the efficiency never arrives and you have spent three roster-years on a player who cost you field goal percentage every week. In a 10-team league with 25 roster spots, that second outcome is expensive. Own him if you have the patience and the spare slot; do not reach.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 13th, 9th and 9th in this class.
