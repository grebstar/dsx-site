SCOUTING REPORT

The rebounding is the anchor and it is genuinely exceptional. 11.8 boards a night in 34.6 minutes leads this class and is not close. Attach 18.5 points on 57.7% shooting, 1.2 blocks and 1.1 steals, and you have a six-category contributor with only one dead stat — and even there he made 0.6 threes, which is not nothing for a center. His nine-cat composite of +3.08 is the fifth best in the entire class.

Summer League moved him further. Three double-doubles in five games, including back-to-back 20-point double-doubles to close, at 15.8 points and 9.2 rebounds in 24.3 minutes. Coverage highlighted his hands, his ability to run the floor for his size, and — the part that matters most for the ceiling — a budding three-point jumper and pick-and-pop potential. That is category expansion, and it is what separates a good real-life center from a great fantasy one.

The role is unusually clean for a 14th pick. Post-draft analysis flagged him as a candidate to be Charlotte's starting center on day one, and the Hornets have no entrenched incumbent. His Pathway to Early Minutes score of 79 is among the highest outside the top ten. At 20.2 years old he also carries a real development curve, unlike the other immediate producers in this range.

The read. At $93.47 he is the fifteenth listing on the board and one of the clearer mispricings on the board relative to production. Angel has him twelfth; Dizzle and Roto have him fifteenth. The gap is about whether you believe a rebounding center without elite blocks can be a top-100 dynasty asset. The efficiency profile says yes, and the emerging jumper says the ceiling is higher than the archetype suggests.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 19th, 20th and 17th in this class.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 11.8 rebounds per game leads the entire class by a full 1.5 boards
  • 57.7% FG paired with 75.9% FT — a combination almost no high-volume center offers
  • Fifth-best nine-cat composite in the class (+3.08)
  • Three double-doubles in five Summer League games; possible Day-1 starter in Charlotte

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 1.2 blocks is modest rim protection for a center at his size
  • 1.6 assists means the passing hub role isn't there yet
  • The three-point jumper is emerging, not established — 0.6 makes per game in college
  • Charlotte is an unstable organization and the starting job isn't formally locked
