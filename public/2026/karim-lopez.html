SCOUTING REPORT

The line is modest and the context matters. 11.9 points, 6.1 rebounds, 1.9 assists, 1.2 steals, 1.0 block and 1.0 made three in 25.6 minutes on 49.4% shooting. Nothing there is a headline. But look at the shape: he is a positive contributor in six categories at 19.3 years old in a limited role, with a block and a steal per game from a forward. The nine-cat composite of −1.32 is driven by low volume, not bad rates — on a per-36 basis it looks considerably better.

The free throw shooting at 73.9% is the one soft spot in the profile and it is the standard concern for young forwards. The 1.5 turnovers in 25.6 minutes is clean.

Memphis is a meaningful upgrade in circumstance. The Grizzlies traded back twice in the first round, collecting five second-round picks, and still landed a player they wanted at 21. That is an organization that knows what it is doing with mid-round assets. Lopez will not be rushed, and Memphis's timeline — built around Boozer now — matches his.

The read. At $62.59 he is the nineteenth listing on the board. Three boards are tight on him: Dizzle and Roto both have him twenty-second and Angel twenty-sixth. Three boards are far higher — Sporting News tenth, Yahoo fifteenth and CBS sixteenth in this class. A sixteen-spot spread is the widest of any profile in the top twenty, and it is a real divergence between how scouts and category-league managers see a 19-year-old forward. This is a legitimate mid-round rookie-draft target where the age alone justifies the slot.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 15th, 10th and 16th in this class.
