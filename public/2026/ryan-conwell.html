SCOUTING REPORT

The senior season was legitimately excellent. 18.8 points, 4.8 rebounds, 2.7 assists and 1.1 steals in 30.9 minutes, shooting 40.8% from the field, 34.5% from three (on huge volume — 3.3 makes a game, top-15 nationally) and 83.2% from the line. He scored 638 points in his final season, a career high, closing a four-school journey through South Florida, Indiana State and Xavier before finding his best fit at Louisville. He had fifteen 20-point games and two 30-point outings as a senior, and led his team in nearly every meaningful counting stat.

The situational fit is what pushes this from "solid shooter" to genuinely interesting. Miami acquired Giannis Antetokounmpo this offseason and, per league observers, immediately needed shot creation and movement shooting in the backcourt to complement him. One prominent draft analyst put it directly: Conwell's game "reminds me so much of Norm Powell" — and Powell may not be back in Miami. That is about as clean a role-vacancy match as exists anywhere in this draft class, at pick 37.

The catch is size and defensive projection. At 6'2", he's undersized for shooting-guard minutes, and while he competed hard defensively in college, that will be tested against NBA length. His handle has "wiggle" per scouts but he isn't a lead ball-handler — he is a play-finisher who needs the ball delivered to him in rhythm.

The read. Miami traded assets it could barely afford to spend, against a hard cap, specifically to get him. That is the strongest buy signal in this group, on a player whose per-game production already ranks among the best of any second-rounder in the class.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 34th, 38th and 32nd in this class.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 18.8 points on elite three-point volume (3.3 makes/game, top 15 nationally) as a senior
  • Scouted directly as a Norm Powell comparable — the exact role Miami now needs
  • Miami traded up and spent cash to get him despite operating near the hard cap
  • Led Louisville in points, steals, made threes and minutes as the go-to senior

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Undersized at 6'2" for shooting-guard minutes at the NBA level
  • Not a lead ball-handler; value is contingent on the ball being delivered to him
  • Defensive translation against NBA length is unproven
  • Four college stops in four years is a real developmental-stability question
