SCOUTING REPORT

Brazile's last college season, his fourth at Arkansas after one at Missouri, was a 13.0 point, 7.3 rebound, 1.6 assist year in 31.5 minutes over 36 games (35 starts), with 1.6 blocks and 1.5 steals. He shot 52.5% from the field, 34.1% from three (45-for-132) and 71.7% at the line (86-for-120), for a .619 true shooting mark and a 20.9 PER. Arkansas went 28-9 and lost to Arizona in the Sweet 16. The Denver Gazette reported he was the only Division I player that season with 50 assists, 50 steals and 50 blocks. Earlier, a torn ACL ended his 2022-23 season after nine games, and he averaged 8.6 and 6.8 points the next two years.

Denver traded the 26th pick (Tarris Reed Jr.) to San Antonio on the first night of the draft for No. 35 and two future second-round picks, and Brazile was the 35th pick on June 24, 2026. Three boards covered him, placing him between 34th and 52nd, for a composite rank of 42nd. At the combine he measured 6-9.5 without shoes and 225.6 pounds, with a 7-3.75 wingspan and a 41.5-inch maximum vertical. Bleacher Report's Jonathan Wasserman compared him to Obi Toppin.

He has no completed NBA season. In three Las Vegas Summer League games he averaged 18.7 points and 8.3 rebounds on 45.5% shooting and 47.4% from three, and scored 32 against Oklahoma City on July 14 with 6-of-12 threes; he missed a July 11 game with a shoulder strain. In the preseason he scored 22 points on 8-of-10 shooting against the Clippers on Oct. 14 and started against the Lakers on Oct. 16.

Denver signed him to a standard contract on July 10, 2026, not a two-way deal. HoopsHype's Michael Scotto reported the terms on July 13: four years and about $9.3 million, with the first two seasons fully guaranteed.

Brazile has played all four of Denver's games (2-2 through Oct. 28) at 9.5 points and 7.5 rebounds in 23.8 minutes, on 14-for-28 shooting and 5-for-15 from three. His debut at Oklahoma City on Oct. 22 was 18 minutes of 1-for-6 shooting, 0-for-4 from three and a team-high 12 rebounds. In the Oct. 24 home opener he had 17 points, 9 rebounds and 2 blocks in 28 minutes of a 141-135 overtime win over New Orleans. With Cameron Johnson out, he made his first start on Oct. 28 at New Orleans and had 11 points and 8 rebounds in 32 minutes of a 104-91 win.
