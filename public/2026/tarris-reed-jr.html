SCOUTING REPORT

The production is good and should not be dismissed on the strength of the situation alone. 14.7 points, 9.0 rebounds, 2.3 assists and 2.0 blocks on 60.7% shooting in 27.3 minutes at UConn is a genuinely strong line, and the 2.3 assists from a center indicates real feel. His nine-cat composite of +1.44 ranks twenty-third in the class, somewhat better than his twenty-ninth composite ranking implies.

Three things pull it back down. First, 61.7% free throw shooting is a live nine-cat liability at his likely volume. Second, zero made threes means a full category is dead. Third and most importantly, he is 23.0 years old — he was drafted at an age where most prospects on this board will be entering their third NBA season.

The situation is the killer. San Antonio took him at 26 as depth, alongside Jayden Quaintance at 20 — two centers in one first round, both behind Wembanyama. His Pathway score of 32 is generous. Post-draft coverage framed him plainly as the safe pick to complement the risk they took on Quaintance, which is another way of saying: backup.

The read. At $25.57, twenty-ninth on the board. Dizzle and Roto are the most bullish at twenty-fifth and Angel the most bearish at twenty-ninth, while Yahoo has him thirtieth, Sporting News forty-second and CBS twenty-eighth in this class. He is a real NBA player and probably a long-tenured one. He is also 23, capped at four categories, and third in line on his own roster. In a 10-team dynasty league those three facts together make him a waiver-wire name rather than a rookie-draft pick.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 30th, 42nd and 28th in this class. CBS is the high mark at 28th and Sporting News the low at 42nd.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 60.7% from the field with 9.0 rebounds and 2.0 blocks — legitimately efficient production
  • 2.3 assists from a center indicates genuine passing feel
  • Twenty-third-best nine-cat composite in the class (+1.44), ahead of his composite rank
  • A first-round pick by a well-run organization; he will have a long NBA career

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 23.0 years old — no development premium remains
  • Zero made threes and 61.7% free throw shooting cap the category ceiling at four
  • Drafted behind Victor Wembanyama and alongside another first-round center
  • Explicitly projected as a backup center in Year 1
