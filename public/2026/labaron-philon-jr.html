SCOUTING REPORT

The season is legitimately strong. 22.0 points, 5.0 assists, 3.5 rebounds, 1.2 steals and 2.5 made threes in 30.9 minutes on 50.1% from the field and 79.8% from the line. Fifty percent shooting from a guard scoring 22 a night is genuinely efficient volume, and his nine-cat composite of +2.05 ranks thirteenth in the class — ahead of Lendeborg and Okorie.

He was taken twenty-second by Philadelphia, and that is where the story stops being about basketball. The Sixers spent the offseason adding two high-usage veterans to a roster that already had them. The consensus assessment is that his rookie minutes are close to zero. His Pathway to Early Minutes score of 24 is the second lowest of any first-round pick on this board, ahead of only Aday Mara.

What you are buying is patience plus optionality. He is 20.7 years old, the analysts who cover him still like the playmaking upside a great deal, and second-round rookie contracts on blocked guards get moved. The upside case requires a trade, and trades happen.

The read. At $104.27 he is the fourteenth listing on the board and his production ranks thirteenth, so price and production now line up. Whatever discount is left is situation. If you have roster space and a two-year horizon, this is one of the cleaner situational discounts in the 2026 class. If you need contribution, he is unownable.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 12th, 13th and 15th in this class.
