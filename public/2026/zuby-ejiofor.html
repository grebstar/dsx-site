SCOUTING REPORT

Run the numbers first. 16.3 points, 7.3 rebounds, 3.5 assists, 1.2 steals and 2.1 blocks in 30 minutes on 53.6% from the field and 71.8% from the line at St. John's. Feed that into the class-wide nine-category model and he grades +2.55, eighth best in the class. He is above the class median in six categories including all three defensive-adjacent ones.

The specific thing that makes him interesting is 3.5 assists from a big who also blocks 2.1 shots. In a 10-team league, assists from your frontcourt slots are the single hardest thing to source, and pairing them with rim protection is rarer still. Atlanta took him at twenty-three with their second first-round pick and has an open frontcourt behind their starters.

The bear case is age and ceiling. He is 22.3, which puts him in the older half of this board and means the appreciation curve is shorter. The 71.8% free throw shooting is a mild negative and the 2.1 turnovers is average. Nothing here suggests a future top-50 dynasty player; the case is that he is a top-150 asset available at a price closer to top-250.

The read. At $23.39 he is the thirtieth listing on the board and, on production-per-dollar, the single best value on this board. Dynatze's own universe rank has him 246th overall, which prices in essentially no development at all. If your rookie draft runs three rounds, taking a player with one of the ten best category profiles in the class at pick 2.06 or later is close to free money.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 36th, 28th and 34th in this class. Sporting News is the high mark at 28th and Yahoo the low at 36th.
