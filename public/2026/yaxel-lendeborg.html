SCOUTING REPORT

The production case is genuinely strong and should not be dismissed. His nine-cat composite of +1.88 ranks sixteenth in the class, and it comes from breadth: he is above the class median in six categories, including both steals and blocks, while shooting 51.5% from the field and 82.4% from the line with only 1.1 turnovers. A big who makes 1.7 threes, dishes 3.2 assists and turns it over once a game is a legitimately valuable roster piece in any format.

Golden State took him eleventh and he was named Summer League First Team. Post-draft analysis put him at the front of the group of rookies most likely to hold redraft value in Year 1, specifically citing the Warriors' injuries to start the season. Among all the priced listings on this board, his Pathway to Early Minutes score of 87 ties for the highest.

Now the age. He turns 24 during his rookie season. In a dynasty format where you are buying the integral of a career rather than a single year, that is the entire discount. By the time a 19-year-old from this class reaches his prime, Lendeborg will be exiting his. Dynatze slots him 120th in the overall player universe — useful, not foundational — and the model's Long-Term Starter Upside score docks him accordingly.

The read. At $131.64 he is the twelfth listing on the board. If your team is competing now, he is arguably the best value in the entire back half of round one — immediate production, no development wait, no efficiency risk. If you are building for 2030, he is the least interesting name in the lottery. Rating him is genuinely a function of your roster state, which is why this is a HOLD rather than a number.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 17th, 11th and 12th in this class.
