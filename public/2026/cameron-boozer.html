SCOUTING REPORT

Start with the season, because it is the whole argument. Boozer posted 22.5 points, 10.2 rebounds and 4.1 assists on 55.6% from the field and 78.9% from the line across 38 games as a freshman at Duke — one of the statistically best freshman campaigns in modern college basketball. In a nine-category format, that line is close to the platonic ideal: elite in points, elite in rebounds, genuinely rare in assists for a big, positive in field goal percentage, positive in free throw percentage, and with 1.4 threes and 1.4 steals he is not a zero anywhere except blocks.

Then the landing spot. Memphis took him third overall and, in the composite's own words, has given him the keys to the franchise. That matters more than the slot number: he does not have to beat out an incumbent, he does not have to wait for a trade, and the Grizzlies' timeline is his timeline. Of the top four picks, he has the cleanest runway to 30-plus minutes on opening night.

The consensus reflects it. Four of the six boards on the composite rank him first — Angel, Dizzle, Roto and Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor. The only dissent: Sporting News has him fourth and CBS third. That is a three-spot bid-ask spread, coverage of 6-for-6, and a beta of 0.80 — low, and not because he's boring. It's because there is very little left to argue about.

The bear case, such as it is. He has the lowest ceiling of the top four in the one place ceilings are usually made: he is not the scorer Dybantsa is and not the shot-creator Peterson is. His 0.6 blocks are a real hole for a 6-foot-9 forward, and if the three-point volume (3.6 attempts) doesn't scale, he tops out as a very good multi-category asset rather than a first-round overall dynasty piece. That's a ceiling question, not a floor one — there will be nights this season where two or three guards from this class outscore him by fifteen points, and that's fine as long as the floor never moves.

The read. He is priced at $1,000.00, the highest offer price on the board and 24% above the second listing. Dynatze slots him 12th in the entire dynasty player universe as a rookie. There is no scenario where this is a bad pick and several where it is the obvious one. Take him and stop thinking about it.
