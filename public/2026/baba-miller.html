SCOUTING REPORT

The physical profile is legitimately special. A "near 7-footer in shoes" with NBA-level athletic tools, Miller capped his college odyssey — Florida State to Florida Atlantic to Cincinnati — with a senior season of 13.0 points, 10.3 rebounds, 3.7 assists and 1.2 blocks on 52.9% shooting, earning All-Big 12 Second Team honors. The 3.7 assists from a player his size is a genuinely rare data point; scouts have compared his frame and mobility to Jonathan Isaac, and he tested exceptionally well at the combine after a strong final college season.

The Los Angeles Clippers took him at 36, betting on the tools translating with more strength and more consistent minutes than his winding college path allowed. Born in Mallorca and developed in Real Madrid's youth academy before ever reaching a U.S. campus, he has been a top-10-caliber international name on scouting radars since age 17 — the talent evaluation was never really in question.

The shooting is the genuine crisis in the profile. 19.2% from three on real volume, his worst mark since limited freshman minutes, means defenses simply ignore him beyond the arc — and NBA teams increasingly cannot play two non-shooters together. One evaluator put it bluntly: there's a real chance he's "not an NBA player" long-term if that doesn't improve. The frame is also still thin at 208 pounds for his height, another multi-year project.

The read. This is the single highest-variance profile in this group of seven. The rebounding, passing and defensive tools are drafted at the top of this class; the shooting and strength are drafted at the bottom of it. Buy this one only if you have the roster patience for a true swing.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 46th, 31st and 42nd in this class. Sporting News is the high mark at 31st and Yahoo the low at 46th.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 10.3 rebounds and 3.7 assists from a near-7-footer is an exceptionally rare statistical combination
  • Elite physical tools: 7'1.75" wingspan, strong combine testing, real defensive versatility
  • Finally put a full, winning season together as a senior after three years of inconsistency
  • International pedigree (Real Madrid academy) evaluators have tracked since age 17

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 19.2% from three is close to unplayable at NBA volume and defenses will exploit it
  • Four college programs in four years reflects a genuine consistency problem
  • Still underweight at 208 lbs for a 6'10.5" frame
  • At least one evaluator's honest assessment: real chance he never sticks in the league
