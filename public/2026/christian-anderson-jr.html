SCOUTING REPORT

Nobody in this class produced a fuller stat line. 18.5 points, 7.4 assists, 3.6 rebounds, 1.5 steals and 3.3 made threes in 38.4 minutes at Texas Tech, with 80.5% free throw shooting. Run that through the class-wide nine-category model and he grades second in the class — +3.69, behind only Cameron Boozer's +5.06 — so on pure category breadth he is at the very top. Assists, threes and steals are three of the four most valuable guard categories and he leads or nearly leads the class in all three.

Charlotte took him eighteenth, pairing him with Steinbach, and the Hornets' backcourt is not a wall. His Pathway score of 67 is strong for a late-first pick.

The costs are the whole bear case. 3.3 turnovers in a nine-category format is a full category running underwater every single week, and it is the category that is hardest to fix — high-usage creators rarely reduce turnovers meaningfully in the NBA, they usually increase them against better defenses. And 47.2% from the field at 38.4 minutes of usage is a below-median efficiency mark. Two of nine categories are structurally negative.

The read. At $84.15 he is the sixteenth listing on the board, and he is one of the largest disagreements on this board between the production model and the consensus rank. If you weight raw category output, he belongs in the top ten. If you weight efficiency and turnovers — which nine-cat leagues do — the sixteenth ranking is fair. This is the definitive "how does your league score it" pick.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 14th, 17th and 22nd in this class. Yahoo is the high mark at 14th and CBS the low at 22nd.
