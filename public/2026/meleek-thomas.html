SCOUTING REPORT

The relevant numbers: 15.6 points, 3.8 rebounds, 2.5 assists, 1.5 steals and 2.2 made threes in 30.5 minutes, on 43.5% from the field, 84.3% from the line, and 1.0 turnover. That turnover figure is the lowest of any high-usage guard on this entire board. Combine it with the steals, the threes and the free throw percentage and you have a player who is a clean positive in four categories and neutral in three more. He is 20.0 years old.

Cleveland acquired him at pick 34 and he proceeded to earn Summer League First Team honors — the only second-rounder in the class to do so. That is not a guarantee of anything, but it is the strongest possible signal available in July, and it came from a player the boards now rank twenty-eighth.

The field goal percentage is the honest problem. 43.5% for a guard is below median, and if it stays there he is a three-point-and-steals specialist rather than a starter. Cleveland's roster is also not a wide-open runway; his Pathway score of 10 is among the lowest on this board and Year-1 contribution should be assumed to be zero.

The read. At $27.93 he is the twenty-eighth listing on the board, and Dynatze's universe rank of 266 prices in almost nothing. Angel has him twenty-third, Sporting News twenty-second and Yahoo twenty-fourth — four to six spots above the composite — while CBS has him thirtieth. In a three-round rookie draft, a 20-year-old with the best turnover discipline on the board, real three-point volume, 1.5 steals and Summer League First Team honors is exactly the profile you want in round three.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 24th, 22nd and 30th in this class. Sporting News is the high mark at 22nd and CBS the low at 30th.
