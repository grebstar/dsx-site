SCOUTING REPORT

Jefferson's senior season at Iowa State was the most efficient all-around production line of any role player in this class: 16.4 points, 7.4 rebounds, 4.8 assists and 1.6 steals on 47.1% shooting, and the shooting split included a genuine improvement to 34.5% from three on real volume. He was the nation's only player to average that stat line at that efficiency, and he did it as a First-Team All-Big 12 selection and consensus second-team All-American — the first for Iowa State since Georges Niang in 2016.

The 4.8 assists are the actual headline. Scouts have called him one of the best passing forwards in the class, and the underlying numbers back it: a 1.88 assist-to-turnover ratio at that usage is genuinely rare for a frontcourt player. Brooklyn's own front office was explicit about the fit — the roster had accumulated scorers during its rebuild without anyone who elevates the players around him, and Jefferson was drafted specifically to be that connective piece. Multiple outlets called him one of the biggest potential steals of the back half of the first round.

The limiters are athleticism and age, and both are real. He tested poorly at the combine, shot only 39.2% on drives, and struggled to finish in ball-screen situations against NBA-level length. He'll turn 23 by November, which for a rebuilding team's asset is a real cost — there isn't much runway left to grow into a bigger role. His outside shot, while improved, is still a one-year sample after four years of inconsistency (31.5% for his career).

The read. Brooklyn is rebuilding around scorers and needed connectivity; Jefferson supplies exactly that, at pick 28, with a floor that looks like an immediate rotation piece. The ceiling isn't a star. The floor is a genuinely useful NBA role player who makes the four guys around him better, which on a young roster full of shot-hunters is worth more than the draft slot suggests.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 38th, 37th and 31st in this class. CBS is the high mark at 31st and Yahoo the low at 38th.
