SCOUTING REPORT

The per-minute profile is the most interesting thing on this section of the board. In 22.6 minutes, Graves produced 11.8 points, 6.5 rebounds, 1.9 steals, 0.9 blocks and 1.1 made threes on 51.2% shooting, with 0.7 turnovers. Extrapolate that to a starter's 32 minutes and you are looking at roughly 17 points, 9 rebounds, 2.7 steals and 1.3 blocks. Steals are the scarcest category in nine-cat basketball, and nobody in this class generates them at his rate.

The catch is that extrapolation is exactly the thing that goes wrong with low-minute prospects. Steal rates in particular are notoriously usage- and scheme-dependent, and a 22-minute role against Santa Clara's schedule is a different environment than an NBA rotation. The 75.0% free throw shooting is fine, and the low turnover count is genuine discipline, but the total production base is thin.

Toronto took him nineteenth and the honest read is that he is a long shot for Year-1 minutes — one injury away from a steady role on a decent team, which is exactly what post-draft coverage said about him. His Pathway score of 45 is among the lowest of any first-rounder on this board.

The read. At $43.38 he is the twenty-third listing on the board and the best pure-upside speculation in the late first. At 20.0 years old with a category profile built around the two things you cannot buy off the waiver wire — steals and low turnovers — he is the sort of stash that either does nothing for two years or suddenly becomes a top-120 asset when someone gets hurt. That is a real option, and options are worth owning at this price.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 22nd, 29th and 27th in this class. Yahoo is the high mark at 22nd and Sporting News the low at 29th.
