SCOUTING REPORT

The counting stats are the draw. 23.2 points, 3.6 rebounds, 3.6 assists, 1.6 steals and 2.0 made threes in 35.1 minutes, on 46.5% from the field and 83.2% from the line across 31 games at Stanford. Only Dybantsa and Acuff scored more in this class, and both are older. At 19.3 years old he is among the youngest players ranked inside the top twenty on this composite and among the four youngest on the entire board.

The nine-cat shape is better than the raw scoring suggests. 1.6 steals is the best mark among guards in the top twenty, the free throw percentage is a genuine positive, and 1.9 turnovers on 35 minutes of high-usage offense is disciplined. His composite score of +1.84 ranks seventeenth in the class.

Detroit moving up for him is the part the boards haven't priced. Oklahoma City originally held 17 and the Pistons traded up to take him. The public composite now has him seventeenth, and the boards split on him: Angel, Dizzle and Roto all have him thirteenth, while Yahoo has him sixteenth, Sporting News twenty-fifth and CBS twenty-sixth in this class. That thirteen-spot bid-ask spread is tied for the second widest in the top twenty and reflects a market still sorting out what the landing spot means.

The bear case is the shooting and the depth chart. 46.5% from the field is fine rather than good for a guard at his usage, and Detroit is not a rebuilding team — the Pistons have real players and post-draft analysis was direct that he is a long shot for meaningful Year-1 minutes, only one injury away from steady playing time. In dynasty that is a patience cost, not a disqualifier.

The read. At $75.99 he is one of the best age-adjusted values on the board. Twenty-three points a game at 19 years old with steals and free throw percentage attached is a profile that usually goes in the top eight. He went 17th because of shooting questions and defensive projection, not because of production.

The Yahoo, Sporting News and CBS boards. Yahoo's Kevin O'Connor, Sporting News's Elliott Pohnl and CBS's Adam Finkelstein have him 16th, 25th and 26th in this class. Yahoo is the high mark at 16th and CBS the low at 26th.
