SCOUTING REPORT

Sarr made the ACC All-Defensive Team as a true freshman — real, external recognition for a multi-positional defender — while producing modestly on offense at 6.4 points, 3.8 rebounds and 1.1 assists on 40.4% shooting (32.3% from three, a rough 53.2% from the line). He came to Duke directly from a professional stint in Spain with Barcelona, where he reportedly shot closer to 43% from three, so the college dip is a real adjustment-period story rather than a skill ceiling.

He chose to return to Duke for a sophomore year rather than declare, specifically to rebuild his stock from what had become a second-round grade — a notable, explicit decision that says something about how he and his camp read this season.

▲ BULL CASE

  • ACC All-Defensive Team as a true freshman — legitimate external recognition for multi-positional defense
  • Shot roughly 43% from three during his professional stint in Spain, suggesting the college dip is an adjustment issue, not a ceiling
  • Season-high 19-point, 3-steal game against Army West Point shows the offensive ceiling in flashes
  • Chose to return to Duke specifically to rebuild stock — a deliberate, self-aware development plan

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 40.4% FG / 32.3% 3P / 53.2% FT as a full-season starter is genuinely rough efficiency, whatever the explanation
  • Free-throw shooting in particular (53.2%) is a real red flag regardless of the sample
  • The gap between his reported Spain shooting numbers and his Duke numbers is large enough to need in-season resolution, not just explanation
