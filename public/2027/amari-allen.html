SCOUTING REPORT

Allen made the SEC All-Freshman Team and was named SEC Freshman of the Week three times, including after a strong showing at the Players Era Festival, on 11.4 points, 6.9 rebounds, 3.1 assists, 1.0 steal and 0.7 blocks on 44.6% shooting (34.1% from three, 73.8% from the line) across 32 games. A 6-8 frame producing nearly seven boards and three assists a game as a true freshman is a genuine forward/wing-hybrid versatility signal.

He briefly left the SEC opener against Kentucky with an apparent ankle/leg issue but returned the same game and finished with 11 points, 9 rebounds and 3 assists — worth noting as a recurring minor-durability thread rather than a missed-time injury.

▲ BULL CASE

  • SEC All-Freshman Team, 3x SEC Freshman of the Week as a true freshman
  • 6.9 rebounds and 3.1 assists a game at 6-8 — real positional versatility, not just scoring
  • Strong Players Era Festival showing against high-level competition
  • IMG Academy pedigree entering a featured sophomore role

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Showed up on multiple in-season availability/injury-report mentions, even without missing games outright
  • Modest scoring average (11.4) for a player this highly recruited
