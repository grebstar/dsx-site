SCOUTING REPORT

Krivas missed nearly all of 2024-25 after foot surgery — 8 games played before he was ruled out for the year — and returned for a full, healthy 39-game 2025-26 season that earned him All-Big 12 First Team and All-Big 12 Defensive Team honors: 10.4 points, 8.2 rebounds, 1.9 blocks and 0.7 steals on 56.9% shooting. The growth curve is real — 4.2 rebounds and 0.5 blocks as a freshman to 8.2 and 1.9 as a healthy junior — and he went from shooting 0.0% from three in each of his first two seasons to a credible 30.8% this year.

At 7-2/260, he's the biggest true center in this composite. RealGM's own class labeling calls this his third college season ("junior") given the lost year, which puts his eligibility timeline a year behind his listed class.

▲ BULL CASE

  • All-Big 12 First Team and All-Big 12 All-Defensive Team after a full healthy season

  • Real statistical growth year over year, including a 30.8% three-point clip after shooting 0% in each of his first two seasons
