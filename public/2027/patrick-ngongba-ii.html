SCOUTING REPORT

Ngongba posted 10.1 points, 5.8 rebounds, 2.0 assists, 1.1 blocks and 0.6 steals on an efficient 60.6% shooting across 32 games — his second college season, not his first, despite some sourcing elsewhere labeling it a freshman year. Babcock Hoops praises his soft hands and polished post footwork, strong finishing through contact, and real passing for a big man, while flagging limited lateral mobility and perimeter defense as the projection concern.

He missed 5 games late in the season, including a questionable/out designation for Duke's NCAA Tournament openers — a durability flag worth tracking as his role grows.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 10.1/5.8/2.0 on 60.6% shooting — efficient, well-rounded production for a young big man
  • Soft hands and polished post footwork specifically praised by scouts
  • Real passing instincts for a center — 2.0 assists a game is above-average for the position
  • Second full college season already banked, more experience than most of this class

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Missed 5 games late in the year, including NCAA Tournament openers — a documented durability flag
  • Limited lateral mobility projects as a below-average space defender at higher levels
  • 25.8% from three on low volume means he's not a floor-spacing threat yet
