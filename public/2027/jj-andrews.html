SCOUTING REPORT

Andrews was named 2025-26 MaxPreps Arkansas High School Basketball Player of the Year on 30.7 points, 16.9 rebounds, 3.1 assists and 2.3 steals on 60.9% shooting — 19 double-doubles in the 22 games he played, with a career-high 45 against Marion. He finished 5th on the Arkansas all-time state career scoring list at 2,865 points and is a four-time all-state selection.

The 22-games figure matters: he missed 9 games to an in-season injury (the specific type wasn't published in available reporting), which is the honest caveat on an otherwise complete statistical profile. His coach's scouting report praises his size and four-position versatility and points to real three-point improvement (50% in a Nike EYBL session) while flagging ball-handling and shot creation as the next developmental step. He signed with Arkansas.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 30.7/16.9/3.1/2.3 with 19 double-doubles in 22 games — elite two-way production whenever he was on the floor
  • 5th on Arkansas's all-time state career scoring list at 2,865 points; 4x all-state
  • Coach's report cites real shooting improvement (50% from three in a Nike EYBL session) layered onto elite rebounding
  • Can defend and play four positions at 6-6/6-7, 220 lbs, left-handed

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Missed 9 games to an in-season injury — durability is a genuine open question entering a bigger college role
  • Ball-handling and shot creation are the explicitly flagged developmental gaps by his own high school coach
  • 16.9 rebounds a game as a wing is partly a product of a lopsided HS schedule and won't translate directly
