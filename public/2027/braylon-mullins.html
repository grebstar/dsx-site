SCOUTING REPORT

Mullins was UConn's headline recruit in his class and delivered a Big East All-Freshman Team season at 12.0 points, 3.5 rebounds, 1.4 assists and 1.0 steals on 42.1% shooting (33.5% from three, 88.9% from the line) — three-time Big East Freshman of the Week, despite missing over a month with an ankle injury suffered in mid-October. He returned under a 15-minute restriction and worked his way back into a full role.

The efficiency profile is the interesting tension entering his sophomore year: 88.9% from the line is elite touch, but the 42.1% overall clip on modest volume suggests shot-selection and role questions that a bigger sophomore usage rate will start to answer one way or the other.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Big East All-Freshman Team and 3x Freshman of the Week despite missing over a month
  • 88.9% from the free-throw line — elite touch/shooting indicator even while the overall clip lags
  • UConn's headline recruit in his class, now stepping into a larger role as a sophomore
  • Already has a full freshman season of high-major tape to evaluate, unlike most of this class

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 42.1% FG / 33.5% 3P efficiency is modest for the shot volume — a real swing skill to watch as usage rises
  • Ankle injury cost him over a month as a freshman — some durability history on the record
  • Role/shot-selection questions are explicit, not implied, in how the season is being read
