SCOUTING REPORT

Oladotun's senior-season box score (3.0/1.1/0.3 across just 8 games at Blake HS) badly understates what's actually going on: an ankle injury sidelined him indefinitely starting in mid-November, and his signing with Maryland was already locked in before that injury hit, so it had no bearing on his recruitment. The more representative data point is his 2025 EYBL grassroots line — 13.2 points, 5.8 rebounds, 1.2 steals on 47.5% shooting — from the spring before his senior year.

He skipped a grade entirely, reclassifying from the true 2027 class up to 2026 in August 2025, and was ranked #1 nationally in that original 2027 class before the move. Maryland head coach Buzz Williams: "his length, athleticism, and high basketball IQ make him one of the top players in the country."

▲ BULL CASE

  • Was the #1-ranked player in the true 2027 class before reclassifying up a full year — an extraordinary underlying pedigree
  • 2025 EYBL line (13.2/5.8/1.2 on 47.5% shooting) is a much stronger and more representative data point than his injury-limited senior season
  • Maryland's own head coach specifically cites his length, athleticism and IQ
  • Versatile forward who can handle, shoot over defenders and attack the mid-post per scouting notes

▼ BEAR CASE

  • The senior-season box score (8 games, 3.0 PPG) is real but is not a meaningful read on his talent — it's an injury-shortened sample, not a decline
  • Upright playing posture at times is explicitly flagged as affecting his efficiency
  • Two different 247Sports-branded articles disagree on his exact national/position ranking — an unresolved data conflict
