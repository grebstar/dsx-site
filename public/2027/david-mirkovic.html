SCOUTING REPORT

Mirkovic made the All-Big Ten Freshman Team and All-Big Ten Honorable Mention at 13.3 points, 8.0 rebounds and 2.6 assists on 48.4% shooting (37.5% from three) across 37 games — the 2nd-most rebounds by an Illini freshman ever (243 entering the postseason). He came up through SC Derby in Montenegro's domestic ABA league before making his senior national-team debut in 2024, and has spoken directly about adjusting from a slower-paced, resource-limited European league to NCAA speed and physicality.

Multiple sources in this research pass identify his nationality as Montenegrin, which is worth reconciling against any earlier sourcing that had listed Serbia.
