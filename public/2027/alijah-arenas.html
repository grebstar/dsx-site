SCOUTING REPORT

Arenas's 2025-26 has to be read through what happened before it: in April 2025 he crashed his Tesla Cybertruck into a fire hydrant, and smoke inhalation was severe enough that he was placed in a medically induced coma. That summer, during recovery-phase training, he suffered a separate meniscus injury requiring surgery. He debuted for USC on January 22, 2026 against Northwestern — under nine months after the crash — and finished 14 games at 14.1 points, 2.9 rebounds, 2.1 assists, 0.9 steals and 0.5 blocks on 34.1% shooting (21.3% from three, 79.2% from the line). USC coach Eric Musselman noted afterward that Arenas was effectively thrown into Big Ten play after missing an entire offseason.

He's the son of former NBA All-Star Gilbert Arenas — background, not a stat. The shooting efficiency reads as rust and a compressed development runway rather than a settled skill level, which is exactly why this is the highest-variance evaluation in the composite.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Made it back to college competition at all under nine months after a medically induced coma is itself a remarkable outcome
  • 14.1 points a game as a true freshman with almost no real practice runway or offseason development time
  • NBA bloodline (son of Gilbert Arenas) with real shot-creation pedigree
  • The compressed sample size means there is significant unrealized development still ahead

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 34.1% FG / 21.3% 3P efficiency reads as rust from a missed offseason, not a finished skill level
  • Only 14 games of college tape exist — the smallest verified college sample in the top tier of this class
  • Recovery timeline (coma, then a separate meniscus surgery) is a real and documented medical history, not a minor footnote
