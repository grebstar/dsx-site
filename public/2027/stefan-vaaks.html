SCOUTING REPORT

Vaaks's true freshman season was played at Providence, not Illinois — he's transferring in for 2026-27 after entering the portal — where he made the All-Big East Freshman Team and led the conference with 91 made threes (2.9 a game) at 15.8 points, 2.5 rebounds and 3.2 assists on 40.3% shooting overall, 35.0% from three, 83.9% from the line across 31 games. He scored in double figures in 29 of those 31 games and was named Big East Freshman of the Week during the season.

High-volume shot-making (8.4 three-point attempts a game, top-30 nationally) is the clear carrying skill; the accuracy on that volume (35.0%) is the efficiency question that comes with it.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Led the entire Big East in made threes as a true freshman — elite, proven shot-making volume
  • All-Big East Freshman Team; scored in double figures in 29 of 31 games
  • 3.2 assists a game shows real secondary-playmaking value beyond pure scoring
  • Estonian national-team pedigree with real international experience already

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 35.0% on that high volume is good, not great — the efficiency-to-volume ratio is the specific swing skill
  • Thin rebounding and defensive box-score numbers (2.5 REB, 0.8 STL) for a wing his size
  • Transferring programs after one season (Providence to Illinois) is a fit/continuity question
