SCOUTING REPORT

Coleman closed a back-to-back Missouri Class 3 state championship run with a 50-point title-game performance (18-of-21 from the field, 5-of-7 from three, 8 rebounds, 7 steals) and finished his senior year at 23.1 points, 6.1 rebounds, 3.9 assists and 2.7 steals on a 64.9/50.6/89.7 shooting split — elite efficiency across the board. He finished his career with 1,639 points at Principia.

He rose to five-star status in the final rankings update of the cycle, and his recruitment had a real twist: he was previously committed to Wake Forest, was released from that letter of intent, and signed with Illinois instead.
