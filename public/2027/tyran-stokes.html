SCOUTING REPORT

Stokes has been the name at the top of this class for two years running, and the senior-year line — 31.0 points, 13.0 rebounds, 6.0 assists and 4.0 steals on 53% shooting for a 29-1 Rainier Beach team — is exactly the do-everything production a unanimous #1 is supposed to post. Eleven of the 14 qualifying boards on this composite have him first, and none has him lower than fifth.

The one real wrinkle is off the court, not on it. He withdrew from Notre Dame (Sherman Oaks, CA) in early November after a disciplinary issue and finished the season at Rainier Beach in Seattle — a mid-senior-year program change that is worth knowing about even though it did nothing to his on-court output (a 63-point senior night is still a 63-point senior night). He signed with Kansas.

▲ BULL CASE

  • First on 11 of the 14 qualifying boards and inside the top five on all of them
  • 31.0/13.0/6.0/4.0 as a senior, the exact translatable production profile teams look for at the top of a draft
  • Scored a career-high 63 points on senior night; debuted at Rainier Beach with 31 in a blowout win
  • Every physical/handle/passing tool a modern wing needs is already there at 6-8

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Mid-senior-year transfer stemmed from a disciplinary withdrawal — a genuine makeup/maturity question a #1 pick doesn't usually carry
  • Two programs in one senior season complicates a clean read on system fit and coaching continuity
  • Consensus #1 status also means there is nowhere to go but down if Kansas doesn't showcase him the way HS did
