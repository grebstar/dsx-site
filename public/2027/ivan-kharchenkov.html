SCOUTING REPORT

Kharchenkov posted 10.4 points, 4.3 rebounds, 2.3 assists and 1.4 steals on 49.2% shooting (31.7% from three, 72.3% from the line) across 39 games as part of Arizona's Final Four run — but "freshman" undersells his readiness, per a Yahoo Sports feature explicitly making that point: he played EuroLeague for Bayern Munich starting at 16 under Pablo Laso, Luka Dončić's former Real Madrid mentor, who told Arizona coach Tommy Lloyd "you are going to be blown away how ready he is on day one." He backed it up with a 12-point, 10-rebound double-double in his college debut against Florida.

His nationality is a genuine, documented dual situation rather than a source error: born in Russia, raised in Germany from a toddler, with RealGM listing both.
