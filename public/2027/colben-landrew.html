SCOUTING REPORT

Landrew was named 2025-26 MaxPreps Georgia High School Basketball Player of the Year after averaging 24.9 points, 6.8 rebounds and 3.2 assists on 54.1% shooting (80.3% from the line), closing with a 29-point, 12-rebound title-game performance as Wheeler HS won back-to-back Class 6A championships (27-6 overall). 247Sports' Adam Finkelstein calls him a "big-bodied wing with developing guard skills" — a 6-10 wingspan, soft catch-and-shoot touch (46% on open threes cited) and strong offensive rebounding and rim finishing (65%) stand out.

He also played football at his previous school (Thompson, AL) before transferring to Wheeler, and his coach specifically credits that background for his physicality: "an alpha on the court."
