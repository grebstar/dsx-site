SCOUTING REPORT

Ekezie reclassified from 2027 to 2026 in May and committed to Louisville, so his last completed season was his 2025-26 junior year at Southeastern Prep in Orlando. Louisville's official bio credits him with 12.4 points, 7.8 rebounds and 1.8 blocks on about 60% shooting. Outlets disagree on the scoring: On3 lists 17.8 ppg, The Louisville Cardinal 17.1, and Hoops HQ cited 11.9 at midseason via MaxPreps.

His father, Obinna Ekezie, was drafted by the Vancouver Grizzlies in 1999. Louisville now lists him at 7-2, 240, up from 7-0 and 215-220 when he committed. Coming back from shoulder surgery, he posted 16 points, 8 rebounds, 5 blocks and 4-of-6 from three in an exhibition win at Kansas. In the Nov. 2 opener he played just 9 minutes and scored 5 points.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Five-star who reclassified up a year; was the No. 1 center in the 2027 recruiting class
  • Louisville lists him at 7-2, 240, about 20-25 lbs heavier than his recruiting listings when he committed
  • Can stretch the floor: 4-of-6 from three with 16 points and 5 blocks in an 89-83 exhibition win at No. 17 Kansas
  • Protects the rim: 1.8 blocks per game as a prep junior, plus 4 blocks against Mercyhurst

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Played only 9 minutes in the opener, 2 of them in the second half, which Pat Kelsey called a 'coach's decision'
  • Coming off shoulder surgery that kept him out of full basketball activity for about six months
  • Skipped his senior prep season, and reports of his junior-year scoring range from 11.9 to 17.8 ppg
