SCOUTING REPORT

Muurinen's senior year did not look like most of this class's: rather than a final US high-school season, he spent it in a limited role for KK Partizan in Serbia's Liga ABA (8 games, 4.1 points, 2.9 rebounds, 41.2% shooting) and logged just 11 total minutes across six EuroLeague appearances, reportedly amid a coaching conflict. Before that move he played his junior year (2024-25) at AZ Compass Prep in the US, and represented Finland's senior national team at 2025 EuroBasket, shooting roughly 35% from three there.

247Sports/ESPN scouting notes describe a long, mobile stretch-four with a soft shooting touch and good transition ability, while flagging a historically inconsistent jumper and unproven post defense — concerns the thin Partizan role did nothing to resolve either way.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Long, mobile stretch-four archetype with a genuinely soft shooting touch per scouting reports
  • Represented Finland's senior national team at 2025 EuroBasket while still a teenager
  • Both parents played college/professional basketball — real basketball pedigree
  • Signed with Arkansas, giving him a clean, featured role to reset his developmental track

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Historically inconsistent jump shot and unproven post defense, per scouting reports predating the Partizan stint
  • Height/weight varies meaningfully across sources (6-10/205 to 7-0/223)
