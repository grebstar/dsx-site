SCOUTING REPORT

Branch reclassified from the 2027 class into 2026 last November and still closed his final prep year at 18.0 points, 10.0 rebounds, 4.0 assists and 2.0 blocks for Prolific Prep, per BYU's own signing release. 247Sports' Adam Finkelstein describes him as "a big wing who is long and skilled with developing guard skills" — a 7-1 wingspan on a 6-7 frame is the standout physical measurable, and it's why Kentucky and Duke were both in his recruitment before he chose BYU.

A McDonald's All-American, he's still very young for his grade — 247's own scouting note flags that he won't turn 18 until late October of his freshman college year.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 18/10/4/2 across the board as a reclassified underclassman playing up an age group
  • 7-1 wingspan on a 6-7 frame — elite length for a perimeter player
  • Drew Kentucky and Duke interest before choosing BYU
  • 247's Finkelstein specifically praises developing guard skills in a wing-sized frame

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Full shooting splits and games-played were not published alongside the counting-stat line — the sample is thinner than the headline numbers suggest
  • Very young for his class (turns 18 in October of his freshman year) — physical development is still ahead of him, not behind him
  • Pre-reclassification ranking within the true 2027 class is disputed between sources (#1 vs #2) — recorded, not resolved
