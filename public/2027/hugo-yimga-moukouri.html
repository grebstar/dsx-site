SCOUTING REPORT

Yimga-Moukouri plays for Nanterre 92 in Betclic Élite — France's top professional division, not the second-tier Pro B league some earlier sourcing had attached to him. In 2025-26 he posted 9.2 points, 4.1 rebounds on a genuinely elite two-way shooting split — 49.3% from the field, 41.8% from three, 84.6% from the line — across 35 games, per RealGM; Proballers' slightly different game-log cutoff shows a close 8.9/3.8 line on a similar split. He won a "Rising Star" award for the season and has represented France at multiple FIBA youth championships.

The role is still a complementary one — 0.3 assists a game reflects low offensive usage at 18 years old — but shooting that cleanly as a teenager in a top European pro league, rather than a youth or development circuit, is a real and rare signal.
