SCOUTING REPORT

Smith was named the 2025-26 MaxPreps National Player of the Year — the sport's single highest individual high-school honor — after averaging 26.6 points, 6.3 rebounds, 5.6 assists and 3.2 steals on 56.0% shooting (36.9% from three, 72.4% from the line) for a Paul VI Catholic team that went 33-3 and beat 14 of 15 nationally-ranked opponents it faced. 247Sports' Adam Finkelstein singled out his two-way competitive impact and intangibles as the defining trait scouts keep coming back to.

He signed with Arkansas in February. Twelve of the 14 qualifying boards have him inside the top five overall.

▲ BULL CASE

  • MaxPreps National Player of the Year — the sport's top individual HS honor
  • 26.6/6.3/5.6/3.2 with a 56/37/72 shooting split, a genuinely complete statistical profile
  • Went 14-1 against nationally-ranked competition, the toughest schedule of anyone in this tier
  • 247Sports' national director of scouting cites elite two-way intangibles specifically

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Listed height varies 6-2 to 6-3 across sources — undersized for a lead-guard role if the growth has stalled
  • Arkansas' backcourt usage plan for him is still unproven
  • GP not published in the available MaxPreps recap — full shooting-volume context is thinner than the headline stats suggest
