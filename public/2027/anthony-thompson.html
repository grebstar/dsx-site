SCOUTING REPORT

Thompson posted 24.9 points, 7.9 rebounds and 2.3 assists on 47% shooting (35% from three) in his senior year at Western Reserve Academy, after transferring there from Lebanon, Ohio. Ohio State's own program has called him its highest-rated commit since Jared Sullinger in 2010 — a real marker for the program, even if it's their own framing.

He also played up with the Team USA U18 camp and on the Adidas 3SSB circuit with Indiana Elite, where he posted 22.1 points a game to go with 1.9 blocks and 1.6 steals — club numbers, not high-school-season numbers, but a useful second data point on his two-way tools.
