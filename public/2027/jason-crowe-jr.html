SCOUTING REPORT

Crowe closed his high school career with 4,718 total points, described by MaxPreps as a California all-time-caliber scorer, and averaged an extraordinary 43.6 points, 4.4 rebounds, 5.9 assists, 3.6 steals and 0.8 blocks across 31 games as a senior. He set the Hoophall Classic scoring record and is a McDonald's All-American. Shooting splits were not published alongside the counting line — a single Nike Hoop Summit box score is not representative of a season and isn't treated as one here.

A left-handed shot-maker, 247Sports' Adam Finkelstein calls him "one of the most prolific scorers in high school basketball," citing exceptional ball-handling and deep shooting range while flagging physical strength and defensive consistency as the areas to add at the next level. He signed with Missouri.
