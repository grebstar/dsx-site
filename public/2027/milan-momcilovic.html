SCOUTING REPORT

Momčilović played his junior season, 2025-26, at Iowa State, not Kentucky. He transferred to Lexington for 2026-27 as a senior after withdrawing from the 2026 NBA Draft before the May 27 deadline. For the Cyclones he averaged 16.9 points in 37 starts, shooting 50.6% from the field, 48.7% from three and 87.8% at the line. His 136 made threes set Iowa State's single-season record.

The 6-foot-9, 220-pound forward was ESPN's No. 1 player in the portal, and CBS reported he went back to school even with a second-round promise on the table. In three Kentucky exhibitions he averaged 16.7 points and 5.7 rebounds. For NBA teams the question is everything besides the jumper: last season he averaged 1.0 assist, 0.8 steals and 0.2 blocks a game.
