SCOUTING REPORT

Harris's breakout sophomore season came at Wake Forest, not Tennessee. He transferred to Knoxville for 2026-27 after scoring 21.4 points a game in 35.1 minutes, up from 6.1 points as a freshman, with 6.5 rebounds and 1.3 steals. He shot .444/.332/.783, made 87 threes, and won ACC Most Improved Player plus Second Team All-ACC honors.

He declared for the 2026 draft in April and was invited to the combine, then withdrew on May 5, a day after committing to the Vols. SEC coaches put him on the preseason All-SEC second team. A back issue kept him out of an October scrimmage against Davidson, but he was back at practice by Oct. 29.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Proven high-volume scorer: 21.4 ppg, starting all 35 games for Wake Forest in 2025-26
  • 6-7, 200-lb frame with positional size on the wing; SI credits him with scoring at all three levels
  • Rebounds well for a wing: 6.5 rpg, with a 15-rebound career high vs. California
  • Huge year-over-year jump (6.1 to 21.4 ppg) and gets to the line: 195-of-249 FT (78.3%)

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Inconsistent outside shot: 33.2% from three on 262 attempts
  • Defense is the swing skill, per SI, and Rick Barnes has publicly asked for more rebounding effort
  • Little playmaking so far (1.9 apg), and he joins a Tennessee roster with nine newcomers
