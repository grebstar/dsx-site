SCOUTING REPORT

Tanner averaged 19.5 points, 3.6 rebounds, 5.1 assists and 2.4 steals on 48.5% shooting (36.8% from three, 85.3% from the line) across 36 games — his steal rate led the SEC, built on instincts and positioning at a genuinely undersized 6-0. His three-point percentage jumped from 26.6% as a freshman to 36.8% as a sophomore, a real and meaningful improvement, though the size of that jump raises its own sustainability question.

He's an elite finisher at the rim (66%, 16 dunks on the season) despite his height, and a scouting report compares his profile to a Tre Jones type. His signature win was a career-high 26 points against SMU in the ACC/SEC Challenge.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Led the SEC in steals (2.4/game) through instincts and positioning, not just size
  • 19.5/5.1 with 66% shooting at the rim despite being generously listed at 6-0
  • Real, verified improvement from 26.6% to 36.8% from three year over year
  • Career-high 26-point game against SMU in a marquee non-conference matchup

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Undersized at 6-0 — a real NBA-translation question at either guard spot
  • Scouting reports flag shot selection: he sometimes 'dribbles into worse shots' rather than taking an open three
  • The 3-point improvement is a single-year jump — worth watching for sustainability, not assuming
