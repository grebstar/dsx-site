SCOUTING REPORT

Chinyelu declared for the 2026 NBA Draft on April 20 while keeping his college eligibility, then withdrew and announced on May 21 that he would return to Florida for his senior season. The Nigerian center transferred from Washington State and started for the Gators' 2025 national title team. In 2025-26 he averaged 10.9 points and 11.2 rebounds on 58.4% shooting across 35 games, and set school single-season records with 19 double-doubles and 137 offensive rebounds.

He won Naismith and NABC National Defensive Player of the Year, the first Gator to win either award, and ESPN ranks him No. 15 in its 2026-27 preseason top 100. The open questions are on offense: no made threes and a 69.5% free-throw rate. He opened the season with 6 points, 14 rebounds and 3 blocks before fouling out of a 77-67 win over No. 14 Miami.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2025-26 Naismith and NABC National Defensive Player of the Year (the first Gator to win either) plus SEC Defensive Player of the Year
  • 11.2 rpg, with school records of 137 offensive rebounds and 19 double-doubles; ESPN says he led the nation in rebound percentage
  • Efficient interior finisher: at least 58.4% from the field in all three college seasons
  • Started all 75 games of his two Florida seasons and won the 2025 NCAA title

▼ BEAR CASE

  • No perimeter shooting: no made threes in any of his three college seasons
  • Limited scoring: 10.9 ppg as a junior and 6 points in the 2026-27 opener
  • Foul trouble and free throws: fouled out of the opener, 0.7 assists a game and 69.5% FT (after .489 and .582 in earlier seasons)
