SCOUTING REPORT

Goosby transferred to Dynamic Prep for his senior year and logged 16.2 points, 4.8 rebounds, 2.9 assists, 1.5 steals and 0.5 blocks over just 13 games — a mid-year-transfer sample, not a full season. 247Sports' Adam Finkelstein calls him an elite passer with a dominant-hand finishing package and real athleticism, but flags inconsistent outside shooting as the clear college-level swing skill.

A 6-9 wingspan on a 6-5 frame gives him extra defensive range. He chose Texas over Duke, BYU and Baylor.
