SCOUTING REPORT

Gaskins nearly doubled his production from junior to senior year — 13.6 to 22.5 points, with 9.1 rebounds, 2.9 assists, 1.0 steal and 0.8 blocks across 31 games — and was the first five-star signee of Miami head coach Jai Lucas's tenure. 247Sports' Adam Finkelstein calls him a "long, strong, and physical four-man who defends, rebounds," with real mid-range scoring; a 7-1 wingspan stands out on a 6-8/6-9 frame.

He's also notably young for his class — he doesn't turn 18 until August, after graduation — which cuts both ways as a developmental data point. Three-point consistency (28% in grassroots EYBL play, not the HS season) and further offensive creation are the flagged areas to add.
