SCOUTING REPORT

Moreno made the SEC All-Freshman Team at 7.8 points, 6.3 rebounds, 1.8 assists and 1.5 blocks on 58.2% shooting across 36 games, and briefly declared for the 2026 NBA Draft before withdrawing to return to Kentucky — confirming him as a genuine returning sophomore rather than a draft-and-stay case. Babcock Hoops credits his rim protection and mobility for a 7-footer, with flashes of high-post passing, while flagging that he still lacks functional strength and has no perimeter game yet.

He dealt with minor leg soreness ahead of the NCAA Tournament and a brief injury exit from a Team USA event in June 2025, with no extended absence in either case.

▲ BULL CASE

  • SEC All-Freshman Team production at 7.8/6.3/1.8/1.5 blocks as a true freshman
  • Genuine mobility for a 7-footer, plus flashes of real high-post passing
  • Declared for the 2026 draft, gathered real feedback, and made the deliberate choice to return and develop further
  • 58.2% shooting shows efficient, high-value finishing within his current role

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Still lacks functional strength for physical post play, per his own scouting report
  • No perimeter game yet — floor-spacing projection is entirely open
  • Modest shot creation/vertical pop limits his offensive role for now
