SCOUTING REPORT

Joksimovic is the most unusual data case on this board: a genuine professional at Baskonia in Spain's ACB, but a fringe rotation piece whose per-game line depends entirely on which competitions get counted. Across all competitions (RealGM), he's at 4.3 points, 1.7 rebounds, 0.9 assists on 38.6% shooting over 26 games; in ACB-only minutes (Proballers) that drops to 1.9 points in 15 games, and in his five EuroLeague appearances he played under five minutes a night. None of those numbers is wrong — they're three different competitions, and none of them is a full-season read on a rotation player.

What the film and the scouting reports agree on is more useful than the box score: Babcock Hoops calls him a "fluid, fast, explosive athlete who plays above the rim" with a full ball-handling, passing and shooting package at 6-6/6-7, left-handed, able to defend 1 through 3. ESPN's summer scouting notes name him alongside Stokes as a top follow specifically for the 2027 cycle. He needs strength for ACB physicality, which is exactly why his minutes are still limited.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Already a rotation piece in Spain's ACB at 17 — playing professional minutes against grown men before most of this class starts college
  • Scouts describe an unusually complete offensive package for a teenager: ball-handling, passing and shooting, left-handed, 1-3 positional flexibility
  • Named alongside Stokes on ESPN's dedicated 2027 follow-list
  • Multiple qualifying boards (11 of 14) rank him in the top 10 overall despite the light minutes

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Extremely limited playing time at every level of Baskonia's program — this is projection off practice reputation and spot minutes, not a real season sample
  • Needs to add strength before he can hold up physically in ACB rotation minutes, let alone the NBA
  • Nationality is consistently reported as Slovenia everywhere, but he was born in Belgrade, Serbia, where his father played professionally — worth knowing as background, not a live dispute
