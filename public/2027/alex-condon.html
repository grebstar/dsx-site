SCOUTING REPORT

Condon declared for the 2026 NBA Draft, withdrew, and announced a return to Florida for a senior season, nearly doubling his scoring from his sophomore year while adding real playmaking: 15.1 points, 7.5 rebounds, 3.6 assists and 1.4 blocks on 55.6% shooting across 34 games. Babcock Hoops highlights advanced post footwork and touch, an elite rebounding motor, and a surprisingly good frontcourt passing hub at 6-11 — genuinely unusual playmaking numbers for a big man his size.

The three-point shot (17.0% on limited attempts) is the specific, explicitly flagged efficiency gap that caps his floor-spacing upside for now.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 3.6 assists a game at 6-11 is genuinely rare frontcourt playmaking production
  • Nearly doubled his scoring average year over year while adding rebounding and passing, not just volume
  • Elite rebounding motor and advanced post footwork/touch per scouting reports

▼ BEAR CASE
