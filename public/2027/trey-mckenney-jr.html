SCOUTING REPORT

McKenney made the All-Big Ten Freshman Team at 9.9 points, 2.8 rebounds and 0.9 assists on 46.2% shooting (39.1% from three, 89.5% from the line) across 40 games — modest counting numbers for a five-star, McDonald's All-American, two-time Michigan Gatorade Player of the Year, but a genuinely efficient shooting profile in a limited role. A Wolverine Report piece specifically praised him for playing "like a walk-on" despite the pedigree — high motor, low ego, in a deep rotation.

He won Team USA gold at the 2024 U18 AmeriCup and closed the year with a 9-point, 8-rebound game against UConn in April.
