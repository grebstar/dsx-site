SCOUTING REPORT

Able's true freshman season was played at NC State, not North Carolina — he's transferring in for 2026-27 — posting 9.0 points, 3.5 rebounds, 1.0 assists and 1.2 steals on 41.7% shooting (35.5% from three, 82.0% from the line) across 33 games. Early-season struggles under then-coach Will Wade limited his minutes, and per SI his draft stock faded as a subsequent coaching change favored other guards for the backcourt mold; his season-high 19-point game (five threes) came, fittingly, against his new team, North Carolina.

Pre-season scouting called him "a well-built wing with broad shoulders," a strong catch-and-shoot shooter with an "excellent left hand" and a competitive defender — the tools were never the question, the role and coaching fit were.
