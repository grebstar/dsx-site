Richmond made the SEC All-Defensive Team at 11.2 points, 4.3 rebounds, 2.0 assists, 1.1 steals and 0.8 blocks on an efficient 56.3% shooting across 37 games, and declared for the 2026 NBA Draft before withdrawing to confirm his return to Arkansas for a junior season. A film-study scouting report describes elite athleticism ("bunny-like hops"), efficient downhill driving and a 91% finishing rate on dunk attempts, paired with real defensive activity on both ends.

The same report identifies his central swing skill plainly: he plays "a step too fast," which produces self-inflicted turnovers and rushed possessions alongside the highlight plays.
