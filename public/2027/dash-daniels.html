SCOUTING REPORT

Daniels, the younger brother of Hawks guard Dyson Daniels, is an NBL Next Star who joined South East Melbourne Phoenix for NBL27 after he and Melbourne United agreed to part ways. With United in 2025-26 he averaged 4.1 points, 2.4 rebounds and 0.9 assists in 14.3 minutes across 35 games (11 starts), shooting 41.6% FG and 36.0% from three (18-50) but only 37.9% FT (11-29). In April 2026 he chose not to enter the 2026 draft.

His NBL27 start has been far stronger: 24 points and 12 rebounds in his Phoenix debut, 16-8-6 against Illawarra and 18 points (3-7 3PT) in 31 minutes against Adelaide. His listings conflict: the NBL and the Phoenix list him at 196 cm, ESPN's player page has 6'7" and ESPN's reporting has 6'5".
