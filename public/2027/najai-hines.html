SCOUTING REPORT

Hines played his true freshman season at Seton Hall, not UConn — he's transferring for 2026-27 — and posted 6.5 points, 5.5 rebounds, 2.2 blocks and 0.6 assists on 60.2% shooting across 31 games. His debut (12 points, 9 rebounds, 6 blocks against Saint Peter's) had the home crowd chanting "Baby Shaq," and 247Sports describes a physical, football-background frame that "bullies opponents on both ends."

The offensive role stayed minimal as a freshman — no three-point attempts, very low assist rate — which is normal for a rim-running rookie big but means his half-court offensive game is still almost entirely unproven.
