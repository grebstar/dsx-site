SCOUTING REPORT

Nicknamed "the Italian unicorn," Suigo is a 7-3 stretch-big prospect who moved mid-cycle from Olimpia Milano to Mega Basket, posting 7.9 points, 5.1 rebounds and 0.7 assists on 53.5% shooting in ABA Liga play (26 games), plus a stronger 14.5-point, 7.5-rebound line in four EuroLeague Next Generation Finals games with Olimpia Milano before the move. He's represented Italy at the U18 EuroBasket and U17 World Cup and attended Basketball Without Borders in both 2024 and 2025.

His nationality is consistently reported as Italian across every source — the real conflict is his club country (an Italian club to a Serbia-based ABA League team mid-season), which is likely the source of confusion elsewhere. He signed with Villanova.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Elite size for a stretch big at 7-3, already producing real professional minutes at 19
  • Stronger EuroLeague Next Generation Finals line (14.5/7.5) shows upside beyond his ABA Liga role numbers
  • Represented Italy at multiple FIBA youth championships and attended Basketball Without Borders twice
  • Won two LBA Next Gen Cups with Olimpia Milano before the move to Mega Basket

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Mid-season club move (Olimpia Milano to Mega Basket) disrupts a clean read on a single-season sample
  • 247Sports carries no numeric composite rating for him at all — a real recruiting-industry data gap
  • Height is reported two ways (7-3 vs. roughly 7-1) across sources
