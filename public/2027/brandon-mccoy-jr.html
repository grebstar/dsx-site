SCOUTING REPORT

McCoy was named the 2025-26 MaxPreps California High School Basketball Player of the Year and a McDonald's All-American after leading Sierra Canyon to the CIF State Open Division title (78-70 over Salesian). A clean full-season per-game line could not be verified — MaxPreps' stats page for his senior year renders only a single logged game — so the numbers below draw on his junior year for trajectory context: 16.8 points, 6.1 rebounds, 3.5 assists, 3.8 steals and 0.8 blocks in 17 games.

247Sports' Adam Finkelstein calls him "one of the most physically imposing guards in the class," citing elite defense, rebounding and rim finishing, while flagging his shooting range and off-ball projection as the swing skill. He drew 22 major offers before choosing Michigan.
