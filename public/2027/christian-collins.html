SCOUTING REPORT

Collins's path is genuinely unusual: broken knee cartilage in 8th grade limited him through freshman and sophomore years — including JV time — before he broke out after transferring to St. John Bosco for his junior season (13.3 points, 9.7 rebounds that year) and closed his senior season at 25.5 points, 10.4 rebounds, 3.6 assists, 1.3 steals and 1.0 blocks across 26 games. 247Sports has him a five-star, #8 nationally and #3 at power forward.

He's specifically praised for work ethic and a team-first mentality — one cited example has him scoring just 2 points in a Peach Jam quarterfinal but drawing praise anyway for winning-focused play — and chose USC over Kentucky and Louisville finalists.
