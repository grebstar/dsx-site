SCOUTING REPORT

Holt transferred twice in his final two prep years — Buckhorn (Huntsville, AL) to Grayson (Loganville, GA) to Prolific Prep (Fort Lauderdale, FL) — and closed it out as Grind Session MVP, leading Prolific to the circuit title at 19.5 points, 8.2 rebounds, 3.1 assists and 2.3 steals on 47.3% shooting, 42.6% from three on real volume (5.3 attempts a game). A 6-9 wingspan on a 6-5 frame gives him more defensive range than most guards in the class.

Ten of the 14 boards on this composite have him inside the top three overall, and he's the only player besides Stokes both sources agree belongs in a class of his own at the top. He signed with Arizona.
