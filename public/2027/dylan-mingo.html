SCOUTING REPORT

Mingo is a composite top-12 recruit at Long Island Lutheran — the same program that produces Kiyan Anthony, Carmelo Anthony's son, as a teammate — but a clean, full 2025-26 season stat line could not be verified from any approved source for this build; only isolated tournament box scores exist (8 pts/13 reb/4 ast/3 stl/1 blk vs. Veritas Academy; 16 points on 40% three-point shooting vs. Orangeville). That gap is recorded honestly rather than guessed at.

What's well documented is the recruitment: he originally committed to North Carolina in February, decommitted, and flipped to Baylor in late April to join his older brother Kayden Mingo, a Baylor baseball/basketball athlete. Scouting notes highlight his rebounding and defensive activity as well as improving perimeter shooting versatility.
