SCOUTING REPORT

Haugh jumped from 9.7 points a game as a sophomore on Florida's national-title team to 17.1 points, 6.1 rebounds and 2.1 assists on 46.0% shooting as a junior, starting all 34 games. RealGM's draft report calls him an elite offensive rebounder and an active, switchable defender who can play small-ball five — a real two-way role player whose usage kept expanding.

The 3-point shot (32.6% on 5.2 attempts a game) is the explicit swing skill scouts are watching, alongside a "positional tweener" question that comes with his age relative to the class.
