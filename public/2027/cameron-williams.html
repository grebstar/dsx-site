SCOUTING REPORT

Williams was named the 2025-26 MaxPreps Arizona High School Basketball Player of the Year and is a McDonald's All-American, but a clean full-season per-game line could not be verified for this build — MaxPreps' own career page renders a distinct row through his junior year (18.0/11.2/3.6/1.8 stl/2.6 blk) but not a separate senior-season row, and 247Sports separately cites approximate current-season figures of 8.5 rebounds and 3.3 blocks without confirming full-season scope. That gap is recorded rather than guessed at.

What's not in doubt: 247Sports' composite has him #4 nationally and #1 at power forward, and Adam Finkelstein's scouting report cites mobility, athleticism, a budding face-up game and defensive versatility, with a 7-1.5 wingspan and real strength still to add. He led St. Mary's to the Open Division state championship game and signed with Duke on November 20, before most of the rest of this class had even committed.

  • 7-1.5 wingspan with real defensive versatility

  • Height/weight is unsettled between 6-10/185 and 6-11/200 across sources
