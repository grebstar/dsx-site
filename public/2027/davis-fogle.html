SCOUTING REPORT

Fogle posted 8.6 points, 3.1 rebounds and 1.3 assists on 51.8% shooting (35.0% from three, 72.3% from the line) as a Gonzaga freshman, described locally as "proving a point" after a recruiting cycle in which some questioned his final ranking. His three-point efficiency stood out on a Gonzaga offense reported to be searching for consistency as a whole.

The counting numbers are modest for a role player finding his footing, which is normal for a freshman in a deep rotation — the efficiency, not the volume, is the signal here.

▲ BULL CASE
