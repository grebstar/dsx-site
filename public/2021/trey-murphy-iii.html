SCOUTING REPORT

Memphis took him #17 overall out of Virginia. He had just gone for 11.3 points, 3.4 rebounds and 1.2 assists in 25 games at 29.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #21, pick 17. The boards ranged from #15 to #30 on him, a spread of 15 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 62 games, 1 starts, 13.9 minutes — 5.4 / 2.4 / 0.6 on 39.4% shooting. That ranked him #29 in the class and marked him at $25.57, -50.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 66 games, 66 starts, 35.5 minutes — 21.5 / 5.7 / 3.8 on 47.0% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +521.9% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $51.92, today's mark of $322.90 is +521.9%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

He never played for Memphis. On Aug. 7, 2021, New Orleans received Jonas Valanciunas, Devonte' Graham and the draft rights to Trey Murphy III and Brandon Boston Jr.; Memphis received Steven Adams, Eric Bledsoe and the draft rights to Ziaire Williams and Jared Butler. The picks in that deal are described nowhere in this class: RealGM lists a conditional 2022 first and basketball-reference lists two seconds, and the board records the disagreement instead of choosing.

He has stayed in New Orleans and is signed through 2028-29 on a fully guaranteed four-year, $112 million extension agreed Oct. 21, 2024, at cap hits of $25M, $27M, $29M and $31M. The availability is the problem. A torn meniscus in an offseason workout reported Sep. 7, 2023 carried a 10-to-12 week projection and cost him the start of 2023-24; a shoulder injury ended 2024-25 at 53 games; and 2025-26 carried right shoulder soreness through February — he left the Feb. 11 game at Miami after 27 minutes and was doubtful with a contusion on Feb. 20 — then a right ankle sprain in late March with a setback in early April that kept him out from Apr. 4 to the end. He still started all 66 games he played at 35.5 minutes for 21.5 points on .470 and .379. New Orleans finished 26-56 and missed the play-in.
