SCOUTING REPORT

New Orleans took him #35 overall out of Alabama. He had just gone for 11.2 points, 6.6 rebounds and 3.3 assists in 33 games at 27.3 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #39, pick 35. The boards ranged from #33 to #43 on him, a spread of 10 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 78 games, 69 starts, 29.9 minutes — 9.5 / 3.8 / 2.1 on 47.6% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +3305.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 56 games, 56 starts, 28.4 minutes — 8.9 / 3.4 / 2.8 on 38.3% shooting. That ranked him #19 in the class and marked him at $62.59, +560.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $9.48, today's mark of $62.59 is +560.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished sixth in the All-Rookie vote among 2021 draftees.

He has never been traded, which for a second-round pick who has started 56 games a year is its own statement. He signed a veteran extension with New Orleans in July 2025 that runs through 2028-29 with a player option for 2029-30; the money is reported as three years and $68 million by NBA.com and CBS and as two years and $43.39 million by RotoWire, so the page states the term structure both agree on and no figure. RotoWire reported in August 2026 that other teams inquired and the Pelicans set a high asking price.

2025-26 was 56 games, all of them starts, at 28.4 minutes with 1.6 steals — and .383 from the field and .309 from three, the lowest shooting of his career. A mild right calf strain in November carried a one-week re-evaluation; a right ankle sprain suffered against Dallas on Dec. 22, 2025 cost him 11 of the next 12 games, with FOX logging a further eight-game absence in late January. New Orleans finished 26-56 and eleventh in the West, fired Willie Green at 2-10 and went 24-46 under James Borrego; Jones was rested down the stretch as the team turned to younger players. A posterior labrum tear in his right shoulder had ended 2024-25 at 20 games, after 18 games already missed to the same shoulder.
