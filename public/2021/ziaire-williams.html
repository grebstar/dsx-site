SCOUTING REPORT

New Orleans took him tenth overall out of Stanford. He had just gone for 10.7 points, 4.6 rebounds and 2.2 assists in 20 games at 27.8 minutes a night. He went 13 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #23, drafted 10. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #18 to #27 on him, a spread of 9 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 62 games, 31 starts, 21.7 minutes — 8.1 / 2.1 / 1.0 on 45.0% shooting. That ranked him #22 in the class and marked him at $47.43, +9.3% against his IPO. 2025-26: 56 games, 13 starts, 22.9 minutes — 10.2 / 2.4 / 1.1 on 42.5% shooting. That ranked him #24 in the class and marked him at $39.70, -8.5% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $43.38, today's mark of $39.70 is -8.5%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

He never signed with the team that drafted him. On Aug. 7, 2021, Memphis received the draft rights to Ziaire Williams, Steven Adams, Eric Bledsoe and the draft rights to Jared Butler; New Orleans received Jonas Valanciunas, Devonte' Graham and the draft rights to Trey Murphy III and Brandon Boston Jr. The pick compensation attached to that deal is described on no page in this class: RealGM records a conditional 2022 first-round pick and basketball-reference records two second-round picks, and the board will not choose between them. He signed his rookie contract with Memphis on Aug. 8, 2021.

Memphis moved him on Jul. 19, 2024: Brooklyn received Williams, Dallas's own 2030 second-round pick and a trade exception; Memphis received Mamadi Diakite, the draft rights to Nemanja Dangubic and a trade exception. He set a personal scoring mark in Brooklyn at 10.2 a game and Sports Illustrated's season grades described Jordi Fernandez using him as a chess piece across positions, crediting 1.4 steals a night, while writing that the percentages need to improve for him to be more than a bench player. A back fracture he called small and very minor cost him time in October 2025, and a foot injury took four games in April 2026. Brooklyn declined its $6.25 million team option on Jun. 28, 2026 and he signed with the Lakers for one year and $3 million on Jul. 13, 2026.
