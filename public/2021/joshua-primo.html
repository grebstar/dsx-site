San Antonio took him twelfth overall out of Alabama. He had just gone for 8.1 points, 3.4 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 30 games at 22.4 minutes a night. He went 20 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #32, drafted 12. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #22 to #60 on him, a spread of 38 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 50 games, 16 starts, 19.3 minutes — 5.8 / 2.3 / 1.6 on 37.4% shooting. That ranked him #25 in the class and marked him at $36.38, +86.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. No NBA row — his last recorded NBA season was 2023-24.

Against a frozen IPO of $19.52, today's mark of $11.89 is -39.1%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

He was the youngest player in the class and he has never been traded. San Antonio waived him in October 2022; the league later imposed a four-game suspension, and a lawsuit brought by a team therapist was settled. Those are the formal facts and the page states no more than that. His last NBA appearance came in 2023-24 with the Clippers, who waived him on Apr. 13, 2024 after two games and a left ankle sprain. Chicago signed him to an Exhibit 10 in October 2024 stating the deal was to facilitate his rehabilitation with no plan to roster him; he played one Windy City Bulls game and was waived that December.

2025-26 is a blank at every level. RealGM's game log for the season is empty, he does not appear on the Windy City Bulls' opening-night roster of Nov. 7, 2025, and his most recent competitive basketball before the gap was Chicago's July 2025 Summer League roster. No source found records an injury behind it, so the carry-forward rule does not reach him and the board prices the absence as it stands.
