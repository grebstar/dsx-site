SCOUTING REPORT

the Clippers took him #25 overall out of Houston. He had just gone for 17.8 points, 5.7 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 30 games at 32.7 minutes a night. He went 12 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #37, drafted 25. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #30 to #60 on him, a spread of 30 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 46 games, 6 starts, 17.1 minutes — 6.0 / 2.0 / 1.0 on 40.4% shooting. That ranked him #28 in the class and marked him at $27.93, +135.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 75 games, 19 starts, 29.4 minutes — 13.4 / 3.6 / 3.3 on 45.0% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, +883.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $11.89, today's mark of $116.93 is +883.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Four moves in five years, beginning on draft night. New York received the draft rights to Grimes and a future second-round pick; the Clippers received the draft rights to Keon Johnson, whose page on this board describes the same swap from the other side. On Feb. 8, 2024, Detroit received Grimes, Evan Fournier, Malachi Flynn, Ryan Arcidiacono and New York's own 2028 and 2029 second-round picks; New York received Bojan Bogdanovic and Alec Burks. On Jul. 6, 2024, Dallas received Grimes; Detroit received Tim Hardaway Jr. and three second-round picks — a 2025 pick via Toronto and two 2028 picks. On Feb. 4, 2025, Philadelphia received Grimes and a 2025 second-round pick; Dallas received Caleb Martin.

A knee injury — lingering muscle soreness from a bone bruise, no surgery — ended 2023-24 after six Detroit games. He played 2025-26 on a one-year, $8.7 million qualifying offer with a no-trade clause, having stalled in restricted free agency, and turned it into 75 games at 29.4 minutes and 13.4 points. Philadelphia went 45-37, beat Boston 4-3 and was swept by New York in the conference semifinals. He signed with the Lakers on Jul. 1, 2026 for four years and $60 million with a player option in the fourth.
