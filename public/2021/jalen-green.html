SCOUTING REPORT

Houston took him second overall out of G League Ignite in the NBA G League. He had just gone for 17.9 points, 4.1 rebounds and 2.8 assists in 15 games at 32.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #3, pick 2. The boards ranged from second to third on him, a spread of 1 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 67 games, 67 starts, 31.9 minutes — 17.3 / 3.4 / 2.6 on 42.6% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, -36.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 32 games, 27 starts, 25.9 minutes — 17.8 / 3.6 / 2.8 on 42.2% shooting. That ranked him #15 in the class and marked him at $93.47, -85.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $623.16, today's mark of $93.47 is -85.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished fifth in the All-Rookie vote among 2021 draftees.

Houston traded him on Jul. 6, 2025, and the deal is worth reading in full because the headline name went the other way. Phoenix received Green, Dillon Brooks, the draft rights to Koby Brea, Rasheer Fleming and Khaman Maluach, a more favourable 2026 second-round pick and Houston's own 2032 second. Houston received Kevin Durant, the draft rights to Alex Toohey, a least favourable 2026 second and a more favourable 2032 second. Green is the asset Houston spent to buy Durant, not the return for him.

The first Phoenix season barely happened. A right hamstring strain aggravated in the preseason cost him the opener; he played two games, aggravated it again against the Clippers on Nov. 8, 2025 and was ruled out four to six weeks in a diagnosis reported Nov. 11, with the window later extended; it recurred once more against Atlanta on Jan. 23, 2026. He finished with 32 games of 82. Phoenix went 45-37, lost its first play-in game to Portland, beat Golden State in the second to take the eight seed, and was swept by Oklahoma City. He is under a three-year extension signed in October 2024 carrying a player option for 2027-28 and a 10 per cent trade kicker; the reported total is given as $106 million in one account and roughly $105.3 million in another, so neither is stated here.
