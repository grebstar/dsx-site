SCOUTING REPORT

Indiana took him #13 overall out of Oregon. He had just gone for 17.1 points, 4.6 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 26 games at 34.0 minutes a night. He went 5 picks ahead of his composite, which was #18. The boards ranged from #14 to #23 on him, a spread of 9 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 55 games, 39 starts, 28.0 minutes — 13.1 / 4.1 / 2.1 on 43.2% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +147.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Spain — signed with Unicaja after four NBA seasons.

Against a frozen IPO of $68.95, today's mark of $10.64 is -84.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished tenth in the All-Rookie vote among 2021 draftees.

Two trades, both of them as the smaller piece. On Jul. 6, 2023, Sacramento received Duarte; Indiana received Dallas's own 2028 second-round pick and Sacramento's own 2030 second-round pick. On Jul. 8, 2024, Chicago received Duarte, Sacramento's own 2025 and 2028 second-round picks, cash and a trade exception; Sacramento received DeMar DeRozan. Chicago waived him on Feb. 3, 2025 after 17 games at 4.4 minutes.

He is not an injury case in 2025-26. He signed a two-year deal with Unicaja Malaga in July 2025 with a team option on the second year, and played the season in Spain: RealGM has 27 Liga ACB games at 11.4 points on .447 and .358 from three, and 12 Basketball Champions League games at 9.5 on .506. Eurohoops counts 43 appearances across all competitions, which RealGM's two-competition table does not reconcile, so both counts are recorded and neither is asserted. Unicaja and Duarte terminated the contract by mutual agreement in July 2026 and he signed a two-year deal with Baskonia on Jul. 17, 2026, moving up to the EuroLeague. The rookie year, meanwhile, ended three games early with a sore left big toe that had bothered him since mid-February; his last game was Mar. 15, 2022.
