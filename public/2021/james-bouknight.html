SCOUTING REPORT

Charlotte took him eleventh overall out of UConn. He had just gone for 18.7 points, 5.7 rebounds and 1.8 assists in 15 games at 31.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #10, pick 11. The boards ranged from sixth to #24 on him, a spread of 18 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 31 games, 0 starts, 9.8 minutes — 4.6 / 1.7 / 0.8 on 34.8% shooting. That ranked him #39 in the class and marked him at $9.48, -94.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. No NBA row — his last recorded NBA season was 2023-24.

Against a frozen IPO of $170.70, today's mark of $13.22 is -92.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

He has never been traded, which in his case means he was simply let go. Charlotte declined his fourth-year option and waived him on Feb. 8, 2024; Portland signed him to an Exhibit 10 on Oct. 15, 2024 and waived him the next day. A left knee meniscus injury in a 2023 training-camp practice needed arthroscopic surgery that October and a four-week re-evaluation, and he played 14 games that season.

He is not an injury case in 2025-26 and the board does not price him as one. He played the whole season in the G League for the Mexico City Capitanes as their lead scorer, after a Portland Summer League run in July 2025 that included 25 points on 8-of-11 against Houston. Vitor Galvani called him a natural scorer after a home opener with 12 points, three rebounds and five assists. The season line is reported several ways — RealGM has 36 games at 19.4 points on .404 from three, Proballers 48 games at 19.7 on .416 — and he scored 38 against Sioux Falls on Mar. 22, 2026 and 35 with six threes against Rip City on Apr. 1.
