SCOUTING REPORT

Cleveland took him third overall out of USC. He had just gone for 16.4 points, 8.7 rebounds and 2.4 assists in 33 games at 33.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #2, pick 3. The boards ranged from second to third on him, a spread of 1 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 69 games, 69 starts, 33.8 minutes — 15.0 / 8.3 / 2.5 on 50.8% shooting. That ranked him second in the class and marked him at $808.24, +0.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 65 games, 63 starts, 31.9 minutes — 18.2 / 9.0 / 3.6 on 54.6% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, -66.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $808.24, today's mark of $269.91 is -66.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished third in the All-Rookie vote among 2021 draftees.

He has never been traded and has started essentially every night of his career. A sprained right elbow in November 2021, when his arm tangled with Enes Kanter under the basket in a play J.B. Bickerstaff called a freak thing, cost him two to four weeks; a Grade 1 left calf strain confirmed by MRI on Dec. 14, 2025 cost him another two to four, the injury turning up after a 130-126 win over Washington in which he had 23 points and 13 rebounds. He signed a five-year rookie-scale maximum extension in July 2024. The figure is reported as $224 million and as up to $269 million depending on the escalator, so the page states neither.

2025-26 was his best offensive season and his emptiest trophy case. He set career marks at 18.2 points on .546 shooting with 9.0 rebounds, and then made neither All-Defensive team a year after winning Defensive Player of the Year — that award went to Victor Wembanyama on all 100 first-place votes. Cleveland finished 52-30, beat Toronto in seven, came back from 0-2 to beat No. 1 Detroit in seven, and was swept by New York in the conference finals. Mobley played all 18 playoff games at 35.6 minutes and shot .338 from three in them, above his regular-season .297.
