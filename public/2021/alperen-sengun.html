SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him #16 overall out of Besiktas in the Turkish BSL. He had just gone for 18.6 points, 8.9 rebounds and 2.7 assists in 34 games at 28.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #12, pick 16. The boards ranged from tenth to #19 on him, a spread of 9 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 72 games, 13 starts, 20.7 minutes — 9.6 / 5.5 / 2.6 on 47.4% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, -11.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: 72 games, 72 starts, 33.3 minutes — 20.4 / 8.9 / 6.2 on 51.9% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +373.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $131.64, today's mark of $623.16 is +373.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

The slot on this board says Oklahoma City, and the draft-night deal is why. Houston received the draft rights to Sengun; Oklahoma City received a conditional 2023 first-round pick owed by Detroit and a conditional 2023 first-round pick owed by Washington — two future firsts Houston controlled. Oklahoma City made the selection on Houston's behalf. He has never been traded since, and Houston signed him to a five-year, $185 million rookie-scale extension in October 2024 with a player option in the final year.

He is the offensive hub of a 50-win team. In 2025-26 he played 72 games at 33.3 minutes for 20.4 points, 8.9 rebounds and 6.2 assists on .519 shooting, anchoring the frontcourt alongside Kevin Durant, and was named an All-Star for a second straight season — as a replacement, in February 2026, having not been among the announced reserves. He did not appear on any 2025-26 All-NBA team. Houston recorded a second consecutive 50-win season and lost the first round to the Lakers in six, ending May 1, 2026; Sengun averaged 20.3 points and 10.2 rebounds in 38.7 minutes across those six games, on .125 from three. As of September 2026 he is playing FIBA World Cup European qualifying for Turkey.
