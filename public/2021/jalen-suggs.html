SCOUTING REPORT

Orlando took him fifth overall out of Gonzaga. He had just gone for 14.4 points, 5.3 rebounds and 4.5 assists in 30 games at 28.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 5. All 5 boards had him at fourth. There was no disagreement to price.

2021-22: 48 games, 45 starts, 27.2 minutes — 11.8 / 3.6 / 4.4 on 36.1% shooting. That ranked him twelfth in the class and marked him at $131.64, -73.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 57 games, 56 starts, 27.6 minutes — 13.8 / 3.9 / 5.5 on 43.5% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, -53.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $229.10 is -53.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

He has never been traded, and the reason his page reads thin is availability. The list is long and it is all his: a left thumb sprain that ended his 2021 Summer League after an MRI; a fractured right thumb against Philadelphia on Nov. 29, 2021 that Orlando called an extended absence and that held his rookie year to 48 games; 53 games missed in 2022-23; arthroscopic surgery on the left knee in late January 2025 to remove cartilage after back spasms in Toronto, ending that season; and a Grade 1 MCL contusion in the right knee in January 2026, confirmed by MRI with no structural damage, by which point he had played 23 of Orlando's first 35 games.

What he did play in 2025-26 was his best playmaking: 5.5 assists and 1.8 steals in 27.6 minutes across 57 games, 56 of them starts. He is signed through 2029-30 on the five-year extension agreed Oct. 21, 2024 and reported at $150.5 million. Orlando took the eight seed, led Detroit 3-1 in the first round and lost it in seven; Suggs started all seven and shot .299 from the field and .241 from three.
