SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him #29 overall out of North Carolina. He had just gone for 9.5 points, 7.6 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 29 games at 19.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #28, pick 29. The boards ranged from #21 to #35 on him, a spread of 14 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 32 games, 8 starts, 12.2 minutes — 6.2 / 5.0 / 0.5 on 57.7% shooting. That ranked him #27 in the class and marked him at $30.50, +9.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: 62 games, 7 starts, 18.7 minutes — 8.7 / 6.7 / 2.3 on 60.1% shooting. That ranked him #18 in the class and marked him at $68.95, +146.9% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $27.93, today's mark of $68.95 is +146.9%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

He never played for Phoenix. The draft-night deal, agreed Jul. 29, 2021 and completed Aug. 6: Brooklyn received Jevon Carter and the draft rights to Day'Ron Sharpe; Phoenix received Landry Shamet. RealGM's ledger adds a trade exception to Brooklyn's side that the news accounts do not list, so the exception is recorded here and not asserted. The rookie year was 32 NBA games behind Brooklyn's veteran frontcourt; the development happened at Long Island, where he started all ten G League games he played and averaged 18.8 points and 13.0 rebounds in 31.9 minutes.

2025-26 was his fifth Brooklyn season and his best: 62 games, 18.7 minutes, 8.7 points, 6.7 rebounds and 2.3 assists on .601 shooting, with NBA.com noting he led Brooklyn's bench in points (462), steals (55) and minutes (963). It ended on Mar. 12, 2026 with surgery on a torn ulnar collateral ligament in his left thumb. He re-signed with Brooklyn on Sep. 3, 2025; the terms are reported as two years and $12.5 million with a team option and as two years and $20 million, so no figure appears. The Nets went 20-62. RotoWire projects him into the starting centre job for 2026-27 after Nic Claxton was traded to Chicago.
