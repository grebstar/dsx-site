SCOUTING REPORT

Orlando took him eighth overall out of Michigan. He had just gone for 12.5 points, 6.5 rebounds and 3.0 assists in 28 games at 31.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #8, pick 8. The boards ranged from eighth to ninth on him, a spread of 1 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 79 games, 79 starts, 30.7 minutes — 15.2 / 4.5 / 2.9 on 46.8% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, +116.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: 34 games, 32 starts, 30.0 minutes — 20.6 / 5.2 / 3.3 on 48.1% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, -14.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $229.10, today's mark of $196.79 is -14.1%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished fourth in the All-Rookie vote among 2021 draftees.

He has never been traded. He signed a five-year rookie-scale maximum extension in July 2024; Hoops Rumors puts the floor at $224 million and the ceiling near $269 million under Rose Rule language, which is why no number appears on this page.

Three consecutive seasons have been cut into. A torn right oblique against Philadelphia on Dec. 7, 2024 carried a four-week re-evaluation and held him to 60 games. A left high ankle sprain suffered on Dec. 7, 2025 — a year to the day — cost him 25 games, survived a failed late-January return, and had him ruled out indefinitely again on Feb. 18, 2026 with imaging confirming more rehab was needed; he finished with 34 games. Then a right calf strain in the second half of Game 4 against Detroit ended his postseason. What he played was his best offence: 20.6 points on .481 and .345 from three, the latter up from .295. Orlando led that series 3-1 without him for the last three and lost it in seven on May 3, 2026.
