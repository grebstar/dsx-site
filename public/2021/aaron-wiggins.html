SCOUTING REPORT

Wiggins' last college season at Maryland was a 14.5 point, 5.8 rebound, 2.5 assist year in 33.0 minutes over 31 games (30 starts), with 1.1 steals, 1.8 made threes, 44.6% shooting and 77.2% at the line. He was an All-Big Ten honorable mention that year after winning Big Ten Sixth Man of the Year in 2020, and he scored a career-high 27 points in his final game, a 96-77 NCAA tournament second-round loss to Alabama.

Oklahoma City took him 55th overall on July 29, 2021, with no draft-night trade, and signed him to a two-way contract after a few Summer League games. Four of the five boards ranked him, between 48th and 56th, and the fifth left him off, for a composite rank of 52nd.

As a rookie he started 35 of 50 games at 8.3 points and was converted to a standard contract on Feb. 12, 2022. In 2022-23 he played 70 games with 14 starts and shot 39.3% from three. In 2023-24 he played 78 games, starting four, at 15.7 minutes and 6.9 points on 56.2% shooting and 49.2% from three, for a 57-25 team that lost in the second round to Dallas. His 2024-25 season was his most productive: 12.0 points on 48.8% shooting in 76 games (26 starts), with a 41-point, 14-rebound game against Sacramento on Feb. 1, 2025, and 18 points with five threes in a 123-107 Game 2 win over Indiana in the Finals, which Oklahoma City won in seven games. In 2025-26 his scoring fell to 9.4 points on 43.1% shooting in 65 games, and he did not play in Game 7 of the West finals.

Oklahoma City declined his $1.99 million 2024-25 option in June 2024, then re-signed him on July 7, 2024 to a five-year, $45 million deal, according to Spotrac, with a 2028-29 team option. On July 6, 2026 the Thunder sent him to Atlanta for two future second-round picks; the Hawks absorbed his salary with a trade exception.

His 2026-27 base salary is $8,831,776, per Spotrac. Through Atlanta's first five games he had come off the bench each night, averaging 14.6 points in 26.4 minutes and shooting 43.8% from three, including 19 points on 7-of-9 shooting in the 128-81 win at Charlotte on Oct. 23, 2026.
