SCOUTING REPORT

Indiana took him #54 overall out of Seton Hall. He had just gone for 17.5 points, 7.6 rebounds and 3.2 assists in 27 games at 35.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #53, pick 54. The boards ranged from #53 to #60 on him, a spread of 7 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 41 games, 3 starts, 9.9 minutes — 3.8 / 2.0 / 0.5 on 49.6% shooting. That ranked him #40 in the class and marked him at $8.31, up from an unlisted IPO. 2025-26: 80 games, 13 starts, 21.9 minutes — 11.2 / 4.9 / 1.9 on 52.3% shooting. That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, up from an unlisted IPO.

He was priced at $0.00 at IPO — below the replacement line at composite rank 50 — so there is no percentage to quote from zero. He is carried as FROM UNLISTED.

He played his first two NBA seasons with Milwaukee, and the Bucks requested waivers on him, then a two-way player, on Mar. 1, 2023. San Antonio claimed him from waivers on Mar. 3, 2023, converting him to a standard contract and waiving Isaiah Roby to make room, and he spent parts of three seasons with the Spurs.

He became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2025 and signed a multi-year contract with Toronto, reported at two years and $5.5 million. After the 2025-26 season he declined a $2.8 million player option (reported Jun. 29, 2026), became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2026 and signed with the Los Angeles Lakers on Jul. 7, 2026, a four-year, $52 million deal with a player option in Year 4 that was reported as fully guaranteed.
