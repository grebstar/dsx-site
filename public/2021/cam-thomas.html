SCOUTING REPORT

Brooklyn took him #27 overall out of LSU. He had just gone for 23.0 points, 3.4 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 29 games at 33.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #25, pick 27. The boards ranged from #22 to #27 on him, a spread of 5 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 67 games, 2 starts, 17.6 minutes — 8.5 / 2.4 / 1.2 on 43.3% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, +56.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 42 games, 8 starts, 21.0 minutes — 13.5 / 1.7 / 2.6 on 41.0% shooting. That ranked him #22 in the class and marked him at $47.43, +30.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $36.38, today's mark of $47.43 is +30.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

He was never traded. He was waived twice in eleven weeks. He played 2025-26 on the qualifying offer he signed with Brooklyn on Sep. 4, 2025, which functioned as a de facto no-trade clause; when the deadline passed without a deal, Brooklyn placed him on waivers on Feb. 5, 2026, Hoops Rumors reporting he had asked for release if not traded so he could pick his next team. He signed with Milwaukee on Feb. 8 for the rest of the season at a reported $905,366, saying he chose the Bucks because they wanted him and had been interested for years. Milwaukee waived him on Mar. 23, 2026, past the Mar. 1 cutoff for playoff eligibility, to convert Pete Nance to a standard contract. Doc Rivers said the team had run out of bodies, and added that there were things they did not need to talk about.

The basketball inside that: 24 games in Brooklyn at 24.3 minutes and 15.6 points on .399/.325/.843, after a strained hamstring in his first eight games kept him out until Dec. 27; then 18 games in Milwaukee at 16.6 minutes and 10.6 points on .431/.275/.754, including 27 points in 21 minutes against New Orleans on Feb. 21. He is an unrestricted free agent as of September 2026 with no reported contract. A left hamstring strain in late November 2024 had already cost him 56 of Brooklyn's final 64 games the season before.
