SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took him #20 overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 11.2 points, 6.1 rebounds and 2.2 assists in 13 games at 21.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #16, pick 20. The boards ranged from seventh to #22 on him, a spread of 15 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 22 games, 0 starts, 5.5 minutes — 2.4 / 1.2 / 0.1 on 53.7% shooting. That ranked him #47 in the class and marked him at $2.17, -97.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 72 games, 72 starts, 35.2 minutes — 22.5 / 10.3 / 7.9 on 48.9% shooting. That ranked him second in the class and marked him at $808.24, +860.5% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $84.15, today's mark of $808.24 is +860.5%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He made All-NBA Third Team in 2025-26 with 125 vote points, which is why he sits where he does at that mark.

He has never been traded, and he is signed through 2029-30 on a fully guaranteed five-year, $150 million extension agreed Oct. 21, 2024 at a flat $30 million a year. The rookie year was 22 games at 5.5 minutes behind John Collins and Danilo Gallinari on a team chasing seeding; the work happened at College Park, where he played 12 G League games at 20.1 points, 11.8 rebounds and 4.1 assists in 37 minutes.

2025-26 is the season the board is pricing and it was a full one: 72 games, 72 starts, 35.2 minutes, 22.5 points, 10.3 rebounds and 7.9 assists on .489 shooting, with 49 double-doubles and 13 triple-doubles. Atlanta traded Trae Young during the year and Johnson became the primary ball-handler. He was an All-Star for the first time and made All-NBA Third Team on May 24, 2026 with Jamal Murray, Tyrese Maxey, Chet Holmgren and Jalen Duren. Atlanta finished 46-36 as the six seed and lost the first round to New York 4-2, closing with a 140-89 Game 6 defeat on Apr. 30; Johnson averaged 19.5 points, 7.7 rebounds and 5.2 assists in the series on .435 and .290. A torn labrum in his left shoulder had required surgery in January 2025 and held the previous season to 36 games.
