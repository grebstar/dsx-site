SCOUTING REPORT

the Lakers took him #22 overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 8.4 points, 6.6 rebounds and 0.7 assists in 25 games at 20.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #20, pick 22. The boards ranged from #13 to #24 on him, a spread of 11 places across 5 of them.

2021-22: 36 games, 15 starts, 15.0 minutes — 8.3 / 4.1 / 0.3 on 56.3% shooting. That ranked him #21 in the class and marked him at $51.92, -8.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: 55 games, 14 starts, 16.5 minutes — 6.7 / 5.3 / 0.9 on 63.7% shooting. That ranked him #27 in the class and marked him at $30.50, -46.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $56.94, today's mark of $30.50 is -46.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

The board's slot reads the Lakers because the pick travelled through the Russell Westbrook framework. On draft night Indiana received the No. 22 selection, which became Isaiah Jackson; Washington received Aaron Holiday and the No. 31 pick, which became Isaiah Todd on this same board. The Pacers announced on Aug. 11, 2021 that they had acquired Jackson from the Lakers in a five-team trade also involving Brooklyn, San Antonio and Washington; the full five-team asset list is not reproduced on the page that announcement appears on, so it is not stated here.

A torn right Achilles tendon, non-contact, in the fourth quarter at New Orleans on Nov. 1, 2024, ended that season five games in with surgery scheduled for Nov. 4. Indiana re-signed him anyway as a restricted free agent on Jul. 11, 2025 for three years and $21 million — $7.6M in 2025-26, $7.0M in 2026-27. On Feb. 5, 2026 he was moved again: the Clippers received Bennedict Mathurin, Jackson, a 2026 first-round pick, a 2029 first-round pick and a 2028 second-round pick; Indiana received Ivica Zubac and Kobe Brown. He played 55 games in his first year back on .637 shooting, and .764 across 17 games after the trade. A right ankle sprain cost him the last eight regular-season games; he was available for the Clippers' play-in game on Apr. 15, 2026.
