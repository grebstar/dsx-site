SCOUTING REPORT

Wells is the best second-round value in this class and the boards mostly missed him — RookieScale had him 42nd and Rotoworld 50th. What they were looking at: 12.6 points and 4.6 rebounds on 41.7% from three with 2.1 makes a game and only 0.7 turnovers, in a single Division I season after transferring up from Sonoma State.

That is a clean 3-and-D wing profile on paper — efficient shooting, low mistakes, decent rebounding for the position — and Memphis had wing minutes available. The reason nobody paid up is the sample and the level: one year at Washington State, against a Pac-12 that was disintegrating, with 0.5 steals and 0.2 blocks that do not support the "D" half of the label.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 41.7% from three on 2.1 makes with only 0.7 turnovers — efficient and mistake-free
  • 4.6 rebounds from a wing and 81.4% from the line
  • Memphis had genuine wing minutes available for a second-round contract
  • 40.0% from three across six Summer League games

▼ BEAR CASE

  • One season at the Division I level after transferring up from Division II
  • 0.5 steals and 0.2 blocks — no defensive production behind the 3-and-D label
  • 43.6% from the field; the efficiency is shooting-driven, not creation-driven
  • Ranked 42nd by RookieScale and 50th by Rotoworld — nobody saw a rotation player
