SCOUTING REPORT

Johnson skipped college for the NBL Next Stars programme and played 7.7 minutes a game for the Illawarra Hawks, scoring 2.8 points on 36.7% shooting. There is no statistical case here of any kind. He is a 6'5" guard with a first step and a jumper that flashes, drafted 23rd on the strength of what scouts saw in practice rather than anything a box score recorded.

The boards priced it accordingly: RookieScale 37th, Rotoworld 36th, the largest negative gap between draft slot and consensus in the first round. That is the market telling you the pick was reach.

Milwaukee is the worst possible landing spot for the profile — a win-now roster built around Giannis Antetokounmpo and Damian Lillard, with no minutes to spend on a 19-year-old who has never produced. He did average 11.2 points and 2.8 assists in Las Vegas, which is the first evidence of anything, on 32.3% shooting. This is a three-year hold with a real chance of returning nothing, and it is priced near the bottom of the listed board.

  • Chose professional competition over college; two years of pro habits already
  • 11.2 points and 2.8 assists in Las Vegas — the first real production of his career

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 36.7% from the field and 53.8% from the line against NBL competition
