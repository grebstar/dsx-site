SCOUTING REPORT

Filipowski is the largest board-versus-draft-slot gap in the entire class after Jaylen Wells. RookieScale ranked him 24th, Rotoworld 19th and NBA Draft Room 20th — all seven boards inside the first round — and he fell to 32.

The Duke sophomore line justifies them: 16.4 points, 8.3 rebounds, 2.8 assists, 1.5 blocks and 1.1 steals on 50.6% shooting, with 34.8% from three on a make a game. That is five categories from a big man who can pass, and it is the kind of shape that wins weeks in a category league. Utah is also the emptiest frontcourt in the league.

He fell for real reasons: he is a 6'11" big whose defence in space was questioned all season, 67.1% from the free-throw line is a drag, and 2.1 turnovers is high for a player who is not a primary creator. But a second-round contract for a first-round board consensus is the definition of a value listing, and it is why he is the only pick after 30 — other than Wells — to get a page here at all.
