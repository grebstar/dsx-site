SCOUTING REPORT

Carrington was the youngest player in the 2024 draft class at 18.9 and produced a full stat line doing it: 13.8 points, 5.2 rebounds and 4.1 assists with 2.0 made threes in 33 minutes as a Pittsburgh freshman. Rebounds and assists together from a guard is a genuinely useful shape, and Washington's roster offered him the ball immediately.

The efficiency is the problem and it is not marginal: 41.2% from the field and 32.2% from three. He is a volume creator who has not yet earned the volume, and 0.6 steals with 0.2 blocks means the defensive categories are empty. The 78.5% from the line is the one indicator suggesting the jumper eventually gets there.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Youngest player in the draft class at 18.9 with a complete statistical profile
  • 5.2 rebounds and 4.1 assists from a guard — two categories most guards give you one of
  • Washington has nothing at point guard and every reason to play him 30 minutes
  • Rotoworld 11th and NBA Draft Room 8th, both above his draft slot

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 41.2% from the field and 32.2% from three — poor efficiency on real volume
  • 0.6 steals and 0.2 blocks; both defensive columns are empty
  • Washington will be the worst team in the league, which caps everything but usage
  • 32.9% shooting across five Summer League games
