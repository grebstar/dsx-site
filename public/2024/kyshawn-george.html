SCOUTING REPORT

George shot 40.8% from three on 1.7 makes as a Miami freshman, at 6'8", after a season in the French second division. That combination — wing size, real shooting volume, European professional habits at 20 — is what New York took at 24 before sending him to Washington within the hour for the rights to Dillon Jones and a second-rounder.

The rest of the line is modest: 7.6 points, 3.0 rebounds, 2.2 assists in 22.9 minutes, with 0.9 steals and 0.4 blocks. He does a little of everything and not much of anything, and 1.5 turnovers against 2.2 assists is unremarkable.

Two things count in his favour here. Washington is the worst roster in basketball and will hand him a starting job on arrival — Pathway 86 against a model base of 62. And he was one of the best shooters in Las Vegas at 51.7% from the field and 47.6% from three across five games. RookieScale ranked him 19th, five spots above where he went.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 40.8% from three on real volume at 6'8" — a genuinely scarce shape
  • Washington's depth chart offers immediate starter minutes
  • 51.7% from the field and 47.6% from three across five Summer League games
  • Two years of professional basketball in France before his one college season

▼ BEAR CASE

  • One season of Division I basketball and only 31 games of it
  • 7.6 points in 22.9 minutes; nothing about the volume is impressive
  • 1.5 turnovers against 2.2 assists on low usage
  • Washington will be historically bad, which caps team-context value
