SCOUTING REPORT

Mitchell was Big West Player of the Year in 2023 and a first-team all-conference selection as a junior, when he put up 20.0 points, 4.0 rebounds and 4.0 assists on 50.4% from the field, 39.3% from three and 85.8% at the line in 29 games. Six boards covered him, and he came in at #39 on the composite.

The Knicks took him 38th and moved the pick to Oklahoma City on draft night. He started on a two-way deal, had toe surgery in January 2025, and was converted to a standard contract in February while the Thunder went on to win the title.

In 2025-26 he played 57 games with 16 starts at 13.6 points, 3.3 rebounds and 3.6 assists and was part of OKC's 11-game run in the 2026 playoffs, with a calf strain ending his Western Conference Finals.

The Knicks selected Mitchell 38th on draft night (Jun. 2024) and traded the pick to Oklahoma City for the No. 40 pick and cash. The Thunder signed him to a two-way contract on Jul. 6, 2024.

After the Jan. 10, 2025 toe surgery, OKC converted the deal to a two-year, $6M standard contract on Feb. 6, 2025. On Jul. 8, 2025 he agreed to a new three-year, $8.7M contract, with the first year fully guaranteed and a team option on the third.

On Mar. 21, 2026 the NBA suspended him one game (and Washington's Justin Champagnie one game) after an on-court altercation in which Champagnie struck him in the face.
