SCOUTING REPORT

Missi is a category specialist and nothing else, which in a nine-cat league is often enough. 1.5 blocks in 23.0 minutes on 61.4% shooting as a Baylor freshman is a per-minute rim-protection rate that only Donovan Clingan beats in this draft, and the field goal percentage is a quiet third contribution on top of the blocks and 5.6 rebounds.

The rest of the profile is empty on purpose. 0.4 assists, 0.0 three-point attempts — not zero makes, zero attempts, all season — and 61.6% from the free-throw line, which is a real weekly cost. He is a lob catcher and a shot blocker.

What lifts him is the landing spot. New Orleans had no established centre and every reason to start a 20-year-old immediately, and Rotoworld ranked him 14th against a 21st draft slot on exactly that logic. Pathway 88 against a model base of 64 is the largest positive opportunity adjustment on this board. You are buying blocks, boards and field goal percentage at a late-first price, with the free-throw line as the toll.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 1.5 blocks in 23 minutes — second-best per-minute rim protection in the class
  • 61.4% from the field protects your percentages while filling two scarce columns
  • New Orleans has no incumbent centre; the starting job is available immediately
  • Rotoworld ranked him 14th, seven spots above where he went

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 61.6% from the line is a genuine weekly drag
  • Zero three-point attempts as a freshman; the offensive range is nil
  • 0.4 assists and 0.6 steals — the profile is two categories wide
  • New Orleans will be bad, which caps everything but raw minutes
