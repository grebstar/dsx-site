SCOUTING REPORT

Clingan is the purest category asset in this draft. 2.5 blocks and 7.4 rebounds in 22.6 minutes on 63.9% shooting as a national-champion sophomore, then 12.3 rebounds and 4.3 blocks a game in Las Vegas, where he led the entire Summer League in blocks. Rotoworld ranked him 2nd in the class, NBA Draft Room 3rd.

Blocks and rebounds are the two categories a dynasty roster is hardest to build in, and there is no other player here who supplies both at these rates. A 7'2" center shooting 63.9% also protects your field goal percentage, which is a third quiet contribution.

Two real costs. 58.3% from the free-throw line is a genuine weekly liability, not a rounding error, and 0.1 made threes means zero spacing. And Portland is crowded — Deandre Ayton and Robert Williams III were both on the roster, which is why his Pathway score of 72 sits below his talent. This is a buy-and-wait, priced accordingly.
