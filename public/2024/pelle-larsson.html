SCOUTING REPORT

Larsson spent his 2023-24 senior year at Arizona after starting at Utah, scoring 12.8 points with 4.1 rebounds and 3.7 assists on 51.9% shooting in 36 games and earning All-Pac-12 Second Team. Six boards covered him and he sat around the middle of the second round.

Houston took him 44th and Miami acquired his rights in a swap for Nikola Djurisic's rights. He played 14.2 minutes as a rookie, then moved into the starting lineup in 2025-26 for 11.4 points and 3.4 assists in 70 games. Miami exercised his option in June 2026 and RealGM lists a four-year extension agreed Sep. 22, 2026.
