SCOUTING REPORT

Ware's Indiana sophomore line is a category manager's shopping list: 15.9 points, 9.9 rebounds and 1.9 blocks on 58.6% from the field and 42.5% from three. A center rebounding at nearly ten a game, blocking two, and hitting better than 40% from deep fills five columns at once.

He fell to 15 because scouts questioned his motor and consistency, not his output — and a category league cares considerably less about that than a front office does. He then averaged 18.3 and 8.4 across eight Summer League games on 58.4% shooting, the best summer of any big in the class, which is why his post-Vegas re-rating is one of the largest positives on this board.

Miami is a mixed bag: Bam Adebayo is entrenched, but the Heat had nothing behind him. 63.4% from the line is the one number that will cost you weekly.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 9.9 rebounds and 1.9 blocks with 42.5% from three — five categories from one roster slot
  • 58.6% from the field; the efficiency is genuine, not shot-selection noise
  • 18.3 and 8.4 in Summer League, the best of any big in the class
  • Fell to 15 on effort concerns rather than production — a fantasy discount, not a fantasy problem

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 63.4% from the line is a real weekly cost
  • Bam Adebayo occupies his position and Miami is a win-now roster
  • Motor and consistency concerns were unanimous enough to drop him nine spots
  • 1.5 assists and 0.6 steals; the profile is frontcourt-only
