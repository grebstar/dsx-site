SCOUTING REPORT

Collier entered USC as a consensus top-three recruit and left as the 29th pick after a season that scouts read as disappointing and category managers read as underpriced. The line is real: 16.3 points, 4.3 assists and 1.5 steals on 49.0% shooting, with genuine downhill pressure.

Assists and steals from a lead guard is the highest-leverage combination in the format, and Rotoworld ranked him 16th against a 29th draft slot — the largest positive gap of any player in the back half of this first round.

The costs are severe and honest. 3.3 turnovers is among the worst figures in the class, 33.8% from three on 1.0 makes is close to a dead category, and 67.3% from the line offers no comfort. Utah is the emptiest depth chart in the league, which is why his Pathway score of 84 towers over the model base of 58.
