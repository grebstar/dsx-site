SCOUTING REPORT

Salaün is the purest projection pick in the lottery, and the boards split on him accordingly — RookieScale 9th, NBA Draft Room 9th, Rotoworld 17th. The case is entirely about what a 6'10" forward who was 18 years old and already starting in the French first division might become.

The case against is the line itself. 9.5 points on 39.0% from the field and 32.7% from three is not efficient shooting; it is a young player being allowed to take shots. He contributed 4.1 rebounds and a useful 1.1 steals, and 1.5 made threes a game means the volume is there even if the accuracy is not.

For a category league this is the least appealing shape in the top ten: no column he wins, poor percentages that actively cost you, and a Las Vegas showing of 10.0% from three across three games that gave nobody any comfort. Charlotte will hand him minutes because Charlotte has nothing else, which is the only reason to hold him at all in year one. This is a two-year hold priced as one.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Eighteen years old and already a rotation starter in a top European league
  • 6'10" with the mobility to defend the perimeter; 1.1 steals from a forward
  • 1.5 made threes a game — the volume and the mechanics are already there
  • Charlotte's roster guarantees developmental minutes he would not get anywhere else

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 39.0% from the field and 32.7% from three — both actively hurt you
  • No category he wins and 0.2 blocks; the profile is empty in year one
  • 10.0% from three across three Summer League games
  • Rotoworld ranked him 17th, eleven spots below his draft slot
