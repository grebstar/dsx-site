SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took Risacher first overall and only two of the seven boards agreed. RookieScale had him 1st, but Rotoworld ranked him 9th and NBA Draft Room 2nd — and the Rotoworld number is the honest one, because the fantasy objection is written plainly in the French line: 11.0 points, 4.5 rebounds and 0.9 assists in 23.1 minutes.

That is a role-player stat sheet. He shot 46.0% from the field and 35.9% from three with 1.3 makes a game, which is fine, and he contributed 0.8 steals and 0.5 blocks, which is fine, and there is not a single category where he is going to win you a week. In a nine-cat format the first pick in a draft should move a column. This one does not.

What Atlanta bought is a 19-year-old 6'9" wing with real defensive tools and a repeatable jumper on a roster that will hand him 30 minutes immediately. The floor is a starting NBA wing. The ceiling is the question, and it is why he is priced second on this composite rather than first — five of the seven boards had at least one other player ahead of him, Rotoworld as low as ninth.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Guaranteed immediate minutes as the No. 1 pick on a rebuilding roster
  • 35.9% from three on real volume at 19, in a professional league
  • 6'9" with the length and mobility to defend multiple positions
  • Contributes in both defensive categories without being a specialist in either

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 11.0 points and 0.9 assists — a role-player line from a first overall pick
  • Rotoworld ranked him 9th; only two of seven boards had him at the top
  • No category he wins, which is the specific problem in a nine-cat format
  • Atlanta is not a good enough situation to inflate counting stats
