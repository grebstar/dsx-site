SCOUTING REPORT

Edey is the most productive college player in this draft by a distance: 25.2 points, 12.2 rebounds and 2.2 blocks on 62.3% shooting as a senior, back-to-back National Player of the Year, in a major conference. Rotoworld ranked him 7th and NBA Draft Room 7th; RookieScale had him 17th and ClutchPoints 31st, and that spread is the entire argument.

For a category league the appeal is obvious. A 7'4" center producing double-digit rebounds and two blocks while shooting above 60% fills four columns, and Memphis had an actual opening at the position. The 2.2 blocks and 12.2 rebounds are the kind of raw volume you cannot find in the second round.

The objection is mobility, and it is not a small one — a center this size in a league playing five-out has to survive in space, and Edey's 0.3 steals hint at how little ground he covers. He is also 22.1, the second-oldest player in the lottery, and 71.1% from the line with zero three-point range caps the offensive profile. You are buying immediate production and a short runway.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 25.2 and 12.2 with 2.2 blocks on 62.3% — the most productive college season in the draft
  • Two-time National Player of the Year in a major conference
  • Memphis had a genuine opening at center and no reason to slow-play him
  • Fills points, rebounds, blocks and field goal percentage from one roster slot

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Mobility in space is the whole question, and 0.3 steals suggests the answer
  • 22.1 years old — second-oldest lottery pick, with almost no development runway
  • Zero three-point range in a league that increasingly requires it from centers
  • RookieScale ranked him 17th; the market was genuinely split
