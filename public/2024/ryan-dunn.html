SCOUTING REPORT

Dunn is the most extreme profile in the draft. 2.3 blocks and 1.3 steals from a 6'7" wing, plus 6.9 rebounds, on the 2024 ACC All-Defensive Team. In a nine-category league, a perimeter player who fills both defensive columns at those rates is close to unique — it is the same argument as Matas Buzelis, at a fraction of the price.

Rotoworld ranked him 15th against a 28th draft slot, the second-largest positive gap in the round. Denver took him and sent his rights to Phoenix as part of the DaRon Holmes II package.

The offence is the reason he was available. 20.0% from three on 0.2 makes and 53.2% from the free-throw line are two active category liabilities, and 8.1 points on 0.8 assists means there is nothing to offset them. Phoenix has minutes for a defensive wing on a roster of scorers, so he will play — but you are buying two columns and paying for them with two others. Priced 25th.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2.3 blocks and 1.3 steals from a wing — the scarcest category pair in the format
  • 6.9 rebounds and 54.8% from the field; the non-shooting parts are all positive
  • 2024 ACC All-Defensive Team; the defence is not a projection
  • Rotoworld ranked him 15th, thirteen spots above his draft slot

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 20.0% from three and 53.2% from the line — two active liabilities
  • 8.1 points and 0.8 assists; there is no offensive contribution to offset them
  • 7.7% from three across Summer League
  • Phoenix is built to win now, which limits patience with a non-shooter
