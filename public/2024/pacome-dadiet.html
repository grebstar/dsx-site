SCOUTING REPORT

Dadiet was one of the two youngest players in this draft and the efficiency at that age is real: 53.0% from the field and 38.3% from three for ratiopharm Ulm in the German BBL, in 15.3 minutes, as an 18-year-old. He added a EuroCup season on top of it. A wing making better than a third of his threes against grown professionals before his 19th birthday is a legitimate thing to buy.

Everything else argues wait. The volume is tiny — 6.9 points and 0.7 assists — and the defensive categories are empty at 0.5 steals and 0.1 blocks. His Las Vegas week was poor: 30.8% from the field and 10.5% from three across five games, the worst three-point mark of any first-rounder that July, which is why his post-Vegas re-rating is the most negative on this board.

And New York is the wrong roster entirely. The Knicks won 50 games with Jalen Brunson, Mikal Bridges and OG Anunoby, and there is no wing minute available for two years. RookieScale ranked him 31st and Rotoworld 28th, both below his draft slot. This is a stash on age and shooting, and nothing else.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 53.0% from the field and 38.3% from three in a top European league at 18
  • One of the two youngest players in the entire draft class
  • Two professional seasons of habits before his NBA rookie year
  • New York can afford to develop him slowly with zero pressure

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 10.5% from three across five Summer League games — the worst of any first-rounder
  • 6.9 points and 0.7 assists; the raw volume is negligible
  • 0.5 steals and 0.1 blocks — both defensive columns are empty
  • A 50-win Knicks roster has no wing minutes for two seasons
