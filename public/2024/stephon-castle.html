SCOUTING REPORT

Castle is the sharpest disagreement among the top five on this board. NBA Draft Room ranked him 5th and RookieScale 4th; Rotoworld ranked him 12th, behind Bub Carrington and Cody Williams. The gap is entirely about the jump shot.

He shot 26.7% from three on 0.6 makes a game at UConn, which in a nine-cat format is a dead category and an active drag on your percentages. Around that hole is a genuinely promising 19-year-old: 11.1 points, 4.7 rebounds and 2.9 assists on 47.2% shooting, with the size to defend three positions and a national championship pedigree.

San Antonio is a good landing spot with a caveat. The Spurs have Victor Wembanyama and a clear runway for a young lead guard — but they also took Rob Dillingham's rights and traded them, and Chris Paul was brought in to run the offense. Castle will play. Whether he handles the ball enough to fill the assist column in year one is the open question, and it is why his Pathway score sits a touch below his draft slot.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 6'6" lead guard with the size to defend up and a national title pedigree
  • 47.2% from the field at 19 while carrying real creation duty
  • San Antonio's timeline points directly at handing him the offense
  • 4.7 rebounds from the guard slot is a genuine category contribution

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 26.7% from three on 0.6 makes — a fully dead category and a percentage drag
  • Rotoworld ranked him 12th, behind two players taken well after him
  • Chris Paul's arrival caps the year-one assist share
  • 37.0% shooting across three Summer League games
