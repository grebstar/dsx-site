SCOUTING REPORT

Buzelis produced 2.1 blocks and 0.8 steals from a forward slot for G League Ignite at 19, alongside 14.3 points and 6.9 rebounds. A wing who supplies both defensive categories at that rate is the single most valuable archetype in a nine-cat league, and it is why he sits in the top eight here despite going 11th.

The hole is the jumper: 27.3% from three on 0.9 makes, with 67.9% from the line offering little reassurance. He also turned it over 2.2 times a game against 2.0 assists. Chicago's rebuild guarantees minutes, and Rotoworld ranked him 8th, RookieScale 5th — consistent top-ten placement from both.
