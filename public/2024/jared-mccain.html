SCOUTING REPORT

McCain shot 41.4% from three on 2.4 makes a game and 88.5% from the line as a Duke freshman — the best free-throw mark in the class and the strongest single predictor of shooting translation there is. Add 5.0 rebounds and 1.1 steals from a guard and the category profile has no dead spots except blocks.

Philadelphia is the constraint. The Sixers had Tyrese Maxey, Kyle Lowry and Eric Gordon in the backcourt and were built to win immediately, so year-one minutes were never going to be clean. His Summer League was also poor — 28.6% from the field across eight games — which is why the post-Vegas mark is negative despite consistent board placement in the teens.

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Maxey, Lowry and Gordon all ahead of him on a win-now roster
  • 0.1 blocks and only 1.9 assists — a scorer more than a creator
  • Philadelphia's injury history cuts both ways on rotation stability
