SCOUTING REPORT

Topic went 12th to Oklahoma City with a partially torn ACL already diagnosed, and Rotoworld still ranked him 4th in the class — ahead of Risacher, Castle and Edey. That is an unusual thing for a board to do, and it is a measure of how much they liked the profile: 14.5 points and 5.5 assists on 49.8% shooting with 87.8% from the line at 18 in the Adriatic League.

An 18-year-old lead guard producing 5.5 assists efficiently against grown professionals is the rarest thing in this draft. The free-throw number in particular suggests the jumper follows.

Everything else argues for patience. He did not play Summer League, so there is no post-draft sample at all and no post-Vegas re-rating. Oklahoma City won 57 games the year before and had Shai Gilgeous-Alexander plus a full guard rotation, so even healthy there was nowhere to play him. The Pathway score of 30 against a model base of 74 is the largest downgrade on this board, and it is a medical judgment as much as a depth-chart one.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 5.5 assists at 18 in a real professional league on 49.8% shooting
  • 87.8% from the line — the strongest shooting indicator of any guard in the class
  • Rotoworld ranked him 4th knowing about the ACL
  • Oklahoma City has no pressure to rush him and the league's best development program

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Drafted with a torn ACL; no Summer League and no post-draft sample of any kind
  • 2.3 turnovers against 5.5 assists, and 30.6% from three
  • A 57-win roster with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander has nowhere to play a rookie guard
  • The largest opportunity downgrade on this board, on medical and depth-chart grounds together
