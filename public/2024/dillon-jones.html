SCOUTING REPORT

Jones put up 20.8 points, 9.8 rebounds, 5.2 assists and 2.0 steals as a Weber State senior, which is a stat line you basically never see outside mid-major basketball. Four columns filled at high volume, with 85.7% from the free-throw line on top. Rotoworld ranked him 26th; RookieScale had him 43rd, and that seventeen-place gap is the argument about level.

Washington drafted him, New York took his rights in the Kyshawn George swap, and Oklahoma City then paid five future second-round picks for him — a striking price for a 22-year-old from the Big Sky.

The problem is that they had just won 57 games and were about to win 68. There is no wing minute in Oklahoma City for years, he is already 22.7, and the production came against competition several tiers below the NBA with 3.0 turnovers and 32.4% from three. A category league buys the shape and cannot buy the opportunity. Pathway 34 is the lowest on this board.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 20.8 / 9.8 / 5.2 with 2.0 steals — four categories at genuine volume
  • 85.7% from the line suggests the jumper eventually arrives
  • Oklahoma City surrendered five second-round picks to acquire him

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Big Sky competition; RookieScale ranked him 43rd on exactly that basis
