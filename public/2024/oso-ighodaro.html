SCOUTING REPORT

Ighodaro's last college season at Marquette was a 13.4 point, 6.9 rebound, 2.9 assist year in 32.5 minutes over 36 games, with 1.3 blocks and 1.1 steals and 57.6% shooting. Six boards covered him, placing him between 30th and 47th, for a composite rank of 42nd.

The profile was a rim-running, passing big: 0-for-2 from three and 62.3% at the line as a junior. Those two shooting numbers have defined his NBA path. As a rookie he played 61 games at 17.1 minutes; in year two he played all 82 and made 24 starts.

Phoenix reached the playoffs at 45-37 and lost in the first round to Oklahoma City, with Ighodaro starting all four games. Heading into 2026-27 his salary has been fully guaranteed.

Ighodaro went 40th overall in the 2024 draft. Basketball-Reference lists Portland as the drafting team, while RealGM and Hoops Rumors say a series of draft-night trades ended with him in Phoenix. He signed a four-year contract with the Suns, reported by Hoops Rumors as worth about $7.9 million, fully guaranteed for two seasons, partially guaranteed in year three with a team option in year four.

On Jun. 2, 2026, RealGM, citing Bobby Marks of ESPN, reported Phoenix moved up the guarantee on his 2026-27 salary, which had started at $250,000 guaranteed and would have reached $2.3 million at the Jan. 10 guarantee date. RealGM lists his contract status as team option in 2027 and unrestricted free agency in 2028.
