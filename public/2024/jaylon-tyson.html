SCOUTING REPORT

Tyson's junior year at California is a genuinely large all-round line: 19.6 points, 6.8 rebounds and 3.5 assists with 1.2 steals, on 46.5% shooting. A wing who rebounds and passes at those rates fills three columns most wings fill one of, and he backed it up with the best all-round Summer League of any late first-rounder — 15.4 / 7.0 / 4.4 on 58.8% from the field.

Two real discounts. He is on his third school in three years (Texas, then Texas Tech, then California), and the production came on a bad Pac-12 team where he had the ball constantly — 3.1 turnovers is the cost of that usage. The boards were cool on him: Rotoworld 22nd, RookieScale 30th.

The bigger discount is Cleveland. The Cavaliers won 48 games the year before with Donovan Mitchell, Darius Garland, Evan Mobley and Jarrett Allen, and they are trying to win now. There is no wing rotation spot and no reason to create one. Pathway 44 against a model base of 66 is the largest opportunity downgrade in the back half of this round; this is a stash, and it is priced as one.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 19.6 / 6.8 / 3.5 — a three-column line from a wing, with 1.2 steals on top
  • 15.4 / 7.0 / 4.4 on 58.8% shooting in Las Vegas, the best all-round summer of any late first-rounder
  • Cleveland's development infrastructure is among the league's best
  • Rotoworld ranked him 22nd, comfortably above his draft slot

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Cleveland won 64 games; there is no rotation spot for two seasons
  • 3.1 turnovers a game — the usage produced the counting stats and the mistakes
  • Three schools in three years and one good season out of three
  • RookieScale ranked him 30th; the consensus was a late-first flier
