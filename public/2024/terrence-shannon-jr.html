SCOUTING REPORT

Shannon averaged 23.0 points on 47.5% shooting with 2.4 made threes as an Illinois senior, second in the Big Ten in scoring, and added 0.9 blocks from the wing, which is unusual. He is a downhill, explosive scorer who gets to the line — 80.1% on heavy attempts — and NBA Draft Room ranked him 16th in the class on that basis.

Rotoworld ranked him 39th. The gap is about age and role: he turns 24 in July, which makes him the oldest player taken in the first round, and a specialist scorer with 2.3 assists has a narrow path to fantasy relevance on a good team.

Minnesota is a good team — 56 wins the previous season, with Anthony Edwards and Jaden McDaniels ahead of him — so the minutes are not there in year one. It should also be recorded plainly that he was charged in December 2023, suspended by Illinois, reinstated by court order, and found not guilty by a unanimous jury on June 13, 2024, two weeks before the draft. Teams treated the matter as closed. Priced 31st, between RookieScale and Rotoworld.
