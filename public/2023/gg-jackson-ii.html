SCOUTING REPORT

Jackson is the youngest player in the draft by four months — 18.5 on draft night — because he reclassified into the 2022 high-school class and played his college season at 17. That context is the whole listing.

The raw line is not good: 15.4 points on 38.4% from the field with 2.7 turnovers, 0.8 assists and 67.7% from the line, for a losing South Carolina team. He was a five-star recruit playing a year early against grown men, and it looked like it.

What is buyable is 5.9 rebounds and 0.8 blocks with 1.7 made threes from a 6'9" forward who will not turn 20 until his second NBA season. RookieScale ranked him 37th against a 45th slot; Fantrax had him 46th. Memphis won 51 games but was already deep in a youth movement. This is the longest-dated bet on the listed board, priced 35th.
