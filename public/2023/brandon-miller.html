SCOUTING REPORT

Miller is the most complete freshman scoring profile in the class: 18.8 points, 8.2 rebounds and 2.9 made threes at 38.4% in the SEC, at 6'9", with 85.9% from the free-throw line. A wing that size rebounding at eight a game while shooting that volume from deep is a genuinely scarce shape, and it is why he went second overall.

Four of five boards ranked him third rather than second, behind Scoot Henderson, which is a mild demotion rather than an argument — the reasoning is that Henderson touches the ball more and Miller's 2.1 assists mean he does not create for others.

The category objection is efficiency. 43.0% from the field on that shot diet is ordinary, and 0.9 steals with 0.9 blocks means the defensive columns are contributions rather than assets. Charlotte won 27 games the previous season and has no one to keep him off the floor, so year-one volume is guaranteed. Priced third here, between the two guards.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 18.8 and 8.2 with 2.9 made threes — scoring, rebounding and shooting from one wing slot
  • 85.9% from the line, the strongest shooting-translation indicator in the class
  • 6'9" with genuine positional size for the archetype
  • Charlotte has nothing at the position; the usage is his on arrival

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 43.0% from the field is unremarkable for the volume he takes
  • 2.1 assists — a scorer who does not yet make others better
  • All five boards ranked him behind Henderson
  • 26.1% from three across three Summer League games
