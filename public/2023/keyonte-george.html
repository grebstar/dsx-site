SCOUTING REPORT

RookieScale and Fantrax both ranked George 16th — the exact slot Utah took him at. What they were pricing is volume creation from a 19-year-old: 15.3 points and 2.8 assists with 2.3 made threes, plus 4.2 rebounds and 1.1 steals from the guard slot.

The efficiency is bad and the boards knew it. 37.6% from the field and 33.8% from three, with 2.9 turnovers against 2.8 assists, is a freshman taking shots he has not earned. The one indicator arguing the shot arrives is 79.3% from the line.

What lifts him above the model is Utah. The Jazz had traded away their veteran guards and had nothing at the position, so a 19-year-old was going to get the ball and keep it regardless of what happened — Pathway 86 against a base of 76. He also had the best Las Vegas week of any guard in the class, 21.7 points and 6.3 assists on 52.5/44.4 shooting, which is why the post-Vegas mark is positive.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Utah has no guards; the ball is his from day one regardless of results
  • 2.3 made threes and 4.2 rebounds from a 19-year-old lead guard
  • 21.7 points and 6.3 assists on 44.4% from three in Las Vegas
  • 79.3% from the line is the indicator that the efficiency eventually follows

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 37.6% from the field — poor efficiency on genuinely heavy usage
  • 2.9 turnovers against 2.8 assists is a losing ratio
  • 0.2 blocks and a thin frame; the defensive profile is limited
  • RookieScale and Fantrax ranked him exactly at his slot — no market edge either way
