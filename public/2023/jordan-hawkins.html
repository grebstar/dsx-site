SCOUTING REPORT

Hawkins shot 2.9 threes a game at 38.8% with 88.7% from the free-throw line as UConn's leading scorer on a national championship team. That volume-plus-accuracy-plus-free-throws combination is the strongest pure shooting profile in the class, and Fantrax ranked him 9th on it — five spots above his draft slot.

RookieScale was more sceptical at 15th, and the reason is everything else. 40.9% from the field, 1.3 assists, 0.7 steals, 3.8 rebounds: a specialist who lives entirely off movement shooting, with no secondary category to fall back on if the shot goes cold.

He is also 21.1, a sophomore rather than a freshman, so there is less growth to buy than the players around him. New Orleans won 42 games with a crowded wing rotation, but shooting is the one thing every roster needs. Priced 14th — a narrow, immediately useful asset with a low ceiling.

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 1.3 assists and 0.7 steals — no secondary category anywhere
  • 21.1 years old, with less development runway than the players around him
  • 25.0% from three across five Summer League games
