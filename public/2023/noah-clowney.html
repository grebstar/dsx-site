SCOUTING REPORT

Clowney was one of the youngest players in the draft — 18 years old on draft night — and rebounded like a starter: 7.9 boards in 25.4 minutes with 0.9 blocks, on 48.6% shooting, for an SEC contender. Rebounds and blocks from a teenager is a legitimate thing to buy on a three-year horizon.

The boards diverged more than usual: RookieScale 21st, Fantrax 27th. The six-place gap is a disagreement about the jumper, because the whole modern case for a 6'10" four rests on it, and he shot 28.3% from three on 0.9 makes with 64.9% from the line — the second number being the more worrying one, since it is the better predictor.

Brooklyn won 45 games but was heading for a teardown, so the runway is genuine and the pressure is nil. His Las Vegas week was poor at 22.6% from the field. This is a two-to-three-year hold on age and rebounding, priced 25th.
