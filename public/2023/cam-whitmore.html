SCOUTING REPORT

Whitmore is the largest board-versus-slot gap in the first round. RookieScale ranked him 8th; he sat in the green room and fell to 20th, twelve places below where RookieScale had him. Fantrax was cooler at 15th, which still puts him five spots above his pick.

The Villanova line is a scorer's line with a defensive bonus: 12.5 points and 5.3 rebounds on 47.8% shooting with 1.4 steals, in only 26 games. He is 6'7" and built like a strong safety, and the steal rate suggests the athleticism translates to defence rather than just highlight finishes.

Then he went to Las Vegas and won Summer League MVP, leading the entire event in scoring at 19.3 a game — which is why his post-Vegas re-rating is the largest positive on this board. What kept teams away was reportedly medical and interview-related rather than statistical, and 0.7 assists confirms he is a scorer who does not create. Houston had a crowded young wing rotation, so the minutes are the constraint. Priced 7th, thirteen spots above his draft slot.
