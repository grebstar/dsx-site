SCOUTING REPORT

Jaquez is the most polished player in the back half of this first round and the one with the least left to develop. 17.8 points, 8.2 rebounds and 1.5 steals on 48.1% shooting as a UCLA senior, Pac-12 Player of the Year, four years of high-level college basketball. Everything he does, he already does.

Fantrax ranked him 12th and RookieScale 19th, and the seven-place gap is a disagreement about whether that polish is worth anything. He is 22.3, the oldest wing taken in the round.

The category concern is the jumper: 31.7% from three on 0.9 makes, which in the modern NBA caps a wing's minutes regardless of how good the rest is. What offsets it is Miami. The Heat had an immediate need at his position and a track record of extracting production from exactly this archetype, so the Pathway score of 86 towers over a model base of 74. Priced 22nd, four spots below his draft slot.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 17.8 and 8.2 with 1.5 steals — three columns filled from a wing slot
  • 48.1% from the field; the efficiency is real and came against top competition
  • Miami has an immediate need and a record with this exact archetype
  • Pac-12 Player of the Year; nothing about the skill set is projection

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 31.7% from three on 0.9 makes — the jumper caps the ceiling
  • 22.3 years old after four college seasons; almost no growth left to buy
  • RookieScale ranked him 19th, one spot below his draft slot
  • His Summer League ended after two games with a shoulder injury
