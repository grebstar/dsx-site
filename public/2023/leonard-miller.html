SCOUTING REPORT

Miller's pre-draft season with the G League Ignite was a 16.9 point, 10.1 rebound, 1.6 assist year in 29.7 minutes over 38 games (30 starts), counting the Showcase Cup and the regular season, with 53.7% shooting, 30.4% from three on 92 attempts and 79.2% at the line. He had 17 double-doubles, played in the Rising Stars and Next Up games at 2023 All-Star Weekend, and averaged 20.7 points and 13.0 rebounds over the 11 regular-season games after the break.

San Antonio took him 33rd overall on Jun. 22, 2023, and sent his rights to Minnesota the next day for two future second-round picks. Five boards covered him, placing him between 13th and 30th, for a composite rank of 19th.

Minnesota kept him mostly in the G League for two years. He played 17 NBA games at 3.1 minutes as a rookie and 13 at 2.5 minutes in 2024-25, with three playoff appearances each spring, while averaging 19.4 and then 24.7 points for the Iowa Wolves. After 19 games at 5.0 minutes in 2025-26, Minnesota traded him and Rob Dillingham to Chicago on Feb. 5, 2026 for Ayo Dosunmu and Julian Phillips. With the Bulls he played 27 games, started 12 and averaged 11.7 points and 5.8 rebounds, with a career-high 26 points on Apr. 9 at Washington.

He signed a four-year, $8,319,739 contract with Minnesota on Jul. 8, 2023. Chicago exercised the $2,406,205 option for 2026-27 in late June 2026, and he is set to become an unrestricted free agent in 2027.

Under new coach Tiago Splitter he led the Bulls in preseason scoring at 17.5 points per game. Through five regular-season games he has come off the bench each night, averaging 9.8 points and 4.4 rebounds in 20.2 minutes.
