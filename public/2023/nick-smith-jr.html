SCOUTING REPORT

Smith is one of the widest disagreements in the class: RookieScale 18th, Fantrax 31st. Thirteen places, and the argument is entirely about whether you price the recruit or the season.

The recruit was a consensus five-star and a projected top-five pick. The season was 17 games, wrecked by a persistent knee injury, in which he shot 37.6% from the field and 33.8% from three and grabbed 1.6 rebounds a game. On the raw line there is nothing to buy; on the pedigree and the explanation there is quite a lot.

Charlotte won 27 games and had guard minutes available, which helps. But 1.6 rebounds and 0.1 blocks from a 6'2" guard means the only category he can ever win is threes, and the durability score of 3.0 reflects a knee that has already cost him most of a season. Priced 20th, between RookieScale and Fantrax, which is the honest place for a prospect this unresolved.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Consensus five-star recruit once projected in the top five
  • 1.4 made threes at 33.8% despite playing hurt all season
  • RookieScale ranked him 18th, nine spots above his draft slot
  • Charlotte's backcourt has minutes available immediately

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 17 games and a knee that cost him most of his college season
  • 37.6% from the field — the efficiency was poor even when he played
  • 1.6 rebounds and 0.1 blocks; nothing outside the jumper
  • Fantrax ranked him 31st, four spots below the second round
