SCOUTING REPORT

Wallace's Kentucky line is built on the scarcest category in the format: 2.0 steals a game, alongside 4.3 assists and 1.4 made threes at 34.6%. A guard who defends at that level while running an offence is a genuinely valuable roster piece, and the draft-night trade says so — Dallas drafted him 10th and sent him, with Davis Bertans, to Oklahoma City for the 12th pick and Dereck Lively II.

The boards were split. RookieScale ranked him 14th, Fantrax 20th, and the six-place gap is about upside: he does not create his own shot at volume, and 11.7 points on 32.1 minutes is a modest scoring rate for a lottery pick.

The real discount is where he landed. Oklahoma City won 40 games the previous season with Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Josh Giddey and Lu Dort already absorbing the guard minutes, and was about to become a 57-win team. There is no path to volume for years. Pathway 56 against a model base of 84 is the largest opportunity downgrade in the lottery, and it is why a genuinely good prospect prices 16th here.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2.0 steals per game — the scarcest category in the format, at an elite rate
  • 4.3 assists with 1.4 made threes; the offensive profile is not empty
  • Oklahoma City moved up two spots and absorbed Davis Bertans' contract to get him
  • Elite point-of-attack defence translates faster than any offensive skill

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Oklahoma City won 40 games with three guards ahead of him
  • 11.7 points in 32.1 minutes — a low scoring rate for a top-ten pick
  • Fantrax ranked him 20th, ten spots below his draft slot
  • 34.6% shooting across five Summer League games
