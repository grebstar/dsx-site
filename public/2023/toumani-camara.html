SCOUTING REPORT

Camara was a Dayton senior at 13.9 points, 8.6 rebounds and 1.7 assists with 1.2 steals and 0.8 blocks in 30.0 minutes over 34 games, after two seasons at Georgia. He was 23.1 on draft night. Five boards covered him (composite rank 55, ask 48, bid 55), which priced him as a second-round rotation forward.

Phoenix took him 52nd and sent him to Portland three months later in the Lillard trade. He started 49 games as a rookie, then all 78 in 2024-25 at 1.5 steals and made the All-Defensive Second Team.

In 2025-26 he started all 82 games at 13.4 points and 2.7 threes a game, and Portland extended him for four years and $82 million. He now sits as a long-term starter on a 42-40 team.

Phoenix drafted him 52nd overall on Jun. 22, 2023 and signed him to a multi-year contract on Jul. 3, 2023. On Sep. 27, 2023 he went to Portland with Deandre Ayton and Jrue Holiday in the three-team deal that sent Damian Lillard to Milwaukee.

He debuted on Oct. 25, 2023 and started 49 of 70 games as a rookie. On Oct. 19, 2025, Portland declined its 2026-27 team option on him and agreed to a four-year, $82 million extension through 2029-30, per ESPN's Shams Charania via RealGM.
