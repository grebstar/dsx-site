SCOUTING REPORT

Amen Thompson's Overtime Elite line is the most unusual in the class: 16.7 points, 6.2 rebounds, 6.0 assists and 2.2 steals on 53.4% shooting. A 6'7" player producing at point-guard rates with the steal volume of an elite perimeter defender is exactly the archetype a nine-category roster is built around, and the boards had him 4th or 5th.

The athleticism is the headline — he is arguably the best pure athlete in a draft that also contains his twin brother — and it converts directly into the two categories that are hardest to buy, steals and efficient interior scoring.

Then the shot, and it is severe. 24.1% from three on 0.7 makes and 66.7% from the free-throw line: two dead categories and an active percentage drag. 3.1 turnovers against 6.0 assists compounds it. The competition question is also real, because Overtime Elite is a developmental league with no established translation baseline. Houston won 22 games and can afford the wait; this is priced as a two-year hold.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2.2 steals with 6.0 assists from a 6'7" forward — a shape almost nobody in the league has
  • 53.4% from the field; the interior scoring is efficient, not just athletic
  • 16.7 / 6.2 / 6.0 is a three-column line before the defence is counted
  • Houston has 22 wins and every reason to develop him without pressure

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 24.1% from three and 66.7% from the line — two dead categories and a drag
  • 3.1 turnovers against 6.0 assists
  • Overtime Elite has no established translation baseline for NBA projection
  • His Summer League ended after one game with a Grade 2 ankle sprain
