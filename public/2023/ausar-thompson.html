SCOUTING REPORT

The twins are separated by almost nothing on this board — RookieScale had Amen 4th and Ausar 5th, Fantrax reversed them — and the statistical difference is one column. Ausar gives you 2.2 steals AND 1.0 blocks from a wing, where his brother gives you assists.

Filling both defensive categories from a single perimeter slot is the rarest thing a category-league roster can buy, and Ausar does it while also producing 16.0 points, 6.2 rebounds and 5.1 assists. He then went to Las Vegas and pulled 10.0 rebounds a game across four games, which is a wing rebounding like a centre.

He carries the same two holes as his brother, slightly less severely: 29.5% from three and 65.9% from the line, with 3.1 turnovers. Detroit won 17 games and has nothing at the position, so the minutes are immediate and unconditional. The offer is for the defence, and it assumes the shooting stays broken.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2.2 steals and 1.0 blocks from a wing — both defensive columns from one slot
  • 10.0 rebounds a game in Las Vegas; the rebounding is real and positional-defying
  • 16.0 / 6.2 / 5.1 against professionals at 20
  • Detroit won 17 games; there is nothing between him and 30 minutes

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 29.5% from three and 65.9% from the line — the same two holes as his brother
  • 3.1 turnovers a game
  • Overtime Elite provides no translation baseline
  • 45.5% from the field is modest for a player who scores mostly inside
