SCOUTING REPORT

No player in this class divides RookieScale and Fantrax further. Fantrax ranked Strawther 24th; RookieScale ranked him 42nd — eighteen places, on the same Gonzaga season.

Fantrax was looking at the shooting and the size: 2.2 made threes at 40.8% from a 6'7" wing, with 6.2 rebounds, which is a lot of rebounding for a perimeter player and the reason the profile has more than one column. RookieScale was looking at 1.3 assists, 0.8 steals and the fact that he turns 21 before his rookie season, at a position where teams draft for upside.

Indiana selected him 29th and traded him to Denver, and Denver is the problem for a category league. The Nuggets were the defending champions with Michael Porter Jr., Aaron Gordon and Kentavious Caldwell-Pope absorbing every wing minute. Pathway 48 against a base of 62 — the lowest opportunity score on this board. Priced 39th, between the two boards that could not agree.
