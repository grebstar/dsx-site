SCOUTING REPORT

Jackson-Davis produced the most complete big-man season in the class and went 57th. 20.9 points, 10.8 rebounds, 4.0 assists and 2.9 blocks on 58.2% shooting as an Indiana senior — a consensus First-Team All-American who left as the school's all-time leader in both rebounds and blocks.

The gap between that line and the 57th pick is the largest on the board, and RookieScale and Fantrax disagreed about how much of it to close: RookieScale ranked him 34th, Fantrax 51st. Washington drafted him and sold his rights to Golden State for cash.

The reasons he fell are real. He is 23.3, he attempted zero three-pointers all season, and 69.5% from the line is a drag. A 6'9" centre with no range in a five-out league has a narrow path. But blocks and rebounds at those rates from a second-round contract is the definition of a value listing, and it is why he is one of only two players outside the first round to get a page here. Priced 41st.
