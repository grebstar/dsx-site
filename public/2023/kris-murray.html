SCOUTING REPORT

Murray's Iowa junior year is a big, well-rounded forward line: 20.2 points, 7.9 rebounds, 2.3 made threes and 1.2 blocks on 47.6% shooting. Points, boards, threes and blocks from one frontcourt slot is four columns, and Fantrax ranked him 18th on it, five spots above his draft slot.

The discount is age and inheritance. He is 22.8, a three-year college player whose twin brother Keegan went fourth overall a year earlier and immediately became a starter — which invites a comparison Kris cannot win. RookieScale had him 23rd, exactly at his slot.

The category concerns are the percentages: 33.5% from three despite the volume, and 72.9% from the line, both of which will cost you weekly. Portland won 33 games and was tearing down, so a 22-year-old forward will get real minutes immediately — which is worth something now and very little in three years. Priced 18th.
