SCOUTING REPORT

All five boards ranked Henderson second, ahead of the player Charlotte took second overall, and the case is the shape of the line: 16.5 points, 6.5 assists and 5.4 rebounds for G League Ignite at 18, against paid professionals rather than college freshmen. A lead guard who rebounds at five a game is filling a column most point guards leave empty.

He is also the best athlete in the class by a distance — a downhill force who gets to the rim at will and finishes through contact at 6'2". Two years of professional basketball before his 19th birthday is unusual preparation, and it shows in the passing reads.

Two real costs. 27.5% from three on 0.7 makes means defences will go under every screen, and 3.5 turnovers against 6.5 assists is a poor ratio even for a teenager with the ball constantly. And Portland is a genuine complication: Damian Lillard was still on the roster on draft night, and a rookie point guard behind a franchise point guard has no path. That situation resolved in September, which is why the offer treats it as temporary.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 16.5 / 6.5 / 5.4 against professionals at 18 — the most complete guard line in the class
  • Elite downhill athleticism; he gets to the rim whenever he decides to
  • All five boards ranked him second, above his draft slot
  • Two full professional seasons of habits before his rookie year

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 27.5% from three on 0.7 makes — defences will simply go under
  • 3.5 turnovers against 6.5 assists; the decision-making is not there yet
  • Damian Lillard was on the roster on draft night, with no clear runway
  • His Summer League lasted one game before a shoulder injury
