SCOUTING REPORT

Sensabaugh scored 16.3 points in 24.4 minutes as an Ohio State freshman on a shooting line almost nobody in this class matches: 48.0% from the field, 40.5% from three on 1.8 makes, 83.0% from the free-throw line. Per-minute, he is the most efficient volume scorer in the draft.

Fantrax ranked him 21st and RookieScale 27th, both at or above the 28th slot Utah used on him. What pushed him down draft boards was defence: 0.5 steals and 0.4 blocks from a forward, with a body and a motor scouts questioned all season.

He also missed the entire 2023 Summer League with a knee injury — the same knee that ended his college season — so there is no post-draft sample and no post-Vegas re-rating. Utah's roster was the emptiest in the league, which is why his Pathway score of 78 sits well above the model base of 64. This is a bet on shot-making at a late-first price.
