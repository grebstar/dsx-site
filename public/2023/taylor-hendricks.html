SCOUTING REPORT

Hendricks has the cleanest nine-category shape in the draft after Wembanyama. 1.7 blocks and 1.8 made threes at 39.4% from the same 6'9" player is the rarest combination the format offers — rim protection and floor spacing almost never arrive together, and buying them in one roster slot is worth a premium. Add 7.0 rebounds and 78.2% from the line and there is no dead column anywhere.

Fantrax ranked him 7th and RookieScale 9th, both at or above his draft slot, on precisely this reasoning. Utah won 37 games and had an open frontcourt, so the minutes were available immediately.

Two caveats. The volume is moderate — 15.1 points on 34.7 minutes is not a scorer's rate — and 1.4 assists means he does not touch the ball much. And he did not play a single Summer League game, sidelined by injury, so there is no post-draft sample and no post-Vegas re-rating here at all. That is the one thing keeping him behind the top eight.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 1.7 blocks and 39.4% from three — the scarcest category pair in the format
  • 7.0 rebounds and 78.2% from the line; there is no dead column in the profile
  • Fantrax ranked him 7th, two spots above his draft slot
  • Utah's frontcourt was open and his path to 25 minutes was immediate

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 15.1 points in 34.7 minutes; the offensive volume is modest
  • 1.4 assists — a low-touch player dependent on others creating
  • Missed the entire 2023 Summer League injured; no post-draft sample exists
  • 47.8% from the field is ordinary for a player taking that many threes
