SCOUTING REPORT

Sasser shot 2.7 threes a game at 38.4% with 84.8% from the line and added 1.6 steals for a Houston team built around its defence. Shooting plus steals from a guard is two real columns, and Detroit thought enough of it to send Boston the 31st pick and two future seconds to move up and take him.

The boards were cooler than the front office: Fantrax 28th, RookieScale 33rd, both below his slot. The reason is age and size. He is 22.8, a four-year senior, and at 6'2" with 2.8 rebounds he has no secondary category to fall back on.

Detroit won 17 games and had genuinely nothing at the position, so year-one minutes were assured — Pathway 76 against a base of 64. He then closed Summer League with 40 points on 16-of-25 with zero turnovers. This is priced 36th: immediately useful, with the shortest runway of anyone listed.
