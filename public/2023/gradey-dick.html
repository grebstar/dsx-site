SCOUTING REPORT

Dick is a consensus pick — RookieScale, Fantrax and CBS all ranked him exactly 10th. The Kansas line explains the consensus: 2.3 made threes at 40.3% with 85.4% from the free-throw line, the two numbers that predict NBA shooting better than any others.

What lifts him above a pure specialist is the rest of it. 5.1 rebounds and 1.4 steals from a shooting guard are real contributions in two columns most shooters leave blank, and at 6'8" he has the size to be more than a spot-up option.

The limits are ordinary rather than damning: 1.7 assists and 0.2 blocks, and 44.2% from the field, meaning almost all of the value routes through the jumper. Toronto won 41 games and was beginning a teardown, so the minutes are available and the pressure is low. This is priced as a safe, narrow asset rather than a swing.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 40.3% from three on 2.3 makes, plus 85.4% from the line — the two best shooting indicators
  • 5.1 rebounds and 1.4 steals from a wing; the profile is wider than the archetype
  • RookieScale, Fantrax and CBS ranked him 10th, above his draft slot, with no disagreement among them
  • 6'8" with the size to survive defensively while the shot carries him

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 1.7 assists and 0.2 blocks; the value is almost entirely one skill
  • 44.2% from the field — ordinary efficiency inside the arc
  • 29.6% from three across four Summer League games
  • Toronto is beginning a rebuild, which caps team-context value
