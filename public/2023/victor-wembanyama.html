SCOUTING REPORT

All five boards had him first and nobody argued. That never happens — the 2024 class could not agree on its No. 1 pick and the 2025 class split three ways on the second slot. Wembanyama is the only prospect this exchange has priced at $1,000.00 without a dissenting voice anywhere.

The French line is why: 20.7 points, 10.2 rebounds and 3.0 blocks in 32.2 minutes for Metropolitans 92, with 1.3 made threes a game at 7'4". In a nine-category format that is four columns at once from one roster slot, and the blocks alone would justify a top-three price. He also shot 82.2% from the line, which for a big man of any size is the number that separates an asset from a liability.

There is no bear case worth the name, only risks. He is listed at 7'4" on a frame the NBA has never successfully stress-tested over a career, and 2.8 turnovers say the handle invites pressure. San Antonio won 22 games the year before and will give him the ball, the minutes and the losses. This is the offer the whole board is measured against.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 3.0 blocks and 1.3 made threes from the same player — a combination that has no precedent
  • 20.7 and 10.2 against grown professionals at 19, in a real European league
  • 82.2% from the free-throw line, which removes the usual big-man category drag
  • First on all five boards, with no dissent anywhere in the class

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 7'4" on a slight frame; durability is the entire long-term question
  • 2.8 turnovers a game — the ball-handling invites NBA pressure
  • San Antonio is a 22-win roster with nothing around him in year one
  • 46.9% from the field is unremarkable for a player who should live at the rim
