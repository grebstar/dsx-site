SCOUTING REPORT

Howard shot 2.7 threes a game at 36.8% with 80.0% from the line as a Michigan freshman, at 6'8". Size plus shooting volume is a legitimately valuable combination, and it is the entire case — Fantrax ranked him 14th, RookieScale 20th, both below the 11th slot Orlando spent on him.

Everything around the jumper is thin. 2.8 rebounds from a 6'8" wing is startlingly low, 0.4 steals means no defensive playmaking, and 41.4% from the field says the inside game does not exist. He is a specialist without the specialist's efficiency.

Orlando is also the wrong place. The Magic won 34 games the year before and were about to win 47, with Paolo Banchero, Franz Wagner and a young defensive core already occupying the wing minutes — and Howard's defensive profile is exactly what a team like that hides. Pathway 54 against a base of 80. Priced 21st, ten spots below his draft slot.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2.7 made threes a game at 6'8"; the size-and-shooting combination is real
  • 80.0% from the line supports the jumper translating
  • Orlando's spacing needs are genuine, whatever the depth chart says
  • 40.0% from three across three Summer League games

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 2.8 rebounds from a 6'8" wing — an alarming figure
  • 0.4 steals and 41.4% from the field; nothing works outside the jumper
  • All five boards ranked him below his draft slot
  • Orlando's wing rotation is full and its defensive standards are high
