SCOUTING REPORT

The draft-night trade is the most interesting fact about him. Washington held the 8th pick and wanted the 7th, so it sent that pick and two second-rounders to Indiana for the right to move up one place and take Bilal Coulibaly. Indiana slid down a spot, collected the two seconds, and took Walker — which is to say the Pacers were paid to draft him. RookieScale and Fantrax thought that was a bargain: RookieScale 7th, Fantrax 6th, above his slot.

The Houston line is a defender's line: 6.8 rebounds, 1.3 blocks and 1.0 steals in 27.6 minutes on a top-ranked defence, from a 6'8" forward with the strength to guard fives and the feet to guard threes. He adds 1.0 made threes at 34.7%, which is enough to keep the floor honest.

Two things hold the price down. The scoring is modest at 11.2 points, and 66.3% from the line is a genuine drag. More importantly Indiana won 35 games with a crowded young frontcourt and a fast timeline, so year-one minutes were never likely. Pathway 64 against a model base of 88 is the largest opportunity downgrade in the top ten.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 1.3 blocks and 1.0 steals from a forward, on a Houston team built around its defence
  • Indiana was paid two second-round picks to slide down a spot and take him
  • RookieScale and Fantrax ranked him above his draft slot
  • 6'8" with the frame to defend both frontcourt positions from day one

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 11.2 points; there is not much offensive volume to buy
  • 66.3% from the line is a real weekly cost
  • Indiana's frontcourt is crowded and its timeline is fast
  • 17.9% from three across four Summer League games
