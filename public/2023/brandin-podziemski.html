SCOUTING REPORT

Podziemski's Santa Clara season is statistically enormous: 19.9 points, 8.8 rebounds and 3.7 assists with 2.5 made threes at 43.8% and 1.8 steals, on 48.3% shooting. A 6'4" guard rebounding at nearly nine a game while shooting 43.8% from deep is a shape almost nobody produces at any level.

RookieScale and Fantrax were both sceptical — RookieScale 22nd, Fantrax 23rd — and the reason is the West Coast Conference. Transfer-up production against mid-major defences has a long history of not travelling, and he is a below-the-rim athlete who will have to survive NBA guards.

Then Las Vegas made it worse: 26.3% from the field and 21.7% from three across five games, which is why his post-Vegas re-rating is the most negative on this board. Golden State won 44 games with Stephen Curry and Klay Thompson in front of him. This is a bet that the college line was real and the summer was noise.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 8.8 rebounds and 1.8 steals from a 6'4" guard — two columns guards rarely fill
  • 43.8% from three on 2.5 makes; the shooting volume and accuracy are both there
  • 19.9 / 8.8 / 3.7 is a genuinely rare statistical shape at any level
  • Golden State's system rewards exactly this kind of connective off-guard

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 26.3% from the field across five Summer League games
  • West Coast Conference production; the level is a real translation risk
  • All five boards ranked him below his draft slot
  • A below-the-rim athlete who will be hunted defensively in the NBA
