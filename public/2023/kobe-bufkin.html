SCOUTING REPORT

Bufkin's Michigan sophomore year is a balanced, efficient guard line with no obvious hole: 14.0 points, 4.5 rebounds, 2.9 assists and 1.3 steals on 48.2% from the field, with 35.5% from three and 84.9% from the line. He also contributed 0.7 blocks, which is unusual for a 6'4" guard.

The problem is the mirror image of the strength. There is no column he wins. RookieScale liked the completeness at 13th; Fantrax discounted the lack of a standout skill at 19th, and in a category format that scepticism is the better instinct — roster spots are won by players who move a column, not by players who touch six.

Atlanta is a difficult landing spot: Trae Young and Dejounte Murray were both under contract, giving the Hawks two established starting guards ahead of him. And his Summer League was genuinely poor — 33.3% from the field and 13.8% from three across five games — which is why the post-Vegas mark here is among the most negative on the board.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 48.2% from the field and 84.9% from the line — efficient at both ends of the shot chart
  • 1.3 steals and 0.7 blocks from a 6'4" guard; both defensive columns contribute
  • RookieScale ranked him 13th, two spots above his draft slot
  • A complete, low-risk skill set with no glaring technical hole

▼ BEAR CASE

  • No category he wins — the specific problem in a nine-cat format
  • Trae Young and Dejounte Murray are both ahead of him in Atlanta
  • 13.8% from three across five Summer League games
  • 1.9 turnovers against 2.9 assists on moderate usage
