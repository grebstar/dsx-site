SCOUTING REPORT

Prosper is a 6'7" forward with a 7'1" wingspan who shot 51.2% from the field at Marquette while contributing 4.7 rebounds and 0.9 steals. The efficiency is real, the frame is NBA-ready, and Sacramento drafted him 24th before selling his rights to Dallas for cash.

The category profile is narrow in a way RookieScale and Fantrax both noticed — RookieScale 24th, Fantrax 29th. He had 0.7 assists and 0.1 blocks, which for a defensive forward is a strange absence, and 33.9% from three on 1.1 makes is not enough volume to matter.

Dallas won 38 games and was chasing the play-in with an established rotation, so a low-usage rookie forward has no obvious minutes. He was also the last first-round pick in the class to sign, on July 16. This is priced 26th — a useful body with a two-column ceiling.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 51.2% from the field with a 7'1" wingspan; efficient and physically ready
  • 4.7 rebounds and 0.9 steals from a wing-forward
  • RookieScale ranked him 24th, exactly at his draft slot
  • Dallas paid cash to acquire his rights rather than take another player

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 33.9% from three on 1.1 makes; not enough volume to be a spacer
