SCOUTING REPORT

Lively barely scored at Duke and it did not matter. 2.4 blocks in 20.6 minutes on 65.8% from the field is a per-minute rim-protection rate matched by nobody else in this class, and blocks are the category a dynasty roster is hardest to build in. Fantrax ranked him 8th, four spots above his draft slot, on exactly that.

Dallas agreed, and paid to be sure of him. The Mavericks held the 10th pick and traded it, along with Davis Bertans, to Oklahoma City for the 12th — moving down two spots and attaching a rotation player to do it. Oklahoma City took Cason Wallace at 10; Dallas took Lively at 12 and shed a contract on the way. He then rebounded at 8.0 a game on 68.0% shooting in Las Vegas.

The costs are honest and mostly cosmetic in a category league. 5.2 points is nothing, 0.1 made threes means no spacing at all, and 60.0% from the free-throw line is a weekly tax. Dallas won 38 games the previous year and had no established centre. You are buying two columns and the field goal percentage, and paying for them at the line.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 2.4 blocks in 20.6 minutes — the best per-minute rim protection in the class
  • 65.8% from the field, which protects your percentages while filling scarce columns
  • Dallas gave up the 10th pick and a rotation player to move down and still land him
  • 8.0 rebounds on 68.0% shooting across five Summer League games

▼ BEAR CASE

  • 5.2 points a game; there is no offensive volume of any kind
  • 60.0% from the line is a persistent nine-cat drag
  • 0.1 made threes — zero floor spacing in a five-out league
  • 1.1 assists and 0.5 steals; the profile is two categories wide
