SCOUTING REPORT

Whitehead arrived at Duke as a consensus top-three recruit and left as the 22nd pick, and the entire distance between those two facts is his right foot. He broke a fifth metatarsal before the college season, had a second surgery on the same foot in the weeks before the draft, and played 28 games in between.

In those 28 games he shot 42.4% from three on 1.5 makes with 79.3% from the line — genuinely excellent shooting from a 6'7" wing who started only ten times. That is the asset. RookieScale and Fantrax priced the risk differently: Fantrax 22nd, RookieScale 26th.

He did not play Summer League, so there is no post-draft sample of any kind and no post-Vegas re-rating. The durability score of 2.5 is the lowest on this board and it is a medical judgment, not a stylistic one: two operations on the same bone before a professional debut is a pattern, not an accident. Brooklyn's rebuild gives him time. This is the highest-variance listing in the class.

▲ BULL CASE

  • 42.4% from three on real volume at 6'7" — the shooting is legitimately good
  • A consensus top-three high school recruit; the raw talent is not in question
  • 79.3% from the line supports the jumper being repeatable
  • Brooklyn is rebuilding and can wait as long as it takes

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Two surgeries on the same right foot before playing an NBA game
  • Started only ten of 28 college games; the sample is small and compromised
  • Missed Summer League entirely; no post-draft evidence exists
  • 2.4 rebounds and 0.2 blocks — nothing on the ledger outside shooting
