SCOUTING REPORT

Brown's Missouri senior year is efficient in a way almost nobody's is: 55.3% from the field and 45.5% from three, with 6.4 rebounds, 2.5 assists and 1.5 steals. Four columns and elite percentages. Fantrax ranked him 25th; RookieScale ranked him 43rd, an eighteen-place gap identical in size to Strawther's.

The scepticism is about age and shot volume. He turned 23 in January, making him the oldest player taken in the first round, and 45.5% came on only 1.5 makes a game — a small enough sample that regression is close to certain.

The Clippers won 44 games with Kawhi Leonard and Paul George and had no minutes for a 23-year-old rookie forward. Pathway 52 against a base of 60. Priced 37th, second-to-last among the listed first-rounders, on the reasonable view that a finished 23-year-old on a contender is the least valuable thing a dynasty roster can hold.
