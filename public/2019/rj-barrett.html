SCOUTING REPORT

New York took him third overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 22.6 points, 7.6 rebounds and 4.3 assists in 38 games at 35.3 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #3, pick 3. The boards ranged from second to fifth on him, a spread of 3 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 56 games, 55 starts, 30.4 minutes — 14.3 / 5.0 / 2.6 on 40.2% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, -68.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 57 games, 57 starts, 30.3 minutes — 19.3 / 5.3 / 3.3 on 49.1% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, -36.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $623.16, today's mark of $396.43 is -36.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished ninth in the All-Rookie vote among 2019 draftees.

New York took him third on Jun. 20, 2019 and kept him four and a half seasons. On Dec. 30, 2023 the Knicks sent him, Immanuel Quickley and a 2024 second-round pick to Toronto for OG Anunoby, Precious Achiuwa and Malachi Flynn. He signed a four-year extension on Sep. 1, 2022 that runs through 2026-27, so he enters its final year; the value is reported at $107 million and at up to $120 million and is not stated here. Toronto is reported to be content to let the season run before revisiting it.

2025-26 was 57 games at 19.3 / 5.3 / 3.3 in 30.3 minutes, then seven playoff starts in which he scored at least 18 every time, including the go-ahead three in overtime of Game 6 against Cleveland on May 2, 2026. Toronto finished 46-36 as the fifth seed in the East and lost Game 7 at home, 114-102.
