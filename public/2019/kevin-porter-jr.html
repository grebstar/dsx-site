SCOUTING REPORT

Milwaukee took him #30 overall out of USC. He had just gone for 9.5 points, 4.0 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 21 games at 22.1 minutes a night. The market liked him a great deal more than the room did: composite #17, drafted 30. The boards ranged from ninth to #60 on him, a spread of 51 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 50 games, 3 starts, 23.2 minutes — 10.0 / 3.2 / 2.2 on 44.2% shooting. That ranked him #18 in the class and marked him at $68.95, -9.3% against his IPO. 2025-26: 38 games, 36 starts, 33.2 minutes — 17.4 / 5.2 / 7.4 on 46.5% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +720.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $75.99, today's mark of $623.16 is +720.0%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Milwaukee took him thirtieth on Jun. 20, 2019 and his rights reached Cleveland by way of Detroit, with the Cavaliers sending a 2020 and a 2021 second. Three trades since: to Houston on Jan. 21, 2021 for a protected second; to Oklahoma City on Oct. 17, 2023 with two seconds for Victor Oladipo, Jeremiah Robinson-Earl and a trade exception, after which Oklahoma City waived him; and from the Clippers to Milwaukee on Feb. 6, 2025 for MarJon Beauchamp. He signed a two-year Bucks deal on Jul. 7, 2025 with a player option and exercised the option in June 2026; the value is reported two ways and is not stated.

2025-26 was 38 games at 17.4 / 5.2 / 7.4 with 2.2 steals on .465 shooting, including two triple-doubles. A right knee injury ended it: Doc Rivers said in late March he would be surprised to see him play again that year, and Porter had arthroscopic surgery in April with a return timeline of about Oct. 1, 2026. Milwaukee finished 32-50 and eleventh in the East.
