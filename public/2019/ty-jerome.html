Philadelphia's pick, Boston's night. Boston took Matisse Thybulle at 20 and sent him to Philadelphia for the No. 24 pick that became Jerome and the No. 33 pick that became Carsen Edwards; Boston then flipped the No. 24 pick and Aron Baynes to Phoenix for a 2020 first, so he was moved twice in an hour.

Two more trades. On Nov. 16, 2020 Phoenix sent him, Kelly Oubre Jr., Ricky Rubio, Jalen Lecque and a 2022 first to Oklahoma City for Chris Paul and Abdel Nader; in late September 2022 Oklahoma City sent him, Derrick Favors, Maurice Harkless, Theo Maledon and a second to Houston, who waived him. Golden State, Cleveland and then Memphis were free-agent signings. He agreed a three-year Memphis deal on Jun. 30, 2025 with a player option in the last year; the total is reported at $28 million and at $27.6 million and is not stated.

2025-26 was 15 games. A high-grade right calf strain in an October preseason game against Miami pushed his Memphis debut to Feb. 2, 2026, and an ankle problem cost him early April including the finale. Inside those 15 games he was at 19.7 / 2.8 / 5.7 on .474 / .420 / .875. Memphis finished 25-57.
