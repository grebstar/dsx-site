SCOUTING REPORT

Detroit took him #15 overall out of Limoges CSP in the LNB Pro A. He had just gone for 7.7 points, 3.2 rebounds and 0.7 assists in 29 games at 19.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #15, pick 15. The boards ranged from ninth to #60 on him, a spread of 51 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 38 games, 19 starts, 19.8 minutes — 6.4 / 3.1 / 0.5 on 39.0% shooting. That ranked him #24 in the class and marked him at $39.70, -57.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Koshigaya Alphas — Japanese B.League, then Sagesse Beirut — Lebanese Division A.

Against a frozen IPO of $93.47, today's mark of $27.93 is -70.1%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Detroit took him fifteenth on Jun. 20, 2019 at 18. Two trades: on Sep. 3, 2021 Detroit sent him and Jahlil Okafor to Brooklyn in a DeAndre Jordan salary arrangement for four second-round picks, and on Oct. 6, 2021 Brooklyn sent him and a second to Houston for tax relief. Houston waived him and he signed a two-way deal with the Lakers. His last NBA appearances were two games in 2021-22.

2025-26 was two continents. He spent the season with the Koshigaya Alphas of Japan's B.League, scoring in the high teens with seven-plus rebounds a night for a club that spent April fighting relegation at 17-35, and then signed with Sagesse Al Hekmeh Beirut of the Lebanese Division A on Jun. 7, 2026, playing 14 games there at 16.1 / 4.5 / 3.1 in 35.6 minutes. The Koshigaya game total is reported as 39 by RealGM and 36 by Proballers, so no count is stated. In September 2026 San Pablo Burgos of Spain's Liga Endesa was reported in advanced talks to sign him; nothing was official.
