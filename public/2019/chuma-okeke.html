Orlando took him #16 overall out of Auburn. He had just gone for 12.0 points, 6.8 rebounds and 1.9 assists in 38 games at 29.1 minutes a night. He went 11 picks ahead of his composite, which was #27. The boards ranged from #13 to #60 on him, a spread of 47 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: he did not appear in an NBA game. No NBA row — Orlando drafted him while he was rehabbing the ACL he tore at Auburn in March 2019; he sat out the entire season and debuted in 2020-21. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Real Madrid — EuroLeague and Liga ACB.

Against a frozen IPO of $30.50, today's mark of $25.57 is -16.2%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

Orlando took him sixteenth on Jun. 20, 2019 knowing he would not play that season, and he has never been traded. He signed his rookie-scale deal in the 2020 offseason and debuted in 2020-21. Later moves were free agency: two 10-day contracts with Philadelphia in early 2025 and a standard Cleveland deal that April.

2025-26 was a EuroLeague season. He signed with Real Madrid on Jul. 30, 2025 on a two-year deal through Jun. 2027 with an NBA out clause, and played 37 EuroLeague regular-season games at 5.8 / 3.9 on .479 shooting and .396 from three, six EuroLeague playoff games, and 25 Liga ACB games. Real Madrid reached the Final Four and lost the championship game to Olympiacos 92-85 on May 25, 2026, then went out of the ACB quarterfinals to La Laguna Tenerife on Jun. 6. In July the club declined to activate the exit clause, keeping him for 2026-27.
