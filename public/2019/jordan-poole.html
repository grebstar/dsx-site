SCOUTING REPORT

Golden State took him #28 overall out of Michigan. He had just gone for 12.8 points, 3.0 rebounds and 2.2 assists in 37 games at 33.1 minutes a night. He went 13 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #41, drafted 28. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #40 to #60 on him, a spread of 20 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 57 games, 14 starts, 22.4 minutes — 8.8 / 2.1 / 2.4 on 33.3% shooting. That ranked him #19 in the class and marked him at $62.59, +759.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: 39 games, 8 starts, 23.9 minutes — 13.4 / 2.0 / 3.1 on 37.2% shooting. That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, +1055.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $7.28, today's mark of $84.15 is +1055.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Golden State took him twenty-eighth on Jun. 20, 2019 and kept him four seasons, one of them ending in a championship. Two trades: in the summer of 2023 the Warriors sent him, Ryan Rollins and draft compensation to Washington for Chris Paul, and in June 2025 Washington sent him, Saddiq Bey and the No. 40 pick in that draft to New Orleans for CJ McCollum, Kelly Olynyk and a future second. He is under contract through 2026-27 on the four-year extension he signed with Golden State in October 2022; the salary is single-sourced and is not stated.

2025-26 was 39 games at 13.4 / 2.0 / 3.1 in 23.9 minutes on .372 shooting. He lost time to a quad strain and fell out of the rotation for stretches, including nine consecutive did-not-plays in early 2026, after averaging 20.5 a game for Washington the season before. New Orleans finished 26-56 and eleventh in the West.
