SCOUTING REPORT

Utah took him #23 overall out of Princeton HS (Cincinnati, OH). He had no pre-draft season to price. He committed to Syracuse, decommitted, announced for the G League and then went to neither: he spent 2018-19 on a reported million-dollar New Balance internship and was drafted out of high school a year later. He went 16 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #39, drafted 23. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #34 to #60 on him, a spread of 26 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 61 games, 9 starts, 18.5 minutes — 5.6 / 4.0 / 0.7 on 39.4% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, +500.5% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Ningbo Rockets — Chinese CBA, then Leones de Ponce — Puerto Rico's BSN.

Against a frozen IPO of $9.48, today's mark of $21.38 is +125.5%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Memphis selected him twenty-third on Jun. 20, 2019 and traded his rights to Oklahoma City, with a 2024 second, for the No. 21 pick that became Brandon Clarke. On Feb. 9, 2023 Oklahoma City sent him to Phoenix for Dario Saric, a 2029 second and cash.

2025-26 was a season that ended in a termination. He played for the Lakers at the July 2025 Las Vegas Summer League and left the Jul. 17 game against Boston in a wheelchair with an injury the club never named. He signed with the Ningbo Rockets of the CBA on Mar. 7, 2026 and played 12 games at 4.9 / 3.8 / 1.2 before the club terminated the contract on Apr. 11, citing a violation of professional ethics after an on-court confrontation with a teammate against Shanghai. He also appears in Puerto Rico's BSN with Leones de Ponce, 11 games at 12.5 / 10.5 / 3.0. No 2026-27 club is reported.
