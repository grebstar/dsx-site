SCOUTING REPORT

Atlanta took him tenth overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 13.5 points, 3.7 rebounds and 1.9 assists in 36 games at 29.7 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #7, pick 10. The boards ranged from sixth to tenth on him, a spread of 4 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 58 games, 34 starts, 26.7 minutes — 10.5 / 3.7 / 1.5 on 38.4% shooting. That ranked him #16 in the class and marked him at $84.15, -68.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. BC Siauliai — Lithuanian LKL, then the Austin Spurs and San Diego Clippers — G League.

Against a frozen IPO of $269.91, today's mark of $33.31 is -87.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Atlanta took him tenth on Jun. 20, 2019 and traded him on Jan. 13, 2022 to New York, with Solomon Hill and a 2025 second, for Kevin Knox and a protected first. Thirteen months later he was moved again, in the February 2023 trade activity that sent him and Matisse Thybulle to Portland and Jalen McDaniels to Philadelphia while Josh Hart went to New York. Basketball-Reference and NBA.com record that as one four-team trade; CBS, ESPN and KGW record it as two transactions on consecutive days. The three pages of this board that it touches all name all three men and none of them asserts a count.

2025-26 was two countries and no NBA game. The Lakers had waived him in March 2025. He opened the season at BC Siauliai in the Lithuanian LKL — nine games, eight starts, 14.7 / 3.8 / 1.4 with 2.0 steals — then left for personal reasons, signed a G League contract in January, and played nine regular-season games plus one playoff game for the San Diego Clippers at 9.7 / 3.4 / 1.4 on .508 shooting. He appeared at the July 2026 Las Vegas Summer League with Orlando without a reported NBA contract.
