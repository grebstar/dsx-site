SCOUTING REPORT

Indiana took him #18 overall out of Buducnost Voli in the ABA League. He had just gone for 10.4 points, 4.8 rebounds and 0.4 assists in 19 games at 20.4 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #19, pick 18. The boards ranged from sixth to #60 on him, a spread of 54 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 54 games, 2 starts, 8.7 minutes — 3.2 / 2.0 / 0.4 on 46.7% shooting. That ranked him #29 in the class and marked him at $25.57, -59.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 64 games, 3 starts, 15.2 minutes — 5.9 / 5.0 / 1.3 on 67.6% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, -9.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $62.59, today's mark of $56.94 is -9.0%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

Indiana took him eighteenth on Jun. 20, 2019 and he has never been traded. Indiana waived him in February 2023 and he signed with Orlando as a free agent, then re-signed there in July 2024 for three years and $25 million, a figure ESPN and Yahoo state identically. He enters the final year of it. Orlando turned down inquiries from the Clippers in June 2026 and kept him as centre depth.

2025-26 was 64 games and three starts at 5.9 / 5.0 / 1.3 with a block a game on .676 shooting, in 15.2 minutes. He moved ahead of Moritz Wagner as Wendell Carter Jr.'s backup in late March and then did not play at all in Orlando's Game 7. The Magic finished 45-37, eighth in the East, and lost the first round to Detroit 4-3.
