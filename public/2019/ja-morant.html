SCOUTING REPORT

Memphis took him second overall out of Murray State. He had just gone for 24.5 points, 5.7 rebounds and 10.0 assists in 33 games at 36.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #2, pick 2. The boards ranged from second to third on him, a spread of 1 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 67 games, 67 starts, 31.0 minutes — 17.8 / 3.9 / 7.3 on 47.7% shooting. That ranked him first in the class and marked him at $1,000.00, +23.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 20 games, 20 starts, 28.5 minutes — 19.5 / 3.3 / 8.1 on 41.0% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, -66.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $808.24, today's mark of $269.91 is -66.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished first in the All-Rookie vote among 2019 draftees.

Memphis took him second on Jun. 20, 2019 and he played seven seasons there. He signed a five-year rookie-scale maximum extension in 2022 running through 2027-28; the remaining salary is single-sourced and is not stated here. On Jun. 30, 2026 Memphis traded him to the Portland Trail Blazers for Jerami Grant, Kris Murray and cash, so the contract's last two years are Portland's.

2025-26 was 20 games. Memphis suspended him one game on Nov. 1, 2025 for conduct detrimental to the team. He sprained the UCL in his left elbow in late January, took a platelet-rich plasma injection, and the Grizzlies shut him down in late March. Memphis finished 25-57 and thirteenth in the West. Across the last three seasons he has appeared in 79 games in total.
