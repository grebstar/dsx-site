SCOUTING REPORT

Minnesota took him eleventh overall out of North Carolina. He had just gone for 16.9 points, 5.8 rebounds and 2.4 assists in 36 games at 29.9 minutes a night. He went 15 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #26, drafted 11. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #24 to #60 on him, a spread of 36 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 57 games, 9 starts, 22.0 minutes — 8.8 / 3.3 / 1.2 on 43.5% shooting. That ranked him #14 in the class and marked him at $104.27, +213.1% against his IPO. 2025-26: 54 games, 54 starts, 30.5 minutes — 12.2 / 3.8 / 2.4 on 48.0% shooting. That ranked him #13 in the class and marked him at $116.93, +251.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $33.31, today's mark of $116.93 is +251.1%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished #14 in the All-Rookie vote among 2019 draftees.

Minnesota drafted him eleventh on Jun. 20, 2019 and sent him to Phoenix the same night, with Dario Saric, for the No. 6 pick that became Jarrett Culver. Two trades since: on Feb. 9, 2023 Phoenix sent him, Mikal Bridges, Jae Crowder, four unprotected firsts and a swap to Brooklyn for Kevin Durant and T.J. Warren; and at the end of June 2025 Brooklyn sent him to Denver for Michael Porter Jr. and an unprotected 2032 first.

He is in the final season of the four-year, $108 million deal he signed with Brooklyn on Jul. 6, 2023, at $22.5 million for 2026-27, with unrestricted free agency after it. In 2025-26 he played 54 games at 12.2 / 3.8 / 2.4 on .480 shooting and .430 from three; a right-knee bone bruise taken at Dallas on Dec. 23, 2025 was set for reevaluation in four to six weeks and cost him 28 games. Denver finished 54-28 and third in the West and lost the first round to Minnesota 2-4. Five teams were reported in June as interested in the expiring contract.
