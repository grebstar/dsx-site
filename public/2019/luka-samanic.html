SCOUTING REPORT

San Antonio took him #19 overall out of Petrol Olimpija in the ABA League. He had just gone for 6.4 points, 3.8 rebounds and 0.8 assists in 20 games at 15.8 minutes a night. He went 12 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #31, drafted 19. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #26 to #60 on him, a spread of 34 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 3 games, 1 starts, 16.0 minutes — 5.3 / 3.3 / 2.0 on 31.3% shooting. That ranked him #49 in the class and marked him at $0.81, -96.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Baskonia, then Zenit St. Petersburg — VTB United League.

Against a frozen IPO of $21.38, today's mark of $23.39 is +9.4%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him.

San Antonio took him nineteenth on Jun. 20, 2019 and he has never been traded: every move since has been a waiver or a signing. San Antonio waived him in October 2021; a Knicks two-way followed, then a Boston camp deal, then a 10-day and a standard contract in Utah across 2023 and 2023-24. He has 86 NBA games and none since.

2025-26 was two clubs and a mid-October exit. He opened at Baskonia — five EuroLeague games and two in the ACB — and terminated the contract by mutual agreement for personal reasons on Oct. 22, 2025. In November he signed with Zenit St Petersburg of the VTB United League for the rest of the season, scoring in double figures with around five rebounds a night; Proballers counts 27 games and Sportando 42, so no total is stated. Zenit finished third in the regular season at 28-12, lost the semifinal to UNICS and then the bronze series to Lokomotiv-Kuban. In July 2026 he signed with UNICS Kazan on a 1+1.
