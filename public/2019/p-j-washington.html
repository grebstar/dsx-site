SCOUTING REPORT

Charlotte took him twelfth overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 15.2 points, 7.5 rebounds and 1.8 assists in 35 games at 29.3 minutes a night. He went 6 picks ahead of his composite, which was #18. The boards ranged from eleventh to #60 on him, a spread of 49 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 58 games, 57 starts, 30.3 minutes — 12.2 / 5.4 / 2.1 on 45.5% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, +291.4% against his IPO. 2025-26: 56 games, 53 starts, 31.0 minutes — 14.2 / 7.0 / 1.8 on 45.0% shooting. That ranked him ninth in the class and marked him at $196.79, +185.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $68.95, today's mark of $196.79 is +185.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished seventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2019 draftees.

Charlotte took him twelfth on Jun. 20, 2019 and traded him once, on Feb. 8, 2024, to Dallas for Grant Williams, Seth Curry and a protected 2027 first — a straight exchange of two members of this class, five years after they were picked ten apart. Forbes and CBS describe the pick as Dallas's own 2027 first with top-two protection and the wire report describes it only as a first, so the protection is not stated on either page.

He agreed a veteran extension with Dallas on Sep. 3, 2025 and signed it a week later; it begins in 2026-27 and carries him to unrestricted free agency in 2030. The value is reported at four years and $90 million and at four years and $88 million, so no figure is stated. In 2025-26 he started 53 of 56 games at 14.2 / 7.0 / 1.8 with 1.1 blocks in 31.0 minutes; Dallas finished 26-56 and twelfth in the West.
