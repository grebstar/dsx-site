SCOUTING REPORT

Portland took him #25 overall out of North Carolina. He had just gone for 9.8 points, 4.6 rebounds and 0.6 assists in 36 games at 18.2 minutes a night. The market liked him a great deal more than the room did: composite #10, drafted 25. The boards ranged from eleventh to #22 on him, a spread of 11 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 48 games, 5 starts, 11.9 minutes — 3.6 / 2.3 / 0.5 on 43.0% shooting. That ranked him #28 in the class and marked him at $27.93, -83.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Chiba Jets — Japan's B.League.

Against a frozen IPO of $170.70, today's mark of $19.52 is -88.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Portland took him twenty-fifth on Jun. 20, 2019 and traded him once, on Sep. 27, 2023, in the three-team deal that sent Damian Lillard to Milwaukee: Portland received Jrue Holiday, Deandre Ayton, Toumani Camara and a 2029 first, and Phoenix received him along with Jusuf Nurkic, Grayson Allen and Keon Johnson. Phoenix waived him on Aug. 21, 2024 with three years and roughly $22 million left on the four-year, $28 million extension he had signed in October 2022, and stretched the money; the term still runs through 2026-27.

2025-26 was a full season in Japan. He signed a one-year deal with the Chiba Jets in July 2025 after a G League year with Sioux Falls, and played at roughly 16.5 / 7.6 / 2.1 — Proballers counts 53 games and Eurobasket 58, so no total is stated. Chiba beat Gunma in a best-of-three quarterfinal, with 20 points from him in the May 12 clincher, then lost both semifinals to Nagasaki Velca, the second in overtime.
