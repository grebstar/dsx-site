SCOUTING REPORT

Brooklyn took him #17 overall out of Virginia Tech. He had just gone for 16.2 points, 4.1 rebounds and 4.0 assists in 34 games at 34.3 minutes a night. The boards had him 5 places above where he went, at #12. The boards ranged from tenth to #23 on him, a spread of 13 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 47 games, 1 starts, 12.6 minutes — 5.7 / 1.8 / 1.9 on 36.8% shooting. That ranked him #23 in the class and marked him at $43.38, -67.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 78 games, 71 starts, 33.4 minutes — 20.8 / 3.4 / 3.7 on 45.9% shooting. That ranked him first in the class and marked him at $1,000.00, +659.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $131.64, today's mark of $1,000.00 is +659.6%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Brooklyn used the seventeenth pick, Atlanta held his rights for the length of one trade call, and he played for neither. On draft night, Jun. 20, 2019, his rights went from Atlanta to New Orleans alongside the No. 8 pick that became Jaxson Hayes and the No. 35 pick that became Didi Louzada, with the No. 4 pick that became De'Andre Hunter, Solomon Hill and the No. 57 pick that became Jordan Bone going the other way.

Four trades since. On Feb. 8, 2022 New Orleans sent him, Didi Louzada, Josh Hart and Tomas Satoransky to Portland for CJ McCollum, Larry Nance Jr. and Tony Snell — two members of this class moved together a second time. A day later Portland sent him to Utah in a three-team deal; on Feb. 9, 2023 Utah sent him to Minnesota in the Mike Conley trade; and on Jun. 30, 2025 Minnesota sign-and-traded him to Atlanta for cash and a 2027 second, on four years and $62 million with a player option and a trade kicker.

Then he won an award. 78 games and 71 starts at 20.8 / 3.4 / 3.7 on .459 / .399 / .902 with 251 threes made, after two Minnesota seasons at 8.4 a game. He opened as a reserve and took the starting job when Trae Young's knee gave way and Young was traded. Atlanta finished 46-36, sixth in the East, and lost the first round to New York 4-2.
