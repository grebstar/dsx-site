SCOUTING REPORT

the Lakers took him fourth overall out of Virginia. He had just gone for 15.2 points, 5.1 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 38 games at 32.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #5, pick 4. The boards ranged from fourth to twelfth on him, a spread of 8 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 63 games, 62 starts, 32.0 minutes — 12.3 / 4.5 / 1.8 on 41.0% shooting. That ranked him twelfth in the class and marked him at $131.64, -66.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: 45 games, 25 starts, 26.1 minutes — 13.7 / 4.1 / 2.0 on 41.5% shooting. That ranked him #15 in the class and marked him at $93.47, -76.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $396.43, today's mark of $93.47 is -76.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished twelfth in the All-Rookie vote among 2019 draftees.

He was the whole point of a five-man trade. On draft night, Jun. 20, 2019, New Orleans sent the No. 4 pick — which Atlanta used on him — plus Solomon Hill and the No. 57 pick that became Jordan Bone to Atlanta; Atlanta sent back the picks that became Jaxson Hayes at 8, Nickeil Alexander-Walker at 17 and Didi Louzada at 35, along with a protected 2020 first. Five members of this class changed hands in one transaction, and the pick compensation is itemized differently by CBS, NBA.com and Hoops Rumors, so the protections are not described on any of their pages.

Two NBA trades since. On Feb. 7, 2025 Atlanta sent him to Cleveland for Caris LeVert, Georges Niang, three second-round picks and two pick swaps. In February 2026 Cleveland sent him on to Sacramento in a three-team deal that brought back Dennis Schroder and Keon Ellis and routed Dario Saric to Chicago. He is in the final year of the extension he signed with Atlanta in 2024, an expiring deal at $24.9 million for 2026-27.
