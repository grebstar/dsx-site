SCOUTING REPORT

Chicago took him seventh overall out of North Carolina. He had just gone for 16.1 points, 3.5 rebounds and 4.1 assists in 35 games at 28.5 minutes a night. He went 7 picks ahead of his composite, which was #14. The boards ranged from fifth to #60 on him, a spread of 55 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 65 games, 1 starts, 25.8 minutes — 13.2 / 3.5 / 2.7 on 39.4% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, +209.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 50 games, 26 starts, 25.0 minutes — 17.4 / 3.4 / 4.0 on 44.6% shooting. That ranked him tenth in the class and marked him at $170.70, +63.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $104.27, today's mark of $170.70 is +63.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished sixth in the All-Rookie vote among 2019 draftees.

Chicago took him seventh on Jun. 20, 2019 and kept him six and a half seasons. In February 2026 the Bulls sent him and Mike Conley to Charlotte for Collin Sexton, Ousmane Dieng and two 2031 second-round picks; NBA.com dates it Feb. 5-6 and ESPN's game log Feb. 24, so no date is stated. The deal was amended after his physical showed a left calf strain, with Charlotte keeping a 2029 second it had been sending out. He re-signed with the Hornets in the 2026 offseason on a three-year, $74 million contract, a figure ESPN and NBA.com state identically.

The calf had already cost him the start of the season; he debuted on Nov. 16, 2025. He played 29 games for Chicago at 18.6 / 3.7 / 4.7 and 21 for Charlotte at 15.6 / 3.0 / 3.0, with his minutes falling after the move. Charlotte went 44-38, finished ninth in the East, beat Miami in the play-in and lost the elimination game to Orlando 121-90.
