SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him sixth overall out of Texas Tech. He had just gone for 18.5 points, 6.4 rebounds and 3.7 assists in 38 games at 32.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 6. The boards ranged from second to eighth on him, a spread of 6 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 63 games, 35 starts, 23.9 minutes — 9.2 / 3.4 / 1.7 on 40.4% shooting. That ranked him #17 in the class and marked him at $75.99, -84.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Sendai 89ers — Japanese B.League.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $36.38 is -92.7%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

He was drafted sixth by Phoenix and immediately moved: on draft night Minnesota sent the No. 11 pick that became Cameron Johnson and Dario Saric to Phoenix for the No. 6 pick, and the Timberwolves used it on him. On Aug. 25, 2021 Minnesota traded him and Juancho Hernangomez to Memphis for Patrick Beverley. His last NBA appearances were ten games in 2022-23.

He is now a lead scorer in Japan. After a 2024-25 season with the G League's Osceola Magic he signed with the Sendai 89ers of the B1 League in June 2025, and in 2025-26 he played 60 games at 26.5 / 6.0 / 3.2 on .465 shooting in 31.4 minutes. Sendai finished 35-25 and sixth in the Eastern Conference. The club announced on Jul. 1, 2026 that he had agreed to return for 2026-27. This is what the board's zero mark means and does not mean: he is not in the NBA, and he is not out of basketball.
