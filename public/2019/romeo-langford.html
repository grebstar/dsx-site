Boston took him #14 overall out of Indiana. He had just gone for 16.5 points, 5.4 rebounds and 2.3 assists in 32 games at 34.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #11, pick 14. The boards ranged from eighth to #22 on him, a spread of 14 places across 5 of them.

2019-20: 32 games, 2 starts, 11.6 minutes — 2.5 / 1.3 / 0.4 on 35.0% shooting. That ranked him #38 in the class and marked him at $10.64, -92.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. No NBA row — signed and waived by New York in October 2025, and no club could be confirmed for the rest of the season.

Against a frozen IPO of $149.42, today's mark of $30.50 is -79.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Boston took him fourteenth on Jun. 20, 2019 and traded him on Feb. 10, 2022, with Josh Richardson and a protected 2022 first, to San Antonio for Derrick White. His last NBA appearance was in 2022-23.

The 2025-26 row is the honest one and it is a blank. He signed an Exhibit 10 contract with New York on Oct. 16, 2025 and was waived the next day, a move made to route him to the Westchester Knicks, who held his G League returning rights. He was not on Westchester's opening-day roster announced Nov. 6, and he does not appear in Westchester's season statistics. No club in any league on Basketball-Reference, RealGM or USbasket records a 2025-26 game for him. His last recorded professional appearances are three games for BCM Gravelines-Dunkerque of France's Betclic Elite in November 2024.
