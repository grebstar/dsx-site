Utah drafted him thirtieth, then immediately traded him and Thomas Bryant (42) to the Lakers for the rights to Tony Bradley (28), whom Utah had just drafted with its own pick. On Jul. 6, 2019, the Lakers traded him, Lonzo Ball (2), Brandon Ingram and other assets to New Orleans, in a three-team deal also involving Washington, for Anthony Davis. On Feb. 8, 2022, New Orleans traded him to Portland as part of the CJ McCollum deal. A four-team trade sent him from Portland to the New York Knicks on Feb. 8-9, 2023. He signed a 4-year, $81 million extension with New York on Aug. 10, 2023.
