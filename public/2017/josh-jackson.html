SCOUTING REPORT

Phoenix took him fourth overall out of Kansas. He had just gone for 16.3 points, 7.4 rebounds and 3.0 assists in 35 games at 30.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #3, pick 4. The boards ranged from second to fifth on him, a spread of 3 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 77 games, 35 starts, 25.4 minutes — 13.1 / 4.6 / 1.5 on 41.7% shooting. That ranked him eighth in the class and marked him at $229.10, -63.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. No recorded season anywhere — no NBA, G League or international row for 2025-26.

Against a frozen IPO of $623.16, today's mark of $43.38 is -93.0%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished eighth in the All-Rookie vote among 2017 draftees.

Phoenix took him fourth and traded him two years later. On Jul. 7, 2019 he went to Memphis with De'Anthony Melton and future second-round considerations, for Jevon Carter, Kyle Korver and trade exceptions. Memphis let him walk in November 2020; he signed with Detroit that December.

On Feb. 10, 2022 he was traded again, to Sacramento, with Trey Lyles, in a four-team deal that also sent Semi Ojeleye from Milwaukee to the Clippers: Detroit received Marvin Bagley III from Sacramento, Milwaukee received Donte DiVincenzo from Sacramento and draft compensation, and the Clippers sent Serge Ibaka and cash to Milwaukee for Rodney Hood and Ojeleye. Jackson left Sacramento as a free agent that July, signed a training-camp deal with Toronto in August 2022, was waived that October, and signed with the Stockton Kings (Sacramento's G League affiliate) in January 2023 before being waived after one appearance.

No NBA, G League or professional signing anywhere has been found for him since January 2023. A federal civil suit filed in October 2023 in the Southern District of New York alleges he sexually assaulted a woman at a Manhattan hotel in February 2022 and orchestrated a subsequent armed intimidation incident against her; he has denied the allegations through his attorney, and no resolution of the suit has been reported.
