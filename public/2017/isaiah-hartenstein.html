Houston took him #43 overall out of Zalgiris in the Lithuanian LKL. He had just gone for 4.7 points, 3.4 rebounds and 0.7 assists in 29 games at 12.7 minutes a night. The market liked him a great deal more than the room did: composite #22, drafted 43. The boards ranged from #13 to #34 on him, a spread of 21 places across 4 of them.

Against a frozen IPO of $47.43, today's mark of $149.42 is +215.0%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Houston took him 43rd on Jun. 22, 2017. He signed a multi-year contract with the Rockets on Jul. 25, 2018, was placed on waivers on Jun. 23, 2020, and signed with Denver on Nov. 30, 2020. On Mar. 25, 2021 Cleveland acquired him in a trade for JaVale McGee.

He became a free agent on Aug. 3, 2021 and signed with the Los Angeles Clippers on Sep. 13, 2021, then with the New York Knicks on Jul. 12, 2022. On Jul. 6, 2024 he signed a multi-year contract with Oklahoma City, and he is listed by Basketball-Reference as a 2025 NBA champion with the Thunder.

On Jun. 26, 2026 he and the Thunder agreed to a three-year, $75 million extension that included a 15% trade kicker and a 2028 mutual option, reworking a $28.5 million team option for 2026-27. Houston also drafted Dillon Brooks two picks later (#45); Brooks's rights went to Memphis the same day.
