SCOUTING REPORT

Indiana took him #18 overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 16.3 points, 8.2 rebounds and 2.4 assists in 35 games at 29.9 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #18, pick 18. The boards ranged from #16 to #24 on him, a spread of 8 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 53 games, 0 starts, 8.7 minutes — 2.9 / 1.5 / 0.2 on 47.1% shooting. That ranked him #36 in the class and marked him at $13.22, -80.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Maccabi FOX Tel Aviv — EuroLeague and the Israeli BSL.

Against a frozen IPO of $68.95, today's mark of $25.57 is -62.9%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Indiana drafted him eighteenth directly; no draft-night trade. He was traded, with a 2027 second-round pick, to Oklahoma City for Jalen Lecque on Nov. 25, 2020, and waived by OKC that December. A two-way contract with Portland followed in Apr. 2021, closing out his NBA career at 146 games. He has played in China's CBA (Guangzhou Loong Lions, Beijing Ducks, Nanjing Monkey Kings) since 2022, and signed a three-year contract with Maccabi Tel Aviv in the Israeli Premier League and EuroLeague on Jun. 29, 2025.
