SCOUTING REPORT

Minnesota took him seventh overall out of Arizona. He had just gone for 15.6 points, 7.2 rebounds and 0.9 assists in 37 games at 30.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #9, pick 7. The boards ranged from seventh to twelfth on him, a spread of 5 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 68 games, 68 starts, 29.7 minutes — 15.2 / 7.5 / 1.2 on 43.4% shooting. That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, +151.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 42 games, 42 starts, 34.4 minutes — 26.7 / 6.9 / 2.1 on 47.7% shooting. That ranked him third in the class and marked him at $623.16, +216.7% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $196.79, today's mark of $623.16 is +216.7%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished fourth in the All-Rookie vote among 2017 draftees.

Minnesota drafted him seventh and traded him to Chicago that same night as the headline return in the Jimmy Butler deal: Chicago sent Butler and the rights to Justin Patton (16) to Minnesota for Kris Dunn, Zach LaVine and Markkanen's rights. He was a Timberwolf only on paper. Chicago sign-and-traded him to Cleveland in a three-team deal completed Aug. 2021, with Portland also involved and Larry Nance Jr. going the other way; he signed a 4-year, $67 million contract with Cleveland as part of it. On Sep. 3, 2022, Cleveland traded him, along with Collin Sexton, Ochai Agbaji and a package of future first-round picks and swaps, to Utah for Donovan Mitchell. He signed a 5-year, $238 million extension with Utah on Aug. 7, 2024.
