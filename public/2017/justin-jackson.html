SCOUTING REPORT

Portland took him #15 overall out of UNC. He had just gone for 18.3 points, 4.7 rebounds and 2.8 assists in 40 games at 32.0 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #13, pick 15. The boards ranged from tenth to #21 on him, a spread of 11 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 68 games, 41 starts, 22.1 minutes — 6.7 / 2.8 / 1.1 on 44.2% shooting. That ranked him #23 in the class and marked him at $43.38, -62.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. No recorded season anywhere — no NBA, G League or international row for 2025-26.

Against a frozen IPO of $116.93, today's mark of $33.31 is -71.5%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Portland drafted him fifteenth and traded him, along with Harry Giles (20), to Sacramento that same night for Zach Collins (10), whom Sacramento had just drafted with its own pick. He was traded from Sacramento to Dallas during the 2018-19 season. He was waived by Oklahoma City on Apr. 5, 2021, signed a two-way contract with Milwaukee on Apr. 21, 2021, and was on Milwaukee's roster when the team won the 2021 NBA championship. Journeyman stretches followed with Dallas, the Texas Legends, Boston, Phoenix and Oklahoma City; he averaged 22.3 points, 7.0 rebounds and 3.2 assists across 38 G League games with the Texas Legends in 2022-23, which led to a 10-day contract with Minnesota on Feb. 22, 2024. That remains his most recent confirmed NBA action; he returned to the Texas Legends that March, and no professional team has been confirmed for him since.
