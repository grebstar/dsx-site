SCOUTING REPORT

Portland took him #26 overall out of Purdue. He had just gone for 18.5 points, 12.5 rebounds and 3.0 assists in 35 games at 32.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #24, pick 26. The boards ranged from #17 to #37 on him, a spread of 20 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 27 games, 3 starts, 7.0 minutes — 2.3 / 2.0 / 0.5 on 40.0% shooting. That ranked him #38 in the class and marked him at $10.64, -73.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. His last NBA season was 2019-20 and he was not signed again. He died on June 20, 2022, aged 25 — two seasons after the league had stopped employing him, which is why this zero is priced as a zero.

Against a frozen IPO of $39.70, today's mark of $16.15 is -59.3%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Purdue's Big Ten Player of the Year and Pete Newell Big Man Award winner as a sophomore, Portland drafted him twenty-sixth (no draft-night trade) and signed him Jul. 3, 2017. His NBA run covered parts of three seasons — Portland in 2017-18 and 2019-20, plus a brief mid-2018-19 stretch with Sacramento — with G League stints for the Canton Charge, Texas Legends and Stockton Kings in between. He was on Portland's roster for the shortened 2019-20 season but did not join the team for the Jul. 2020 Orlando restart, and no team signed him afterward; contemporaneous reporting referenced weight and conditioning issues as a contributing factor. He last played in the NBA in 2019-20. He died June 20, 2022, at 25, two seasons after the league had stopped employing him.
