SCOUTING REPORT

Orlando took him sixth overall out of Florida State. He had just gone for 12.0 points, 7.8 rebounds and 1.2 assists in 32 games at 26.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #6, pick 6. The boards ranged from fourth to sixth on him, a spread of 2 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 27 games, 10 starts, 19.9 minutes — 5.4 / 3.7 / 0.7 on 37.9% shooting. That ranked him #26 in the class and marked him at $33.31, -89.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 52 games, 0 starts, 10.0 minutes — 2.6 / 2.5 / 0.4 on 42.2% shooting. That ranked him #20 in the class and marked him at $56.94, -82.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $322.90, today's mark of $56.94 is -82.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Never traded. Orlando drafted him sixth and he has been on the roster, in some contractual form, every year since. Two separate ACL tears in the same left knee wiped out the 2020-21 and 2021-22 seasons entirely. He signed a 5-year, $84 million extension with Orlando around 2024. On Jun. 27, 2026, the Magic waived him to create cap flexibility, saving roughly $6.5 million against the cap while still owing him $8 million already guaranteed; four days later, on Jul. 1, 2026, Orlando re-signed him to a new one-year, veteran-minimum contract, combining the guaranteed money into a package worth about $10.4 million total.
