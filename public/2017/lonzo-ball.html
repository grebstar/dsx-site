SCOUTING REPORT

the Lakers took him second overall out of UCLA. He had just gone for 14.6 points, 6.0 rebounds and 7.6 assists in 36 games at 35.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #2, pick 2. The boards ranged from second to fourth on him, a spread of 2 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 52 games, 50 starts, 34.2 minutes — 10.2 / 6.9 / 7.2 on 36.0% shooting. That ranked him sixth in the class and marked him at $322.90, -60.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 35 games, 3 starts, 20.8 minutes — 4.6 / 4.0 / 3.9 on 30.1% shooting. That ranked him #17 in the class and marked him at $75.99, -90.6% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $808.24, today's mark of $75.99 is -90.6%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished sixth in the All-Rookie vote among 2017 draftees.

The Lakers took him second and traded him two years later. On Jul. 6, 2019 he went to New Orleans, with Brandon Ingram and Josh Hart, plus draft-pick considerations, in the trade that sent Anthony Davis to the Lakers. He was sign-and-traded from New Orleans to the Bulls on Aug. 8, 2021 (Chicago sent back Tomas Satoransky, Garrett Temple, a 2024 second-round pick and cash), and on Jun. 28-Jul. 6, 2025 the Bulls traded him to the Cavaliers for Isaac Okoro.

Knee injuries cost him large stretches of his Chicago tenure. In 2025-26 he played a reserve role in Cleveland.
