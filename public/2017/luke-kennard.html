SCOUTING REPORT

Detroit took him twelfth overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 19.5 points, 5.1 rebounds and 2.5 assists in 37 games at 35.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #16, pick 12. The boards ranged from eleventh to #30 on him, a spread of 19 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 73 games, 9 starts, 20.0 minutes — 7.6 / 2.4 / 1.7 on 44.3% shooting. That ranked him #17 in the class and marked him at $75.99, -9.7% against his IPO. 2025-26: 78 games, 6 starts, 21.6 minutes — 8.4 / 2.3 / 2.2 on 53.3% shooting. That ranked him #15 in the class and marked him at $93.47, +11.1% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $84.15, today's mark of $93.47 is +11.1%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished #16 in the All-Rookie vote among 2017 draftees.

Detroit drafted him twelfth. On Nov. 19, 2020, Detroit traded him and Justin Patton (16) to the LA Clippers in a three-team deal also involving Brooklyn: Detroit sent Kennard and Patton to the Clippers and Bruce Brown to Brooklyn, and received Rodney McGruder plus draft-pick considerations from the Clippers and Dzanan Musa from Brooklyn. On Feb. 9, 2023, a three-team trade with Memphis and Houston sent him to the Grizzlies. Memphis declined his team option in Jun. 2024, then re-signed him a month later. He signed with Atlanta in free agency Jul. 8, 2025, was traded from Atlanta to the Lakers on Feb. 5, 2026 (Atlanta received Gabe Vincent and a 2032 second-round pick), then left the Lakers in free agency that July to sign with Phoenix on a taxpayer mid-level deal.
