Houston selected him 45th on Jun. 22, 2017 and the same day his draft rights went to Memphis, where he signed on Jul. 21, 2017. He signed a veteran extension with the Grizzlies on Feb. 5, 2020 and became a free agent on Jul. 1, 2023.

On Jul. 8, 2023 he signed a multi-year contract with Memphis in a sign-and-trade and went to Houston, the team that had drafted him. On Jul. 6, 2025 Houston traded him to Phoenix in a seven-team trade that also brought Jalen Green to the Suns. He signed a veteran extension with Phoenix on Aug. 8, 2026.
