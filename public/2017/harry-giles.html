SCOUTING REPORT

Portland took him #20 overall out of Duke. He had just gone for 3.9 points, 3.8 rebounds and 0.3 assists in 26 games at 11.5 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #17, pick 20. The boards ranged from #15 to #26 on him, a spread of 11 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: he did not appear in an NBA game. No NBA row — and no G League or international row either. Sacramento redshirted him for the whole of 2017-18 after the knee surgeries that had already cost him most of high school and half a season at Duke; he debuted in 2018-19. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Jiangsu Kendia — Chinese CBA.

Against a frozen IPO of $75.99, today's mark of $23.39 is -69.2%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Portland drafted him twentieth, alongside Justin Jackson (15), and traded both, that same night, to Sacramento for Zach Collins (10), whom Sacramento had just drafted with its own pick. Sacramento's own rebuilt-knee redshirt: a debut had been tentatively planned for Jan. 2018, but on Jan. 18, 2018, the Kings announced he would sit out the rest of the season; his actual NBA debut did not come until Oct. 17, 2018. Injuries and shuffled rosters followed — Sacramento (2018-20), Portland (2020-21), an Agua Caliente Clippers G League stint before a waiver in Jan. 2022, a season out entirely in 2022-23, brief runs with Brooklyn and the Lakers in 2023-24, and a September 2024 deal with Charlotte that ended in a waiver that October before he ever played a game for them. He signed with Jiangsu Dragons in China's CBA on Oct. 9, 2025.

▲ BULL CASE

  • Field-goal percentage in the 87th percentile of the class before the draft
  • Drafted #20 overall, so a team spent real capital on him
  • He found consistent, elevated production in China's CBA in 2025-26, 20.0 points and 9.4 rebounds for Jiangsu Dragons, after years of NBA roster instability.
  • He sustained a professional career across parts of nine years since being drafted despite multiple documented knee surgeries dating to high school and a partial season at Duke.

▼ BEAR CASE

  • Scoring in the 0th percentile of the class before the draft
  • Three-point volume in the 0th percentile of the class before the draft
  • He has not appeared in an NBA game since the 2023-24 season, 16 games with Brooklyn and 7 with the Lakers.
  • He was waived by Charlotte in Oct. 2024 before playing a single regular-season game for the team that had just signed him.
