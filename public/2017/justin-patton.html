Chicago drafted him sixteenth and traded him to Minnesota that same night as part of the Jimmy Butler deal — Minnesota received Butler and Patton's rights for Kris Dunn, Zach LaVine and the rights to Lauri Markkanen (7). He signed with Detroit on Jun. 26, 2020, and was traded from Detroit to the LA Clippers on Nov. 19, 2020, alongside Luke Kennard (12), in the same three-team deal with Brooklyn; the Clippers waived him six days later without his appearing in a game for them. He signed a two-way contract with Houston on Feb. 19, 2021, his last NBA contract, and has played since in Israel, France, China, Taiwan, the Dominican Republic and Greece, signing with Al Ahli in Saudi Arabia on Sep. 3, 2025.
