SCOUTING REPORT

Milwaukee took him #17 overall out of Michigan. He had just gone for 11.0 points, 5.3 rebounds and 1.3 assists in 38 games at 30.4 minutes a night. He went 21 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #38, drafted 17. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #20 to #60 on him, a spread of 40 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 22 games, 0 starts, 3.2 minutes — 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.1 on 56.3% shooting. That ranked him #44 in the class and marked him at $4.55, -57.2% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Jilin Northeast — Chinese CBA, then Cangrejeros de Santurce — Puerto Rico's BSN.

Against a frozen IPO of $10.64, today's mark of $27.93 is +162.5%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Milwaukee drafted him seventeenth directly; no draft-night trade. He played four seasons there (2017-2021), appearing in 8 playoff games in 2018-19, then signed with Houston for 2020-21 (23 games, 6.1 points). He moved through multiple 10-day contracts with Toronto in 2021-22, then a partially guaranteed two-year deal reported Jul. 8, 2022. He signed an Exhibit 10 deal with Orlando in Sep. 2023, was waived that October, and spent early 2024 with Orlando's G League affiliate, the Osceola Magic (19.4 points, 9.5 rebounds across that stretch), before a 10-day contract with Philadelphia on Mar. 19, 2024 — his most recent confirmed NBA action. He played in China's CBA in 2024-25, with reporting disagreeing on the exact club.
