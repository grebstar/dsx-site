SCOUTING REPORT

Philadelphia took him first overall out of Washington. He had just gone for 23.2 points, 5.7 rebounds and 5.9 assists in 25 games at 35.7 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #1, pick 1. All 4 boards had him at first. There was no disagreement to price.

2017-18: 14 games, 0 starts, 18.1 minutes — 7.1 / 3.1 / 3.8 on 40.5% shooting. That ranked him #24 in the class and marked him at $39.70, -96.0% against his IPO. 2025-26: 5 games, 0 starts, 7.2 minutes — 0.8 / 0.2 / 1.6 on 18.2% shooting. That ranked him #21 in the class and marked him at $51.92, -94.8% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $1,000.00, today's mark of $51.92 is -94.8%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Philadelphia took him first and traded him a year and a half later. On Feb. 7, 2019 he went to Orlando for Jonathon Simmons, a top-20-protected 2020 first-round pick (it converted to a 2022 second) and a 2019 second-round pick. Orlando exercised his 2020-21 option that September and signed him to a 3-year, $50 million extension on Dec. 21, 2020, then let his rights lapse in the summer of 2024 without a new deal.

He went unsigned through most of 2024-25 before a rest-of-season contract with Sacramento on Feb. 12, 2025 (21 games, 2.9 points a night), then spent part of 2025-26 with Toronto's G League affiliate, Raptors 905, before signing a 10-day contract with the Raptors on Mar. 23, 2026 — his first NBA appearance in close to a year.
