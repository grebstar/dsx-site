Sacramento took him fifth overall out of Kentucky. He had just gone for 16.7 points, 4.0 rebounds and 4.6 assists in 36 games at 29.6 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #4, pick 5. The boards ranged from second to ninth on him, a spread of 7 places across 4 of them.

That ranked him fourth in the class and marked him at $495.29, +0.0% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $495.29, today's mark of $495.29 is +0.0%. The board reads that as MARKET GOT IT RIGHT — he is priced about where the consensus put him. He finished tenth in the All-Rookie vote among 2017 draftees.

Sacramento kept him for eight seasons. On Feb. 3-5, 2025 he was traded to San Antonio as part of a three-team, seven-player, seven-pick deal with Chicago, completed the same day as the Doncic-Davis trade between the Lakers and Mavericks: San Antonio received Fox and Jordan McLaughlin; Sacramento received Zach LaVine, Sidy Cissoko, three future first-round picks and three future second-round picks; Chicago received Zach Collins, Tre Jones, Kevin Huerter and its own 2025 first-round pick, previously owed to San Antonio. He agreed to a 4-year, $229 million maximum extension with San Antonio on Aug. 4, 2025, running from 2026-27.
