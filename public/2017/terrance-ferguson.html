SCOUTING REPORT

Oklahoma City took him #21 overall out of Adelaide 36ers in the Australian NBL. He had just gone for 4.6 points, 1.1 rebounds and 0.6 assists in 30 games at 15.2 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #20, pick 21. The boards ranged from #19 to #31 on him, a spread of 12 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 61 games, 12 starts, 12.5 minutes — 3.1 / 0.8 / 0.3 on 41.4% shooting. That ranked him #33 in the class and marked him at $17.77, -68.8% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Santos de San Luis Potosi — Mexico's LNBP.

Against a frozen IPO of $56.94, today's mark of $21.38 is -62.4%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player.

Oklahoma City drafted him twenty-first directly; no draft-night trade. On Dec. 8, 2020, OKC traded him, Danny Green and Vincent Poirier to Philadelphia for Al Horford, the draft rights to Theo Maledon and Vasilije Micic, and a protected 2025 first-round pick. On Mar. 25, 2021, a three-team deal sent him (with Poirier and draft considerations) to the New York Knicks, who waived him four days later without his appearing in a game. He has since played in Greece, the G League, Poland, Kosovo (a contract terminated after one game), and Mexico, and is now with Club Atletico Welcome in Uruguay.
