San Antonio took him #29 overall out of Colorado. He had just gone for 18.1 points, 4.1 rebounds and 4.4 assists in 34 games at 32.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #32, pick 29. The boards ranged from #19 to #60 on him, a spread of 41 places across 4 of them.

That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, +1931.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $19.52, today's mark of $396.43 is +1931.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

San Antonio drafted him twenty-ninth directly and signed him Jul. 6, 2017; no draft-night trade. He fractured his wrist during a G League assignment to the Austin Spurs that October. He signed a 4-year, $73 million rookie-scale extension with San Antonio on Dec. 21, 2020. On Feb. 10, 2022, San Antonio traded him to Boston for Josh Richardson, Romeo Langford, a 2022 first-round pick (which became Blake Wesley) and a 2028 first-round pick swap; he debuted for Boston the next day. He won the 2024 NBA championship with Boston and a 2024 Paris Olympics gold medal with Team USA, added to the roster after Kawhi Leonard's injury withdrawal, and signed a 4-year, $125.9 million extension with Boston on Jul. 1, 2024.
