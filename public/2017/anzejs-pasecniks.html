SCOUTING REPORT

Orlando took him #25 overall out of Gran Canaria in the Spanish ACB. He had just gone for 7.8 points, 3.1 rebounds and 0.3 assists in 35 games at 16.5 minutes a night. He went 14 picks earlier than the boards had him — composite #39, drafted 25. Somebody in that room saw it differently. The boards ranged from #18 to #60 on him, a spread of 42 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: he did not appear in an NBA game. Dreamland Gran Canaria — Spanish ACB and the EuroCup. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Tofas SC — Turkish BSL and the FIBA Europe Cup.

Against a frozen IPO of $9.48, today's mark of $17.77 is +87.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay.

Orlando drafted him twenty-fifth and immediately traded him to Philadelphia for a top-20-protected 2020 first-round pick (which became Tyrese Maxey) and a 2020 second-round pick (which became Yam Madar). He played 2019 Summer League with Washington, signed there, was waived Oct. 19, 2019, then joined Washington's G League affiliate before a two-way contract and his NBA debut on Dec. 18, 2019. Since leaving the NBA he has played for Real Betis and Zunder Palencia in Spain, Metropolitans 92 in France, a two-way stint with the Milwaukee Bucks (signed Aug. 2024, waived that October without a game), Tofas in Turkey, and Hsinchu Lioneers in Taiwan, before signing with Aliaga Petkim in Turkey on Aug. 26, 2026.
