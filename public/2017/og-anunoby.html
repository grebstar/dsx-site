SCOUTING REPORT

Toronto took him #23 overall out of Indiana. He had just gone for 11.1 points, 5.4 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 16 games at 25.1 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #26, pick 23. The boards ranged from #15 to #60 on him, a spread of 45 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 74 games, 62 starts, 20.0 minutes — 5.9 / 2.5 / 0.7 on 47.1% shooting. That ranked him eleventh in the class and marked him at $149.42, +348.6% against his IPO. 2025-26: 67 games, 67 starts, 33.2 minutes — 16.7 / 5.2 / 2.2 on 48.4% shooting. That ranked him seventh in the class and marked him at $269.91, +710.4% against his IPO.

Against a frozen IPO of $33.31, today's mark of $269.91 is +710.4%. The board reads that as MARKET UNDERVALUED HIM — he is worth more now than the pre-draft consensus was willing to pay. He finished eleventh in the All-Rookie vote among 2017 draftees.

Toronto drafted him twenty-third directly; no draft-night trade. He signed a 4-year, $72 million extension with Toronto on Dec. 21, 2020. On Dec. 30, 2023, Toronto traded him, Precious Achiuwa and Malachi Flynn to the New York Knicks for RJ Barrett, Immanuel Quickley and a 2024 second-round pick; he debuted for New York Jan. 1, 2024. He declined a $19.93 million player option that June and re-signed with New York on Jul. 6, 2024, a 5-year, $212.5 million contract with a 2028-29 player option.
