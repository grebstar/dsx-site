SCOUTING REPORT

Dallas took him ninth overall out of NC State. He had just gone for 18.1 points, 4.6 rebounds and 6.2 assists in 32 games at 34.8 minutes a night. The boards and the draft room agreed almost exactly: composite #7, pick 9. The boards ranged from sixth to ninth on him, a spread of 3 places across 4 of them.

2017-18: 69 games, 69 starts, 29.7 minutes — 15.2 / 3.8 / 5.2 on 39.5% shooting. That ranked him fifth in the class and marked him at $396.43, +46.9% against his IPO. 2025-26: he did not appear in an NBA game. Real Madrid — Spanish ACB and the EuroLeague, two games in each.

Against a frozen IPO of $269.91, today's mark of $36.38 is -86.5%. The board reads that as MARKET OVERVALUED HIM. That is a statement about the pre-draft price, not a verdict on the player. He finished fifth in the All-Rookie vote among 2017 draftees.

Dallas drafted him ninth. On Jan. 31, 2019, Dallas traded him, DeAndre Jordan and Wesley Matthews, plus two future first-round picks, to New York for Kristaps Porzingis, Tim Hardaway Jr., Courtney Lee and Trey Burke. New York traded him again in Feb. 2021, sending him and a 2021 second-round pick to Detroit for Derrick Rose. Detroit declined his qualifying offer that August, and he has since played on training-camp and one-year deals with Portland (2021-22), Charlotte (2022-23) and Brooklyn (2023-24), plus a season with Real Madrid in the EuroLeague (2024-25). He signed a training-camp deal with Dallas, the team that drafted him, on Sep. 18, 2025, but was waived Oct. 17, 2025, without making the regular-season roster; he joined the Sioux Falls Skyforce, Miami's G League affiliate, that January.
